Transcribe this recording exactly as it was spoken. It is a member of staff speaking an answer for
their monthly performance check-in or review: what they did, what is behind and what comes next.

- Write only the words that were said, in the order they were said. No title, no notes, no
  summary, no labels.
- Keep the speaker's own words and grammar, including Nigerian English, Pidgin and any Yoruba,
  Igbo or Hausa. Do not translate, correct or tidy them: tidying is a separate step the speaker
  chooses.
- Write figures, amounts, dates and percentages as the speaker said them. Spell names, places and
  products as they sound when you cannot be sure of them.
- Use ordinary punctuation and start a new paragraph when the speaker changes subject.
- If there is no speech in the recording, reply with nothing at all.
