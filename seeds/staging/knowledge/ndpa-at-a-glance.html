Data Protection

NDPA at a Glance

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 on one page: who it covers, the rules for using personal data, people's rights, and what to do when something goes wrong.

1Who it covers

Anyone who collects or uses personal data in Nigeria, or about people in Nigeria, on a computer or on paper. That includes MyCityApp and everyone who works here. Section 2

Personal data is anything that identifies a person: a name, a phone number, an ID number, a location, an online identifier.

2The regulator

The Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) registers organisations, hears complaints, investigates and can impose penalties. It replaced the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau. Sections 4 to 7, 64

3Six principles

  1. Fair, lawful and open.
  2. For a clear purpose, and only that purpose.
  3. No more than the purpose needs.
  4. Kept no longer than needed.
  5. Accurate and up to date.
  6. Kept secure.

Section 24

4A lawful reason for every use

  • Consent, freely given and clear; never a pre-ticked box.
  • Contract with the person.
  • Legal obligation.
  • Vital interests of a person.
  • Public interest or official authority.
  • Legitimate interests that do not override the person's rights.

Sections 25 and 26

5People's rights

To be told, to get a copy, to correct, to delete, to restrict, to withdraw consent, to object (absolutely, for marketing), to move their data, and not to be judged by a machine alone. They can complain to the NDPC.

Sections 34 to 38, 46

6Sending data abroad

Only to a country or recipient with adequate protection, or under a condition the Act allows, such as the person's informed consent or a contract with them. The reason is recorded each time.

Sections 41 to 43

7When something goes wrong

A breach is any loss, change or exposure of personal data: a lost phone, an email to the wrong person, a hacked account, a sheet shared with anyone who has the link.

NowTell your line manager and the Technology team, even if you are not sure.
Within 72 hoursThe company tells the NDPC of a breach likely to put people at risk. Section 40(2)
Immediately, if the risk is highThe company tells the people affected, in plain words. Section 40(3)

8Penalties

Up to the greater of ₦10 million or 2% of annual gross revenue for an organisation of major importance; up to the greater of ₦2 million or 2% for others. An employer answers for what its staff do at work.

Sections 48 and 53
