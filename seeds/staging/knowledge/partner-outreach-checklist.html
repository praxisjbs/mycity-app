Partner Outreach Checklist

Keep it beside you when you approach a restaurant, a venue, an event organiser or any business that could partner with MyCityApp.

1Before you reach out

  • It fits: a business our users would want to find, in a city we cover.
  • There is a reason to talk now: an opening, an event, an out of date listing.
  • It is not already a lead or a partner in Partnerships.
  • You know who decides: the owner, the manager or the marketing lead.
  • You have one specific thing to mention from your research.

2The first message

  • Their name, never "Dear Sir or Madam".
  • Your reason, from your research, in the first line.
  • What is in it for them: more diners, a fuller night, tickets sold.
  • One ask: a fifteen-minute call or visit, with two times to choose from.
  • Short enough to read on a phone.

3Following up

  • Each follow-up adds something new: a result, an event, an idea.
  • Never a bare "just following up".
  • Most outreach runs six to eight touches over two to three weeks, then pauses. HubSpot's guidance
  • After the pause, move it to later and come back when something changes.

4The first meeting

  • Ask about their goals, busiest and quietest times, and customers.
  • Ask what they have tried before, and how it went.
  • Listen more than you talk; show examples from similar partners.
  • Agree the next step before you leave.

5After the meeting

  • Within one working day: thanks, with one detail from the meeting.
  • A short recap they can correct.
  • One useful thing: an example listing or a similar partner's results.
  • The next step, with a date and a time.

6Log it

  • Every call, message and meeting on the lead in Partnerships.
  • Its deal moved to the stage it has reached.
  • How and when they prefer to be contacted.
  • The next step, set as a task with its date.

7Data care

  • A business owner's name, phone number and email are personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: keep only what you need, in the company's systems.
  • If someone asks not to be contacted again, stop at once and note it on the lead. For marketing, that right is absolute. NDPA section 36

MyCity Learning & Development. The follow-up figure is HubSpot's published guidance; everything else is MyCityApp's own practice.
