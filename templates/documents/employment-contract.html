Conduct and Conflicts of Interest 9.1 The Employee shall act in the best interests of the Company at all times, avoid conflicts of interest unless disclosed and approved in writing, and comply with the Company's policies and HR handbook as issued from time to time. A breach of Company policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. ### 10. Intellectual Property and Company Property 10.1 All work the Employee creates in the course of their employment belongs absolutely to the Company. At the Company's request and expense, the Employee will do everything necessary to vest all rights in it fully in the Company. 10.2 All Company property, including equipment, documents, data, accounts and access credentials, must be returned when the employment ends. ### 11. Termination 11.1 During probation, either party may end this employment by giving two weeks' written notice or payment in lieu of notice. After confirmation, either party may end it by giving one month's written notice or payment in lieu of notice. 11.2 The Company may end the employment without notice for gross misconduct, a breach of confidentiality, an undisclosed conflict of interest, conduct that brings the Company into disrepute, or a criminal conviction, or for any reason recognised as sufficient in law. 11.3 On termination, the Company will pay all sums accrued to the Employee up to the termination date, less any amounts the Employee owes the Company. ### 12.
