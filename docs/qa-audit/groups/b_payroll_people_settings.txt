=== F01 | Payroll | Configuration / compliance | Critical | Statutory payroll items are not configured
OBSERVED: PAYE has a single band of ₦0 to ₦200 at 10%, marked 'No Legal Source'. Employee and employer pension, NHF, ITF, NSITF, rent relief and minimum wage are all 'Not Set'. Round 4: this is setup not done, not a missing capability. Payroll → Configurations holds each statutory item with a figure, base, legal source and status, and its help text says items are entered only once they apply ('pension from three employees, ITF from 25. No PAYE at or below the minimum wage') and that later runs compute them from the item's date while locked runs never change. Each employee record has a 'Contributes to NHF' switch, NHF number, pension administrator, state of residence and a yearly rent-relief declaration. There is no per-employee switch to exempt someone from pension or PAYE. The Management role can only view Payroll, so the items could not be entered in this audit.
RECOMMENDED: Load the current statutory PAYE bands, pension, NHF, NSITF and ITF rates from their official sources, with the source recorded on each item. Block computing a run while any mandatory item is 'Not Set'. Have Operations & People enter the 2026 items now: PAYE bands under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (first ₦800,000 at 0%, then 15%, 18%, 21%, 23%, 25% above ₦50M), pension 8% employee and 10% employer, NHF 2.5% of basic, NSITF 1%, ITF 1%, rent relief 20% capped at ₦500,000, minimum wage ₦70,000. Ask the vendor for a per-employee pension/PAYE exemption with a reason and evidence.
WHERE: /payroll?tab=configurations
AUDITOR VERDICT: Confirmed (configuration capability checked; entry needs Operations & People) / Config / product gap
EVIDENCE: Setup not done. The capability exists (dated items with legal source; per-employee NHF and rent relief). Missing: per-employee pension or PAYE exemption. Entry needs an Operations & People login.
