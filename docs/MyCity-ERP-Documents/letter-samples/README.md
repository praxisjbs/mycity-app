# MyCity's Letter Samples, Redacted

The six disciplinary letters MyCity App Ltd's Operations & People sent JBS Praxis on 15/10/2026,
as text, with every name, date and offence detail replaced by a placeholder in square brackets. The
originals name real employees and stay with MyCity; this repository is public, so only these copies
live here (AJ, 27/10/2026).

The shipped letter templates in `templates/documents/` take their wording from these
(`docs/guides/queries.md` §6.7). A template fills the placeholders from the query or sanction.

## What the handbook says

From the MyCityApp Handbook 2026, the sections the chain and the letters rely on:

- **§4.6 Classification of Offences.** Offences are Misconduct, Serious Misconduct or Gross
  Misconduct; a sanction is one or more of a verbal or written warning, suspension, termination,
  dismissal, or civil or criminal action.
- **§4.7.1 Misconduct and Serious Misconduct.** A written query by email from the Line Manager,
  setting out the particulars; the employee answers in writing **not later than 24 hours after
  receipt**. This is the 24-hour response window.
- **§4.7.3 Written Warning.** A second written query within 12 months of a written warning is
  treated as Serious Misconduct.
- **§4.7.4 Suspension.** As a sanction, suspension is **without pay** and usually not more than 7
  days; decided in consultation with the CEO and the HR Manager, and approved in writing by the HR
  Manager. Hence one suspension rung, without pay, approved by the CEO from rank 4. The suspension
  with pay in §4.7.4 and §4.8 is precautionary, during an investigation, and is not a rung.
