The schedule

Ten build weeks, one handover week

M0 and M1 share a window: M1 cannot be demonstrated until the foundation is standing. Two pairs run as parallel tracks to recover the nine days lost to the late start and still land on 18 December.

Where the work is

Reuse against net-new build

Measured against the two reference codebases, file by file, and revised after the client requirements meeting of 10 October. The weight beside each bar is the contracted share of the work. Two milestones carry far more new code than their weight suggests, and they are adjacent.

Environment

One server, two environments, six surfaces

Staging matches production in every respect but data: real SMTP, every feature on, every public URL live. It is marked, never crippled, so training in December runs on a system that behaves exactly like the live one.
