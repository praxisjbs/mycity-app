# MyCityApp Incentive Plan, October 2026

One sheet. Source: `MyCityApp Incentive Plan - Oct 2026.xlsx`. The offer's Incentives text and
Section 4 of the standard offer wording point to this policy. It has no figures personal to anyone.

## Revenue incentives

Every revenue category has a minimum threshold of ₦500,000. The rate is chosen by the band the
revenue falls in and applies to the whole eligible revenue, not only the part above a band.

| Band | Standard rate | Renewal and listing rate |
|---|---|---|
| ₦500,000 to ₦999,999 | 5% | 3% |
| ₦1,000,000 to ₦2,499,999 | 4% | 2.5% |
| ₦2,500,000 to ₦4,999,999 | 3% | 2% |
| ₦5,000,000 and above | 2% | 1% |

| Category | Eligible revenue | Rates | Calculation and conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ticket sales | Eligible ticket revenue per event | Standard | Each event assessed separately; revenue received and reconciled |
| New strategic partnership | Revenue received from a new approved partnership | Standard | Collected revenue attributable to the employee; written attribution and approved commercial terms |
| Partnership renewal | Revenue from a renewed partnership | Renewal and listing | Collected renewal revenue; the employee must materially contribute |
| Advertising / sponsored placement | Advertising or sponsored placement revenue | Standard | Collected revenue; approved pricing and documented sales attribution |
| Paid business listing / vendor package | Revenue from approved paid listings or vendor packages | Renewal and listing | Collected revenue; only approved paid packages |
| Sponsorship / activation / campaign | Revenue from approved sponsorships or commercial campaigns | Standard | Collected revenue; attribution approved and documented before the activity |

## Individual quarterly performance bonus

- Eligible: a consistent monthly performance score of at least 80% throughout the quarter, after the
  performance review and approval.
- Amount: 30% of annual gross salary, paid quarterly, scaled by the quarterly performance score.
- Nothing below an 80% score.
- Paid quarterly after the performance review and Management approval.

## Quarterly team bonus

- Eligible: the team reaches at least 100% of its approved quarterly target for eligible hotel,
  shortlet and restaurant booking revenue.
- Pool: 2% of eligible booking or commission revenue above the team's quarterly target.
- Shared equally among eligible team members unless Management approves another written allocation.
- Paid quarterly after quarter-end reconciliation, on verified results and approved targets.
