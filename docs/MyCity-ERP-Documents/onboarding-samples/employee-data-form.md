# Employee Data Form (blank)

Four pages, filled by hand by the new joiner. Source: `EMPLOYEE DATA FORM.pdf`. Header: MyCityApp
NG, Company, Lagos, Nigeria; a box to affix a passport photograph; Names (Surname First); Position
Applied for; Human Resources Department.

## 1. Personal details

- Surname; Other Names; Former Name(s) (if any)
- Residential Address; Nearest Bus Stop/Landmark
- Native Home Address; Nearest Bus Stop/Landmark
- Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Age
- Nationality; Tribe; State; Local Government; Religion
- Phone Number
- Marital Status: Married / Single / Divorced / Widow

## 2. Family

- (a) Next of Kin: Name, Address, Phone Nos, Relationship
- (b) Particulars of Children: table of Name, Date of Birth, Age, Sex

## 3. Education

Educational, professional or trade qualifications: table of Name of Institution, Date,
Qualification(s), Course of study. "Please attach photocopy of credential."

## 4. Employment

- (a) Employment Records, past and present: table of Period From and To, Name & Address of
  Employer, Position Held, Last Salary, Reason for Leaving
- (b) If presently employed, duties performed and years of experience (free text)
- (c) Authority to Check References: the applicant authorises the Company to check the
  employment record with the employers in 4(a)

## 5. References

Two persons of good standing familiar with the applicant's character or qualifications: table of
Name, Address, Phone, Occupation, How Long Known.

## 6. General

- (i) How soon can you be available after selection
- (ii) Languages well spoken
- (iii) Hobbies

## 7. Declaration

The particulars are correct to the best of the applicant's knowledge; misrepresentation or
omission of facts is a cause for dismissal. Signature of Applicant / Date.

## Bank Account Details

Recommended bank: Providus Bank. Bank Name; Bank Account Number.

## For Official Use Only

- Employment Details: Date Engaged, Position Offered, Department, Salary, Grade, Dept. Manager's
  Approval
- Comments by Personnel Manager
- Approval by CEO

## Notes for M5

- Addresses are picked, never typed (F27): the residential and native home addresses become
  `AddressField`s; the bus stop or landmark rides on the place's note.
- The bank account number duplicates the Salary Account form; BVN is a number only (decision 4).
- The Official Use block is what the offer and the vacancy already hold; it is not asked again.
