# Offer of Employment, Short Form (redacted)

The second sample offer letter, two pages. Source: `Sample Offer Letter- MCA.pdf`. The standard
wording of 06/11 is longer (Sections 1 to 14 and Schedule A); this one adds the deductions and the
per-unit incentives, which the offer's Benefits and Incentives fields already carry.

---

PRIVATE & CONFIDENTIAL

Date: [ ]

Dear [First name],

OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT: [JOB TITLE IN CAPITALS]

We are pleased to offer you employment with MyCityApp Nigeria as [Job title]. We look forward to
having you join our team and contribute to the growth and strategic direction of MyCityApp and
MyLagosApp.

Please find the details of our offer below:

**Employment Details**

- Position: [Job title]
- Reporting Line: [Role reported to, e.g. Chief Executive Officer]
- Employment Type: [Full-time]
- Work Arrangement: [Hybrid (2 days in the office and 3 days remotely)]
- Location: [City]
- Resumption Date: [Start date, e.g. 3rd November 2026]
- Probation Period: [Three (3) months]
- Working Hours: [Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.]

**Remuneration**

- Basic Salary: [ ]
- Internet & Recharge Allowance: [ ]
- Transportation Allowance: [ ]

Total Monthly Compensation: [ ]

**Deductions**

- Applicable Personal Income Tax (PAYE).
- Employee pension contribution.

**Benefits & Incentives**

- Fifteen (15) working days annual leave after six (6) months of continuous service.
- Eligibility for commercial commissions and performance-based incentives under the Company's
  incentive policy.
- Business onboarding incentive of ₦5,000 per successfully onboarded business.
- Job listing incentive of ₦2,000 per successfully listed job.
- Company-funded approved business meetings, events and activations.

If this offer is acceptable to you, kindly signify your acceptance by signing and returning a copy
of this letter.

Upon acceptance, we will proceed with the onboarding process and provide you with a comprehensive
employment contract outlining the full terms and conditions of your employment.

We look forward to welcoming you to MyCityApp Nigeria.

Yours sincerely,

[Signatory name]
Operations & People Lead
MyCityApp Nigeria

**ACCEPTANCE OF OFFER**

I, [ ], hereby accept the offer of employment as outlined above.

Signature: [ ]

Date: [ ]
