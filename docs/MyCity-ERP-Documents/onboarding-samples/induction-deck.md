# HR Induction Deck, Outline (redacted)

Twenty-seven slides presented to every new hire. Source:
`MyCityApp HR Induction Training Slides.pptx`. An outline, not the slides: the management team is
named by role only. PR 6 takes the onboarding plan's steps from slide 26.

## Agenda (slide 3)

1. Who we are: vision, mission, values and our story
2. MycityApp at a glance: business, users, partners and ecosystem
3. What we offer: products, features and city experiences
4. How we show up: brand, voice, content and channels
5. How we work: communication, reporting, approvals and execution
6. Performance and accountability: what good looks like
7. Questions, feedback and next steps

## Reporting cadence (slides 20 and 21)

| Process | Who | When | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Report | All staff | Close of business Friday | Weekly Report Tracker |
| Monthly Report | All staff | Close of business on the 25th | Monthly Report Form |
| Monthly Performance Review | All staff | Close of business on the 25th | MPR Form |
| MPR Meeting | All staff | 26th to 27th | MPR Form and work details |
| MSPR Meeting | Management team | 28th monthly | Performance and strategy review |
| Quarterly Townhall | Relevant teams | First working day | Priorities and alignment |
| Marketing Weekly Meeting | Marketing | Every Monday | Marketing priorities |
| Payout Days | Concerned staff and vendors | Wednesday and Friday | Invoices and receipts |
| Salary | All staff | Last working day of the month | |

## Your First 30 Days (slide 26), the plan's default steps

| Step | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Days 1 to 7 | Understand the business, your team, your role, tools, trackers and key stakeholders |
| Weeks 2 to 3 | Days 8 to 21 | Start owning deliverables; ask questions; build relationships; learn the customer and city context |
| Week 4 | Days 22 to 30 | Demonstrate delivery; review early wins and gaps; align priorities with your manager for the next 60 to 90 days |

The induction session itself (this deck) is the first step of Week 1.
