# Providus Bank Salary Account Opening Form (blank)

Four scanned pages, the bank's own form, completed in capital letters. Source:
`SALARY ACCOUNT FORM.pdf`. It is a third party's form: MyCity passes it to the bank, so the system
only collects the signed copy as a checklist document. Its fields are listed here so the checklist
item and the employee record agree on what is asked.

## Page 1 · Salary Account Opening Form

Title; Surname; First Name; Middle Name; Date of Birth; Bank Verification No. (BVN); Marital Status
(Single, Married, Others); Gender; Mother's Maiden Name; Means of Identification; ID Number; ID
Issue Date; ID Expiry Date; Occupation; Status/Job Title; Position / Office of the Signatory.
Residential address: House Number, Street Name, City/Town, Nearest Bus Stop/Landmark, Local
Government Area, State, Nationality, State of Origin (Nigerian). Religion; Mobile Number (1) and
(2); E-mail; Resident Permit No. with issue and expiry dates (non-Nigerians); dual citizenship; for
US citizens, Tax Identification Number; Employee Identification Number (where applicable); Class of
Signatory; Share Holding; Signature and Date; Employer's Name; Date of Employment.

## Page 2 · Mandate Card, Services, Declaration

- Mandate card: Account Number, Account Name, Mobile Phone No., Other Phone No., Contact Address,
  Title, Name, signature mandate, class of signatory, passport photograph.
- Account services required: debit and credit card preference, online banking (mobile, internet,
  USSD, biometric ATM, soft token), transaction alerts (SMS, email) and threshold, statement
  preferences and frequency, cheque book.
- Declaration to the bank, with Name, Signature and Date.

## Pages 3 and 4 · Terms and Conditions

The bank's standard account terms. Not reproduced: nothing in them is asked of MyCity's system.

## Notes for M5

- The checklist item is the completed, signed form (an upload through the upload engine).
- BVN is entered once on the upload page as a number, 11 digits, restricted and masked
  (decision 4); the form's BVN box is the same number.
- Employer's Name and Date of Employment come from the employee record (the offer's start date).
