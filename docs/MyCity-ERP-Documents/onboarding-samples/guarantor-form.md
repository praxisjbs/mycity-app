# Guarantor Form (blank)

One page, one per guarantor, filled by the guarantor. Source: `GUARANTORS FORM.pdf`. A box for the
guarantor's passport-size photograph.

## Fields

- Date
- Employee name; Post Held
- Guarantor's Full Name; Date of Birth
- Contact Address (not P.O. Box); Nearest Bus Stop/Landmark
- Residential Address (not P.O. Box); Nearest Bus Stop/Landmark; Local Government Area
- State of Origin; Town; Village
- Present Employment/Occupation
- Telephone Nos
- Relationship with the Employee
- How long have you known the Employee
- Has he or she been convicted or involved in criminal offences; if yes, state
- Do you recommend him or her for employment

## Attestation

The guarantor promises to be liable for any misconduct arising from the employee's discharge of
their duties during their stay in the Company.

Attach a photocopy of the guarantor's identity card, driving licence or passport. A civil servant
guarantor attaches a photocopy of their work ID card.

Signed with the Guarantor's Full Name, Signature and thumb print.

## Notes for M5

- Guarantors are structured with a check status (handoff PR 6): each guarantor is a record with
  these fields, the signed form and the ID copy as documents, and a status Operations & People set.
- Both addresses are picked (`AddressField`).
