    Role Overview

    The Head, Sales & Revenue will lead MyCityApp and MyLagosApp's commercial growth by developing and executing the company's sales and revenue strategy.

    The role will own revenue generation, sales targets, pipeline development, client and merchant acquisition, commercial partnerships and sales team performance, while building a structured and scalable sales function.

    1Sales & Revenue Strategy

    • Develop and execute MyCityApp's and MyLagosApp's overall sales and revenue strategy.
    • Identify new revenue opportunities, products and commercial models.
    • Set clear sales targets and revenue objectives.

    2Business Development

    • Identify and pursue high-value businesses, brands, merchants and corporate clients.
    • Lead high-value pitches, presentations and commercial negotiations.
    • Convert opportunities into paying clients and long-term accounts.

    3Sales Team Leadership

    • Build, lead and develop the sales team.
    • Establish clear sales processes, standards and accountability.
    • Coach team members on prospecting, pitching, negotiation and closing.

    Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

      Required Experience and Qualifications

      • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Economics, Finance or a related field.
      • 8 or more years in Sales, Business Development, Revenue Management or commercial roles.
      • Proven experience leading sales teams and delivering revenue targets.
      • A track record of personally closing significant business deals.
      • Strong B2B sales, client acquisition and negotiation experience.

      What We Are Looking For

      A commercial leader, not simply a sales administrator.Someone who can find opportunities, open doors, build relationships, negotiate, close deals and build a high-performing sales engine around them.
