## What everything is called

Alongside the documents above, one thing to look at rather than send: what each screen is called.

We have named every module and every page in the system. These names are not cosmetic. Each one
becomes a web address, an entry in the search bar, a permission your team is granted or denied,
and a line in the training material your staff will be given in December. Changing a name after
the system is built means changing all four, so we would rather agree them now, while it costs
nothing.

The quickest way to review them is in the prototype, where you can click through every one. The
full list is below.

**Your day-to-day**
Home · My Workspace · Employee Self-Service

**Work**
Staff Community · Mailbox

**People**
People · Performance · Queries &amp; Sanctions · Recruitment · Payroll

**Business**
Partnerships · Expenses

**System**
Knowledge Hub · Praxis AI · Settings

**The public site, seen by people outside MyCity**
Careers · Vacancy · Apply · Assessment · Sign Offer · Verify Document

These six are not part of the staff application. They are a separate site at
**go.mycityapp.io**, so a job link you share, or an offer a candidate signs, never touches the
staff system: no sign-in, no staff session, nothing installed. The staff application lives at
**hub.mycityapp.io**.

Fifteen modules and eighty-five screens in total. Three worth a word of explanation:

- **Home** is the landing page, not a dashboard of charts. Three tabs: an overview, the alerts
  that need somebody to act, and your reports.
- **Employee Self-Service** is where a member of staff sees their own record and nothing else.
  Every tab inside it begins with "My" so the boundary is obvious.
- **Queries &amp; Sanctions** is deliberately not called Discipline. Most of what goes through it
  is a question that closes with no action, and naming it after the worst outcome would make
  managers reluctant to use it.

If any of these do not match how MyCity talks internally, tell us the word you use and we will
take yours. That is the whole point of asking now.

If nothing here needs changing, there is nothing for you to do. If something does, tell us by
**Friday 16 October** and we will change it before Milestone 1 is handed over for your testing on
the 19th. After that a rename is a change to four places rather than one.
