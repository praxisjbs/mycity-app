My Workspace is where you plan and do your own work. Your line manager can set tasks for you
here, and you add your own beside them, so everything you have to do sits on one board. It has
five tabs: **Today**, **Tasks**, **Projects**, **Calendar** and **Analytics**.

Everyone at MyCity has My Workspace. If you can set work for other people, as a line manager, a
head of department, Management or Operations and People, you also see their work: **My Team** on
Tasks and Analytics, and **Team This Week** on Today.

## Today {#workspace-today screen=workspace/today icon=check}

Today is your day on one screen: what is due, what is late, your meetings, and your week so far.

![My Workspace, Today](shot:workspace-today)

### Read your day {#workspace-today-read}

1. Open [My Workspace · Today](/workspace?tab=today). The date shows under the title on a
   computer.
2. The four tiles at the top count **Due Today**, **Overdue**, **Meetings** and **Clocked In**.
   **Clocked In** shows the time you clocked in, or **Not Yet**.
3. **Your Day** lists your open tasks that are late or due today, the late ones first. A red pill
   at its top right counts how many are late.
4. **From Your Manager** lists the open tasks someone else set for you. A card on Today shows the
   initials of the person who set it.
5. **Diary** lists today's meetings and deadlines, with their time or **All Day**.
6. **Recent Activity** says what other people did to your tasks since the last working day.
7. Press any task to open it. Read [Open a task](#workspace-task-panel).

When a card is empty it says so: **Nothing due today.**, **Nothing set for you.**,
**Nothing in the diary.** or **Nothing new.**

On a phone the tiles sit two to a row, then **Your Day**, then **My Week**, then the other cards
below.

| A diary line reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | A meeting you made, or one you accepted |
| **Tentative** | An invitation you have not answered yet |
| **Declined** | An invitation you said no to |
| **Deadline** | One of your tasks is due at that time |

### Add a task from Today {#workspace-today-add needs="workspace create"}

1. Under **From Your Manager**, press **Add Task**. On a phone, press the round button with a
   plus at the bottom right, then **New Task**.
2. Fill in the task as [Add a task](#workspace-add-task) says, and press **Create Task**.

### Write your week's line {#workspace-week}

**My Week** on Today is built from your board, so there is no weekly tracker to fill
in. It lists **Done This Week**, **Planned Next Week** and **Blocked**, each with how many. On a
computer each shows its first five; press **View All** for the rest. On a phone, press a line to
see its tasks. An empty list says None.

You add one line yourself: **Support Needed**, what would help you this week. Your manager reads
it.

![Support Needed, with Tidy With AI](shot:workspace-support-needed)

1. Open [My Workspace · Today](/workspace?tab=today) and find **My Week**.
2. Press **Write Support Needed**. A window titled **Support Needed** opens.
3. Type your line in **Support Needed**, for example `Need the venue contract signed by Tuesday`.
4. Press **Save Line**. The hub says **Support Needed saved** and shows your line on the card.
5. To have it tidied instead, press **Tidy With AI** before you save. A short **Summary** of your
   week appears in its place.
6. Press **Use Summary** to keep it. The hub says **Summary saved** and the card shows it as
   **Summary**. Press **Discard Summary** to go back to your own line.
7. To take your line off, empty **Support Needed** and press **Save Line**. Press **Cancel** to close
   without saving.

Tidy With AI only suggests. Nothing changes until you press **Use Summary**. If the field is empty
it says **Write a line first**. If the assistant is switched off, the hub says **Tidy is off** and
your line stays as you wrote it.

### Read your team's week {#workspace-team-week needs="team"}

If you can set work for others, **Team This Week** sits beside **My Week**. It shows each **Person** with
their **Done**, **Planned** and **Blocked** counts and their **Support Needed** line.

1. Open [My Workspace · Today](/workspace?tab=today) and find **Team This Week**.
2. Press a person's row. Their week opens in a window: the same three lists and their line.
3. On a phone, the card shows the first five people. Press **View All** for everyone.

You can read your team's weeks but never write them.

## Tasks {#workspace-tasks screen=workspace/tasks icon=list}

Tasks is your board: every task you lead, share or set for someone else, in four columns,
**To Do**, **In Progress**, **In Review** and **Done**. A task your manager set and a task you added yourself sit on the same
board.

![My Workspace, Tasks](shot:workspace-tasks)

### Read the board {#workspace-tasks-read}

1. Open [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks).
2. The tiles count your **Open** tasks, those **Due This Week**, those **Overdue**, and those you
   finished in the last 30 days, **Completed, 30d**.
3. Each column shows how many tasks it holds. When a column holds more than 25, press **Load More**
   at its foot. An empty column says **Nothing in this stage**. With
   no tasks at all, the board says **No Tasks Yet**.
4. Press **List** at the top to see the same tasks as a table: **Task**, **Assignee**, **Status**,
   **Due** and **Priority**. Press **Board** to go back. Press the **Due** or **Priority** heading to
   sort the list by it; press it again to reverse it.
5. Type in **Search tasks** to find a task by its number or its words.
6. Tick **Show Cancelled** to list cancelled tasks under the board: **Task**, **Assignee**,
   **Cancelled** (the day) and **Reason**. Press one to read it. With none it says
   **Nothing Cancelled**.
7. {needs="team"} Press **My Team** to see the tasks of the people you set work for, and **Mine** to
   see your own again.

On a phone, the columns are chips across the top with their counts. Press a chip to see that
column's tasks. **List** on a phone shows cards: the task, **Assignee**, **Status** and **Due**.
Press **Sort** to order them. Under a board card, **Move To** also offers **Move Up** and
**Move Down**.

A card shows the task's title, small pills and the round initials of the person who leads it.

| A pill reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| A date, **Today** or **Tomorrow**, or **Done** | When it is due, or that it is done |
| *Overdue 2 days* (in red) | It is late, by that many days |
| **No Date** | It has no due date |
| **Urgent** or **High** | Its priority |
| **Commitment** | One of the month's priorities (see [Commitments](#workspace-commitments)) |
| *Shared +2* | Two more people share it with the person who leads it |
| **Blocked** | Something is stopping it (see [Mark a task blocked](#workspace-blocked)) |
| **Waiting** | It waits on another task to be done first |
| *2/5* | Two of its five steps are done |
| **Left** | The person who leads it has left MyCity: give it to someone else |

### Add a task {#workspace-add-task needs="workspace create"}

1. Open [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks).
2. Press **New Task** at the top right. On a phone, it is also on the round button with a plus.
   ![The New Task form](shot:workspace-new-task)
3. Type what needs doing in **Task**, for example `Visit the venue`.
4. Leave **Type** on **Individual** for a task one person does. For work shared by several
   people, read [Share a task](#workspace-share).
5. {needs="team"} To set the task for someone you set work for, search for them in **Assign To** and
   pick them. Leave it empty to keep the task for yourself.
6. To put it in a project, pick it in **Project**.
7. Choose a **Cadence** if it helps: **Daily** is due by the end of the day, **Weekly** by the end
   of the week.
8. Choose the **Due** date, and the **Priority**: **Urgent**, **High**, **Normal** or **Low**.
9. If it is work for one of your scorecard lines, pick it in **Counts Towards** (see
   [Counts Towards](#workspace-counts-towards)).
10. Add anything else in **Notes**, and press **Create Task**.

The hub gives the task its number, such as TSK-0412, and says so. A task you set for someone else
says *TSK-0412 set for* and their name, and they are told.

| Field | What to enter | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Task** | What needs doing, in a few words | Yes |
| **Type** | **Individual** or **Shared** | Yes |
| **Assign To** | Someone you set work for, or nobody for yourself | No |
| **Project** | One of the active projects | No |
| **Cadence** | **None**, **Daily** or **Weekly** | No |
| **Due** | The day it is due, today or later | No, but a commitment needs one |
| **Priority** | **Normal** unless you change it | Yes |
| **Counts Towards** | A scorecard line or key result of the person doing it | No |
| **Commitment** | Tick **Mark as Commitment** for a priority of the month | No |
| **Notes** | Anything that helps | No |

> **Tip:** A field marked **Required** must be filled before **Create Task** works. **Type**
> only shows when there is someone you could share the task with.

### Share a task {#workspace-share needs="workspace create"}

A shared task has one lead, who answers for it, and the people sharing it beside them. Everyone on
it sees it on their board, moves it and comments. When one of them marks it done, it is done for
everyone.

1. Press **New Task** on [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks) and type the **Task**.
2. Under **Type**, press **Shared**.
   ![Sharing a task](shot:workspace-share)
3. {needs="team, people view"} Under **Share With**, choose **People** to pick people one by one, or
   **Department** to share it with a whole department.
4. In **People**, search for each person and pick them. You may pick colleagues in your own
   department, and anyone you set work for.
5. {needs="team"} Choose the **Lead** in its list: **You**, or one of the people you picked whom you
   set work for. For a **Department**, pick the lead from that department's people.
6. Fill in the rest as for any task, and press **Create Task**. The hub says how many people now
   share it.

A shared task counts on its lead's scorecard, board order and week. Only its lead can mark it a
commitment.

### Move a task along {#workspace-move needs="workspace edit"}

1. On a computer, drag the card to another column, or onto another card to put it just above.
2. Or press the three dots at the foot of the card. Choose **Move Up** or **Move Down** to change
   its place in the column, or **Move to** and the column's name, such as Move to Done.
3. On a phone, press **Move To** under the card and choose where it goes.
4. The hub says *Moved to Done*, or **Order saved** when only its place changed.

Moving a late task to **Done** first asks **Why Late**. Pick the reason, such as **Waiting on
Others**, and press **Mark Done**. If it says **Pick a reason**, pick one from the list. If it says
**No late reasons are switched on**, press **Mark Done**: it moves without one.

A task waiting on another stays in **To Do** until that one is done. Moving it says why, with a
warning mark, such as *Waiting on TSK-0397: Book the venue*. Its **Waiting On** lists what it waits on.

Other people's changes to a task you can see appear on your board as they happen.

### Open a task {#workspace-task-panel}

Press a task anywhere in the hub, on Today, the board, the calendar or a link, and its panel opens.
On a computer it slides in at the right; on a phone it fills the screen.

![A task's panel](shot:workspace-task-panel)

The panel has four views at the top: **Details**, **Steps**, **Comments** and **History**.

- **Details** shows the task's **Number**, who leads it (**Assignee**, or **Lead** for a shared
  task, with **Shared With** under it), its **Project**, **Set By**, **Due**, **Priority**,
  **Status** and **Cadence**, then its notes. A blocked task shows the block in a box at the top.
- **Counts Towards** names the scorecard line or key result it is for. Press **Open** beside it to
  go there.
- **Open Record** opens the record the task is about, such as a review or a key result.
- **Waiting On** lists the tasks this one waits for, and **Holding Up** the tasks that wait for
  it.
- **Watchers** lists who is told when it is done, blocked or commented on.
- **Steps** lists its steps with a tick each. **History** lists everything that happened to it,
  newest first, then **Blocked Before** for earlier blocks.
- It also shows **Repeats** when it repeats, **Why Late** once a reason is given, and **Cancelled**
  with the reason. **Reminders** lists the reminders set. A blocked task's box says how many days
  it has been blocked and when it should clear. Press its **Project** line to open the project.

**Steps** and **Comments** show their counts, such as Steps 2/5. An empty list says so, such as
**No steps yet.** or **Nobody is watching.**

The buttons at the foot are **Close**, **Edit Task**, **Mark Blocked** (or **Clear Blocker**), and
**More Actions**, the three dots, for the rest.

A cancelled task opens read only. Its **Cancelled** line gives the reason, and only **Close** stays
at the foot. A done task has no **Mark Blocked** and no **Set Reminders**.

### Plan the steps and links {#workspace-plan needs="workspace edit"}

The person who leads a task, the people sharing it and whoever manages it plan it. Anyone who can
see it may tick a step.

1. Open the task and choose **Steps**.
2. Press **Add Step**. Type the **Step**, such as `Order banners`, and pick a **Due** day if it has
   one. Press **Add Step**. The hub says **Step added**.
3. Tick a step when it is done. Its line then says who did it.
4. Press a ticked step again to untick it. To delete a step, press the bin at the end of its line. A
   task holds up to 25 steps; **Add Step** goes once it has 25.
5. To make the task wait for another one, choose **Details**, then press **Add Link** beside
   **Waiting On**.
6. In **Wait On Task**, search **Waits On** for the other task, pick it and press **Add Link**. The
   hub says *Waiting on TSK-0397*.
7. A waiting task cannot leave **To Do** until the other is done. To start it anyway, press **Go
   Ahead** on the link, give a **Reason** if you like, and press **Go Ahead**. The hub says
   **Going ahead**.
8. To stop waiting on a task, press the cross at the end of its line under **Waiting On**. The hub
   says **Link removed**. A link someone went ahead on reads **Overridden**.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *TSK-0397 already waits on this task* | Linking them would make each wait on the other | Pick a different task |
| **Pick a task** | **Waits On** is empty | Search and pick the task |
| A task waits on, or holds up, at most 25 others | The task is full | Remove one first |

### Watch a task {#workspace-watch}

1. Open the task. Under **Watchers**, press **Watch**. You are told when it is done, blocked,
   cleared, cancelled or commented on. The hub says **Watching**.
2. Press **Stop Watching** when you no longer need to hear about it. The hub says
   **Stopped watching**.
3. {needs="workspace edit"} If you plan the task, you can add someone else: press the plus beside
   **Watchers**, search **Watcher** for them, and press **Add Watcher**. The hub says their name and
   *is watching*. Only people who can already see the task can watch it.
4. {needs="workspace edit"} To take someone off, press the cross beside their name under
   **Watchers**. The hub says **Watcher removed**.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick someone** | **Watcher** is empty | Search and pick the person |
| A task has at most 25 watchers | The task is full | Remove one first |

### Mark a task blocked {#workspace-blocked needs="workspace edit"}

1. Open the task and press **Mark Blocked**.
2. Say **What Is Blocking It**, such as `Legal team on leave until Monday`.
3. Pick **Expected Clear** if you know when it will clear, and press **Mark Blocked**.
4. The hub says **Marked blocked**. The card shows **Blocked**, and the person who set it is told.
5. When it clears, open the task and press **Clear Blocker**. Say **How It Cleared** if you like.
6. Under **Due Date**, leave **Move it on** ticked to push the due date on by the days it was
   blocked. It only shows when the task has a due date. Press **Clear Blocker**. The hub says
   **Cleared, due date moved**, or **Blocker cleared** when the date stayed.

### Comment on a task {#workspace-comment}

1. Open the task and choose **Comments**.
2. Type in **Comment** and press **Add Comment**. The newest comments are at the top.
3. Press **Load More** at the foot to read older ones.

Everyone on the task, and the person who set it, is told about a new comment.

### Nudge, remind, repeat {#workspace-more-actions needs="workspace edit"}

Open the task and press **More Actions**, the three dots at the foot of the panel.

![More Actions on a task](shot:workspace-more-actions)

1. **Send Nudge** sends a reminder now. Type the **Message**. If the task has watchers, choose
   **Send To**: **Assignee**, **Watchers** or **Everyone**. Press **Send Nudge**. The hub says
   **Nudge sent**.
2. **Set Reminders** sets up to three reminders before the due time. Choose **First Reminder**, and
   **Second Reminder** and **Third Reminder** if you like: **At Due Time**, **15 Minutes Before**,
   **1 Hour Before**, **1 Day Before** or **2 Days Before**. Press **Set Reminders**. The hub says
   **Reminders set**. A task needs a due date for this. To clear them, set each to **None** and
   press **Set Reminders**: the hub says **Reminders cleared**.
3. **Set Repeat** makes the task come back. In **Repeat**, choose **Every Working Day**, **Weekly**
   or **Monthly**. For **Weekly**, tick the days under **On**. For **Monthly**, pick the day in
   **On Day**. Press **Save Repeat**. The hub says **Repeat set**. Details then shows **Repeats**.
   Only the person who manages the task sees it.
4. On a task that repeats the menu reads **Change Repeat**. To stop it, choose **Does Not Repeat**
   and press **Save Repeat**. The hub says **Repeat stopped**.
5. **Say Why Late** shows on a late task. Pick the reason in **Why Late** and press **Save
   Reason**. The hub says **Reason saved**.

A repeat needs a due date to count from. Without one, **Repeat** says **Needs a due date**.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Nobody else to tell** | Only you would get the nudge | Add a watcher first, or nudge a task someone else leads |
| **That time has passed** | A reminder would fall before now | Pick a later reminder, or move the due date |
| **Pick a day** | **Weekly** has no day ticked | Tick at least one day under **On** |
| **Pick a reason** | **Why Late** is empty, or the reason was just switched off | Pick a reason from the list |

### Edit or cancel a task {#workspace-edit-task needs="workspace edit"}

1. Open the task and press **Edit Task**. If you manage it, the form holds the same fields as New
   Task. If someone else set it for you, it holds only **Priority**, **Notes** and, if you lead it,
   **Commitment**.
2. Change what you need and press **Save Task**. The hub says **Task saved**, and the open panel
   takes the new title at once. Clearing
   **Assign To** gives the task back to you. An approved commitment keeps its person, mark, month
   and criteria.
3. To cancel it, press **More Actions**, then **Cancel Task**. Type the **Reason** and press
   **Cancel Task**. The hub says **Task cancelled** and the panel closes. Press **Keep Task** to
   stop.

Who may change what:

- Its title, due date, cadence, lead, who shares it, project, Counts Towards and repeat belong to
  whoever manages it: the person who set it, and the managers of the person doing it. If someone
  else set you a task, you change its notes, priority and status.
- Only whoever manages a task can cancel it. **Cancel Task** shows only to them.
- An approved commitment cannot be cancelled. Rescope it instead (see
  [Rescope or recover a commitment](#workspace-rescope)).

### Commitments {#workspace-commitments needs="workspace create"}

A commitment is one of the month's priority tasks, with what "done" means written down. Mark the
month's commitments and send them together within its first three weekdays, public holidays aside.
Your reviewer approves the month, and from then on each commitment's date is fixed.

1. Press **New Task**, or open a task you lead and press **Edit Task**. Or press ⌘K, type commitment
   and choose **New Commitment**: the **New Commitment** form opens with **Mark as Commitment**
   already ticked.
2. Under **Commitment**, tick **Mark as Commitment**.
3. Write the **Acceptance Criteria**, what must be true when it is done, such as
   `All four certificates filed in People`.
4. Choose the **Month**, this month or next, and a **Due** date inside that month.
5. Press **Create Task** or **Save Task**. The card shows **Commitment**.
6. {needs="workspace edit, performance view"} When the month's commitments are marked, press
   **Send Commitments** at the top of [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks).
   ![Send Commitments](shot:workspace-send-commitments)
7. {needs="team, workspace edit, performance view"} To send for someone you set work for, pick them
   in **Person**.
8. {needs="workspace edit, performance view"} Choose the **Month**. The list shows each
   **Commitment** that will go, its **Due** day and its **State**. If it says **Nothing to Send**,
   mark a commitment for that month first: **Send Commitments** shows once there is one.
9. {needs="workspace edit, performance view"} Press **Send Commitments**. The hub says
   **Commitments sent for approval**. If your reviewer sent them, it says **Commitments approved**.

You can also send them from [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance),
under **My Commitments**.

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Not Sent** | Marked, but its month has not been sent |
| **Awaiting Approval** | Sent, waiting for your reviewer |
| **Returned** | Your reviewer asked for changes: edit it and send again |
| **Due** | Approved and still to do |
| **On Time** | Done by the end of its original date |
| **Late** | Done after its original date |
| **Missed** | Not done when its month ended |
| **Rescoped** | Changed by agreement; it no longer counts for or against you |

A commitment's panel shows its **Acceptance Criteria**, **Month**, **State** and **Original
Date**, the date agreed when the month was approved, then **Rescope Reason** or **Recovery** when it
has one. A later due date never moves it.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Mark a commitment for that month first** | No commitment of that month is waiting to go | Tick **Mark as Commitment** on a task, then send |
| **This month is already waiting for approval** | You sent it already | Wait for your reviewer |
| **Not in that month** | The due date is outside the commitment's month | Pick a date in that month |
| **Give the criteria** | **Acceptance Criteria** is empty | Write what done means |
| **Approved commitments are rescoped, not changed** | Its month is approved | Rescope it instead |
| **Pick a due date** | A commitment needs a due date | Choose a **Due** date in the commitment's month |

### Rescope or recover a commitment {#workspace-rescope needs="workspace edit"}

1. If an approved commitment can no longer be done as agreed, open it and press **Rescope
   Commitment**.
2. Type the **Reason** and press **Send Rescope**. The hub says **Rescope sent for approval**.
3. Whoever approves your commitments is told. On the task's panel they see the **Rescope Reason**
   you gave.
4. To agree, they press **Approve Rescope**. The hub says **Rescope approved**, and the commitment
   reads **Rescoped**.
5. To send it back, they press **Request Changes**, type the **Reason** and press
   **Request Changes**. The hub says **Sent back**.
6. {needs="workspace create"} A commitment that reads **Missed** needs a recovery plan. Open it and
   press **Set Recovery**.
7. Pick the **Owner**, yourself or someone you set work for, and the **Due** date, and press
   **Set Recovery**. The hub says
   **Recovery set**. The owner gets a High task named Recovery and the commitment's title. The
   commitment shows **Recovery** with their name and date.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **A rescope is already waiting** | Someone asked for a rescope at the same moment | Wait for its decision |
| **This commitment already has a recovery** | Someone set its recovery first | Nothing to do |

### Counts Towards {#workspace-counts-towards needs="workspace create"}

**Counts Towards** links a task to the work it serves: one of the scorecard lines of the person
doing it, or a key result they own. Done tasks then show as evidence in their monthly check-in.

1. In **New Task** or **Edit Task**, find **Counts Towards**.
2. Search the list and pick the line or key result. Each shows **Scorecard Line** or **Key Result**
   under it.
3. Press **Create Task** or **Save Task**.

If the person has no approved scorecard and owns no key result, the field says **No lines or key
results**.

### Change the late reasons {#workspace-late-reasons needs="cap workspace.late_reasons"}

When a task is late, people pick why from a short list. You keep that list.

1. Open [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks) and press the cog at the top, **Late
   Reasons**.
   ![Late Reasons](shot:workspace-late-reasons)
2. Press the switch beside a reason to turn it off or on. A reason switched off is no longer
   offered, but answers already given keep it. The hub names the reason and says *switched off* or
   *switched on*.
3. To add one, type it in **Reason** and press **Add Reason**. The hub names it and says *added*. It
   joins the foot of the list.
4. Press **Close**.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Already a reason** | That reason is on the list | Switch the old one back on instead |
| **Use some letters** | The reason has no letters | Type it in words |
| **Required** or *At most 60 characters* | **Reason** is empty, or over 60 characters | Type a short reason |
| Why Late holds at most 25 reasons | The list is full | Reuse a reason that is switched off |

### When something is refused {#workspace-tasks-refused}

The hub says what went wrong in a short message at the foot of the screen, or on the field.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | A field that must be filled is empty | Fill it in |
| *Waiting on TSK-0397* and its title | The task waits on another that is not done | Finish that one, or press **Go Ahead** |
| **This task is already blocked; clear that first** | It is blocked already | Press **Clear Blocker** first |
| **Only whoever set this task, or the lead's manager, can cancel it** | Someone else manages it | Ask them |
| **Only the person who set this task can change that** | You are working the task, not managing it | Ask whoever set it |
| **Not in your team** | You picked someone who does not report to you | Pick someone in your team |
| **Pick a lead** | A department share has no lead | Pick the **Lead** |
| **Nobody to share with** | The department has nobody but the lead | Pick people instead |
| **That card has moved, reload the board** | Someone moved it while you did | Pull down or press refresh, then try again |
| *Project: Project is closed* | The project is complete | Pick another, or ask its owner to reopen it |
| **Needs a due date** | A repeat with no due date | Set **Due** first |
| **Give the task a due date** | A reminder with no due date | Set **Due** first |
| **Cannot see this task** | That watcher cannot open the task | Pick someone who can |
| *At most 200 characters*, or another number | The text is longer than the field takes | Shorten it |
| **Pick people** | **Shared** is chosen with nobody in **People** | Pick at least one person, or choose **Individual** |
| **Pick a department** | **Department** is chosen but empty | Pick the department |
| **Lead it yourself** | You picked a colleague from your department, and someone else leads | Choose **You** as **Lead**, or take them off |
| More than 25 people | That department has more than 25 people | Pick people one by one instead |
| **At most 25 people** under **People** | You picked a 26th person | Share it with fewer people, or pick a department |
| *Counts Towards: Not one of their lines* | The line belongs to someone else | Pick it again after choosing who does the task. Changing **Assign To** clears a line picked for someone else |
| **Not on the staff** | Someone you picked has left MyCity | Take them off and pick someone else |
| **Not saved. Try again.** | The hub could not reach the server | Check your connection, then press the button again |
| **This task was cancelled** | Someone cancelled it while you had it open | Close it. Nothing more can be done to it |
| **This task is closed** | It was moved to **Done** while you had it open | Close the panel and open it again |
| **That task does not exist**, **That step does not exist** or **That link does not exist** | It was removed, or you can no longer see it | Close the panel and open it again |

## Projects {#workspace-projects screen=workspace/projects icon=brief}

A project gathers tasks that belong together, such as a city launch. Anyone can start one, and
everyone can see the list. A project never shows you a task you could not already see.

![My Workspace, Projects](shot:workspace-projects)

### Find a project {#workspace-projects-read}

1. Open [My Workspace · Projects](/workspace?tab=projects).
2. The list shows each **Project** with its number, its **Owner**, when it is **Due**, and how
   many of its tasks you can see are **Open** and **Done**. Press the **Project** or **Due** heading
   to sort by it.
3. Press **Completed** to see finished projects, and **Active** to come back.
4. Type in **Search projects** to find one by number or name.
5. Press a project to open its page.

On a phone each project is a card with its owner, due date and open tasks. **Sort** orders the
cards. With none, the list says **No Projects Yet**.

### Start a project {#workspace-projects-new needs="workspace create"}

1. Press **New Project** at the top right of [My Workspace · Projects](/workspace?tab=projects).
   On a phone it is on the round button with a plus.
2. Type its **Name**, such as `Lekki Launch`. Add **Notes** and a **Due** date if you like.
3. Press **Create Project**. The hub gives it a number, says it started, and opens its page.

You own the projects you start.

### Work in a project {#workspace-project-page needs="workspace create"}

A project's page is a board of its tasks, with the same four columns as Tasks.

![A project's page](shot:workspace-project-page)

1. Open the project from [My Workspace · Projects](/workspace?tab=projects). On a computer, its
   number, owner and due date show under its name.
2. Press **New Task** at the top right. On a phone it is under the project's name, and on
   **Quick Actions**. The form opens with the project already picked.
3. Type in **Search tasks** to find one of its tasks by number or words.
4. Move and open its tasks as on [Tasks](#workspace-move).
5. Press **Projects** at the top to go back to the list.

From any task, press its **Project** line on **Details** to open the project. A project with no tasks
you can see says **Nothing Here**.

You can also put any task you manage into a project from its **Edit Task** form, under
**Project**.

### Change or complete a project {#workspace-project-edit needs="workspace edit"}

Only a project's owner sees these buttons, and people whose access reaches everyone's work, such as
Management.

1. Open the project and press **Edit Project**. Change the **Name**, **Notes** or **Due** and press
   **Save Project**. The hub says **Project saved**.
2. When the work is over, press **Complete Project**. It shows **Completed** and takes no new
   tasks. Its open tasks stay where they are. The hub says its name and *completed*.
3. To bring it back, open it under **Completed** and press **Reopen Project**. The hub says its name
   and *reopened*.

## Calendar {#workspace-calendar screen=workspace/calendar icon=calendar}

Your calendar: your meetings, the meetings you are invited to, your tasks' due dates, your approved
leave and your performance dates. It is always your own; a manager follows the team's work on
Tasks.

![My Workspace, Calendar](shot:workspace-calendar)

### Read your calendar {#workspace-calendar-read}

1. Open [My Workspace · Calendar](/workspace?tab=calendar). It opens on this month.
2. Press **Month**, **Week** or **Day** to change the view. In **Week**, press a day's name to see
   that day.
3. The arrows beside **Today** go back or on by a month, a week or a day. Press **Today** to come
   back to now.
4. **Coming Up** lists what is ahead in the next 30 days. When nothing is ahead, it says
   **Nothing in the next 30 days.**
5. Press an event to open it, a due date to open its task, or a leave line to open your leave
   request.

On the month, deadlines and performance dates show in yellow. On a phone, **Today** and the arrows sit on
one line, with **Month**, **Week** and **Day** under them.

Each line says what it is: **Meeting**, **Deadline**, **Leave**, **Campaign**, **Interview** or
**Event**. Performance dates read **Check-In Due**, **Review Due**, **Key Result Due** or **PIP
Review**; press one to open its record.

### Add an event {#workspace-event-new needs="workspace create"}

1. Press **New Event** at the top of [My Workspace · Calendar](/workspace?tab=calendar). On a phone
   it is also on **Quick Actions**, the round button with a plus.
   ![The New Event form](shot:workspace-new-event)
2. Type the **Event**, such as `MTN venue walkthrough`, and pick the **Date**.
3. **Start** and **End** begin at 10:00 and 11:00. Change them, and the **Kind**, which begins on
   **Meeting**.
4. Search **Attendees** and pick each person you are inviting.
5. Add a **Location**, such as a room or a call link, if it has one.
6. Choose **Who Can See**. **Attendees** is you and the people invited. **Everyone** puts it on
   every calendar. **Only Me** keeps it private.
7. Choose a **Reminder**: **None**, **At Start**, **15 Minutes Before** (the default),
   **1 Hour Before** or **1 Day Before**.
8. Press **Add Event**. The hub says **Added to the calendar**.

Everyone you invite is told in the hub. Those with a work email also get the invitation by email,
which adds it to Outlook, Gmail or their phone's calendar.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Name the event** | Type the event's name |
| **Pick a date** | Pick its date |
| **Ends before it starts** | Set an end time after the start |
| **Not on the staff** (on **Attendees**) | Someone invited has left MyCity: take them off **Attendees** and save again |

### Add to an empty day {#workspace-calendar-day needs="workspace create"}

1. On the month, press an empty day from today on. On **Week** or **Day**, press **Add To Day**
   beside **Nothing booked.**
2. Choose **New Task**, due that day, or **New Event**, on that day.
3. Fill in the form and save it.

Sundays and days already past cannot be added to from the calendar.

### Answer an invitation {#workspace-calendar-answer}

1. Press the event on your calendar. Its window shows **When**, **Kind**, **Organiser**,
   **Reminder**, **Where** and the **Attendees**. Each name says (pending) or (declined) until they
   accept.
2. Press **Accept** or **Decline**. The hub says **Accepted** or **Declined**, and the organiser is
   told.
3. To change your mind, open it again: the other button is still there. Press **Close** to leave it
   as it is.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This event was called off** | The organiser called it off while you had it open | Nothing to do: it leaves your calendar |
| **You are not invited to this event** or **That event does not exist** | The organiser took you off it | Nothing to do. Ask them if you need to be there |

### Change or call off your event {#workspace-calendar-edit needs="workspace edit"}

Only the person who made an event can change it.

1. Press the event and press **Edit Event**. Change what you need and press **Save Event**. The hub
   says **Event changed**.
   ![An event's window](shot:workspace-event) The people invited are told when its time, title or place changes.
2. To cancel it, press **Call Off**. A window titled **Call Off Event** names it. Press **Call Off**
   again, or **Cancel** to keep it.
3. The hub says **Event called off**, and the people invited are told.

## Analytics {#workspace-analytics screen=workspace/analytics icon=chart}

Analytics shows how work is moving: yours, or your team's.

![My Workspace, Analytics](shot:workspace-analytics)

### Read the figures {#workspace-analytics-read}

1. Open [My Workspace · Analytics](/workspace?tab=analytics). On a computer, the dates it covers
   show under the title.
2. Choose the period: **30 Days**, **90 Days** or **This Year**.
3. {needs="team"} Choose **My Team** for the people you set work for, or **Mine** for your own work.
   It opens on **My Team**.
4. The tiles show the **Completion Rate**, how many are **Overdue**, the **Avg Completion** time
   in days, and how many **Open Tasks** there are.
5. **Tasks Completed per Week** charts the work done each week.
6. **Workload by Person** shows how many open tasks each person leads. On a phone, press **View
   All** for more than five.
7. **Tasks by Priority** splits the open tasks by priority.
8. **Overdue Tasks by Reason** counts the late tasks by why they are late. **No Reason Recorded**
   means nobody said why.
9. **Overdue Aging** groups late tasks by how late they are, from **1 to 3 Days** to **15 Days or
   More**. Under it, **Done**, **Cancelled** and **Avg Cycle Time**, the days from starting a task
   to finishing it.
10. **Blocked Work** lists each blocked task, what is **Blocked By**, and how long it has been
    **Held For**. Press a row to open the task.

**Overdue Tasks by Reason** shows each **Reason** with its **Count** and **Share**. An empty card says
so: **Nothing Overdue**, **Nothing Blocked** or **No open tasks.** A rate with nothing to count reads
**None yet**.

The completion rate is the tasks done, out of those done or due in the period. A cancelled task
counts only under **Cancelled**.
