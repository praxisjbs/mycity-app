Staff Community is where MyCity talks to itself: company channels, direct messages between
colleagues, recognitions and celebrations, and announcements from People and the CEO.

Open it from **Staff Community** in the menu on a computer, or **Community** in the bar at the bottom
of a phone. A number on it counts your unread messages.

## Channels {#community-channels screen=community/channels icon=msg}

A channel is a group conversation. Everyone is in **#general** and **#announcements**. Other
channels list only the people added to them.

![Channels](shot:community-channels)

### Read a channel {#community-channels-read}

1. Open [Staff Community · Channels](/community?tab=channels). **Pinned** channels come first, then
   **Channels**. Each row shows the channel's last message, its time and how many are unread.
2. Press a channel. On a computer it opens beside the list. On a phone it opens full screen; press
   **Back** to return to the list.
   ![A channel, open](shot:community-channel-open)
3. A line marks where your unread messages start. Scroll up for older messages; they load as you go.
4. While you read older messages, an arrow at the foot brings you back to the newest.
5. Type in **Search channels** above the list to find a channel by name.

Each morning the hub posts a card for a colleague's birthday or work anniversary. Press
**Celebrate** to join in, or **Send Wishes** to write a short wish under the card.

### Post a message {#community-channels-post}

1. Type in the box at the bottom, which reads *Message #general* or the channel's name.
2. Press **Send**. On a computer, Enter sends and Shift with Enter starts a new line. On a phone,
   Enter starts a new line.
3. To send a voice note, leave the box empty and press **Record a voice note**. Speak, then send it
   or cancel it. Under a voice note, **Transcribe** writes it out as text.
4. To send a photo, a video or a document, press the plus, **Add to message**, then
   **Attach a File**. Pick the files, add a **Caption** if you like, and press **Send Files**.
5. A link you paste shows a small card with its page's title. Press **Remove Preview** to send the
   link without it.

If a channel reads **Only admins post here**, only its admins post in it. If a message cannot be
sent, the message at the foot of the screen says why, and your words stay in the box to send again.

### Recognise a colleague {#community-channels-recognise}

Thank a colleague in front of the whole company. A recognition always appears in **#general**.

1. Press the plus beside the message box, **Add to message**.
   ![The plus menu](shot:community-plus)
2. Choose **Recognise a Colleague**.
3. Pick the **Colleague**. You cannot pick yourself.
4. Write a **Note** if you like. Leave it empty and a thank you note is written for you.
5. Press **Post Recognition**. The message **Posted to #general** confirms it.

The same menu offers **Celebrate** to a channel's admins, for a birthday or work anniversary the
hub has not posted, and **Start a Poll** where you may run one: type a **Question** and two to four
options, then press **Post Poll**.

### Do something with a message {#community-channels-message}

Press the three dots beside a message, **Message actions**. You only see what you may do:

- **Edit** changes your own message, for 15 minutes after you send it, or your own poll until
  someone votes.
- **Delete** removes your own message. A channel's admins can delete any message in it.
- **Close Poll** stops the voting on your poll and keeps the result.
- **Pin Message** keeps a message at the top of the channel for 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days.
- **Forward** sends it to up to five other chats.
- **Select** lets you pick several messages, then **Copy** or **Forward** them together.

### Change how a channel reaches you {#community-channels-settings}

Press the three dots at the top of the channel, **Channel menu**:

- **View Members** lists who is in it. A channel's admins see **Edit Members** instead: **Add People**,
  **Remove** someone, **Make Admin** or **Remove Admin**. A channel always keeps one admin.
- **Channel Details** shows its purpose, its members and **Who Can Post**.
- **Search in Channel** finds words in the channel and steps between the matches.
- **Pin to Top** keeps it at the top of your list, under #general and #announcements. You can pin
  three; **Unpin** one to pin another.
- **Mute Notifications** stops it from notifying you. Its messages still arrive, and its row shows a
  crossed-out bell. **Unmute Notifications** turns them back on.

### Start a channel {#community-channels-new}

1. Press **New Channel** at the top right.
   ![New Channel](shot:community-new-channel)
2. Type the **Channel Name**: small letters, numbers and hyphens, such as `abuja-hub`.
3. Add a **Purpose** if it helps people know what the channel is for.
4. Tick whole **Departments**, or search for **People** one by one, to add them.
5. Under **Who Can Post**, choose **Every Member**, or **Admins Only** for a channel people only read.
6. Press **Create Channel**. You are its first admin.

If the name says **Not a valid name**, use only small letters, numbers and hyphens, from 2 to 30
characters. Two channels cannot share a name.

### Read and acknowledge an announcement {#community-channels-announcement}

**#announcements** holds the announcements from People and the CEO. Nobody writes in it.

1. Open **#announcements** in the channel list. Its row has a lock.
2. Read each announcement: who posted it, when, its headline and its text.
3. An urgent announcement is red and stays across the top of every screen. Press **Acknowledge** to
   say you have read it. It then reads **Acknowledged** with the time.

An announcement with an amber edge is important. You are reminded if you have not read it.

## Direct Messages {#community-direct-messages screen=community/direct-messages icon=user}

One to one conversations with a colleague.

![Direct Messages](shot:community-direct-messages)

### Message a colleague {#community-direct-messages-new}

1. Open [Staff Community · Direct Messages](/community?tab=direct-messages).
2. Press **New Message** and pick the colleague. Pick yourself to keep notes, drafts or files; that
   conversation is called **You**.
3. Type in the box at the bottom and press **Send**. Press **Attach** for a **Photo or Video** or a
   **Document**, or **Record a voice note**.

### Read your conversations {#community-direct-messages-read}

1. Each row shows the colleague, the last message (*You:* when it was yours), its time and how many
   are unread. Choose **Unread** to see only those, **All** for every one.
2. Type a name in **Search people** above the list to find a conversation.
3. Under each message you sent, one tick means sent, two grey ticks mean delivered, and two blue
   ticks mean read.
4. A green dot beside a name means that person is online now. Otherwise the top of the conversation
   says when they were last seen.
5. Press the colleague's name at the top, **View profile**, to see their contact details, the files
   you have shared, and the channels you have in common.

The three dots at the top, **Conversation menu**, offer **View Profile**,
**Search in Conversation**, **Pin to Top** and **Mute Notifications**, as in a channel.

## Announcements {#community-announcements screen=community/announcements icon=bell}

People and the CEO post announcements to everyone, or to chosen departments, and see who has read
them. Everyone else reads them in **#announcements**.

![Announcements](shot:community-announcements)

### Read the figures {#community-announcements-read}

1. Open [Staff Community · Announcements](/community?tab=announcements).
2. The figures count those **Published** and **Scheduled**, the **Average Read** (the share of each
   audience who opened it), the **Audience** and those sent **This Month**.
3. Each announcement is a card: its headline, how many have read it (*19 of 25 read*), its text, and
   when it was posted and by whom. An urgent one counts those who acknowledged it.
4. Press a card to see who has **Read** it and who has **Not Read** it. Press **Remind Them** to send
   one reminder to everyone who has not.

### Post an announcement {#community-announcements-new needs="cap announcements.post"}

1. Press **New Announcement** at the top right.
   ![New Announcement](shot:community-new-announcement)
2. Type the **Headline** and the **Message**.
3. Choose the **Level**: **Normal**, **Important** (the unread are reminded after 4 hours) or
   **Urgent** (red on everyone's screen until they acknowledge it).
4. Under **Audience**, keep **All Staff**, or choose **Departments** and tick them.
5. Under **When**, keep **Now**, or choose **Schedule** and pick the **Publish Date**.
6. Press **Publish Announcement**, or **Schedule Announcement**. The message
   **Published to #announcements** confirms it, and everyone in the audience is told.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add a headline** | Type the headline |
| **Write the message** | Type the message |
| **Choose a department** | Tick at least one department, or choose **All Staff** |
| **Not a real date** | Pick the publish date |

### Change or take down an announcement {#community-announcements-change needs="cap announcements.post"}

1. Press the announcement's card.
2. A scheduled one offers **Edit** (change it, then **Save Changes**) and **Cancel Announcement**.
3. A published one offers **Archive Announcement**: it leaves #announcements, and its reads are kept.
   Confirm with **Archive Announcement**, or press **Keep It**.
