Staff Community is where MyCity talks to itself: company channels, direct messages between
colleagues, recognitions and celebrations, and announcements from People and the CEO.

Open it from **Staff Community** in the menu on a computer, or **Community** in the bar at the bottom
of a phone. A number on it counts your unread messages.

## Channels {#community-channels screen=community/channels icon=msg}

A channel is a group conversation. Everyone is in **#general** and **#announcements**. Other
channels list only the people added to them.

![Channels](shot:community-channels)

### Read a channel {#community-channels-read}

1. Open [Staff Community · Channels](/community?tab=channels). **Pinned** channels come first, then
   **Channels**. Each row shows the channel's last message, its time and how many are unread.
2. Press a channel. On a computer it opens beside the list. On a phone it opens full screen. Press
   the arrow at the top left, **Back to channels**, to return to the list.
   ![A channel, open](shot:community-channel-open)
3. On a phone, press **Switch channel**, the three lines at the top left, to go to another channel
   without going back. The channel list opens; press a channel.
4. The channel opens at your first unread message, under a line such as *3 Unread Messages*. With
   nothing unread, it opens at the newest.
5. Scroll up for older messages. They load 25 at a time as you go. If they stop, press
   **Load Older**.
6. The day's label, such as *Today* or *Yesterday*, stays at the top while you scroll.
7. While you read older messages, the round arrow, **Newest Messages**, takes you back down. A number
   on it counts the messages that came in meanwhile.
8. Type in **Search channels** above the list to find a channel by name.

A channel with nothing in it reads *No messages yet. Say hello.* Beside a message's time, *Edited*
means its sender changed it. *Forwarded* above a message means it came from another chat.
*Message deleted* stands where a message was removed.

### Celebrate a birthday or work anniversary {#community-channels-celebrate}

At 08:00 each day the hub posts a card in **#general** for each birthday and work anniversary.

![A celebration card](shot:community-celebration)

1. Press **Celebrate** on the card. It then reads **Celebrated**, and you join those who
   celebrated. Press it again to take it back.
2. Press **Send Wishes** to write to the person.
3. Type your **Wish**, up to 280 characters, and press **Send Wishes**. The message **Wishes sent**
   confirms it.
4. Your wish shows under the card, never in the channel. The person is told.
5. The card shows the latest two wishes. When there are more, press **View All**. Use **Previous**
   and **Next** to page through them, then **Close**.
6. To remove your own wish, press the bin beside it, **Delete Wish**. Press **Delete Wish** again,
   or **Keep It**. The message **Wish deleted** confirms it.

On a phone, **Celebrate** and **Send Wishes** share a row under the card. A channel's moderators can
delete anyone's wish: its admins, and in #general People and the CEO.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Write a wish** | The wish is empty | Type a wish |
| **Too long** | The wish is over 280 characters | Shorten it |
| **Check this value** | The card was deleted while you wrote | Press **Cancel** |
| **There is no such wish** | Someone deleted the wish first | Nothing to do |

### Vote in a poll {#community-channels-vote}

1. Press an option on the poll. Your choice is outlined, and each option shows its share of the
   votes.
2. To change your vote, press another option. You have one vote.
3. A poll marked **Closed** takes no more votes. Its result stays.

Under a recognition, press the thumbs up to add yours. Press it again to take it back.

### Post a message {#community-channels-post}

1. Type in the box at the bottom, which reads *Message #general* or the channel's name.
2. Press **Send**. On a computer, Enter sends and Shift with Enter starts a new line. On a phone,
   Enter starts a new line.
3. To send a voice note, leave the box empty and press the microphone, **Record a voice note**.
   Allow the microphone if your browser asks.
4. The bar reads *Recording* with the time. Press **Send voice note** to send it, or
   **Cancel recording** to drop it. A note stops and sends itself at 5 minutes.
5. To send photos, videos or documents, press the plus, **Add to message**, then **Attach a File**.
   Your device's file picker opens. If it does not, press **Choose files to send**.
   ![Send Files](shot:community-send-files)
6. Pick up to 10 files, each up to 25 MB. Each shows its progress, then a tile. Press
   **Add more files** to pick more, or the cross on a tile to take one out. A file that failed says
   why, with **Retry** and **Remove**.
7. Type a **Caption** if you like. It goes with the first file. Press **Send Files**. Four or more
   photos or videos sent together show as one album.
8. A link you paste shows a small card with its page's title. Press **Remove Preview** to send the
   link without it.

Press **Play voice note** under a note to listen. **Transcribe** writes it out as text, and
**Hide Transcript** folds it away. **Draft** on the text means only your browser's rough captions
were there; press **Transcribe** again later.

A channel you cannot post in has a lock on its row and **Read Only** beside its name. Its message
box reads **Only admins post here**: only its admins post in it. If a message cannot be sent, your
words stay in the box to send again.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **You cannot post in this channel** | You are no longer one of this channel's admins | Ask one of its admins to post it |
| **There is no such conversation** | You were taken out of the channel | Go back to the list. It has left your list |
| **Check the highlighted fields** | The message is over 4,000 characters, or a recognition note over 500 | Shorten it and send again |
| **Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again.** | Many messages or files went in one minute | Wait a minute and send again, or press **Retry** |
| **Not sent. Try again.** | The connection dropped | Check your connection and press **Send** again |
| **Allow the microphone to record** | The browser was refused the microphone | Allow it in your browser's site settings, then press **Record a voice note** again |
| **This browser cannot record audio** | This browser has no recorder | Use an up to date browser, such as Chrome, Edge or Safari |
| **Could not send the voice note** | The note did not reach the hub | Check your connection and record it again |
| **Could not transcribe** | No transcript could be made now | Press **Transcribe** again later |
| **Choose a file first** | You pressed **Send Files** with no file | Pick a file |
| Larger than 25 MB | The file is too big to send | Remove it and send a smaller copy |
| **This file type is not accepted here.** and the kinds it takes | Chats do not take this kind of file | Remove it, or save it as one of those |
| **This is an old Office file. Save it as .docx or .xlsx and upload it again.** | It is an old .doc or .xls file | Save it as .docx or .xlsx and pick it again |
| **Too long** | The caption is over 4,000 characters | Shorten it |

### Open a photo, video or file {#community-channels-files}

1. Press a photo, a video or a PDF in the conversation. It opens full screen.
2. On a computer, press **Zoom In** or **Zoom Out**. Press **Previous** or **Next**, or the arrow
   keys, for the other files.
3. On a phone, pinch or double tap a photo to zoom. Swipe left or right for the next file.
4. Press **Download** to save a copy. On a phone, **Share** opens your phone's share menu, where it
   offers one.
5. Press **Forward** to send the file to other chats.
6. Press **Close**, or Esc on a computer, to go back. On a phone, swipe down or use the phone's
   back.

A Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV or text file downloads when you press it. If a file reads
**Cannot Open File**, press **Download** and open it on your device.

### Recognise a colleague {#community-channels-recognise}

Thank a colleague in front of the whole company. A recognition always appears in **#general**.

1. Press the plus beside the message box, **Add to message**.
   ![The plus menu](shot:community-plus)
2. Choose **Recognise a Colleague**.
3. Pick the **Colleague**. You cannot pick yourself.
4. Write a **Note** if you like. Leave it empty and a thank you note is written for you.
5. Press **Post Recognition**. The message **Posted to #general** confirms it.

### Post a celebration by hand {#community-channels-celebration}

For a birthday or work anniversary the hub has not posted. A channel's admins post one there; in
#general, People and the CEO do.

1. Press the plus, **Add to message**, then **Celebrate**.
2. Pick the **Colleague**.
3. Under **Occasion**, choose **Birthday** or **Work Anniversary**.
4. Press **Post Celebration**. The card appears in the channel.

If the form says **Choose a colleague**, pick one first. If the screen says
**Not posted. Try again.**, check your connection and post it again.

### Start a poll {#community-channels-poll}

In #general, only People and the CEO start a poll. In any other channel, anyone who may post there
can.

1. Press the plus, **Add to message**, then **Start a Poll**.
2. Type the **Question**, at least four characters.
3. Fill in the first two options. The third and fourth are optional.
4. Press **Post Poll**.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add a question** | Type a question of at least four characters |
| **Add an option** | Fill in both of the first two options |

### Do something with a message {#community-channels-message}

Press the three dots beside a message, **Message actions**. On a computer, point at the message to
show them; on a phone they always show. You only see what you may do:

- **Forward** sends it to up to five other chats. See [Forward messages](#community-channels-forward).
- **Select** picks several messages to copy or forward together. See
  [Select several messages](#community-channels-select).
- **Edit** changes your own message for 15 minutes after you send it, or your own poll until
  someone votes. See [Edit a message or a poll](#community-channels-edit).
- **Close Poll** stops the voting and keeps the result.
- **Pin Message** keeps it above the messages for everyone. **Unpin** takes it down. See
  [Pin a message](#community-channels-pin-message).
- **Delete** removes your own message. See [Delete a message](#community-channels-delete).

A channel's moderators also close any poll, pin and delete any message. They are its admins; in
#general and #announcements, People and the CEO. In a direct message, either of you pins, and only
its sender edits or deletes.

### Edit a message or a poll {#community-channels-edit}

1. Press **Message actions** beside your message, then **Edit**.
2. **Edit Message** opens. Change the **Message** and press **Save Changes**. It then shows *Edited*
   beside its time.
3. For your poll, **Edit Poll** opens with its question and options. Change them and press
   **Save Changes**.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Write something** | The message is empty | Type it, or press **Cancel** |
| **Too late to edit** | 15 minutes have passed since you sent it | Delete it and send it again |
| **Check this value** | The message was deleted meanwhile | Press **Cancel** |
| **People have voted on this poll** | Someone voted while you edited | Delete the poll and start a new one if it must change |
| **This poll is closed** | A moderator closed it meanwhile | Start a new poll if you need one |

### Delete a message or close a poll {#community-channels-delete}

1. Press **Message actions** beside the message, then **Delete**.
2. Press **Delete Message** to confirm, or **Keep It**. The message **Message deleted** confirms it.
   *Message deleted* then stands in its place for everyone.
3. To stop a poll, press **Close Poll**. Press **Close Poll** again, or **Keep Open**. The poll then
   reads **Closed**.
4. Four or more photos sent together form an album. Its three dots, **Album actions**, offer
   **Forward**, **Select** and **Delete**.
5. **Delete** on an album asks once for every photo in it. Press **Delete Photos**, or
   **Keep Them**. The message **Photos deleted** confirms it.

If the screen says **There is no such conversation**, someone deleted the message first. Nothing is
left to do.

### Pin a message {#community-channels-pin-message}

A pinned message stays above the conversation for everyone. In a channel, its moderators pin; in a
direct message, either of you.

1. Press **Message actions** beside the message, then **Pin Message**.
2. Under **Pin For**, choose **24 Hours**, **7 Days** or **30 Days**. **7 Days** is chosen for you.
3. Press **Pin Message**. The message **Message pinned** confirms it.
4. A bar above the messages shows it. Press the bar to bring the message into view.
5. Up to three stay pinned. The bar reads *1 of 3*, and each press shows the next. A fourth pin
   replaces the oldest.
6. To take one down, press the cross on the bar, **Unpin message**, or **Unpin** in its
   **Message actions**. The message **Message unpinned** confirms it.

A pin ends by itself when its time is up.

### Forward messages {#community-channels-forward}

1. Press **Message actions** beside a message, then **Forward**. For several, use **Select** first.
2. **Forward To** opens. Type in **Search chats** to find a channel, a conversation or a colleague.
   ![Forward To](shot:community-forward)
3. Press up to five chats. Each one picked shows above the list; press its cross to take it off.
4. Press **Forward**. The message **Forwarded**, or *Forwarded to 3 chats*, confirms it.

The copy reads *Forwarded* and comes from you. Polls, cards and announcements are not forwarded. On a
phone, **Forward To** opens from the bottom of the screen.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **At most 5 chats** | You picked a sixth chat | Take one off first |
| **Choose a chat** | You pressed **Forward** with no chat picked | Pick a chat |
| You cannot post in, then a channel's name | That channel became read only for you, or you left it | Take it off and press **Forward** again |
| **A message was deleted** | A message you picked was deleted | Press **Cancel** and pick again |
| **Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again.** | Over 20 forwards in a minute | Wait a minute |

### Select several messages {#community-channels-select}

1. Press **Message actions** beside a message, then **Select**. On a phone, you can also press and
   hold a message.
2. Press other messages to tick them. Press a ticked one to untick it.
3. The bar at the bottom shows how many, such as *3 Selected*.
4. Press **Forward** to send them all. See [Forward messages](#community-channels-forward).
5. When every message picked is text, **Copy** puts them on your clipboard. The message **Copied**
   confirms it.
6. Press the cross, **Cancel**, to stop picking.

Polls, cards and announcements cannot be picked. If the screen says
**Copy did not work. Try again.**, your browser refused the clipboard: press **Copy** again, or allow
the clipboard for the hub.

### Change how a channel reaches you {#community-channels-settings}

Press the three dots at the top of the channel, **Channel menu**:

- **View Members** opens **Channel Members**: everyone in it, its admins marked **Admin**. Type in
  **Search members** to find someone. A channel's admins also see **Edit Members**; see
  [Manage a channel's members](#community-channels-members).
- **Channel Details** shows its **Channel**, **Purpose**, **Members** and **Who Can Post**. Its admins
  change it there; see [Rename a channel](#community-channels-rename).
- **Search in Channel** finds words in the channel. See
  [Search in a conversation](#community-channels-search).
- **Pin to Top** keeps it at the top of your list, under #general and #announcements. You can pin
  three; **Unpin** one to pin another. *Pinned #ops* confirms a pin. #general and #announcements are
  always on top, so they offer no **Pin to Top**.
- **Mute Notifications** stops it from notifying you. Its messages still arrive, and its row shows a
  crossed-out bell. *#ops muted* confirms it. **Unmute Notifications** turns them back on, and
  *Notifications on for #ops* confirms it.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Unpin one first** | You already pin three chats | **Unpin** one, then pin this one |
| **Not changed. Try again.** | The change did not reach the hub | Check your connection and try again |

### Manage a channel's members {#community-channels-members}

A channel's admins see **Edit Members** in the **Channel menu** too. #general and #announcements hold
everyone and have no admins.

1. Press **Channel menu**, then **Edit Members**.
2. Press **Make Admin** beside someone to share running the channel. The message
   *Ada Obi is now an admin* confirms it. **Remove Admin** takes it back.
3. Press **Remove** to take someone out at once. They lose the channel and its messages. To remove
   an admin, press **Remove Admin** first.
4. To add people, press **Add People**. Pick **People**, or press whole **Departments**, then press
   **Add People**.
5. Press **Close** when you are done.

The messages *Removed from #ops* and *Added to #ops* confirm a change.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **A channel needs an admin** | You tried to take away the last admin | Make someone else admin first |
| **Only the channel's admins can do this** | You are no longer one of its admins | Ask one of its admins |

### Rename a channel {#community-channels-rename}

A channel's admins change its name and purpose. #general and #announcements keep theirs, and
**Who Can Post** never changes.

1. Press **Channel menu**, then **Channel Details**.
2. Press **Edit Channel**.
3. Change the **Channel Name** or the **Purpose**.
4. Press **Save Changes**. The message **Channel saved** confirms it.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not a valid name** | Use 2 to 30 small letters, numbers and hyphens, starting with a letter or number |
| **Name already taken** | Another channel has that name. Choose another |

### Search in a conversation {#community-channels-search}

1. Press **Channel menu**, then **Search in Channel**. In a direct message, press the magnifier,
   **Search in conversation**.
2. Type in **Search messages**. Matches are marked in the messages, with a count such as *2 of 4*.
3. Press **Previous match** or **Next match** to step between them. On a computer, Enter steps on and
   Shift with Enter steps back.
4. **No match** means nothing in this conversation has those words.
5. Press **Close search**, or Esc, to see every message again.

### Start a channel {#community-channels-new}

1. Press **New Channel** at the top right. On a phone it is under the title.
   ![New Channel](shot:community-new-channel)
2. Type the **Channel Name**: small letters, numbers and hyphens, such as `abuja-hub`. Capitals
   become small letters as you type.
3. Add a **Purpose** if it helps people know what the channel is for.
4. Tick whole **Departments**, or search for **People** one by one, to add them.
5. Under **Who Can Post**, choose **Every Member**, or **Admins Only** for a channel people only read.
6. Press **Create Channel**. The message *#abuja-hub created with 12 members* confirms it, and the
   channel opens. You are its first admin. A **Purpose** becomes its first message.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not a valid name** | Use 2 to 30 small letters, numbers and hyphens, starting with a letter or number |
| **Name already taken** | Another channel has that name. Choose another |

### Read and acknowledge an announcement {#community-channels-announcement}

**#announcements** holds the announcements from People and the CEO. Nobody writes in it.

1. Open **#announcements** in the channel list. Its row has a lock.
2. Read each announcement: who posted it, when, its headline and its text. **Urgent** or
   **Important** beside it gives its level.
3. An urgent announcement is red and stays across the top of every screen. Press **Acknowledge** to
   say you have read it. It then reads **Acknowledged** with the time. Only the people it was sent
   to see **Acknowledge**. On a phone it spans the card.

An announcement with an amber edge is important. You are reminded if you have not read it. Opening
#announcements counts as reading what it shows. An urgent one counts only once you press
**Acknowledge**.

## Direct Messages {#community-direct-messages screen=community/direct-messages icon=user}

One to one conversations with a colleague.

![Direct Messages](shot:community-direct-messages)

### Message a colleague {#community-direct-messages-new}

1. Open [Staff Community · Direct Messages](/community?tab=direct-messages).
2. Press **New Message**, type a name, and press the colleague. The conversation opens at once. Pick
   yourself to keep notes, drafts or files; that conversation is called **You**.
3. Type in the box at the bottom and press **Send**. Press **Attach** for a **Photo or Video** or a
   **Document**, or **Record a voice note**.

A new conversation joins your list once a message is sent.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **That colleague has no login yet** | You pressed **Message** for someone who cannot sign in yet, such as on the Organogram | Reach them another way. People can give them a login |

### Read your conversations {#community-direct-messages-read}

1. Each row shows the colleague, the last message (*You:* when it was yours), its time and how many
   are unread. Choose **Unread** to see only those, **All** for every one.
2. On a phone a conversation opens full screen. Press the arrow at the top left,
   **Back to conversations**, to return to the list.
3. Type a name in **Search people** above the list to find a conversation.
4. Under each message you sent, one tick means sent, two grey ticks mean delivered, and two blue
   ticks mean read.
5. A green dot beside a name means that person is online now. Under their name at the top,
   **Online**, *Last seen* with a time, or **Offline** shows, then their department.

The three dots at the top, **Conversation menu**, offer **View Profile**,
**Search in Conversation**, **Pin to Top** and **Mute Notifications**, as in a channel. The magnifier
beside it, **Search in conversation**, finds words too. See
[Search in a conversation](#community-channels-search).

### See a colleague's profile {#community-direct-messages-profile}

1. Press the colleague's name at the top of the conversation, **View profile**. Or press
   **Conversation menu**, then **View Profile**.
   ![A colleague's profile](shot:community-profile)
2. On a computer it opens as a column at the right; the cross, **Close profile**, closes it. On a
   phone it opens from the bottom.
3. Under their name are their job title, and **Online** or when they were last seen.
4. **Contact** lists their work email, phone, worksite and the time in Lagos.
5. **Shared in Conversation** lists the files you have sent each other. Press one to open it.
6. **Channels in Common** lists the channels you are both in.
7. Switch on **Mute Notifications** to silence this conversation.
8. Press **Search in Conversation** to find words in it.
9. {needs="people view"} Press **View in People** to open their staff record.

## Announcements {#community-announcements screen=community/announcements icon=bell}

People and the CEO post announcements to everyone, or to chosen departments, and see who has read
them. Everyone else reads them in **#announcements**.

If the tab shows **People and the CEO** and *Read them in #announcements.*, you cannot post
announcements. Read them in **#announcements**.

![Announcements](shot:community-announcements)

### Read the figures {#community-announcements-read needs="cap announcements.post"}

1. Open [Staff Community · Announcements](/community?tab=announcements).
2. The figures count those **Published** and **Scheduled**, the **Average Read** (the share of each
   audience who opened it), the **Audience** and those sent **This Month**. On a phone,
   **This Month** is not shown.
3. Each announcement is a card: its headline, how many have read it (*19 of 25 read*), its text, and
   *Posted* with the date and who posted it. A red edge marks an urgent one, which counts
   *acknowledged*; an amber edge marks an important one.
4. A scheduled one reads **Scheduled** and *Goes out* with its date and time.
5. Press a card. **Announcement Reads** shows *Read by 19 of 25* with a bar, then **Not Read** and
   **Read**, each with the people.
   ![Announcement Reads](shot:community-announcement-reads)
6. For an urgent one the lists are **Not Acknowledged** and **Acknowledged**, each person with the
   time they pressed it.
7. *Everyone has read it.* means nobody is left. Press **Close** to go back.
8. Press **Remind Them** beside **Not Read**. The message *Reminder sent to 6 people* confirms it.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Already reminded today** | A reminder went today, from you, a colleague, or the automatic one for an important announcement | Try again tomorrow |

### Post an announcement {#community-announcements-new needs="cap announcements.post"}

1. Press **New Announcement** at the top right. On a phone it is under the title, and the form opens
   from the bottom.
   ![New Announcement](shot:community-new-announcement)
2. Type the **Headline** and the **Message**.
3. Choose the **Level**: **Normal**, **Important** (the unread are reminded after 4 hours) or
   **Urgent** (red on everyone's screen until they acknowledge it).
4. Under **Audience**, keep **All Staff**, or choose **Departments** and tick them.
5. Under **When**, keep **Now**, or choose **Schedule** and pick the **Publish Date**. It goes out at
   09:00 Lagos time that day.
6. Press **Publish Announcement**, or **Schedule Announcement**. **Published to #announcements**
   confirms one sent now; *Scheduled for 30/10/2026* confirms one scheduled. Everyone in the
   audience is told when it goes out.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add a headline** | Type a headline of at least four characters |
| **Write the message** | Type a message of at least four characters |
| **Choose a department** | Tick at least one department, or choose **All Staff** |
| **Not a real date** | Pick the publish date |
| **Time already passed** | Today's 09:00 has gone. Pick tomorrow, or choose **Now** |
| **Check this value** | The headline is over 120 characters, or the message over 4,000. Shorten it |

### Change or take down an announcement {#community-announcements-change needs="cap announcements.post"}

1. Press the announcement's card.
2. A scheduled one offers **Edit** and **Cancel Announcement**.
3. **Edit** opens the same form. Change it, pick the **Publish Date** and press **Save Changes**.
   *Scheduled for* with the date confirms it.
4. **Cancel Announcement** asks once. Press **Cancel Announcement** again, or **Keep It**.
   **Announcement cancelled** confirms it. It is never posted.
5. A published one offers **Archive Announcement**. Press **Archive Announcement** again, or
   **Keep It**. **Announcement archived** confirms it.
6. It leaves #announcements, where it reads *Message deleted*, and this tab and its figures. Its
   reads are kept.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **It has already gone out** | It was published before you saved | Archive it if it must come down |
| **There is no such announcement** | Someone cancelled or archived it first | Close the window. Nothing is left to do |
