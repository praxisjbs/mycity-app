# Staff Community

Staff Community is where MyCity talks to itself: company channels, direct messages and
announcements. Open it from **Community** in the menu.

## Channels

- Every member of staff is in **#general** and **#announcements**. Other channels list only the
  people added to them.
- **New Channel** creates a channel: give it a name and add people. You are its admin.
- **Channel Details** shows who is in a channel. An admin can **Edit Members**: **Add People** or
  **Remove** someone.
- Type in the box at the bottom and press **Send**. The paper clip attaches a **Photo or Video** or
  a **Document**.
- With the box empty, press the microphone to record a voice note, then send it or cancel it.
  **Transcribe** under a voice note writes it out as text.
- The ＋ beside the box offers **Recognise a Colleague**, **Celebrate** and a poll. A recognition
  always appears in #general so the whole company sees it.
- Birthdays and work anniversaries are posted for you each morning from the People records.
- **Search in Channel** finds words in the open channel and steps between the matches.
- **Mute Notifications** stops a channel from notifying you. Its messages still arrive.

## Direct Messages

- Pick a colleague to start a conversation with them. **All** and **Unread** filter the list.
- One tick means sent, two ticks mean delivered, and blue ticks mean read.
- A green dot beside a name means that person is online now. Otherwise you see when they were last
  seen.
- **Profile** shows the colleague's contact details, the files and voice notes you have shared, and
  the channels you have in common.
- **Search in Conversation** works as it does in a channel.

## Announcements

Only People and the CEO post in **#announcements**. Everyone else reads it.

For People and the CEO, the **Announcements** tab shows every announcement with its read count:

1. **New Announcement**: write the headline and the text, then choose the **Audience** (all staff,
   or chosen departments).
2. Publish now or pick a date and time to publish later. A scheduled announcement is listed as
   **Scheduled** until it goes out.
3. Open an announcement to see who has **Read** it and who has **Not Read** it.
   **Remind Them** sends one reminder to everyone who has not read it yet.

Everyone in the audience gets a notification when an announcement is published.
