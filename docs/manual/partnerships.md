Partnerships is where MyCity keeps the organisations it works with. A lead is an organisation you are
pursuing. A deal is what you are offering it. Once a deal is signed, the lead becomes a partner and
keeps its whole history.

The screens are tabs of **Partnerships** in the menu: **Pipeline**, **Categories**, **Partners**,
**Leads**, **Proposals**, **Contracts & MOUs** and **Analytics**. On a phone, press **More** in the bar
at the bottom, then **Partnerships**.

You see the leads, partners and deals you own or belong to. A line manager also sees the team's.
Operations & People see everything. Commission and returns show only to people allowed to see money.

## Pipeline {#partnerships-pipeline screen=partnerships/pipeline}

Pipeline shows every open deal and where it stands. A deal belongs to a lead or a partner, so a row
opens that record.

![The Pipeline](shot:partnerships-pipeline)

### Read the pipeline {#partnerships-pipeline-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Pipeline](/partnerships?tab=pipeline).
2. Read the four cards: **Open Deals**, **Stalled**, **Signed This Year** and **Signed Value**.
3. Hover over **Stalled** to read what it counts. On a phone, press it. It counts open deals with
   something in the way.
4. Hover over **Signed Value** to see how it is worked out. Each deal signed this year counts at its
   agreement's value, or its projected value when it has no agreement.
5. On a computer, **Stages** shows how many open deals sit at each stage. **Longest at a Stage** lists
   the deals that have waited longest, with their days. **Stalled** lists the deals with blockers.
6. On a phone, **Open Deals** lists each deal as a card. It shows the stage, the health and the
   **Days at Stage**.

### Filter the pipeline {#partnerships-pipeline-filter}

1. On a computer, pick a **Stage**, **Category**, **Region**, **Owner** or **Health** above the cards.
   The cards and lists change to match.
   ![Filtering by Health](shot:partnerships-pipeline-filter)
2. Pick **Every Stage**, **Every Category**, **Every Region**, **Every Owner** or **Any Health** to
   clear one filter.
3. On a phone, press **All**, **Stalled**, **Follow-Up** or **On Track** above the list.
   ![The phone's health chips](shot:partnerships-pipeline-chips)

### Open a record from the pipeline {#partnerships-pipeline-open}

1. Press a row in **Longest at a Stage**, **Stalled** or **Open Deals**.
2. The lead or partner opens on its own tab. [Read a partner](#partnerships-partners-read) and
   [Read a lead](#partnerships-leads-read) explain what you see.

### Start a lead from the pipeline {#partnerships-pipeline-new needs="partnerships create"}

1. Press **New Lead** at the top right of [Partnerships · Pipeline](/partnerships?tab=pipeline).
2. Fill it in as in [Add a lead](#partnerships-leads-add).

## Categories {#partnerships-categories screen=partnerships/categories}

A category is a kind of partnership, such as Sponsorship or Hospitality. Each one holds its default
commission, its agreement templates, its delivery checklist and its extra fields. Everyone with
Partnerships can read them. Only people allowed to set Partnerships up can change them.

![The Categories tab](shot:partnerships-categories)

### Read a category {#partnerships-categories-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Categories](/partnerships?tab=categories).
2. Use **Status** to show the categories **In Use**, **Retired** or **All**.
3. Press a category. On a computer it opens beside the list. On a phone it opens as a sheet.
   ![A category open](shot:partnerships-category-open)
4. Read its sections: **Defaults**, **Templates**, **Checklist** and **Fields**. On a computer they sit
   one below another. On a phone, press a name above them to switch.
5. **In This Category** lists its partners and its leads, with their owners.

### Add a category {#partnerships-categories-add needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

1. Press **New Category** at the top right of
   [Partnerships · Categories](/partnerships?tab=categories).
   ![New Category](shot:partnerships-category-new)
2. Type the **Name**. Add a **Description** if it helps.
3. Fill in **Default Commission**, the rate a new deal starts from. A deal may differ.
4. Fill in **Default Validity**, the days a signed deal runs when it names no other.
5. Hover over **Counts as Vendor** to read it. Tick it when Analytics should count the category's leads
   as vendor leads.
6. Press **Add Category**. The message reads *Category added*.

### Edit, retire or restore a category {#partnerships-categories-edit needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

1. Press the category in the list.
2. Press **Edit** beside **Defaults**, change what you need and press **Save Category**.
3. To take it out of the pickers, press **More Actions** at the top of its record, then **Retire**.
   Press **Retire Category** to confirm.
4. To bring it back, press **More Actions**, then **Restore**.

### Add an agreement template {#partnerships-categories-template needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

A template is the wording of an agreement, with fields that fill in from the deal.

1. Open the category. On a phone, press **Templates**.
2. Press **Add Template**.
   ![The template editor](shot:partnerships-template-new)
3. Type the **Name** and pick the **Kind**.
4. Tick **MyCity Signs Too** when MyCity countersigns. Tick **Default for Its Kind** to make it the one
   offered first.
5. Write the **Wording**. Use **Insert Field** to place a field such as the partner's name.
6. Press **Add Ask** for anything the person making the agreement must fill in each time.
7. Press **Save Template**.

To make another template the default, press the dots beside it, then **Make Default**. **Retire** and
**Restore** work as they do for a category.

### Tidy a template with AI {#partnerships-categories-tidy needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

Use this when you already have an agreement as a Word document or a PDF.

1. Press **Tidy With AI** beside **Templates**.
   ![Tidy With AI](shot:partnerships-template-tidy)
2. Paste the agreement's text under **Pasted Wording**.
3. Press **Tidy Wording**. The tidied wording shows, with the fields it found.
4. Check each proposed field. Press the cross beside one to leave it out.
5. Press **Use Draft**. The wording opens in the editor. Check it, then press **Save Template**.

If AI is off or its budget is spent, a message says so and nothing changes.

### Set a delivery checklist {#partnerships-categories-checklist needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

The checklist lists what MyCity delivers once a deal is signed. Each item becomes a task for the
deal's owner.

1. Open the category. On a phone, press **Checklist**.
2. Press **Add Item**.
   ![Add Item](shot:partnerships-checklist-add)
3. Type the **Item**. Add **Details** if it helps.
4. Pick when it is **Due**, and the number of **Days**.
5. Press **Add Item**.
6. Use the up and down arrows beside an item to change the order. **Retire** takes one off future
   deals.

### Add a custom field {#partnerships-categories-field needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

A custom field records something the standard fields do not, such as a ticket model.

1. Open the category. On a phone, press **Fields**.
2. Press **Add Field**.
   ![Add Field](shot:partnerships-field-add)
3. Type the **Label** and pick its **Type**.
4. For a pick list, write the **Choices**, one on each line.
5. Pick what it **Applies To**, and whether it is for this **Category** or all of them.
6. Hover over **Filterable** to read it. Tick it to offer the field as a filter on the lists.
7. Press **Add Field**.

### Change the pipeline's lists {#partnerships-categories-settings needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

Pipeline Settings holds the lists every deal uses: **Stages**, **Potential Impact**,
**Lead Sources** and **Document Kinds**.

1. On a computer, press **Pipeline Settings** above the categories. On a phone, press it in the list
   under the categories.
   ![Pipeline Settings](shot:partnerships-settings)
2. Press **Add Stage**, or the add button of another list.
   ![Add Stage](shot:partnerships-stage-add)
3. Type the **Name**. For a stage, hover over **Counts As** and pick what totals and reports treat it as.
4. Press **Add Stage**. The message reads *Stage added*.
5. Use **Move Up** and **Move Down** to change the order. A stage keeps its place in every report.
6. **Retire** takes a value out of the pickers. A deal already at a retired stage keeps it.

The pipeline must keep a stage that starts a deal, one that signs it and one that loses it.

### Add a proof point {#partnerships-categories-proof needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

A proof point is a fact a proposal may quote, such as a number of users or a partner's logo.

1. On a computer, press **Proof Points** above the categories. On a phone, press it in the list under
   them.
   ![Proof Points](shot:partnerships-proof-points)
2. Press **Add Proof Point**.
   ![Add Proof Point](shot:partnerships-proof-point-add)
3. Pick the **Kind**: **Figure**, **Partner Logo**, **Endorsement** or **Result**.
4. Type the **Label**. Fill in the **Figure**, the **Detail** and the **Source** if you have them.
5. Press **Save Proof Point**.
6. To add an **Image**, press the dots beside the proof point, then **Edit**, and upload it there.
   The kind cannot change after it is saved.

Use **Status** to show proof points **In Use**, **Retired** or **All**.

### Set up from the tracker {#partnerships-categories-tracker needs="cap partnerships.configure"}

Do this once. It reads the stages, categories and impact values from MyCity's partnership tracker.

1. Press **Start From Tracker** at the top right.
   ![Start From Tracker](shot:partnerships-start-tracker)
2. Under **Choose the tracker**, upload the Excel file.
3. Read the **Stages**, **Categories** and **Potential Impact** it found. Press the cross beside any
   you do not want.
4. Press **Apply Lists**. The message reads *Lists set up*, or *Nothing new to add* when every value
   was already there.

The tracker's own rows come in on [Settings · Imports](/settings?tab=imports). Its steps are in
[Import a spreadsheet](#settings-imports-run).

## Partners {#partnerships-partners screen=partnerships/partners}

A partner is an organisation with a signed deal. Each has a 360, one record with its deals, documents,
returns, work, activity and contacts.

![The Partners list](shot:partnerships-partners)

### Read a partner {#partnerships-partners-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Partners](/partnerships?tab=partners).
2. Press **Active**, **Dormant**, **Quiet** or **Closed** to narrow the list. **All** shows every
   partner. Hover over **Quiet** to read it: active partners with no contact for some time.
3. On a computer, use the filters above the list. They work as on
   [the Pipeline](#partnerships-pipeline-filter).
4. Type in **Search partners** to find one by name or number.
5. Press a partner. On a computer its 360 opens beside the list. On a phone it fills the screen.
   ![A partner's 360](shot:partnerships-partner-360)
6. Read the header: the name, category, region and owner, with its status. **Signed Before the Hub**
   marks a partner that signed before MyCity used this system.
7. Read the four cards: **Signed Value**, **Returns This Year** or **Downloads This Year**,
   **Active Deals** and **Days Since Last Contact**. Hover over **Days Since Last Contact** to read how
   it is counted.
8. Use the tabs: **Overview**, **Deals**, **Documents**, **Returns**, **Work**, **Activity**,
   **Contacts** and **Details**. **Returns** shows only to people allowed to see money.

A partner made a minute ago shows zeros and empty tabs. That is expected.

### Add a partner who has already signed {#partnerships-partners-add needs="partnerships create"}

A lead becomes a partner when its deal is signed. Use this task for a partner that signed already.

1. Press **New Partner** at the top right of [Partnerships · Partners](/partnerships?tab=partners).
2. On **Organisation**, type the **Name**, pick the **Category** and the **Region**, and pick the
   **Owner**.
   ![The first step of New Partner](shot:partnerships-partner-new-1)
3. Read any warning that starts *Already here*. It names a partner or lead that looks the same. Press
   **New Deal** in it to add a deal to the partner that exists instead.
4. Press **Advanced** for **Legal Name**, **Website**, **Headquarters** and **Partner Since**. Pick the
   headquarters from the address search.
5. Press **Next**. On **Contact**, fill in the **First Name** and **Last Name**. Add the **Position**,
   **Email** and **Phone** if you have them.
   ![The Contact step](shot:partnerships-partner-new-2)
6. Press **Next**. On **Signed Deal**, type the deal's **Title** and pick its **Stage**.
   ![The Signed Deal step](shot:partnerships-partner-new-3)
7. Under **Signed Copy**, upload the signed agreement. If it was signed before MyCity used this system,
   tick **Signed Before the Hub** instead.
8. Press **Add Partner**. The message reads *Partner added*.

If you press **Add Partner** with neither a copy nor the tick, the form says *Attach the signed copy*.

### Open a deal {#partnerships-partners-deal needs="partnerships create"}

1. Open the partner and press **New Deal**.
   ![New Deal](shot:partnerships-deal-new)
2. Type the **Title**. Add the **Objective** if it helps.
3. Pick the **Stage**, and fill in the **Projected Value** and **Expected Close**.
4. For an event, pick the **Event Dates** and the **Venue** from the address search.
5. To price the deal in lines, press **Add Option**. Name the option, then fill in its
   **Original Price**, **Discount %** and **Deal Price**. Add up to five options.
6. Press **Advanced** for the **Event Title**, **Expected Attendance**, **Next Action**,
   **Next Action Date**, **Validity**, **Team** and **Comments**.
7. {needs="cap partnerships.money"} Under **Advanced**, **Commission %** sets this deal's rate. Hover
   over it to see when it differs from the category's.
8. Press **Open Deal**. The message reads *Deal opened*.

### Move a deal's stage {#partnerships-partners-stage needs="partnerships edit"}

The stages sit in a row on the deal. There is no board. You move a deal with one press.

1. Open the partner or the lead. The deal is on **Overview**.
   ![The stage switcher](shot:partnerships-stage-switcher)
2. Press the stage to move to. The message reads *Moved to* and the stage's name. On a phone, press
   **Next Stage** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Moving back, to **On Hold** or to **Closed Lost** asks you to explain. Type the **Why**, at least
   ten characters, then press **Move Stage**.
   ![Why a deal moves back](shot:partnerships-stage-why)
4. The first move to a signed stage asks for the **Signed Copy**. Upload it and press **Move Stage**.
5. Hover over the **Stage** row to read what the stage counts as in totals and reports.

When someone else moves a deal, its owner is told. Moves are listed under **Stage History** on
**Activity**.

### Set a deal's health {#partnerships-partners-health needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Press **Set Health** on the deal.
   ![Set Health](shot:partnerships-health)
2. Pick **On Track**, **Follow-Up Required** or **Stalled**.
3. For **Stalled**, type the **Blockers**. They are required, so the team can see what is in the way.
4. Press **Set Health**. The message reads *Health set*.

### Edit a deal {#partnerships-partners-edit needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Press **Edit Deal** on the deal.
2. Change what you need, as in [Open a deal](#partnerships-partners-deal).
3. Press **Save Deal**. The message reads *Deal saved*.

### Log an interaction {#partnerships-partners-log needs="partnerships edit"}

Log every call, meeting, email, visit or note so the whole team sees the history.

1. Open the partner or the lead and press **More**, then **Log Interaction**.
   ![Log Interaction](shot:partnerships-interaction)
2. Pick the **Kind**: **Call**, **Meeting**, **Email**, **Visit** or **Note**.
3. Pick the **Day** and **Time**. The day cannot be in the future.
4. Type the **Subject** and the **Summary**.
5. Press **Log Interaction**. The message reads *Interaction logged*.

The latest interactions show on **Overview**. **Activity** lists all of them. Press one to read it in
full. A conversation linked from your mailbox shows there too. See
[Link a conversation to a partner](#mailbox-link-partner).

### Add or change a contact {#partnerships-partners-contact needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open the partner and press **Contacts**.
2. Press **Add Contact**.
   ![Add Contact](shot:partnerships-contact-add)
3. Fill in the **First Name** and **Last Name**. Add the **Position**, **Email** and **Phone**.
4. Tick **Signs Agreements** if this person signs for the organisation.
5. Press **Add Contact**. The message reads *Contact added*.
6. To change a contact, press its row, edit it and press **Save Contact**.

The table marks the main contact **Primary** and a signer **Signs**.

### Keep a partner's documents {#partnerships-partners-documents needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open the partner and press **Documents**.
2. Press **Upload Document**.
   ![Upload Document](shot:partnerships-document-upload)
3. Pick the **Kind**, then choose the **File**.
4. Press **Upload Document**. The message reads *Document uploaded*.

Signed agreements, signed copies and proposals are filed here for you.

> **Tip:** You can also attach a contract or MoU in Praxis AI. It sums the document up and files it
> here with **Add Partner Document** ([Attach a document](#praxis-ask-attach)).

### Enter a month's returns {#partnerships-partners-returns needs="partnerships edit, cap partnerships.money"}

Returns are what a partner's deal earned in a month.

1. Open the partner and press **Returns**.
2. Pick the **Deal** and the **Year** if there are several.
3. Press **Enter Returns**.
   ![Enter Returns](shot:partnerships-returns-enter)
4. Pick the **Month**.
5. Fill in the **Units Sold**, **Gross Sales**, **MyCity Commission**, **Paid to Partner** and
   **Downloads**. Add a **Note** if it helps.
6. Press **Save Returns**. The message reads *Returns saved*.

The cards **Commission**, **Gross Sales**, **Units Sold** and **Downloads** add up the year. **Months**
lists each month and marks it **Entered** or **Imported**. Press a month to change it. The monthly report
comes in through [Settings · Imports](/settings?tab=imports).

### Connect work to a partner {#partnerships-partners-work needs="partnerships view"}

1. Open the partner or the lead and press **Work**.
   ![The Work tab](shot:partnerships-work)
2. Press **Tasks**, **Projects** or **Events** to switch.
3. {needs="workspace create"} Press **New Task**, **New Project** or **New Event**. The form opens
   already connected to the partner.
4. Press a row to open it in My Workspace.

A line such as *2 more tasks you cannot see* counts work connected to this partner that you may not
open. Connecting work never widens who can see it.

### Edit or close a partner {#partnerships-partners-close needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open the partner and press **More**, then **Edit Partner**. Change what you need and press
   **Save Partner**.
2. To end the partnership, press **More**, then **Close Partner**.
   ![Close Partner](shot:partnerships-partner-close)
3. Type the **Why** and press **Close Partner**. The message reads *Partner closed*.

A closed partner is read only. Its history stays.

### Export the partners {#partnerships-partners-export needs="partnerships export"}

Press **Export** at the top right of [Partnerships · Partners](/partnerships?tab=partners). On a computer,
the file follows the filters and search you set, in the tracker's columns. Phones do not offer it.

## Leads {#partnerships-leads screen=partnerships/leads}

A lead is an organisation you are pursuing. It is captured with its first deal. Its status comes from
that deal's stage, so you never type it.

![The Leads list](shot:partnerships-leads)

### Read a lead {#partnerships-leads-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Leads](/partnerships?tab=leads).
2. Press **Open**, **New**, **Qualified**, **Converted** or **Lost** to narrow the list. A lost lead
   leaves **Open**. **Lost** shows it again.
3. Read the cards: **New**, **Qualified**, **Contacted** and **Converted This Month**.
4. On a computer, **Leads by Source** shows where leads come from.
5. Press a lead to open its 360.
   ![A lead's 360](shot:partnerships-lead-360)
6. Hover over the status to read it: read from its deal's stage. **On Hold** marks a deal that waits.
7. Read the cards: **Projected Value**, **Days at Stage**, **Health** and **Days Since Last Contact**.
8. Use the tabs: **Overview**, **Deal**, **Documents**, **Work**, **Activity**, **Contacts** and
   **Details**.

### Add a lead {#partnerships-leads-add needs="partnerships create"}

1. Press **New Lead** at the top right of [Partnerships · Leads](/partnerships?tab=leads).
2. On **Organisation**, type the **Name**, pick the **Category** and the **Region**.
   ![The first step of New Lead](shot:partnerships-lead-new-1)
3. For **Cities**, pick each city from the search.
4. Pick the **Lead Owner**. It starts as you.
5. Read any warning that starts *Already here*. It names a partner or lead that looks the same. It
   never stops you.
6. Press **Advanced** for the **Website**, **Source**, **Impact**, **Impact Rating**, **Impact Note** and
   **Comments**.
7. Press **Next**. On **Contact**, fill in the **First Name** and **Last Name**. Add the **Position**,
   **Email** and **Phone** if you have them.
   ![The Contact step](shot:partnerships-lead-new-2)
8. Press **Next**. On **Deal**, type the **Title**, and the **Objective** if it helps.
   ![The Deal step](shot:partnerships-lead-new-3)
9. Pick the **Stage**, and fill in the **Projected Value** and **Expected Close**. For an event, pick the
   **Event Dates** and the **Venue**.
10. Press **Add Lead**. The message reads *Lead added*, and the lead opens.

Move the lead on as in [Move a deal's stage](#partnerships-partners-stage). Log calls as in
[Log an interaction](#partnerships-partners-log).

### Close a lead as lost {#partnerships-leads-lose needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open the lead and press **More**, then **Close as Lost**.
   ![Close as Lost](shot:partnerships-lead-lose)
2. Type the **Why**.
3. Press **Close as Lost**. The message reads *Lead closed as lost*.

### Convert a lead to a partner {#partnerships-leads-convert needs="partnerships edit"}

A lead also converts by itself when its agreement is signed. Use this when it signed on paper.

1. Open the lead and press **Convert to Partner**.
   ![Convert to Partner](shot:partnerships-lead-convert)
2. Upload the **Signed Copy**.
3. Press **Convert to Partner**. The message reads *Now a partner*, and the partner opens.

Its deal, contacts, documents, history and work move with it. The lead shows **Now a partner.** with
**Open Partner**.

### Change or export leads {#partnerships-leads-edit needs="partnerships edit"}

1. To change a lead, open it, press **More**, then **Edit Lead**, and press **Save Lead**.
2. {needs="partnerships export"} On a computer, press **Export** at the top right to download the
   leads you have filtered.

## Proposals {#partnerships-proposals screen=partnerships/proposals}

A proposal is a designed document you send a lead or partner. You give the Proposal Studio your notes.
It asks for what is missing, writes the proposal, and you edit it before you send it.

![The Proposals list](shot:partnerships-proposals)

### Read the proposals {#partnerships-proposals-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Proposals](/partnerships?tab=proposals).
2. Read the cards: **Sent This Year**, **Viewed, No Reply**, **Accepted** and **Acceptance Rate**.
3. Press a proposal. On a computer it opens beside the list. On a phone it fills the screen.
4. Read its status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Brief** | You have started it and not yet answered its questions |
| **Questions** | It is asking what it needs to know |
| **Writing** | The draft is being written |
| **Draft** | You can edit it |
| **Sent** | It went to your partner |
| **Viewed** | Your partner opened the link |
| **Accepted** | Your partner agreed |
| **Declined** | Your partner said no |
| **Expired** | It was not answered in time |
| **Withdrawn** | You took it back |

The bar at the top shows the steps: **Brief**, **Questions**, **Draft**, **Edit** and **Send**.

### Start a proposal {#partnerships-proposals-start needs="partnerships create"}

1. Press **New Proposal** at the top right of [Partnerships · Proposals](/partnerships?tab=proposals).
   You can also press **More**, then **New Proposal**, on a lead or a partner.
   ![New Proposal](shot:partnerships-proposal-brief)
2. Pick the **Deal**.
3. Paste your **Notes** from the conversations so far.
4. Upload the meeting's **Transcript** if you have one. It must be a text or Markdown file.
5. Add the **Cover Photograph** and the **Partner's Logo** if you have them.
6. Pick a **Template** to start from a proposal you saved.
7. Press **Start Proposal**. The message names it, such as *PRO-0012 started*.

### Answer the questions {#partnerships-proposals-questions needs="partnerships edit"}

The studio reads your notes and asks about what is missing. Each question is a card.

1. Open the proposal. Its cards are grouped: **The Partner**, **The Offer**, **Audience and Reach**,
   **Money**, **Timing** and **Proof**. Press a group to see its cards.
   ![The question cards](shot:partnerships-proposal-questions)
2. Hover over **Why** on a card to read why it asks.
3. Type your **Your Answer**, then press **Save Answer**. The card reads **Answered**.
4. If the card shows a **Suggested** answer, press **Use Suggestion** to take it. Check it first.
5. Press **Skip** on a card marked optional to leave it. A card marked **Required** cannot be skipped.
6. Press **Reshuffle** to swap a card for a different question. A card can be reshuffled three times.
7. On a phone, one card shows at a time. Press **Previous** and **Next** to move between them.
8. When you are ready, press **Write Draft**.

### Wait for the draft {#partnerships-proposals-draft needs="partnerships edit"}

1. The page shows its progress: **Reading**, **Writing**, **Checking Figures** and **Laying Out**.
   ![The draft being written](shot:partnerships-proposal-writing)
2. Stay on the page, or come back. It refreshes by itself.
3. When it finishes, the status reads **Draft** and the proposal shows.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Is Off** | AI is not switched on for Partnerships | Press **Build by Hand** and fill the proposal in yourself |
| **AI Limit Reached** | This month's AI budget is spent | Press **Build by Hand**, or ask an administrator |
| **The draft did not finish** | The writing stopped | Press **Try Again** |
| **The questions did not arrive** | The questions could not be made | Press **Try Again** |

### Edit the proposal {#partnerships-proposals-edit needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open a proposal that reads **Draft**. The proposal shows as your partner will see it.
   ![Editing a proposal](shot:partnerships-proposal-edit)
2. Press a page to select it. Its name and layout show in the panel.
3. Press **Edit Words** to change the text. Press **Save Page** when you are done.
4. Press **Rewrite Section** to have a page rewritten. Type an **Instruction**, such as *Warmer,
   shorter*, and press **Rewrite Section**.
   ![Rewrite Section](shot:partnerships-proposal-rewrite)
5. Read the rewrite. Press **Use Rewrite** to keep it, or **Cancel** to leave the page as it was.
6. On a computer, pick another **Layout**, or press **Move Up** or **Move Down** to reorder.
7. Press **Hide Page** to leave a page out. **Show Page** brings it back.
8. To swap a picture, upload another one in the field that starts with *Replace*.
9. Where a page shows *Confirm* and a figure, check that figure against its source before you send.

A figure the studio could not find in your notes or the records is marked so you check it. A
placeholder picture is named when you send.

### Send the proposal {#partnerships-proposals-send needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Press **Send Proposal**.
   ![Send Proposal](shot:partnerships-proposal-send)
2. Read the notes at the top. **Stand-in pictures** names pictures that are placeholders. **Confirm
   figures** names figures to check first.
3. Check the **To** address. It starts as the contact's email. Add a **Cc** if you want one.
4. Check the **Subject**, and write the **Message**. Press **Write With AI** to have a message drafted.
5. Press **Confirm and Send**. The message reads such as *PRO-0012 sent*.

Your partner gets the PDF and a private link. When they open it, the status reads **Viewed**, you are
told, and Home's **Proposals Out** shows when. Your partner's own steps are in
[Public Site Help](https://go.mycityapp.io/help#public-proposal).

### After you send {#partnerships-proposals-after needs="partnerships edit"}

1. When your partner agrees, press **Mark Accepted**. The status reads **Accepted**. Press **Open Deal**
   to move the deal on.
2. When they say no, press **More**, then **Mark Declined**. Type the **Reason** and press
   **Mark Declined**.
   ![A viewed proposal](shot:partnerships-proposal-sent)
3. To take a sent proposal back, press **More**, then **Withdraw**, and confirm. The link stops
   working.
4. To change a sent proposal, press **Revise**. It starts a new version as a draft.
5. Press **More**, then **Download PDF** to save the proposal, or **Copy Link** to copy its private link.
6. Press **More**, then **Save as Template**. Type the **Name** and press **Save Template** to reuse the
   proposal's design.

## Contracts & MOUs {#partnerships-contracts screen=partnerships/contracts-and-mous}

Agreements run from draft to renewal here. Each is approved by MyCity, signed by the partner, then
sealed with a reference and a QR code anyone can check.

![The Contracts & MOUs list](shot:partnerships-contracts)

### Read the agreements {#partnerships-contracts-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Contracts & MOUs](/partnerships?tab=contracts-and-mous).
2. Read the cards: **Active**, **In Approval**, **Awaiting Signature** and **Renewals 180 Days**.
   Hover over **Renewals 180 Days** to read what it counts.
3. Press **All**, **Draft**, **In Review**, **In Approval**, **Sent**, **Active**, **Expiring** or
   **Closed** to narrow the list.
4. Press an agreement.
   ![An agreement open](shot:partnerships-agreement)
5. On a computer, the line at the top follows it from **Draft** to **Renewed**.
6. **Approval** shows the **Stage** and who it is **Waiting On**. **Signers** shows each signer's state.
   **Reference and QR** shows its number and the page that verifies it.
7. **Term and Renewal** shows **Starts**, **Ends**, **Renewal** and **Value**. Hover over **Renewal**
   to read how the reminder works.

### Make an agreement {#partnerships-contracts-generate needs="partnerships create"}

1. Press **Generate Agreement** at the top right of
   [Partnerships · Contracts & MOUs](/partnerships?tab=contracts-and-mous). You can also press
   **More**, then **Generate Agreement**, on a lead or a partner.
   ![Generate Agreement](shot:partnerships-agreement-generate)
2. Pick the **Deal** and the **Kind**: **Deal Merchant**, **MOU**, **Service Agreement** or
   **Contract**.
3. Press **Generate Agreement**. The message reads *Draft ready to review*.

The draft fills in from the partner, its contact and the deal. A field it could not fill stays marked.

### Edit the draft {#partnerships-contracts-edit needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open the draft. A line such as *Fill the start date before you submit* names what is missing.
2. Press **Edit Draft**.
   ![Edit Draft](shot:partnerships-agreement-edit)
3. Change the **Wording**. Fill in each marked field.
4. Pick **Starts On**, and **Ends On** if it ends.
5. Set the **Notice Days**, the days before the end that the owner is reminded to renew.
6. Press **Save Draft**. The message reads *Draft saved*.

### Send an agreement for approval {#partnerships-contracts-submit needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open the draft and press **Submit for Approval**.
2. **Approval** shows its **Stage** and who it is **Waiting On**.
3. Who approves each stage is set on [Settings · Approvals](/settings?tab=approvals). It starts with the
   owner's Head of Department, then the CEO. Each is told, and decides as in
   [Approve or reject an agreement](#partnerships-contracts-approve).
4. When every stage approves, **Approval** reads **Approved** and **Send for Signature** shows.
5. If an approver rejects it, a line such as *Returned: the date is wrong* says why. Change the draft
   and submit it again.

### Approve or reject an agreement {#partnerships-contracts-approve needs="partnerships approve"}

An agreement waits on its approvers before it can be sent. When it waits on you, it is listed under
**Need You** on Home.

1. Open [Home · Overview](/home?tab=overview) and press the agreement under **Need You**. You can also
   open it from [Partnerships · Contracts & MOUs](/partnerships?tab=contracts-and-mous) by pressing
   **In Approval**, then the agreement.
   ![An agreement waiting on you](shot:partnerships-agreement-approve)
2. Read the wording, then **Approval**. It shows the **Stage** and who it is **Waiting On**.
3. Press **Approve**. The message reads such as *AGR-2026-0007 approved*. The first approval passes it
   to the CEO. The CEO's approval makes **Approval** read **Approved**.
4. To refuse it, press **Reject**.
   ![Reject Agreement](shot:partnerships-agreement-reject)
5. Type the **Reason** and press **Reject Agreement**. The message reads such as
   *AGR-2026-0007 rejected*. The agreement goes back to Draft, and its owner reads your reason.

**Approve** and **Reject** show only when the waiting stage names you. The CEO sees them at every
stage. Nobody decides an agreement they submitted themselves.

### Send it for signature {#partnerships-contracts-send needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Press **Send for Signature**.
   ![Send for Signature](shot:partnerships-agreement-send)
2. Check **Partner's Signer**: the **Full Name**, **Title** and **Email**. It starts as the contact who
   signs agreements.
3. If MyCity countersigns, check **MyCity's Signer** the same way.
4. Add a **Message** if you want to.
5. Press **Send for Signature**. The message reads *Sent for signature*.

Each signer gets an email with a private link. The status reads **Sent**, then **Viewed** once they
open it, then **Signed**. Your partner's own steps are in
[Public Site Help](https://go.mycityapp.io/help#public-agreement).

When it is fully signed, the sealed copy is filed with the partner's documents and the lead becomes a
partner. The deal's checklist becomes tasks, and its event days go on the team's calendar.

### Send it again or correct a signer {#partnerships-contracts-again needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open an agreement that reads **Sent** or **Viewed**.
2. Press **Send Again** to send every signer a new link.
3. To change a signer's name or address, press **More**, then **Correct Signers**.
   ![Correct the signers](shot:partnerships-agreement-again)
4. Press **Send Again**. The message reads *Sent again to every signer*.

If a signer declines, a line says so with their reason. If the link expires, the line reads
*Not signed in time. Send it again.*

### Record a copy signed on paper {#partnerships-contracts-record needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Press **More**, then **Record Signed Copy**.
   ![Record Signed Copy](shot:partnerships-agreement-record)
2. Upload the **Signed Copy**.
3. Pick **Signed On**, and type who it was **Signed By** and their **Title**.
4. Press **Record Copy**. The message reads *Signed copy recorded*.

The agreement reads **Signed Outside the Hub**. It is filed and the lead converts, as for a signature
made on screen.

> **Tip:** Attach the signed copy in Praxis AI instead, and it finds the agreement waiting for its
> signature and draws **Record Signed Copy** with the day and the signer filled
> ([Attach a document](#praxis-ask-attach)).

### Renew an agreement {#partnerships-contracts-renew needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Open a signed or active agreement and press **Renew**.
   ![Renew Agreement](shot:partnerships-agreement-renew)
2. Pick **Starts On**, and **Ends On** if it ends.
3. Tick **Open a New Deal** if the renewal is a new deal.
4. Press **Draft Renewal**. The message reads *Renewal drafted*, and the new draft opens.

The owner is reminded before the renewal date, then 30, 7 and 1 days before it.

### Close an agreement {#partnerships-contracts-close needs="partnerships edit"}

1. Press **More**, then **Close Agreement**.
   ![Close Agreement](shot:partnerships-agreement-close)
2. Type the **Reason**.
3. Press **Close Agreement**. The message reads *Agreement closed*.

A closed agreement stays on record. Any open approval is withdrawn.

### Download an agreement {#partnerships-contracts-download}

Press **More**, then **Download PDF**. You get the signed copy when there is one, and the draft when
there is not.

## Analytics {#partnerships-analytics screen=partnerships/analytics}

Analytics shows how the partnership team is doing in a period: each lead owner's results, and the
deals by month, category, region and stage.

![Analytics](shot:partnerships-analytics)

### Read the analytics {#partnerships-analytics-read}

1. Open [Partnerships · Analytics](/partnerships?tab=analytics).
2. Read the dates under the title. They are the period shown.
3. Read the four cards: **New Prospects**, **Partnerships Signed**, **Meetings Held** and
   **Proposals Sent**.
4. **By Lead Owner** has a row for each owner. Hover over a measure's name to read what it counts:

| Measure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **New Prospects** | Leads the owner identified in the period |
| **Meetings Held** | Meetings logged, and calendar meetings that took place |
| **Proposals Sent** | Proposals sent on the owner's deals |
| **Signed** | Deals that reached a signed stage in the period |
| **Active Partnerships** | Partners the owner keeps with a live deal on the last day |
| **Downloads** | App downloads in the returns of the owner's deals |
| **Vendor Leads** | New prospects in a category that counts as vendor |

5. **Rating** shows an owner's rating from Performance, where Performance allows it to be seen.
6. **Signed per Month** charts the deals signed each month.
7. {needs="cap partnerships.money"} **Returns per Month** charts MyCity's commission from each month.
8. **By Category**, **By Region** and **Stage Conversion** are tables. **Stage Conversion** shows how
   many deals reached the next stage.

On a phone, each owner is a card, and the charts after **Signed per Month** are left off.

### Pick a period {#partnerships-analytics-period}

1. Press **This Month**, **This Quarter** or **This Year** above the cards.
2. For other dates, press **Custom**.
   ![Custom Period](shot:partnerships-analytics-period)
3. Pick the **Period**: the first and last day.
4. Press **Show Period**. The page shows the dates you picked.
5. Press **Change Dates** to pick again.

### Export the analytics {#partnerships-analytics-export needs="partnerships export"}

On a computer, press **Export** at the top right. You get a workbook of the period.
