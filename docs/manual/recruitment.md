Recruitment is how MyCity hires. Operations & People write a vacancy and publish it to the careers
page. Anyone can apply there with a CV. You screen the applications, send tests, interview the best
and keep good people for later.

Open it from **Recruitment** in the menu on a computer. On a phone, press **More** in the bar at the
bottom, then **Recruitment**. This chapter covers five tabs along the top: **Vacancies**,
**Candidates**, **Assessments**, **Interviews** and **Talent Pool**. What candidates see is in
[The Public Site](#public-careers).

> **Important:** Operations & People, Management, Implementation and the CEO see every vacancy. A
> Head of Department sees the vacancies whose hiring manager is in their team, and their candidates.
> Anyone on an interview panel sees that interview, whoever's role it is.

## Vacancies {#recruitment-vacancies screen=recruitment/vacancies icon=brief}

Vacancies lists each role MyCity is hiring for, and the seats on the organogram with no vacancy yet.

![The Vacancies screen](shot:recruitment-vacancies)

### Read the vacancies {#recruitment-vacancies-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. The tiles count the **Open Vacancies**, the **Applicants**, those **In Interview** and the roles
   **Past Closing Date**. A phone shows two tiles a row.
3. The list shows each role with its department, location, applicants, closing day and status.
4. On a computer, the funnel shows how many candidates stand at each stage.
5. Press a vacancy. On a computer it opens beside the list; on a phone it opens in a sheet.
   ![A vacancy's sheet](shot:recruitment-vacancy-sheet)
6. The sheet shows the role's details and its public link. Press **Copy Link** to share it.
7. Press **See Candidates** to open its candidates on the board.
8. Press **Careers Page** at the top to open the careers page as a candidate sees it.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, not on the careers page yet |
| **Open** | On the careers page and taking applications |
| **Paused** | Takes no applications. Its link keeps working |
| **Closed** | Finished: filled, cancelled or put on hold |
| **Closing Date Passed** | Still open here, but the careers page takes no more applications |

### Write a new vacancy {#recruitment-vacancies-new needs="recruitment create"}

The form has three steps: **Role**, **Details** and **Pay and Publish**. Each step checks its own
fields before **Next** lets you on.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **New Vacancy** at the top right. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![New Vacancy](shot:recruitment-vacancy-new)
3. On **Role**, type the job title and pick the department and the hiring manager.
4. Pick who the role reports to, and type how many people you are hiring. Press **Next**.
5. On **Details**, pick the employment type and the arrangement, such as hybrid.
6. Under the arrangement, you may add a note, such as `2 days in office, 3 from home`.
7. Start typing the area under location and pick it from the list. A remote role has no location.
8. Fill in the working days, the probation in months and the years of experience needed.
9. Write the description. It opens with the About MyCityApp paragraph by itself.
10. Fill in **Required Experience and Qualifications** and, if you like, **What We Are Looking For**.
    Press **Next**.
11. On **Pay and Publish**, type the salary band and choose whether the careers page shows it.
12. Pick the target start day and the day it **Closes On**.
13. Press **Publish Vacancy** to put it on the careers page now, or **Save Draft** to finish later.

| Field | What to enter | Needed to publish |
|---|---|---|
| Title | The job title, such as `Content Producer` | Yes |
| Department | The department the role sits in | Yes |
| Hiring manager | An active employee who decides the hire | Yes |
| Location | An area picked from the list | Yes, unless remote |
| Description | What the role is, its duties and how it is measured; 80 characters or more | Yes |
| **Required Experience and Qualifications** | What a candidate must have; 20 characters or more | Yes |
| **What We Are Looking For** | What would help but is not needed | No |
| Salary band | The lowest and highest pay | No |
| **Closes On** | A day after today; it closes at 23:59 Lagos time | Yes |

A new vacancy takes its interview traits from [Careers Settings](#recruitment-vacancies-settings),
and its scorecard at the usual weights. Change either before you publish.

> **Tip:** A draft can stay unfinished. Only **Publish Vacancy** needs every field marked Yes.

### Draft the description with AI {#recruitment-vacancies-write-ai needs="recruitment edit"}

1. On a new vacancy, or one you opened with **Edit**, fill in the title, the department and anything
   you know first.
2. Press **Write With AI**.
3. Read the draft: the description, **Required Experience and Qualifications** and **What We Are
   Looking For**, in MyCity's usual shape.
4. Press **Use Draft** to put it in the form.
5. Change what you need. Nothing is saved until you save the vacancy.

> **Tip:** When **Write With AI** is not shown, AI is off or has reached its limit. Its help term
> says which. Write the description yourself; the form works as before.

### Start from an empty seat {#recruitment-vacancies-suggested needs="recruitment create"}

**Suggested From Organogram** lists the vacant seats on the organogram that have no vacancy yet.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Find the seat under **Suggested From Organogram**.
3. Press **Create Vacancy** beside it.
4. **New Vacancy** opens with the seat's title, department and reporting line filled in.
5. Follow [Write a new vacancy](#recruitment-vacancies-new) from step 3. Nothing is made until you
   save.
6. {needs="people edit"} You can also press **Create Vacancy** on a vacant seat on
   [People · Organogram](/people?tab=organogram).

A seat holds one draft, open or paused vacancy at a time. Once it has one, it leaves the list.

### Publish, pause, resume or close {#recruitment-vacancies-lifecycle needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. For a draft, press **Publish**. It goes on the careers page at its own link.
3. To stop applications for a while, press **Pause**. The link keeps working and says so.
4. Press **Resume** to take applications again.
5. To end it, press **Close** and pick why: filled, cancelled or on hold. Confirm it.

The link is made when you first publish and never changes, even after a pause.

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Its closing day has passed* | **Resume** needs a closing day still to come | Press **Edit**, pick a new **Closes On**, save, then press **Resume** |

A closed vacancy cannot be changed or opened again. Write a new one instead. Its candidates stay
where they are, and their links work until they lapse.

### Change a vacancy {#recruitment-vacancies-edit needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Edit**. The same three steps open with what is saved.
3. Change what you need. Emptying a field that is not needed clears it.
4. Save it. The careers page shows the change at once.

Making a role remote removes its location. A closed vacancy has no **Edit**.

### Set the questions {#recruitment-vacancies-questions needs="recruitment edit"}

A vacancy asks two kinds of question. Its Apply questions are on the form a candidate fills in. Its
interview traits are what each panelist rates, from 1 to 10.

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Questions**.
3. Under the Apply questions, add a question. Pick written, yes or no, or a choice, and whether it
   must be answered.
4. Under the interview traits, take out a trait that does not fit the role, or add one of its own.
   A role has at most 20.
5. To get ideas, press **Suggest With AI**. Keep the suggestions you want and change their words.
6. Save the questions.

A yes or no question that must be answered counts in the candidate's estimate. A question someone
has already answered keeps its words: changing them makes a new question in its place.

### Set the scorecard {#recruitment-vacancies-scorecard needs="recruitment edit"}

The scorecard ranks the role's candidates. It has five parts: screening, psychometric, technical,
case study and interview.

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Scorecard**.
3. Switch off a part this role skips. Screening or interview must stay on.
4. Type each part's weight as a whole number. The parts left on add up to 100.
5. Save the scorecard. Every candidate's total changes at once.

A new role starts at 15, 15, 25, 20 and 25, all on. The board leaves out **Assessment** when all
three test parts are off, and **Interview** when the interview part is off.

### Change the candidate emails {#recruitment-vacancies-emails needs="recruitment edit"}

Candidates get five emails: application received, invitation to apply, assessment, interview and
not progressing.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Candidate Emails** at the top.
   ![Candidate Emails](shot:recruitment-candidate-emails)
3. Press the email you want to change.
4. Change its subject and its words. Keep the words in double braces, such as
   `{{ vacancy_title }}`: the hub fills them in.
5. Save it. The next email sent uses the new words.
6. To go back to the wording MyCity started with, press **Restore Default**.

> **Important:** Only someone who may change every vacancy can save an email, as each one goes to
> every candidate.

### Careers Settings {#recruitment-vacancies-settings}

Careers Settings holds two things every vacancy uses. **About MyCityApp** is the paragraph every
vacancy opens with. **Default Traits** are the interview traits a new vacancy starts with.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Careers Settings** at the top.
   ![Careers Settings](shot:recruitment-careers-settings)
3. {needs="recruitment edit"} Change the **About MyCityApp** paragraph.
4. {needs="recruitment edit"} Under **Default Traits**, add, reword or take out a trait.
5. {needs="recruitment edit"} Save the settings. Vacancies already written keep their own traits.

> **Tip:** Only someone who may change every vacancy can save Careers Settings. Others can read
> them.

## Candidates {#recruitment-candidates screen=recruitment/candidates icon=users}

Candidates is the board: each application as a card, in a column for its stage.

![The Candidates board](shot:recruitment-candidates)

### Read the board {#recruitment-candidates-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. The tiles count the **Candidates**, those **To Screen**, those **In Interview** and those
   **Shortlisted**.
3. Pick a role in the vacancy filter to see only its candidates.
4. The columns are **Application**, **Screening**, **Assessment**, **Interview** and **Shortlist**.
   A role that skips a stage has no column for it.
5. Each card shows the name, the role, the screening score and how many days it has been at its
   stage.
6. On a phone, press a stage at the top to see its cards, one stage at a time.

The best weighted total comes first. A score never moves, rejects or hides anyone; a person does.

| On a card | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Provisional** | The score is an estimate worked out from the form only. The CV has not been read yet |
| A score with no tag | AI has read the CV against the role |

### Open a candidate {#recruitment-candidates-sheet}

1. Press a card on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates). On a computer it opens
   beside the board; on a phone it opens in a sheet.
   ![A candidate's sheet](shot:recruitment-candidate-sheet)
2. The sheet shows their details, their CV, their answers and their assessments and interviews.
3. **Screening** shows the score, **Provisional** while it is the estimate, and the AI's summary.
4. **Scorecard** shows each part's score and the **Weighted Total**. Beside it, *3 of 5 parts* says
   how many parts have a score yet.
5. **Stage History** lists each move, who made it and when.
6. Press the CV to open it.

A CV that is *Being checked* is still being scanned for viruses. It opens once it is clean. A CV
with a virus never opens, and the sheet says so.

### Score one candidate with AI {#recruitment-candidates-score needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Score With AI**.
3. AI reads the CV, the cover letter and the answers against the role and its must-meet conditions.
4. The score, from 0 to 100, and a short summary replace the estimate. **Provisional** goes.

The summary says which conditions are met and what AI could not check. A Word file, or a scan with
no text, is scored without the CV, and the summary says so.

### Screen a role with AI {#recruitment-candidates-screen needs="recruitment edit"}

Screen With AI reads every candidate of one role who has no AI score yet.

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Pick the role in the vacancy filter.
3. Press **Screen With AI**. You can also start it from ⌘K.
   ![Screen With AI](shot:recruitment-screen-ai)
4. Type one to three must-meet conditions, such as `A degree in accounting` or `Lives in Lagos`.
5. Confirm it. The candidates are read one by one, and each card shows its score when it is ready.

The role keeps its conditions. Next time, leave them as they are and they are used again.

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A condition is refused | It names an age, a sex, a marital status, a religion, a tribe or a state of origin | Write a condition about skills, experience or where they live |
| None were read | Every candidate of the role already has an AI score | Use **Score With AI** on one candidate to read them again |

> **Tip:** When **Screen With AI** and **Score With AI** are not shown, AI is off or has reached
> its limit. The estimates still rank the board.

### Move a candidate {#recruitment-candidates-move needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. On a computer, drag the card to its new column.
3. Or open the candidate and press the button for the stage, such as *Move to Interview*. On a phone
   it is at the foot of the sheet.

A card can move to any stage ahead of it, or one stage back. **Shortlist** is the last column for
now. Each move goes in **Stage History**, and the days at stage start again.

### Stop a candidate {#recruitment-candidates-not-progressing needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Not Progressing**.
3. Type the reason, at least 10 characters. Only staff read it.
4. Confirm it.

The candidate is emailed that they are not going further. Their links stop working, and their
unfinished assessments are cancelled. Their interviews are called off, and the panel and the
candidate get the calendar cancel. The card stays at its stage, marked as not progressing.

A candidate of a closed vacancy cannot be stopped or scored.

### Bring a candidate back {#recruitment-candidates-reopen needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Reopen**. They go back to the stage they left.
3. Their old links stay stopped. Send a new assessment or interview if they need one.

### Keep a candidate for later {#recruitment-candidates-pool needs="recruitment create"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Add to Pool**.
3. Type why they are worth keeping, at least 5 characters.
4. Confirm it. They are kept in the [Talent Pool](#recruitment-pool) for 24 months.

Adding someone already in the pool renews the reason and the 24 months. When they are in the pool
from a role you cannot see, the hub says *Already in the pool*.

### Send an assessment {#recruitment-candidates-assess needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Send Assessment**.
3. Pick the assessment. Only active ones are offered.
4. Type how many days they have to start it, from 1 to 14. It starts at 3.
5. For a take-home brief with no time limit, also type how many days they have to finish it after
   starting, from 1 to 14. It starts at 5.
6. Send it. The candidate gets an email with their own link.

A candidate takes each assessment once. To send one to everyone at a stage, use
[Send to Candidates](#recruitment-assessments-send).

### Schedule an interview from the board {#recruitment-candidates-interview needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Schedule Interview**.
3. Follow [Schedule an interview](#recruitment-interviews-schedule) from step 3.

### Invite someone to apply {#recruitment-candidates-invite needs="recruitment create"}

Invite a person you have found, so they apply to one open role.

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Invite to Apply** at the top. You can also start it from ⌘K.
   ![Invite to Apply](shot:recruitment-invite)
3. Pick the open vacancy.
4. Type the person's name and email.
5. Send it. They get an email with an Apply link that works for 7 days.

They apply exactly as anyone does from the careers page, and arrive on the board.

## Assessments {#recruitment-assessments screen=recruitment/assessments icon=check}

Assessments holds MyCity's tests and take-home briefs, and every sitting a candidate has taken.

![The Assessments screen](shot:recruitment-assessments)

### Read the assessments {#recruitment-assessments-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. The tiles count the **Active** assessments, how many were **Sent**, those **Awaiting Marking**
   and the **Average Score**.
3. Press **Assessments** at the top for the library, or **Sittings** for who took what.
4. Each assessment shows its name, type, minutes, questions, how many were sent, the average and its
   status.
5. Press an assessment to see its questions, and the **Points** each option scores.

An assessment is timed, with minutes and a clock, or untimed: a take-home brief due by a day.

### Make a new assessment {#recruitment-assessments-new needs="recruitment create"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **New Assessment** at the top right. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![New Assessment](shot:recruitment-assessment-new)
3. Type the name, such as `Excel Skills Test`, and pick the type: psychometric, technical, case
   study or practical.
4. Write the instructions the candidate reads before they start.
5. For a timed test, type the minutes, from 5 to 240. Leave it empty for a take-home brief.
6. Type the pass score if it has one, then save it.

It gets a code such as *ASM-0001*. It stays off until it has questions and you
[switch it on](#recruitment-assessments-activate).

### Change an assessment {#recruitment-assessments-edit needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Edit**.
3. Change the name, the type, the instructions or the pass score.
4. To make a test untimed, empty the minutes. Save it.

The minutes cannot change once the assessment has been sent to anyone.

### Write the questions {#recruitment-assessments-questions needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Questions**.
3. Add a question and pick its kind.
4. For a choice, type each option and the **Points** it scores. The question's most is its best
   option.
5. For a written or a file answer, type the most it can score. You mark these yourself.
6. Put the questions in order, then save them.

| Kind | How it is marked |
|---|---|
| Choice | By itself, from the points of the option picked |
| Written | By hand, on [Mark Answers](#recruitment-assessments-mark) |
| File | By hand. The candidate uploads a file up to 25 MB, or gives a link to a larger one |

> **Important:** Once an assessment has been sent to anyone, its questions cannot change. Switch it
> off and make a new one.

### Suggest questions with AI {#recruitment-assessments-suggest needs="recruitment edit"}

1. In **Questions**, press **Suggest With AI**.
2. Say what the questions should test and how many you want.
3. To fit them to a role, pick the vacancy.
4. Read the suggestions. Keep the ones you want, and check each option's **Points**.
5. Save the questions. Nothing is kept until you do.

### Switch an assessment on or off {#recruitment-assessments-activate needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Activate**. It needs at least one question. Only active assessments can be sent.
3. To stop sending it, press **Deactivate**. Sittings already sent go on.

### Move an assessment to another hub {#recruitment-assessments-export}

Assessments written on staging reach the live hub this way, so nothing is typed twice.

1. {needs="recruitment export"} Open the assessment on
   [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. {needs="recruitment export"} Press **Export**. A file of the assessment and its questions
   downloads. See [Save a file you download](#start-downloads).
3. {needs="recruitment create"} On the other hub, open
   [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments) and press **Import**.
4. {needs="recruitment create"} Pick the file. A new assessment is made, switched off, for you to
   check and activate.

An assessment with no questions cannot be exported.

### Send to every candidate at a stage {#recruitment-assessments-send needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Send to Candidates**.
3. Pick the assessment, the role and the stage. The stage starts at **Assessment**.
4. Type the days to start it and, for a take-home brief, the days to finish it.
5. Send it. The hub says how many were sent, and how many already had it.

Only active candidates are sent it, each once. Each gets an email with their own link.

### Read the sittings {#recruitment-assessments-sittings}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments) and press **Sittings**.
   ![Sittings awaiting marking](shot:recruitment-sittings)
2. Each sitting shows the candidate, the assessment, where it stands and the score.
3. Press **Awaiting Marking** to see only those waiting for you.
4. Press a sitting to read each answer. Open a file, or the link they gave.

| Where it stands | What it means |
|---|---|
| Sent | The link is out; not started yet |
| In progress | Started and still running |
| Submitted or timed out | Finished. Waiting for marks, or scored already |
| Scored | Every answer has a mark; the score is on the candidate |
| Expired | Never started by its start day |
| Cancelled | Stopped by staff, or by Not Progressing |

### Mark the answers {#recruitment-assessments-mark needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open a sitting under **Awaiting Marking**.
2. Press **Mark Answers**.
   ![Mark Answers](shot:recruitment-mark-answers)
3. For each written or file answer, type its mark, up to the question's most. A comment is optional.
4. Save the marks.

Once every answer has a mark, the sitting is scored and its percentage goes on the candidate. A
question left unanswered scores 0. A mark left empty is refused, never read as 0.

## Interviews {#recruitment-interviews screen=recruitment/interviews icon=calendar}

Interviews holds each interview, its panel and their cards. Each panelist scores alone and cannot see
the others' cards until the round closes.

![The Interviews screen](shot:recruitment-interviews)

### Read the interviews {#recruitment-interviews-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. **Upcoming** shows what is coming; press **Past** for those held.
3. Each shows the candidate, the role, when, how many of the panel have submitted, and the round.
4. Press an interview. On a computer it opens beside the list; on a phone it opens in a sheet.
   ![An interview's sheet](shot:recruitment-interview-sheet)
5. It shows when and where, or the video link, and the **Candidate Details**.
6. The panel shows each person as **Submitted** or **Waiting**.

While the round is open, nobody reads another person's card. That includes the hiring manager and
the CEO.

### Schedule an interview {#recruitment-interviews-schedule needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Schedule Interview**. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![Schedule Interview](shot:recruitment-interview-schedule)
3. Pick the candidate and the role they applied for.
4. Pick the day, the start time and the end time.
5. Pick how it is held: in person at a worksite, by video with its link, or by phone.
6. Pick the panel. Only people who hold Recruitment are offered.
7. Save it.

The candidate moves to **Interview** and gets an email with a calendar file. Each panelist gets a
calendar invite, also on [My Workspace · Calendar](/workspace?tab=calendar).

Nobody sits on a panel about themselves. A candidate who works at MyCity cannot be on the panel.

### Change or call off an interview {#recruitment-interviews-reschedule needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Reschedule** to change the time, the place or the panel. Save it.
3. Everyone gets the new time. A change to the panel alone sends the candidate nothing.
4. To call it off, press **Cancel**, type why and confirm it. Everyone gets the calendar cancel.

A panel needs at least one person. Taking someone off removes their unsent card; a card already
submitted stays.

### Fill in the candidate's details {#recruitment-interviews-details needs="recruitment edit"}

The details are filled once for the whole panel, before or during the interview.

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Candidate Details**.
3. Type their current employer.
4. Pick their academics: **Outstanding**, **Very Good**, **Good**, **Average** or **Poor**.
5. Type their total experience and their experience relevant to MyCity, in months.
6. Save them. Every panelist sees them on their card.

### Fill in your card {#recruitment-interviews-card}

You have a card only for an interview whose panel you sit on.

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **My Card**. On a phone it opens as a full sheet.
   ![My Card](shot:recruitment-my-card)
3. Read the candidate's details at the top. Press **View CV** to open their CV.
4. For each trait, press a star from 1 to 10. The tenth star is 10. On a phone the stars sit in two
   rows of five.
5. Each star saves as you press it. To take a rating back, press the same star again: the trait
   is not rated.
6. Your own score shows as you go: the average of the traits you have rated so far.
7. Pick your decision: **Select**, **On Hold** or **Reject**.
8. Write any comments. They are the form's Others, and optional.
9. To tidy your comments, press **Tidy With AI**, read the result and keep it or not.
10. Write anything for yourself under **Private Notes**. Only you ever see these.
11. The rest of your card saves as you go too. When it is done, press **Submit Card**.

**Submit Card** needs every trait rated and a decision. Once submitted, the card cannot change.

A role with no traits asks for one overall rating from 1 to 10 instead. Until you submit, the hub
reminds you once a day after the interview ends.

### Close a round {#recruitment-interviews-close needs="recruitment edit"}

The round closes by itself when the last panelist submits. To close it before then:

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Close Round**. It works once the interview has started.
3. Confirm it. Only submitted cards count; an unsent card stays its author's.

A closed round never opens again. The hiring manager is told it has closed.

### Read the results {#recruitment-interviews-results}

Once the round closes, everyone who may see the interview reads the results.

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Each trait shows every panelist's rating and the average across the panel.
3. Each panelist's decision and comments show under their name.
4. The total is the panel's average rating times ten, out of 100. It is the interview's part of the
   scorecard.

Private notes never show to anyone but the person who wrote them.

### Write the report {#recruitment-interviews-report needs="recruitment edit or manager"}

The hiring manager writes one report for the panel once the round is closed. Someone who may change
the role's interviews can write it too.

1. Open the closed interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Write Report**.
3. Write what the panel found, at least 40 characters.
4. Pick the decision: **Select**, **On Hold** or **Reject**.
5. Save it. The Interview Assessment Form is made from the whole card.

You can change the report later. Each save makes the form again.

### Download the form {#recruitment-interviews-form}

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews), once its report is
   written.
2. Press **Download Form**. The Interview Assessment Form downloads as a PDF.
3. It holds the candidate's details, each trait with every rating and the average, each decision,
   the total and the report. See [Save a file you download](#start-downloads).

A candidate the panel selects moves to **Shortlist** on the
[board](#recruitment-candidates-move).

## Talent Pool {#recruitment-pool screen=recruitment/talent-pool icon=star}

The Talent Pool keeps good candidates who were not hired this time, for 24 months.

![The Talent Pool](shot:recruitment-pool)

### Read the pool {#recruitment-pool-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Talent Pool](/recruitment?tab=talent-pool).
2. The tiles count who is **In the Pool**, those **Invited Back**, those **Hired From the Pool** and
   the **Average Score**.
3. Each person shows the role they applied for, their score, when they were added and why.
4. **Kept Until** is the day they leave the pool by themselves.
5. **Invited** shows when someone has been invited to a later role.
6. Press a person to open them. On a phone, when they were added and why are in the sheet.

The score is the one from the role they came from, with **Provisional** when it is the estimate.

### Add someone to the pool {#recruitment-pool-add needs="recruitment create"}

People join the pool from their candidate's sheet. See
[Keep a candidate for later](#recruitment-candidates-pool).

### Invite someone back {#recruitment-pool-invite needs="recruitment create, recruitment edit"}

1. Open the person on [Recruitment · Talent Pool](/recruitment?tab=talent-pool).
2. Press **Invite to Apply**. On a phone it is at the foot of the sheet.
3. Pick the open vacancy.
4. Send it. They get an email with an Apply link that works for 7 days.

Their row then shows **Invited**. When they apply, their application comes from the pool.

### Take someone out of the pool {#recruitment-pool-remove needs="recruitment delete"}

1. Open the person on [Recruitment · Talent Pool](/recruitment?tab=talent-pool).
2. Press **Remove**.
3. Type why, and confirm it. They leave the list; nothing about them is deleted.

### Export the pool {#recruitment-pool-export needs="recruitment export"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Talent Pool](/recruitment?tab=talent-pool).
2. Search or filter the list if you want only some of it.
3. Press **Export**. The workbook is made, then downloads on the device you pressed it on.
4. See [Save a file you download](#start-downloads).

Only you, and the CEO, can open the file it makes.
