Recruitment is how MyCity hires. Operations & People write a vacancy and publish it to the careers
page. Anyone can apply there with a CV. You screen the applications, send tests, interview the best
and keep good people for later.

Open it from **Recruitment** in the menu on a computer. On a phone, press **More** in the bar at the
bottom, then **Recruitment**. This chapter covers eight tabs along the top: **Vacancies**,
**Candidates**, **Assessments**, **Interviews**, **Offers**, **Onboarding**, **Talent Pool** and **Analytics**. What candidates see
is in [The Public Site](#public-careers).

> **Important:** Operations & People, Management, Implementation and the CEO see every vacancy. A
> Head of Department sees the vacancies whose hiring manager is in their team, and their candidates.
> Anyone on an interview panel sees that interview, whoever's role it is.

## Vacancies {#recruitment-vacancies screen=recruitment/vacancies icon=brief}

Vacancies lists each role MyCity is hiring for, and the seats on the organogram with no vacancy yet.

![The Vacancies screen](shot:recruitment-vacancies)

### Read the vacancies {#recruitment-vacancies-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. The tiles count the **Open Vacancies**, the **Applicants**, those **In Interview** and the roles
   **Past Closing Date**. A phone shows two tiles a row. **Applicants** counts everyone who applied
   to an open or paused role, whatever became of them.
3. The list shows each role with its department, openings, location, applicants, closing day and
   status.
   A role's applicants are counted the same way, so the tile is the open and paused roles' added up.
4. Press a vacancy. It opens as its own page. Press **Back to Vacancies** to return to the list.
   ![A vacancy's page](shot:recruitment-vacancy-sheet)
5. The sheet shows the role's details and its public link. Press **Copy Link** to share it. Its
   description, required experience and what we look for read with their headings and lists, as
   the careers page shows them.
6. At its foot is the vacancy's next step: **Publish**, **Pause** or **Resume**. Its other actions
   are under **More Actions**.
7. Press **More Actions**, then **See Candidates**, to open its candidates on the board.
8. Press **Careers Page** at the top to open the public website as a candidate sees it.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, not on the careers page yet |
| **Open** | On the careers page and taking applications |
| **Paused** | Takes no applications. Its link keeps working |
| **Closed** | Finished: filled, cancelled or put on hold |
| **Closing Date Passed** | Still open here, but the careers page takes no more applications |

### Write a new vacancy {#recruitment-vacancies-new needs="recruitment create"}

**New Vacancy** asks you about the role one question at a time, then drafts the vacancy from your
answers. The bar at the top counts the questions, such as `Question 3 of 10`. Only the job title is
needed: press **Skip** on any other question you cannot answer yet. Each question's help term says
what a useful answer gives.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **New Vacancy** at the top right. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![New Vacancy](shot:recruitment-vacancy-new)
3. Type the **Job Title** and press **Next**.
4. Pick the **Department** and the **Hiring Manager**. Only people with access to Recruitment, in
   your reach, are offered as **Hiring Manager**. Press **Next**.
5. Type how many **Positions** you are hiring and pick the **Employment** type. Press **Next**.
6. Pick the **Arrangement**, such as hybrid. You may add an **Arrangement Note**, such as
   `2 days in office, 3 from home`, and the **Working Days**. Press **Next**.
7. Start typing the area under **Location** and pick it from the list. A remote role skips this
   question.
8. Type the salary band, **Salary From** and **Salary To**, as you would write it, such as
   `350,000`. Tick **Show Salary on Careers** to show it there.
9. Under **Your Answer**, say what the role must have and what it will do, in your own words. Add the
   **Years of Experience** needed.
10. Add who the role **Reports To**, and anything else candidates should know: what would set
    someone apart, the perks, the team.
11. The hub then asks two questions of its own about this role. Answer them, or press **Skip**.
12. Press **Draft Vacancy**. The vacancy opens in three steps, **Role**, **Details** and **Pay and
    Publish**, with your answers and a drafted **Description**, **Required Experience and
    Qualifications** and **What We Look For**.
13. Check each step and press **Next**. On **Details**, pick the day it **Closes On**. You may add the
    **Probation Months** and the **Target Start**.
14. **Publish Now** is ticked. Leave it to put the vacancy on the careers page now, and press
    **Publish Vacancy**.
15. Or untick it and press **Save Draft** to finish later.

To skip the questions, press **Use Blank Form** on any question. The three steps open with what you
have answered so far, and you fill in the rest yourself.

| Field | What to enter | Needed to publish |
|---|---|---|
| Title | The job title, such as `Content Producer` | Yes |
| Department | The department the role sits in | Yes |
| Hiring manager | An active employee who decides the hire | Yes |
| Location | An area picked from the list | Yes, unless remote |
| Description | What the role is, its duties and how it is measured; 80 characters or more | Yes |
| **Required Experience and Qualifications** | What a candidate must have; 20 characters or more | Yes |
| **What We Look For** | What would help but is not needed. The careers page heads it What We Are Looking For | No |
| Salary band | The lowest and highest pay | No |
| **Closes On** | A day after today; it closes at 23:59 Lagos time | Yes |

> **Tip:** When AI is off or has reached its limit, there are eight questions, not ten, and
> **Draft Vacancy** writes a plain draft from your answers. Read it and add to it before you publish.

A new vacancy takes its interview traits from [Careers Settings](#recruitment-vacancies-settings),
and its scorecard at the usual weights. Change either before you publish.

> **Tip:** A draft can stay unfinished. Only publishing needs every field marked Yes.

### Draft the description with AI {#recruitment-vacancies-write-ai needs="recruitment edit"}

**Draft Vacancy**, at the end of a new vacancy's questions, drafts it for you. To draft the
description again, or for a vacancy you opened with **Edit**:

1. Fill in the title, the department and anything you know first.
2. On **Details**, type a line in **About the Role** and press **Write With AI**.
3. When you have already typed a description, the hub asks first, under **Replace Your Text**.
   Press **Replace Text** to go on.
4. The draft fills the description, **Required Experience and Qualifications** and **What We Look
   For**, in MyCity's usual shape.
5. Read it and change what you need. Nothing is saved until you save the vacancy.

> **Tip:** When **Write With AI** is not shown, AI is off or has reached its limit. Write the
> description yourself; the form works as before.

### Start from an empty seat {#recruitment-vacancies-suggested needs="recruitment create"}

**View Empty Seats** lists the vacant seats on the organogram that have no vacancy yet.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **View Empty Seats** beside the status filter, and find the seat. It shows 25 seats a page;
   page on below it.
3. Press **Create Vacancy** beside it.
4. **New Vacancy** opens with the seat's title, department and reporting line already answered, and
   the seat's manager as the **Hiring Manager** when they have access to Recruitment (otherwise pick
   one). Change any of them if they do not fit.
5. Follow [Write a new vacancy](#recruitment-vacancies-new) from step 3. Nothing is made until you
   save.
6. {needs="people edit"} You can also press **Create Vacancy** on a vacant seat on
   [People · Organogram](/people?tab=organogram). **New Vacancy** opens here filled in the same way.

A seat holds one draft, open or paused vacancy at a time. Once it has one, it leaves the list.

### Publish, pause, resume or close {#recruitment-vacancies-lifecycle needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. For a draft, press **Publish**. It goes on the careers page at its own link.
3. To stop applications for a while, press **Pause**. The link keeps working and says so.
4. Press **Resume** to take applications again.
5. To end it, press **More Actions**, then **Close Vacancy**, and pick why: filled, cancelled or on hold.
6. When candidates are still in progress, tick **Not Progress All** to tell them all now. Each gets
   the not progressing email. A candidate who has been sent an offer is left as they are.
7. Press **Close Vacancy**.

The link is made when you first publish and never changes, even after a pause. When every opening of
a role is hired, the role closes as filled by itself, and its Organogram role is filled by the new
person.

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Its closing day has passed* | **Resume** needs a closing day still to come | Press **More Actions**, then **Edit Vacancy**; pick a new **Closes On**, save, then press **Resume** |

A closed vacancy cannot be changed or opened again. Write a new one instead. Candidates you did not
stop stay where they are, and you can still [stop them](#recruitment-candidates-not-progressing) one
by one.

### Change a vacancy {#recruitment-vacancies-edit needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Edit Vacancy**. The same three steps open with what is saved.
3. Change what you need. Emptying a field that is not needed clears it.
4. Press **Save Vacancy**. The careers page shows the change at once.

Making a role remote removes its location. A closed vacancy has no **Edit Vacancy**.

### Set the questions {#recruitment-vacancies-questions needs="recruitment edit"}

A vacancy asks two kinds of question. Its Apply questions are on the form a candidate fills in. Its
interview traits are what each panelist rates, from 1 to 10.

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Questions**.
3. Under the Apply questions, add a question. Pick written, yes or no, or a choice, and whether it
   must be answered.
4. Under the interview traits, take out a trait that does not fit the role, or add one of its own.
   A role has at most 20.
5. To get ideas, press **Suggest With AI**. Keep the suggestions you want and change their words.
6. Save the questions.

A yes or no question that must be answered counts in the candidate's estimate. A question someone
has already answered keeps its words: changing them makes a new question in its place.

### Set the scorecard {#recruitment-vacancies-scorecard needs="recruitment edit"}

The scorecard ranks the role's candidates. It has five parts: screening, psychometric, technical,
case study and interview.

1. Open the vacancy on [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Scorecard**.
3. Switch off a part this role skips. Screening or interview must stay on.
4. Type each part's weight as a whole number. The parts left on add up to 100.
5. Save the scorecard. Every candidate's total changes at once.

A new role starts at 15, 15, 25, 20 and 25, all on. The board leaves out **Assessment** when all
three test parts are off, and **Interview** when the interview part is off.

### Change the candidate emails {#recruitment-vacancies-emails needs="recruitment edit"}

Candidates get nine emails: application received, invitation to apply, assessment, interview, not
progressing, offer sent, offer signed, contract sent and onboarding documents. The last four go out
from Offers and Onboarding.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Candidate Emails** at the top.
   ![Candidate Emails](shot:recruitment-candidate-emails)
3. Press the email you want to change.
4. Change its subject and its words. Keep the words in double braces, such as
   `{{ vacancy_title }}`: the hub fills them in.
5. Press **Save Email**. The next email sent uses the new words, and you are back at the list.
6. To go back to the wording MyCity started with, press **Restore Default**. **Cancel** takes you back
   to the list without saving.

> **Important:** Only someone who may change every vacancy can save an email, as each one goes to
> every candidate.

### Careers Settings {#recruitment-vacancies-settings}

Careers Settings holds two things every vacancy uses. **About MyCityApp** is the paragraph every
vacancy opens with. The default traits are the interview traits a new vacancy starts with.

1. Open [Recruitment · Vacancies](/recruitment?tab=vacancies).
2. Press **Careers Settings** at the top.
   ![Careers Settings](shot:recruitment-careers-settings)
3. {needs="recruitment edit"} Change the **About MyCityApp** paragraph.
4. {needs="recruitment edit"} Reword any **Default Trait**; they are numbered in order. Press **Add Trait**
   for another, or **Remove** beside one to take it out.
5. {needs="recruitment edit"} Press **Save Settings**. Vacancies already written keep their own traits.

> **Tip:** Only someone who may change every vacancy can save Careers Settings. Others can read
> them.

## Candidates {#recruitment-candidates screen=recruitment/candidates icon=users}

Candidates is the board: each application as a card, in a column for its stage.

![The Candidates board](shot:recruitment-candidates)

### Read the board {#recruitment-candidates-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. The tiles count the **Candidates**, those **To Screen**, those **In Interview** and those
   **Shortlisted**.
3. Pick a role in the vacancy filter to see only its candidates.
4. The columns are **Applied**, **Screening**, **Assessment**, **Interview** and **Shortlist**,
   side by side in one row, then **Offer** once offers are in use: the candidates sent an offer. A role
   that skips a stage has no column for it. On a narrow screen, scroll the board sideways to reach the
   last columns.
5. Each card shows the name, the role, the screening score and how many days it has been at its
   stage.
6. On a phone, press a stage at the top to see its cards, one stage at a time.

The best weighted total comes first. A score never moves, rejects or hides anyone; a person does.

| On a card | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Provisional** | The score is an estimate worked out from the form only. The CV has not been read yet |
| A score with no tag | AI has read the CV against the role |

### Open a candidate {#recruitment-candidates-sheet}

1. Press a card on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates). On a computer it opens
   in a window over the board; on a phone it opens in a sheet.
   ![A candidate's sheet](shot:recruitment-candidate-sheet)
2. The sheet shows their details, their CV, their answers and their assessments and interviews.
3. **Screening** shows the score, **Provisional** while it is the estimate, and the AI's summary.
4. **Weighted Total** shows each part's score and the total. Beside it, *3 of 5 parts* says how
   many parts have a score yet.
5. **Stage History** lists each move, who made it and when.
6. Press **View CV** to open the CV.
7. At its foot is the candidate's next step, such as *Move to Interview*. Everything else you can do
   for them is under **More Actions**, with **Not Progressing** last.
8. {needs="recruitment create"} At **Shortlist**, press **Make Offer** to start an offer for them on
   [Offers](#recruitment-offers-new).
9. At **Offer** or after, press **Open Offer** to see their offer on
   [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).

A CV that is *Being checked* is still being scanned for viruses. It opens once it is clean. A CV
with a virus never opens, and the sheet says so.

### Score one candidate with AI {#recruitment-candidates-score needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Score With AI**.
3. AI reads the CV, the cover letter and the answers against the role and its must-meet conditions.
4. The score, from 0 to 100, and a short summary replace the estimate. **Provisional** goes.

**Score With AI** shows only while the CV is ready and the candidate has no AI score. A candidate is
read once.

The summary says which conditions are met and what AI could not check. A Word file, or a scan with
no text, is scored without the CV, and the summary says so.

### Screen a role with AI {#recruitment-candidates-screen needs="recruitment edit"}

Screen With AI reads every candidate of one role who has no AI score yet.

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Screen With AI**. You can also start it from ⌘K.
   ![Screen With AI](shot:recruitment-screen-ai)
3. Pick the **Vacancy**. The role in the vacancy filter is picked already.
4. Type a condition under the first **Must Meet**, such as `A degree in accounting` or
   `Lives in Lagos`. Press **Add Condition** for another, up to three.
5. Press **Screen Applications**. The candidates are read one by one, and each card shows its score
   when it is ready.

The role keeps its conditions. Next time, leave them as they are and they are used again.

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A condition is refused | It names an age, a sex, a marital status, a religion, a tribe or a state of origin | Write a condition about skills, experience or where they live |
| *None waiting to be read* | Every candidate of the role already has an AI score | Nothing: each is read once, and the board ranks them |

> **Tip:** When **Screen With AI** and **Score With AI** are not shown, AI is off or has reached
> its limit. The estimates still rank the board.

### Move a candidate {#recruitment-candidates-move needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. On a computer, drag the card to its new column.
3. Or open the candidate and press the button for the stage, such as *Move to Interview*. The next
   stage ahead is the button at its foot; any other stage is under **More Actions**.

A card can move to any stage ahead of it, or one stage back, up to **Shortlist**. Nobody drags a card
into **Offer**: sending an offer moves it there, and an offer declined or lapsed brings it back to
**Shortlist**. Once the offer is signed, the candidate continues on Onboarding, and a candidate hired
leaves the board. Each move goes in **Stage History**, and the days at stage start again.

### Stop a candidate {#recruitment-candidates-not-progressing needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Not Progressing**.
3. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters. Only staff read it.
4. Press **Not Progressing**.

The candidate is emailed that they are not going further. Their links stop working, and their
unfinished assessments are cancelled. Their interviews are called off, and the panel and the
candidate get the calendar cancel. The card stays at its stage, marked as not progressing.

You can stop a candidate of a closed vacancy too. They cannot be moved, scored or brought back.

### Bring a candidate back {#recruitment-candidates-reopen needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Reopen**. They go back to the stage they left.
3. Their old links stay stopped. Send a new assessment or interview if they need one.

A hire who was not progressed after signing their offer (at **Documentation** or later) cannot be
reopened: their onboarding ended with it. They apply again instead.

### Keep a candidate for later {#recruitment-candidates-pool needs="recruitment create"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Add to Pool**.
3. Under **Why Keep Them**, type why they are worth keeping, at least 5 characters.
4. Press **Add to Pool**. They are kept in the [Talent Pool](#recruitment-pool) for 24 months.

Adding someone already in the pool renews the reason and the 24 months. When they are in the pool
from a role you cannot see, the hub says *Already in the pool*.

### Send an assessment {#recruitment-candidates-assess needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Send Assessment**.
3. Pick the **Assessment**. Only active ones are offered.
4. Under **Start Within Days**, type how many days they have to start it, from 1 to 14. It starts
   at 3.
5. For a take-home brief with no time limit, also type the **Days to Finish** after starting, from
   1 to 14. Left empty, it is 5.
6. Press **Send Assessment**. The candidate gets an email with their own link, and moves to
   **Assessment** when they were at an earlier stage.

A candidate takes each assessment once. To send one to everyone at a stage, use
[Send to Candidates](#recruitment-assessments-send).

### Schedule an interview from the board {#recruitment-candidates-interview needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the candidate on [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **More Actions**, then **Schedule Interview**.
3. Follow [Schedule an interview](#recruitment-interviews-schedule) from step 4. The candidate is
   picked already.

### Invite someone to apply {#recruitment-candidates-invite needs="recruitment create"}

Invite a person you have found, so they apply to one open role.

1. Open [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates).
2. Press **Invite to Apply** at the top. You can also start it from ⌘K.
   ![Invite to Apply](shot:recruitment-invite)
3. Type the person's **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email**.
4. Pick the open **Vacancy**.
5. Press **Send Invitation**. They get an email with an Apply link that works for 7 days.

They apply exactly as anyone does from the careers page, and arrive on the board.

## Assessments {#recruitment-assessments screen=recruitment/assessments icon=check}

Assessments holds MyCity's tests and take-home briefs, and every sitting a candidate has taken.

![The Assessments screen](shot:recruitment-assessments)

### Read the assessments {#recruitment-assessments-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. The tiles count the **Active** assessments, how many were **Sent**, those **Awaiting Marking**
   and the **Average Score**.
3. Press **Assessments** at the top for the library, or **Sittings** for who took what.
4. Each assessment shows its name, type, minutes, questions, how many were sent, the average and its
   status.
5. Press an assessment to see its questions, and the **Points** each option scores. On a computer it
   opens in a window over the list; on a phone it opens in a sheet.

An assessment is timed, with minutes and a clock, or untimed: a take-home brief due by a day.

### Make a new assessment {#recruitment-assessments-new needs="recruitment create"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **New Assessment** at the top right. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![New Assessment](shot:recruitment-assessment-new)
3. Type the **Name**, such as `Excel Skills Test`, and pick the **Type**: psychometric, technical,
   case study or practical.
4. For a timed test, type the **Minutes**, from 5 to 240. Leave it empty for a take-home brief.
5. Type the **Pass Mark** if it has one.
6. Write the **Instructions** the candidate reads before they start.
7. Press **Make Assessment**.

It gets a code such as *ASM-0001*. It stays off until it has questions and you
[switch it on](#recruitment-assessments-activate).

### Change an assessment {#recruitment-assessments-edit needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Edit**.
3. Change the name, the type, the instructions or the **Pass Mark**.
4. To make a test untimed, empty the **Minutes**. Press **Save Assessment**.

The minutes cannot change once the assessment has been sent to anyone.

### Write the questions {#recruitment-assessments-questions needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Questions**.
3. Press **Add Question**, type the **Question** and pick its **Answer**: choice, written, or file
   or link.
4. For a choice, type each option and the **Points** it scores. Press **Add Option** for another.
   The question's most is its best option.
5. For a written or a file answer, type its **Most Points**. You mark these yourself.
6. Press **Remove** on a question to take it out. Press **Save Questions**.

| Kind | How it is marked |
|---|---|
| Choice | By itself, from the points of the option picked |
| Written | By hand, on [Mark Answers](#recruitment-assessments-mark) |
| File or link | By hand. The candidate uploads a file up to 25 MB, or gives a link to a larger one |

> **Important:** Once an assessment has been sent to anyone, its questions cannot change and
> **Questions** is gone. Switch it off and make a new one.

### Suggest questions with AI {#recruitment-assessments-suggest needs="recruitment edit"}

1. In **Questions**, press **Suggest With AI**.
2. Five questions are added below yours, written from the assessment's type and instructions.
3. Read them. Change their words, check each option's **Points**, and **Remove** those you do not
   want.
4. Press **Save Questions**. Nothing is kept until you do.

### Switch an assessment on or off {#recruitment-assessments-activate needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Activate**. It needs at least one question. Only active assessments can be sent.
3. To stop sending it, press **Deactivate**. Sittings already sent go on.

### Move an assessment to another hub {#recruitment-assessments-export}

Assessments written on staging reach the live hub this way, so nothing is typed twice.

1. {needs="recruitment export"} Open the assessment on
   [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. {needs="recruitment export"} Press **Export**. A file of the assessment and its questions
   downloads. See [Save a file you download](#start-downloads).
3. {needs="recruitment create"} On the other hub, open
   [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments) and press **Import**.
4. {needs="recruitment create"} Pick the file, and once it shows **Done**, press **Import Assessment**.
   A new assessment is made, switched off, for you to check and activate.

An assessment with no questions cannot be exported.

### Send to every candidate at a stage {#recruitment-assessments-send needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the assessment on [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments).
2. Press **Send to Candidates**. It shows on an active assessment.
3. Pick the open **Vacancy** and the **Stage**. The stage starts at **Assessment**.
4. Type the **Start Within Days** and, for a take-home brief, the **Days to Finish**.
5. Press **Send Assessment**. The hub says how many were sent, and how many already had it.

Only active candidates are sent it, each once. Each gets an email with their own link, and those at
an earlier stage move to **Assessment**.

### Read the sittings {#recruitment-assessments-sittings}

1. Open [Recruitment · Assessments](/recruitment?tab=assessments) and press **Sittings**.
   ![Sittings awaiting marking](shot:recruitment-sittings)
2. Each sitting shows the candidate, the assessment, where it stands and the score.
3. Press **To Mark** to see only those waiting for marks. **Awaiting Marking**, at the top, counts them.
4. Press a sitting to read each answer. Open a file, or the link they gave. On a computer it opens
   in a window over the list; on a phone it opens in a sheet.

| Where it stands | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | The link is out; not started yet |
| **In Progress** | Started and still running |
| **To Mark** | Submitted, with answers waiting for marks |
| **Time Up** | The clock ran out. What was answered counts, marked as for To Mark |
| **Scored** | Every answer has a mark; the score is on the candidate |
| **Expired** | Never started by its start day |
| **Cancelled** | Stopped by staff with **Cancel Sitting**, or by Not Progressing |

### Mark the answers {#recruitment-assessments-mark needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Under **Sittings**, press **To Mark**, then press a sitting.
2. For each written or file answer, type its **Mark**, up to the question's most. A **Comment** is
   optional.
   ![Mark Answers](shot:recruitment-mark-answers)
3. Press **Mark Answers**. Marks given so far are kept; a chosen answer is marked by itself.

Once every answer has a mark, the sitting is scored and its percentage goes on the candidate. A
question left unanswered scores 0. A mark left empty is refused, never read as 0.

## Interviews {#recruitment-interviews screen=recruitment/interviews icon=calendar}

Interviews holds each interview, its panel and their cards. Each panelist scores alone and cannot see
the others' cards until the round closes.

![The Interviews screen](shot:recruitment-interviews)

### Read the interviews {#recruitment-interviews-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. **Upcoming and Recent** lists what is coming first, soonest first, then those held. Press
   **My Panels** for the ones you sit on, or **All Interviews** for every one you may see.
3. Each shows the candidate, the role, the date, when it ends, how it is held, the panel, and the
   round: **Scheduled**, how many cards are in, **Closed** or **Cancelled**. On a phone the list
   keeps the candidate, the date, the panel and the round.
4. Press an interview. On a computer it opens in a wide window: the interview, **Blind Panel**,
   **Weighted Scorecard** and **Candidate Details** sit two by two, with **Round Activity** across
   the window under them. Press **Close** at its foot to go back to the list. On a phone it opens
   in a sheet, one part under another.
   ![An interview's sheet](shot:recruitment-interview-sheet)
5. It shows when and where, or the video link, and the **Candidate Details**.
6. **Blind Panel** shows each person as **Submitted** or **Waiting**. A submitted score reads
   **Hidden** until the round closes.
7. **Weighted Scorecard** shows the candidate's score at each part and its weight, with the total.
   It shows only if the role is yours to see; a panelist alone does not see it.
8. **Round Activity** shows who scheduled or changed it, each card submitted, each reminder, the
   close and the report, with when.

While the round is open, nobody reads another person's card. That includes the hiring manager and
the CEO.

A candidate at the **Interview** stage shows here only once an interview is scheduled for them.

### Schedule an interview {#recruitment-interviews-schedule needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Schedule Interview**. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![Schedule Interview](shot:recruitment-interview-schedule)
3. Search for the **Candidate** by name and pick them. Their role shows under their name.
4. Pick **How** it is held: in person at a **Worksite**, by video with its **Meeting Link**, or by
   phone. You may give it a **Round Name**, such as `Interview 1`.
5. Pick the **Day**, when it **Starts** and when it **Ends**.
6. Pick the **Panel**. Only people who hold Recruitment are offered; if someone cannot sit, the form
   names them.
7. Press **Schedule Interview**.

The candidate moves to **Interview** and gets an email with a calendar file. Each panelist gets a
calendar invite, also on [My Workspace · Calendar](/workspace?tab=calendar).

Nobody sits on a panel about themselves. A candidate who works at MyCity cannot be on the panel.

### Change or call off an interview {#recruitment-interviews-reschedule needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Reschedule** to change the time, the place or the panel, then press **Reschedule** at
   the foot.
3. Everyone gets the new time. A change to the panel alone sends the candidate nothing.
4. To call it off, press **Cancel Interview**, type the **Reason** and press **Cancel Interview**.
   Everyone gets the calendar cancel.

A panel needs at least one person. Taking someone off removes their unsent card; a card already
submitted stays. If everyone left on the panel has submitted and the interview has started, the round
closes.

### Fill in the candidate's details {#recruitment-interviews-details needs="recruitment edit"}

The details are filled once for the whole panel, before or during the interview.

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Under **Candidate Details**, press **Edit**.
3. Type their **Current Employer**.
4. Pick their **Academics**: **Outstanding**, **Very Good**, **Good**, **Average** or **Poor**.
5. Type their experience in months: **Total Months**, and **Relevant Months** for what bears on
   MyCity.
6. Press **Save Details**. Every panelist sees them on their card. Each field is optional.

### Fill in your card {#recruitment-interviews-card}

You have a card only for an interview whose panel you sit on.

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **My Card**. On a phone it opens as a full sheet.
   ![My Card](shot:recruitment-my-card)
3. Read the candidate's details at the top. Press **View CV** to open their CV.
4. For each trait, press a star from 1 to 10. The tenth star is 10. On a phone the stars sit in two
   rows of five.
5. Each star saves as you press it. To take a rating back, press the same star again: the trait
   is not rated.
6. **Your score** shows as you go: the average of the traits you have rated so far, and how many
   are rated.
7. Pick your **Decision**: **Select**, **On Hold** or **Reject**. It saves as you pick it.
8. Write any **Comments**. They are the form's Others, and optional.
9. To tidy your comments, press **Tidy With AI**, read the result and keep it or not.
10. Write anything for yourself under **Private Notes**. Only you ever see these.
11. Press **Save Draft** to keep your comments and notes. Only the stars and the decision save by
    themselves.
12. When it is done, press **Submit Card**, then **Submit Card** again to confirm.

**Submit Card** needs every trait rated and a decision. Once submitted, the card cannot change.

A role with no traits asks for one overall rating from 1 to 10 instead. Until you submit, the hub
reminds you once a day after the interview ends.

### Close a round {#recruitment-interviews-close needs="recruitment edit"}

The round closes by itself when the last panelist submits, once the interview has started. If every
card is in before the start, it closes at the first daily check after the start. To close it before
then:

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Close Round**. It works once the interview has started.
3. Confirm it. Only submitted cards count; an unsent card stays its author's.

A closed round never opens again. The hiring manager is told it has closed.

### Read the results {#recruitment-interviews-results}

Once the round closes, everyone who may see the interview reads the results.

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. **Blind Panel** shows each panelist's score and decision.
3. Each trait shows every panelist's rating and the average across the panel.
4. The total is the panel's average rating times ten, out of 100. It is the interview's part of the
   scorecard.

Private notes never show to anyone but the person who wrote them.

### Write the report {#recruitment-interviews-report needs="recruitment edit or manager"}

The hiring manager writes one report for the panel once the round is closed. Someone who may change
the role's interviews can write it too.

1. Open the closed interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews).
2. Press **Write Report**.
3. Under **Report**, write what the panel found, at least 40 characters.
4. Pick the **Decision**: **Select**, **On Hold** or **Reject**.
5. Press **Save Report**. The Interview Assessment Form is made from the whole card.

To change it later, press **Edit Report**. Each save makes the form again.

### Download the form {#recruitment-interviews-form}

1. Open the interview on [Recruitment · Interviews](/recruitment?tab=interviews), once its report is
   written.
2. Press **Download Form**. The Interview Assessment Form downloads as a PDF.
3. It holds the candidate's details, each trait with every rating and the average, each decision,
   the total and the report. See [Save a file you download](#start-downloads).

A candidate the panel selects moves to **Shortlist** on the
[board](#recruitment-candidates-move).

## Offers {#recruitment-offers screen=recruitment/offers icon=doc}

Offers is where a shortlisted candidate's offer of employment is made, approved, signed for MyCity and
sent. The candidate reads and signs it on their phone, and signing it is accepting it.

![The Offers screen](shot:recruitment-offers)

An offer goes through these, in order:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being written. Only a draft can change |
| **With Approval** | Waiting for the Head of Department, then the CEO |
| **Approved** | Approved. The Offer Signatory signs it for MyCity, then it can be sent |
| **With Candidate** | Sent. The candidate has until it expires to sign |
| **Accepted** | Signed by the candidate. It is locked |
| **Declined**, **Expired**, **Withdrawn** | Ended. Another offer can be made for the same candidate |

### Read the offers {#recruitment-offers-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. The tiles count the offers **With Approval**, with the drafts under it, those **To Send**, those
   **With Candidates** and those **Accepted This Month**.
3. Pick a **Status** to see only those offers, or search by number, name or role.
4. Each row shows the offer's number, the candidate, the role, the start date, the year's pay
   (with **Outside Range** when it is), the status, when it was sent and when it expires. On a phone
   the list keeps the candidate and the status.
5. Press an offer. On a computer it opens in a wide window: the offer, with **Signers** under its
   details, beside **Monthly Pay**, with **Approval** under the pay; **History** runs across the
   window under them. Press **Close** at its foot to go back to the list. On a phone it opens in a sheet,
   one part under another.
   ![An offer](shot:recruitment-offer-record)
6. The offer shows the role, the department, the start date, when it expires, and the **Score**
   the candidate had when the offer was made.
7. **Monthly Pay** lists each pay line, the **Monthly Total** and the year's pay. **Vacancy Range**
   is the role's monthly range. A total outside it shows **Outside Range**, with the reason.
8. **Approval**, under the pay on a computer, shows each stage and who approved or rejected it, with any reason.
9. **Signers**, under the offer's details on a computer, shows who signs for MyCity and the candidate, and whether each has signed.
10. **History** lists when it was made, submitted, approved, signed, sent and how it ended.
11. Press **View Letter**, beside **Withdraw Offer**, to read the letter as the candidate will. It opens
    in a window of its own. Once the PDF is made, **Open in Tab** opens it in the browser and
    **Download PDF** saves it.

### Make an offer {#recruitment-offers-new needs="recruitment create"}

Only a candidate at **Shortlist** can get an offer, and only one open offer at a time.

1. Open [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Press **New Offer**. On a phone it is in **Quick Actions**.
   ![New Offer](shot:recruitment-offer-new)
3. Pick the **Candidate**. The form fills in from their vacancy: the **Job Title**, the
   **Department**, who they report to, the work pattern and the job description.
4. Change anything that differs for this offer, then press **Next**.
5. Under **Pay Lines**, type each line's name and its **Monthly Amount**. Press **Add Pay Line** for
   another, such as an allowance. The **Monthly Total** adds them up.
6. If the total is outside the vacancy's range, type **Why Outside the Range**. The approvers read
   it. Press **Next**.
7. Pick the **Start Date**. **Days to Sign** is how long the candidate has once it is sent, 15 unless
   you change it. Fill in the rest that applies, then press **Next**.
8. Type any **Additional Terms** for this offer alone. **Schedule A** is the job description printed
   at the end; change it if this role differs.
9. Press **Draft Offer**. It shows as **Draft**.

To change a draft, press **Edit Offer**, change it and press **Save Offer**.

> **Tip:** From [Recruitment · Candidates](/recruitment?tab=candidates), **Make Offer** on a
> shortlisted candidate's sheet opens New Offer with them picked.

### Send it for approval {#recruitment-offers-submit needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the draft on [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Press **Submit for Approval**. It shows as **With Approval**.

It needs a **Department** and a **Start Date**, and an Offer Signatory named in
[Offer settings](#recruitment-offers-settings). The Head of Department approves first, then the CEO.
If one rejects it, it comes back as **Draft** with their reason under **Approval**.

### Approve or reject an offer {#recruitment-offers-approve}

You approve an offer only when it waits for you. It is also on My Approvals.

1. Open the offer on [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Read the pay and the reason if it is outside the range. Press **View Letter** to read the letter.
3. Press **Approve Offer**. Or press **Reject Offer**, type the **Reason** and press **Reject Offer**.

When the CEO approves, the letter is made with the standard wording then in force, and the Offer
Signatory is told.

### Sign it for MyCity {#recruitment-offers-sign}

Only the Offer Signatory signs, once the offer is **Approved**. You are already signed in, so no code
is asked for.

1. Open the offer on [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Press **Sign for Company**.
   ![Sign for the company](shot:recruitment-offer-sign)
3. Check your **Full Name** and **Title**.
4. Under **Signature**, pick **Draw** and sign in the box, or **Type** and type it under
   **Typed Signature**.
5. Tick **Agree to Sign Electronically**.
6. Press **Sign Offer**. **Signers** shows you as signed.

### Send it to the candidate {#recruitment-offers-send needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the signed offer on [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Press **Send Offer**. The candidate gets an email with their own signing link.
3. It shows as **With Candidate**, with the day it expires. The candidate's card moves to **Offer** on
   the [board](#recruitment-candidates-read).

If they cannot find the email, press **Resend Offer**. A new link goes out and the old one stops
working. What the candidate sees is in [Sign an offer](#public-sign).

When they sign, it shows as **Accepted** and both of you get the signed copy by email. If they
decline, or it expires, their card goes back to **Shortlist** and you can make another offer.

An offer cannot be sent to someone already on MyCity's staff: a move inside MyCity goes through People.

### When the email does not arrive {#recruitment-offers-email needs="recruitment edit"}

If the mail server refuses the candidate's address, the offer says so in red at the top, with the
address and when.

1. Open the offer on [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Press **Change Email**, at the top or beside **Resend Offer**.
   ![Change Email](shot:recruitment-offer-email)
3. Type the right **Email** and press **Send to Email**.
4. A new link goes to the new address and the old one stops working. The candidate's address
   changes on every application of theirs.

An address another candidate already uses is refused with *Another candidate has it*. A refusal that
arrives later as a mail of its own does not show here: if the candidate says nothing arrived, check
the address with them and use **Change Email** or **Resend Offer**.

### Withdraw an offer {#recruitment-offers-withdraw needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open the offer on [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers).
2. Press **Withdraw Offer**, type the **Reason** and press **Withdraw Offer**.

Its link stops working. An offer still **With Approval** can be withdrawn only by who submitted it,
or the CEO. An accepted offer cannot be withdrawn.

### Offer settings {#recruitment-offers-settings needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Offers](/recruitment?tab=offers) and press **Offer Settings**. Only
   Operations & People see it.
   ![Offer Settings](shot:recruitment-offer-settings)
2. Press **Offer Signatory**, pick who **Signs for the Company** and press **Save Signatory**. They
   need a login to sign.
3. Press **Standard Offer Letter** to change the letter's wording. It is Markdown; the names in
   braces under it, such as `{{ job_title }}`, are filled from each offer.
4. Press **Save Letter**. Offers approved from now on use it; those already approved keep theirs.
5. **Restore Default** puts MyCity's own wording back. Yours stays in the history.

## Onboarding {#recruitment-onboarding screen=recruitment/onboarding icon=userplus}

Onboarding takes a hire from a signed offer to their first day and their login. When a candidate
signs their offer, their case opens here: they send their documents and guarantors, MyCity checks
them, both sides sign the contract, and the department's plan is worked through.

![The Onboarding screen](shot:recruitment-onboarding)

A case goes through these, in order:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Documentation** | The hire sends their documents, guarantors, home address and BVN, and the contract is signed |
| **Pre-Onboarding** | Everything is checked and signed. The case waits for the start date |
| **Onboarding** | From the start date: the plan's steps, each with an owner and a due day |
| **Completed** | Every step done and the hire's login sent. They are hired |
| **Cancelled** | Withdrawn, or the candidate was not progressed |

### Read the cases {#recruitment-onboarding-read}

1. Open [Recruitment · Onboarding](/recruitment?tab=onboarding).
2. The tiles count the cases at **Documentation**, with how many documents wait to be verified, at
   **Pre-Onboarding**, at **Onboarding**, with the steps overdue, and those **Hired This Month**.
3. Pick a **Status** to see only those cases, or search by name or role.
4. Each row shows the hire, the role, the department, the start date, how many required documents
   are **Outstanding** and the status. On a phone the list keeps the hire, the start date and the status.
5. Press a case. On a computer it opens in a wide window; on a phone, in a sheet.
   ![A case](shot:recruitment-onboarding-case)
6. The case's own card shows the role, the start date, **Pre-Onboarding** (what it still waits on,
   or the day it got there), whether the **BVN** and **Home Address** are entered and when the
   **Privacy Notice** was accepted. The BVN itself is never shown.
7. **Documents** lists the checklist, each with its status: **Not Sent**, **To Verify**, **Verified**
   or **Rejected**. The bar counts the required ones verified.
8. **Guarantors** shows the two the hire entered, each with their check: **To Check**, **Passed** or
   **Failed**, and what their Guarantor Form says: date of birth, both addresses, **Convicted** and
   **Recommends**.
9. **Contract** shows its number, the signers and where each stands.
10. From the start date, **Onboarding Plan** lists the steps, with their owner, due day and SOP.

### Check what the hire sent {#recruitment-onboarding-verify needs="recruitment edit"}

Operations & People check a hire's documents and guarantors; a Head of Department sees their hires'
cases but does not check them.

1. Open the case on [Recruitment · Onboarding](/recruitment?tab=onboarding).
2. Under **Documents**, press **View File** to open what they sent.
3. Press **Verify** if it is right. A file still being scanned for viruses cannot be verified yet.
4. Or press **Reject**, type the **Reason** and press **Reject Document**. The hire reads it beside
   the document on their page and sends another.

To check a guarantor, press **View Form** and **View ID**, call them, then press **Record Check**
under their name. Pick **Passed** or **Failed** under **Outcome**, add a **Note** if you like, and
press **Record Check**. A guarantor cannot pass until the hire has sent their signed form and ID copy.
A failed guarantor is changed by the hire, and checked again.

When every required document is verified, both guarantors have passed, the BVN and home address are
entered and the contract is signed, the case moves to **Pre-Onboarding** by itself.

### Remind the hire {#recruitment-onboarding-remind needs="recruitment edit"}

If the hire lost their email, or their link expired, press **Send Reminder** on the case. A fresh
link goes to them and the old one stops working. It works only while the case is at **Documentation**.

### Sign the contract for MyCity {#recruitment-onboarding-contract}

The contract is made when the offer is signed. Only the Offer Signatory signs it for MyCity, and the
hire's link goes out after that.

1. Open the case on [Recruitment · Onboarding](/recruitment?tab=onboarding).
2. Under **Contract**, press **Sign for Company**.
3. Check your **Full Name** and **Title**, draw or type your signature, and tick
   **Agree to Sign Electronically**.
4. Press **Sign Contract**. The hire gets an email with their own signing link.

If they cannot find it, press **Resend Contract**: a new link goes out and the old one stops working.
The hire has 15 days from each link to sign. **Download PDF** saves the contract. Once the hire signs,
both of you get the signed copy by email.

### When the hire declines the contract {#recruitment-onboarding-reissue needs="recruitment edit"}

If the hire declines the contract, or does not sign it in time, it shows as **Cancelled** and the case
waits at **Documentation**.

1. Open the case on [Recruitment · Onboarding](/recruitment?tab=onboarding).
2. Under **Contract**, press **Reissue Contract**. A new contract is made from the signed offer with
   the wording in use today.
3. Sign it for MyCity as above. The hire then gets a new link.

### Work through the plan {#recruitment-onboarding-steps needs="recruitment edit"}

On the start date the case moves to **Onboarding** and the department's plan is copied onto it. Each
step's owner is told, and reminded once if it goes past its day (shown as **Overdue**).

1. Open the case on [Recruitment · Onboarding](/recruitment?tab=onboarding).
2. Under **Onboarding Plan**, press **Mark Step Done** on each step as it is done. The hire's Head of
   Department may mark the steps of their own hires too.

### Complete onboarding {#recruitment-onboarding-complete needs="recruitment edit"}

Once every step is done:

1. Press **Complete Onboarding** on the case.
2. Type their **Company Email**. It becomes their work email and the login they are invited on.
3. Press **Complete Onboarding**. The case is **Completed**, the hire gets their invitation, and
   their seat on the Organogram is filled.

An address someone else already uses is refused, and nothing changes.

### Withdraw a hire {#recruitment-onboarding-withdraw needs="recruitment edit"}

If the hire is not joining, press **Withdraw Hire** on the case, type the **Reason** and press
**Withdraw Hire**. **Keep Hire** goes back instead. Their links stop working, an unsigned contract is
cancelled, and their record is archived. The hire is not emailed. A completed case cannot be withdrawn.

### Onboarding settings {#recruitment-onboarding-settings needs="recruitment edit"}

1. Open [Recruitment · Onboarding](/recruitment?tab=onboarding) and press **Onboarding Settings**.
   Only Operations & People see it.
   ![Onboarding Settings](shot:recruitment-onboarding-settings)
2. **Checklist** lists the documents a hire sends. Press one to change it, or **Add Document** for a
   new one: its **Name**, a **Code**, what it **Takes** (**File**, **Value** or **Both**), and
   whether it is **Required** or **Expires**. Untick **Active** to stop asking for it. Press
   **Save Document**. Cases already open keep their list.
3. **Onboarding Plans** lists each department's plan; **Company Default** is for a department without
   one. Press one, or **Add Plan**. Give each step a title, its **Owner**, its **Due Day** counted from
   the start date, and an **SOP** if one applies. **Add Step** adds another. Press **Save Plan**. A
   case keeps the steps it was given.
4. **Standard Contract** is the contract's wording. Change it and press **Save Contract**. Contracts
   made from now on use it; those already made keep theirs. **Restore Default** puts the shipped
   wording back.
