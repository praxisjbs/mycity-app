Queries & Sanctions is how MyCity handles a staff issue fairly. Someone raises a query, or a rule
or the Attendance policy raises one for them. The employee answers it in writing. Someone with authority then decides.
Most queries close with no action. When one is upheld, the outcome is the next step of MyCity's
ladder, written up as a letter.

Open it from **Queries & Sanctions** in the menu on a computer. On a phone, press **More** in the
bar at the bottom, then **Queries**. Its screens are tabs along the top: **Queries**, **Rules**,
**Sanctions** and **Letter Templates**. Your own queries are in
[Employee Self-Service · My Queries](#queries-my).

> **Important:** You see every query only if you work in Operations & People, Management or
> Implementation, or you are the CEO. Everyone else sees only the queries they raised. A query a
> rule or the Attendance policy raised is seen only by those who see every query.

## Queries {#queries-queries screen=queries/queries icon=flag}

Queries lists each query you may see, newest first, with its outcome.

![The Queries list](shot:queries-list)

### Read the queries {#queries-read}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Queries](/queries?tab=queries).
2. The tiles count the queries that are **Open**, **Awaiting Response**, **Closed This Month** and
   **Escalated**. A phone shows the first two.
3. Press **All Queries** to see every query you may see, or **Raised by Me** for your own.
4. Type in the search box to find a query by name, number or subject.
5. Press a query. It opens over the list, with its status at the top.
6. The query shows its **Subject**, who raised it (**Raised By**), when (**Raised**), the
   **Response Due** time, the **Policy** it cites and the employee's **Response**.
7. A query a rule raised also shows **Counted**: the days or tasks the rule counted. One the
   Attendance policy raised shows **Attendance Policy** under **Raised By**, and the late days it
   counted.
8. While a proposed sanction waits, **Waiting On** says who must approve it.
9. Press **View Letter** to read the letter the employee received.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting Response** | The employee has not answered yet |
| **Under Review** | The employee has answered, and a decision is waiting |
| **Escalated** | A sanction was proposed and waits for approval |
| **Closed, No Action** | It was closed without a sanction |
| **Sanctioned** | It ended in a sanction |

On a computer, **Queries by Reason**, under the list, shows how many queries each rule raised, and
how many people raised.

### Raise a query {#queries-raise needs="queries create"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Queries](/queries?tab=queries).
2. Press **Raise a Query** at the top right.
   ![Raise a Query](shot:queries-raise)
3. Under **Employee**, type the person's name and pick them. You cannot raise a query on yourself.
4. Type a short **Subject**, such as `Missing clock-out on Thursday`.
5. Under **What Happened**, say what happened, with the date. Use at least 20 characters.
6. To cite a policy, pick it under **Policy**. Only policies in force are offered.
7. Once a policy is picked, you may type the line it breaks under **Clause**. The letter quotes it.
8. Sign with your finger or mouse in **Your Signature**. It is printed on the letter. If you saved
   [your signature](#queries-signature), it is there already; press **Clear Signature** to redraw.
9. Press **Raise Query**. The hub says the query was raised and its letter sent.

The employee is told in the hub and by email, with the letter. They have 24 hours to respond.

| Field | What to enter | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Employee** | The person the query is about | Yes |
| **Subject** | 4 to 150 characters | Yes |
| **What Happened** | 20 to 4,000 characters | Yes |
| **Policy** | A policy in force | No |
| **Clause** | The line of the policy to quote | No |
| **Your Signature** | Your drawn signature | Yes |

### Write the query with AI {#queries-raise-ai needs="queries create"}

1. On **Raise a Query**, pick the **Employee** first.
2. Press **Write With AI** at the top of the form.
3. Under **What To Say**, describe what happened in your own words.
4. Press **Write Draft**. The draft appears below.
5. Press **Try Again** for another draft, or **Use Draft** to put it in **Subject** and
   **What Happened**.
6. Read the draft and change what you need. Nothing is raised until you press **Raise Query**.

> **Tip:** When the button reads **AI Is Off** or **AI Limit Reached**, AI cannot help now. Fill
> in the form yourself; it works as before.

### Send a reminder {#queries-remind needs="queries create"}

1. Open the query on [Queries & Sanctions · Queries](/queries?tab=queries).
2. While the employee has not answered, press **Send Reminder**.
3. The employee is told in the hub and by email. You can send one reminder a day.

### Record a decision {#queries-decide needs="queries edit"}

You can decide once the employee has responded, or once the 24 hours are over. Nobody decides a
query about themselves.

1. Open the query on [Queries & Sanctions · Queries](/queries?tab=queries).
2. Press **Record Decision**.
   ![Record Decision](shot:queries-decide)
3. Under **Outcome**, press **Close, No Action** or **Sanction**.
4. For **Close, No Action**, type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters. Press **Record Decision**.
   The query is closed and the employee is told.
5. For **Sanction**, pick the step under **Sanction**. The step marked **Next** is the one after the
   highest still in force for this person.
6. For a suspension without pay, check the dates under **From** and **To**.
7. For a step that needs evidence, such as Suspension Without Pay or Dismissal or Termination,
   list it under **Evidence**, one item a line.
8. To add a fine, type the amount under **Fine**. Payroll accepts it before anything is deducted.
9. Type the **Reason**.
10. Press **Record Decision**. The hub says **Sanction sent for approval**, and the query shows
   **Escalated**.

Operations & People approve every sanction, and sign it as they do. From Suspension Without Pay up,
the CEO approves it after them. Whoever proposed it cannot approve it, and nobody approves a
sanction about themselves. Once it is approved, its
letter is made, filed to the employee's documents and emailed to them. With a fine, a Salary
Deduction Letter goes with it.

> **Tip:** Only steps whose letter is ready are offered. A step whose letter is missing shows
> **Needs a Letter** on [Chain Settings](#queries-chain).

### When a decision is refused {#queries-decide-refused needs="queries edit"}

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Wait for the response or the deadline* | The employee still has time to respond | Wait for the response, or for the 24 hours to pass |
| *This query is already closed* | Someone decided it first | Read its status |
| *A sanction already waits for approval* | A sanction was already proposed | Wait for the approval |
| *This sanction's letter is not ready* | The step has no active letter | Ask whoever keeps the letters to finish it |
| *Add the evidence* | That step asks for evidence | Fill in **Evidence**, one item a line, then try again |

### Approve or reject a sanction {#queries-approve needs="queries approve"}

A proposed sanction waits on the query's card for whoever must approve it.

1. Open the query on [Queries & Sanctions · Queries](/queries?tab=queries). Its status is
   **Escalated**, and **Waiting On** names who must approve.
2. If it waits on you, press **Approve Sanction**.
   ![Approve Sanction](shot:queries-approve)
3. At **Operations & People**, sign in **Your Signature**, or keep the one you saved. It is printed
   on the sanction's letters, with your name and job title.
4. Press **Approve Sanction**. The hub says it was sent on to the CEO, or that the sanction was
   approved and its letter follows. The card shows the sanction's number once it is issued.
5. To refuse it, press **Reject**, type the **Reason** and press **Reject**. The hub says
   **Sanction rejected**, the query is back **Under Review**, and its sanction reads **Not
   Approved**.

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Sign in the box* | Operations & People sign when they approve | Sign, then press **Approve Sanction** |
| *This sanction was already decided* | Someone decided it first | Read its status |
| *You cannot decide your own sanction* | The sanction is about you | Someone else in Operations & People decides it |

> **Tip:** A sanction is approved only here, on the query's card, though it also shows in My
> Approvals.

## Rules {#queries-rules screen=queries/rules icon=flag}

A rule raises a query by itself when someone reaches a limit, such as three clock-ins from an
unregistered device in 30 days. It raises one query, not one each time. Lateness, absence and
missing clock-outs are counted by the Attendance policy, which raises their queries itself.

![The Rules screen](shot:queries-rules)

### Read the rules {#queries-rules-read}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Rules](/queries?tab=rules).
2. The tiles count the **Active Rules**, the **Draft** ones, how many **Fired This Month**, and the
   **Queries Raised**.
3. Each rule is a card: what it **Counts**, its **Threshold** such as *3 in 30 days*, the **Policy**
   it enforces, and **Active**, **Draft** or **Paused**.
4. On a computer, **Recent Firings** lists the queries the rules raised, with the **Rule**, when it
   **Fired**, the **Employee** and the **Query**'s status.

| What a rule counts | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Unregistered Device** | A clock-in from a device not on the person's list |
| **Overdue Task** | A task in My Workspace done after its due day, or still open after it |
| **Low Monthly Score** | A monthly review closed rated **Below** |
| **Missed Target** | Not counted yet, so its rule stays a draft with no **Activate** until KPI targets are in use |

### Add a rule {#queries-rules-add needs="queries create, cap queries.configure"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Rules](/queries?tab=rules).
2. Press **New Rule** at the top right.
   ![New Rule](shot:queries-rule-new)
3. Type a **Name**, such as `Overdue Tasks`.
4. Under **What Sets It Off**, pick what the rule counts.
5. Type **How Many Times** and **Within, Days**: three in 14 days is `3` and `14`.
6. Under **Letter**, pick the query letter its queries send. With none picked, they send the Query
   Letter.
7. To have its letters quote a policy, pick it under **Policy**.
8. Once a policy is picked, you may type the line it breaks under **Clause**, up to 300 characters.
   The rule's letters quote it.
9. Press **Save Rule**. The rule is saved as a draft and does nothing yet.

### Change a rule {#queries-rules-edit needs="queries edit, cap queries.configure"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Rules](/queries?tab=rules).
2. On the rule's card, press **Edit Rule**.
3. Change the **Name**, **How Many Times**, **Within, Days**, **Letter**, **Policy** or **Clause**.
   Taking the policy off clears the clause.
4. Press **Save Rule**. The change counts from the next event.

Once a rule has raised a query, **What Sets It Off** is shown but cannot change. Make a new rule
instead.

### Switch a rule on or off {#queries-rules-switch needs="queries edit, cap queries.configure"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Rules](/queries?tab=rules).
2. On the rule's card, press **Activate**. It counts from now on, never before. A **Missed Target**
   rule has no **Activate** yet: nothing counts missed targets until KPIs do.
3. To stop it, press **Pause**. A rule that has fired is paused, never deleted.

### Delete a rule {#queries-rules-delete needs="queries edit, cap queries.configure"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Rules](/queries?tab=rules).
2. On the card of a rule that has never raised a query, press **Delete Rule**.
3. Press **Delete Rule** again to confirm. The rule is gone for good.

A rule that has raised a query has no **Delete Rule**: press **Pause** instead, so its queries keep
their history.

## Sanctions {#queries-sanctions screen=queries/sanctions icon=flag}

Sanctions lists each sanction, and holds MyCity's ladder of steps.

![The Sanctions screen](shot:queries-sanctions)

### Read the sanctions {#queries-sanctions-read}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Sanctions](/queries?tab=sanctions).
2. The tiles show the **Active Sanctions**, those **Awaiting Approval**, the **Fines, This Year**
   and the **Fines Recovered**.
3. **Sanction History** lists each one: **Employee**, **Sanction**, **Query**, **Date** and
   **Status**.
4. Type in the search box to find a person or a reason.
5. Press a sanction to open it: its **Number**, the **Query**, the **Reason**, who **Decided By**,
   the dates it runs **From** and **Until**, any **Fine** and where Payroll stands with it, and any
   **Evidence**.
6. Press **View Letter** for its letter, **Deduction Letter** for a fine's, or **Open Query** for the
   query it follows.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting Approval** | Proposed, and waiting for its approvers ([approve it](#queries-approve)) |
| **Issued** | Approved; its letter was sent |
| **Not Approved** | The approvers refused it; the query went back under review |
| **Withdrawn** | It was withdrawn before a decision |

### Issue a sanction {#queries-sanctions-issue needs="queries edit"}

Every sanction follows a query.

1. Press **Issue Sanction** at the top right.
2. Under **Query**, search for the query by its number, the person's name or its subject, and pick
   it. Only queries ready for a decision are offered: answered, or past the 24 hours unanswered.
3. **Record Decision** opens on it at **Sanction**: follow [Record a decision](#queries-decide) from
   step 5.

A query still waiting for its response, or with a sanction already waiting for approval, shows
**Not Ready**.

### Set the ladder {#queries-chain needs="cap queries.configure"}

The ladder is MyCity's order of sanctions, from the lightest to the heaviest.

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Sanctions](/queries?tab=sanctions).
2. Press **Chain Settings**. The steps show in order.
   ![Chain Settings](shot:queries-chain)
3. Each step shows its **Letter**, its **Financial Effect** and how long it is **In Force For**.
4. Its status reads **Ready**, **Needs a Letter** or **Retired**.
5. Press **Move Up** or **Move Down** to change a step's place.
6. Press **Retire** to stop offering a step. Its past sanctions stay. **Restore** brings it back.

| Financial Effect | What it means |
|---|---|
| **None** | No money changes |
| **Unpaid Days** | Days off without pay, such as a suspension without pay |
| **Ends Employment** | Dismissal or termination |

A fine is not a step. It can be added to any sanction when you [record a decision](#queries-decide).

### Add a step {#queries-chain-add needs="queries create, cap queries.configure"}

1. In **Chain Settings**, press **Add Step** at the bottom.
2. Type the **Name**, such as `Final Warning`.
3. Under **Position**, type where it goes. The steps below it move down one.
4. Under **In Force, Days**, type how long it counts towards the next offence. Leave it empty for
   always.
5. Pick its **Letter**, then its **Financial Effect**. For **Unpaid Days**, type the **Days**.
6. Press **Add Step**.

## Letter Templates {#queries-letters screen=queries/letter-templates icon=doc}

Letter Templates holds the wording of each letter. The hub fills in the name, the dates and the
reason from the record.

![The Letter Templates screen](shot:queries-letters)

### Read a letter {#queries-letters-read}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Letter Templates](/queries?tab=letter-templates).
2. The strip counts the **Templates**, the **Active** ones, those **Awaiting Sample**, and the
   **Letters Sent, 90d**.
3. The list shows each **Template**, what it is **Used By**, and its **Status**.
4. Press a template to see it as the employee receives it: the letter's PDF, with sample details
   and your saved signature. Press **Download PDF** to keep a copy.

A letter **Awaiting Sample** has no wording yet: MyCity has still to send its sample.

### Write or change a letter {#queries-letters-edit needs="queries edit, cap queries.configure"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Letter Templates](/queries?tab=letter-templates) and press the
   template.
2. Press the **⋯** at the right end of the letter's bar, after **Open in Tab**, then **Edit Template**. The letter opens with its
   wording as it is sent.
3. Change the **Name** if you need to. While no step or rule sends the letter, you can also change
   **Used For**.
4. Change the wording under **Letter**. Type `{{ employee.first_name }}`, `{{ query_number }}`,
   `{{ reason }}` or `{{ step_name }}` where the record fills in.
5. To draft it with AI, press **Write With AI**, say what you want under **What To Say**, then
   **Write Draft** and **Use Draft**.
6. Press **Save Letter**. Each change of wording is a new version; a letter keeps the version it
   used.

### Add a letter {#queries-letters-add needs="queries create, cap queries.configure"}

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Letter Templates](/queries?tab=letter-templates).
2. Press **New Template** at the top right.
3. Type the **Name**, such as `Final Warning`.
4. Under **Used For**, press **Queries** or **Sanctions**.
5. Write the wording under **Letter**, as above.
6. Press **Save Draft** to keep it out of use for now, or **Save and Activate** to use it at once.

A step chooses its letter on [Chain Settings](#queries-chain), and a rule under **Letter** on
[New Rule or Edit Rule](#queries-rules-add). Both then show under **Used By**.

### Switch a letter on or off {#queries-letters-activate needs="queries edit, cap queries.configure"}

1. Press the letter on [Queries & Sanctions · Letter Templates](/queries?tab=letter-templates).
2. Press the **⋯** on the letter's bar, then **Activate** for a draft. A letter with no wording cannot
   be switched on.
3. Press the **⋯**, then **Deactivate** for an active letter you no longer use. It goes back to a draft.

| The hub says | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *In use by …* | A step or rule sends it, or it is the Query Letter or the Salary Deduction Letter | Give the step or rule another letter first |

Only an active letter is used. A step of the ladder needs its letter active before it is offered.

### Save your signature {#queries-signature needs="queries view"}

Draw your signature once, and your query letters and the sanctions you approve start from it.

1. Open [Queries & Sanctions · Letter Templates](/queries?tab=letter-templates).
2. Press **Edit Signature** at the top right.
3. Sign with your finger or mouse in **Your Signature**. To start again, press **Clear Signature**.
4. Press **Save Signature**. The hub says **Signature saved**.

A letter already sent keeps the signature it was signed with.

## My Queries {#queries-my screen=ess/my-queries icon=user}

My Queries is your own record of queries and sanctions. It is a tab of Employee Self-Service.

![Employee Self-Service, My Queries](shot:queries-my)

### Read your queries {#queries-my-read}

1. Open [Employee Self-Service · My Queries](/ess?tab=my-queries).
2. **Queries** is chosen at the top, with how many are open. Open queries come first.
3. Press a query. On a computer it opens beside the list; on a phone it opens over it.
4. It shows when it was **Raised On**, the time to **Respond By**, **What Happened**, any
   **Policy** it cites, **Your Response**, and any **Sanction**.
5. Press **View Letter** to read the letter.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting Your Response** | You can still respond, even after the 24 hours |
| **Under Review** | Your response is in; a decision is waiting |
| **Closed, No Action** | It was closed without a sanction |
| **Sanctioned** | It ended in a sanction |

### Respond to a query {#queries-my-respond}

You have 24 hours from when the query was raised. You can respond once, so take your time.

1. Open the query on [Employee Self-Service · My Queries](/ess?tab=my-queries).
2. Press **Respond**. On a phone it is at the bottom of the query.
   ![Respond to a query](shot:queries-respond)
3. Write your answer under **Your Response**. Use bold, lists and paragraphs if they help.
4. Press **Send Response**. The hub says **Response sent**.

Whoever raised the query is told. Your response cannot be changed once it is sent.

### Read your sanctions {#queries-my-sanctions}

1. Open [Employee Self-Service · My Queries](/ess?tab=my-queries).
2. Press **Sanctions** at the top.
3. Each row shows the **Sanction**, the **Query** it follows, the **Reason**, the **Date** and any
   **Fine**.
4. Press a sanction to read its letter. With a fine, the Salary Deduction Letter follows it.

With none, it reads **No Sanctions**.

3. Press **Sanctions** for their sanctions, with the **Fine** and **Status**.
4. Press a row to open its query on [Queries & Sanctions · Queries](/queries?tab=queries).
