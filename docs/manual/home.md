Home is the first screen you see after you sign in. It has four tabs: **Overview**, **Team**,
**Alerts** and **Reports**. Everyone has an Overview, shaped for their role. Team, Alerts and
Reports are for line managers, Operations and People, Management and the CEO.

If a tab reads **For Managers** with a padlock, your role does not open it. There is nothing for you
to do there.

## Overview {#home-overview screen=home/overview icon=home}

The Overview answers three questions: where things stand, what needs you, and what is coming this
week. What it shows depends on your role, so two colleagues see different Overviews.

![The Overview of a member of staff](shot:home-overview)

### Read your Overview {#home-overview-read}

1. Open [Home · Overview](/home?tab=overview). It greets you by your first name, with today's date
   under it on a computer.
2. If you are a member of staff, **Your Day** comes first: **Shift Today**, **Clock-In**,
   **Worksite**, **Status** and **Hours Today**. On a phone it shows the first, second and fourth.
   **Day Off** under Shift Today means today is not one of your working days.
3. Under it are your four cards, each a number that matters to your role, such as
   **My Attendance** or **My Overdue Tasks**. A dash means there is nothing to measure yet.
4. Read the sections under the cards. Which ones you see depends on your role (see the table
   below).
5. **Need You** lists the requests waiting for your decision, oldest first. Press one to open it
   and decide. **Nothing Needs You** means nothing is waiting.
6. **Activity Today** lists what happened across MyCity today that you may see: who joined, leave
   approved, recognitions and celebrations.
7. **Your Week** lists your working days this week with their hours, any public holiday, your leave
   and what is due each day.

| Your role | What the Overview shows under the cards |
|---|---|
| Staff | **My Goals This Quarter**, **Upcoming Leave** with the days you have left this year |
| Operations and People | **Attendance, 14 Working Days**, the **On Time** ring for this month, **Partnership Pipeline**, **Goals This Quarter** |
| Heads of Department | **Monthly Signed Value**, **Partnership Pipeline**, **Goals This Quarter** |
| The CEO | **Organisational Goals**, the **Org KPI** ring with each department's score, **Partnership Pipeline** |

> **Tip:** On the Operations chart, the dips are Saturdays. Saturday is a half day, from 09:00 to
> 15:00.

If you hold alerts to clear, a button beside **Need You** says how many, such as *Clear 15 Alerts*.
Press it to open [Home · Alerts](/home?tab=alerts).

On your first sign-in of a working day, the hub asks you to clock in before you see the Overview.
Your attendance is on [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance).

### See the records behind a card {#home-overview-card}

Every card opens the list it counts, so you can check the number yourself.

1. Press a card, such as **My Attendance**. Its list opens over the Overview. On a phone it slides
   up from the bottom.
   ![The records behind My Attendance](shot:home-card-records)
2. The line at the top sums it up, such as *19 of 21 days on time*.
3. Press a chip, such as **Late**, to see only those records. **All** shows them all again.
4. Type in **Search List** to find one record.
5. Press a record to open it on its own screen, when it has one.
6. Press **Close** to go back.

A list shows 25 records to a page. Use the arrows at its foot for the next page.

### Choose your cards {#home-overview-choose}

You can choose up to four cards, from the ones your role may see.

1. On a computer, press **Choose Cards** above the cards. On a phone, press **More Actions** (the
   three dots at the top), then **Choose Cards**.
   ![Choose Cards](shot:home-choose-cards)
2. Tick a card to add it, or untick it to take it off. The line at the top counts them, such as
   *3 of 4*.
3. Press **Move Up** or **Move Down** on a chosen card to change its place.
4. Press **Save Cards**. Your Overview shows them at once.
5. To go back to the cards your role starts with, press **Use Role Default**.

A card marked **Locked** was chosen for your role, and you cannot take it off.

### Save your Overview as a file {#home-overview-export}

On a computer you can save your Overview to share it or print it.

1. Press **Export** at the top right.
2. Choose PDF or XLSX (a spreadsheet).
3. Wait while the button spins. The file is saved to your computer, and a message names it, such as
   *RPT-0216 downloaded*.

If it says **Export failed. Try again.**, check your connection and press **Export** again.

### Start something new {#home-overview-new}

**New** at the top right lists what you can start from Home. You only see what your role allows.

- {needs="workspace create"} **New Task** opens My Workspace with a new task ready to fill in.
- {needs="ess create"} **Request Leave** opens [My Leave](/ess?tab=my-leave) with the leave form
  open.
- **Add Work Site** asks People to approve a few days working somewhere else (see below).
- {needs="cap alerts.manage"} **New Rule** opens a new alert rule. Read
  [Change the alert rules](#home-alerts-rules).
- {needs="cap home.reports"} **New Report** opens a new report. Read
  [Build a report](#home-reports-new).

On a phone, these are also on the round button with a plus at the bottom right.

### Add a work site {#home-overview-work-site}

Add a work site when you will work somewhere other than your usual office for a few days: a client
visit, a training, an event or an out-of-station trip. Once People approve it, you can clock in
there on those days.

1. Press **Add Work Site** at the top of [Home · Overview](/home?tab=overview).
   ![Add Work Site](shot:home-work-site)
2. Under **Hours**, keep **Usual Shift** for your normal hours. Choose **Site Hours** if the site
   keeps its own hours, then set **Starts At** and **Ends At**.
3. Pick the **Purpose**, such as **Client Visit** or **Training (Attending)**.
4. Type a **Name** for it, such as `Partner Training Week`.
5. In **Address**, start typing and pick the place from the list, or press **Drop a Pin** and pin
   it on the map.
6. Leave **Radius, Metres** at 150 unless the site is large. A clock-in within that distance counts
   as being there.
7. Press **Add Address** if the work moves between several places, up to eight.
8. Choose the **First Day** and **Last Day**. A site lasts at most 90 days.
9. Under **People**, you are already listed. Search for any colleague going with you and pick them.
10. In **Description**, say what the work is, in at least 30 characters. People decide from it.
11. Press **Submit for Approval**. A message names the request, such as *TS-0012 sent for approval*.

Your request waits for People's decision on
[Work Location Requests](/ess?tab=work-location-requests).

| The field says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | It cannot be left empty, or no address was picked | Fill it in. For an address, pick one from the list or drop a pin |
| **At least 3 characters** | The name is too short | Give the site a longer name |
| **At least 30 characters** | The description is too short | Say more about the work |
| **At least 150 m** or **At most 500 m** | The radius is out of range | Type a number from 150 to 500 |
| **Ends before it starts** | The last day or end time is before the first | Correct the date or time |
| **At most 90 days** | The site is longer than 90 days | Split it into shorter sites |
| **Overlaps another site** | Someone is already on another site on those days | Read the message at the foot of the screen, which names them, and change the days or the people |
| **Not available** | **Temporary Home** is switched off for MyCity | Choose another purpose |

## Team {#home-team screen=home/team icon=users}

Team shows who is in today, for the people you look after. A line manager sees the people who
report to them, directly or further down. Operations and People and the CEO see everyone.

![The Team tab](shot:home-team)

### See who is in today {#home-team-today}

1. Open [Home · Team](/home?tab=team). The line under the title says whose roster it is:
   **Your Team**, **The Whole Organisation** or **Just You**.
2. The four cards count today's people: **Present Today** (in, out of those expected),
   **On Site**, **Remote** and **Flags** (late today, or no clock-out yesterday).
3. Press a status chip, such as **Late**, to see only those people. **Everyone** shows them all.
4. On a computer, choose a **Department** to see one team.
5. Type a name in **Search People** to find someone.
6. Read the **Team Roster**: each person's status today, their **Clock-In** time, their **KPI**
   score and their **Open Tasks**. A dash means there is nothing to show yet.
7. Under it, **Attendance This Month** shows each person's month so far: days **On Time** out of the
   days expected, **Late**, **Absent** and **On Leave**.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **On Site** | Clocked in on time at their office |
| **Remote** | Clocked in on time and working remotely |
| **Field** | Clocked in on time at an approved work site |
| **Late** | Clocked in after their start time and its grace minutes |
| **Not In** | Expected today and not clocked in yet |
| **On Leave** | On approved leave today |
| **Day Off** | Today is not one of their working days |

**No Clock-Out Yesterday** beside a status means they clocked in on their last working day and never
clocked out.

### Look at one person {#home-team-person}

1. Press a person's row, or their card on a phone. Their day opens: **Job Title**, **Department**,
   **Today**, **Clock-In**, **Clock-Out**, **Worksite**, **KPI** and **Open Tasks**.
   ![One person's day](shot:home-team-person)
2. Press **View Attendance** to open their attendance on People.
3. Press **Open Profile** to open their record on People.

You only see the buttons for screens your role opens.

Press a card at the top, such as **Present Today**, to see the people it counts, with a chip for
each status.

## Alerts {#home-alerts screen=home/alerts icon=alert}

An alert is raised when something passes a limit MyCity has set: a leave request waiting too long,
a contract about to end, or someone late three times this month. An alert stays open until somebody
clears it, unlike a notification, which you read once.

You see the alerts about the people you look after, for the rules your role clears. Nobody sees an
alert about themselves.

![The Alerts tab](shot:home-alerts)

### Read the alerts {#home-alerts-read}

1. Open [Home · Alerts](/home?tab=alerts).
2. The four cards count **Open Alerts**, the ones that **Needs You**, those open for a week or more
   (**Overdue 7 Days +**), and those **Cleared This Week**.
3. Press a category chip, such as **Attendance**, to see one kind only. **All** shows every one.
4. Read the **Open Alerts** list:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Alert** | What it is about, such as a person's late arrivals this month |
| **Trigger** | What raised it, such as the days they were late |
| **Who Can Clear** | Whose role clears it, such as **People Approvers** |
| **Age** | How long it has been open: **Today**, or a number of days |
| **Over By** | How far past its limit: *+1* means one more than the limit. For a date, the days left, **Due Today** or **Expired** |
| **Severity** | **High**, **Medium** or **Low** |

On a computer, **Alert Sources, 30 Days** under the list shows which rules raised the most alerts
this month. **View Dismissed** lists the alerts somebody dismissed; **View Open** goes back.

### Clear or dismiss an alert {#home-alerts-clear needs="cap alerts.clear"}

Clear an alert once you have dealt with what it is about. Dismiss it when it needs nothing.

1. Press the alert. It opens with its **Trigger**, **Who Can Clear**, **Age**, **Over By** and
   **Severity**.
   ![An alert, opened](shot:home-alert-sheet)
2. Deal with what it says. For a leave request waiting too long, for example, decide the request.
3. Press **Clear Alert**. The message **Alert cleared** confirms it, and it leaves the list.
4. Or press **Dismiss Alert** if it needs nothing. The message **Alert dismissed** confirms it.

Each alert is cleared once. The same thing raises a new alert only when it happens again.

If **Clear Alert** is not there, your role does not clear that alert. **Who Can Clear** says whose
role does.

> **Tip:** A **Query Due** alert comes from the Attendance policy, such as three late arrivals in a
> month. {needs="cap policies.manage"} Press **Set in Attendance Policy** on it to change the policy's
> limits.

If a link opens an alert you may not see, the message **Alert not found** shows instead.

### Change the alert rules {#home-alerts-rules needs="cap alerts.manage"}

A rule says what raises an alert and when. Changing a rule takes effect from the next morning's
check, at 06:00.

1. Press **Alert Rules** at the top of [Home · Alerts](/home?tab=alerts).
   ![Alert Rules](shot:home-alert-rules)
2. The **Attendance Policy** card comes first. It lists the policy's five rules: **Late Arrivals**,
   **Early Departure**, **Absence**, **Forgotten Clock-Outs** and **Location Flags**, each with its
   **Limit** and what it does (**Alert**, **Raise Query**). They change only in the Attendance
   policy: press **Set in Attendance Policy**.
3. Below it, each other rule shows its **Threshold**, **Severity**, **Who Can Clear**, its
   **Open Alerts** and its switch.
4. To stop a rule, switch it off. The hub asks **Switch Off Rule** and warns
   *Its open alerts are dismissed*. Press **Switch Off**. Switch it on again at any time.
5. To change a rule, press **Edit Rule** beside it, change what you need, and press **Save Rule**.

### Add an alert rule {#home-alerts-new needs="cap alerts.manage"}

1. Press **New Rule** at the top of [Home · Alerts](/home?tab=alerts).
   ![New Rule](shot:home-new-rule)
2. Type a **Rule Name**, such as `Approvals Waiting`.
3. Pick the **Metric**: what the rule watches.
4. Type the **Threshold**: the number that raises an alert.
5. If the metric counts over days, set **Window Days**, such as `30`.
6. Choose the **Severity**: **Low**, **Medium** or **High**. A High alert left open 14 days is sent
   to the CEO.
7. Check **Who Can Clear**. It starts with the usual role for that metric.
8. Press **Create Rule**. The message **Rule created** confirms it.

| Metric | Threshold means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Approval Ageing** | Days a request has waited at one step | *Waiting 3 days* |
| **Document Expiry** | Days before a document expires | *30 days before expiry* |
| **Confirmation Due** | Days before a probation ends | *14 days before confirmation* |
| **Contract Ending** | Days before a contract ends | *30 days before it ends* |
| **Partner Inactivity** | Days since a partner was last contacted | *30 days without contact* |
| **Budget Threshold** | Share of a budget spent | *90% spent* |
| **Recruitment SLA** | Days an application has waited at one stage | *7 days at one stage* |

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Required** | Fill it in |
| **Enter the days** | Type the **Window Days** |
| **Whole days only** | Type a whole number, such as `30` |

## Reports {#home-reports screen=home/reports icon=chart}

A report is a file the hub makes for you, as a PDF or a spreadsheet: today's attendance, leave,
open alerts or headcount. It runs when you ask, or on a schedule, and is emailed to the people you
choose. Each person receives only the rows their own role may see.

![The Reports tab](shot:home-reports)

### Read your reports {#home-reports-read}

1. Open [Home · Reports](/home?tab=reports).
2. The cards count your reports that run on a schedule (**Scheduled**) and those **Paused**, the
   files made this month (**Runs This Month**) and the people who receive them (**Recipients**).
3. Each report has a card: its name, its status (**Scheduled**, **Paused** or **On Demand**), its
   **Source**, its **Schedule** in words, such as *Every working day at 18:00*, and its **Audience**.
4. **Recent Runs** lists the files made, newest first: the number, the report, when it ran, who
   received it and its status.

### Run a report now {#home-reports-run}

1. Press **Run Now** on the report's card.
2. If the report makes both a PDF and a spreadsheet, choose which one.
3. Wait while the button spins. The file is saved to your device, and a message names it, such as
   *RPT-0215 downloaded*.

In **Recent Runs**, a file still being made reads **Pending**. Press **Download** on a finished one
to save it again. **Skipped** means its recipient may no longer receive that report. **Failed** means
it could not be made: press **Run Now** to try again.

### Build a report {#home-reports-new needs="cap home.reports"}

The form has three steps. A report you run only when you ask has two.

1. Press **New Report** at the top of [Home · Reports](/home?tab=reports).
   ![New Report, first step](shot:home-new-report)
2. On **Report**, type the **Report Name**, such as `Late Today`.
3. Pick the **Source**: what the report lists. **Daily Attendance**, **Leave**, **Open Alerts** or
   **Headcount** are the ones with records today.
4. Narrow it if you need to. For **Daily Attendance**, choose the **Day** (**Today So Far** or
   **Last Working Day**), tick the **Statuses** you want, and pick a **Department**. Leave the
   statuses unticked to list everyone.
5. Tick **Share With Managers** to let other managers who run reports see it too. Press **Next**.
6. On **Schedule**, choose how often it **Repeats**: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or
   **On Demand** (only when you press Run Now).
7. For a schedule, set the **Start Date** and the **Time** it runs. For **Weekly**, tick its
   **Days**. Keep **Working Days Only** ticked to skip weekends and public holidays. Press
   **Next**.
8. On **Delivery**, add the **Recipients**: search for each person and pick them. You are already
   listed.
9. Choose the **Formats**: **PDF**, **XLSX** or **Both**.
10. Press **Create Report**. The message **Report created** confirms it.

| The field says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | It cannot be left empty | Fill it in |
| **Name already used** | You already have a report with this name | Choose another name |
| **Absent needs Last Working Day** | Nobody is absent until the day is over | Choose **Last Working Day**, or untick **Absent** |
| **Not In needs Today** | Not In applies only to today | Choose **Today So Far**, or untick **Not In** |
| **Pick a day** | A weekly report has no day ticked | Tick at least one of the **Days** |
| **Add a recipient** | Nobody receives it | Add at least one person |
| *Ada Obi: no access* | That person's role does not open this report's records | Take them off, or ask an administrator about their role |

If a person you pick has no login yet, the message says they *cannot sign in yet*, and they are not
added.

### Change, pause or archive a report {#home-reports-change needs="cap home.reports"}

Only the person who made a report, and the CEO, can change it.

1. Press the three dots on the report's card.
2. Choose **Edit Report** to change it. The form is the same, without **Source**. Press
   **Save Report**.
3. Choose **Pause Report** to stop its schedule for now, and **Resume Report** to start it again.
4. Choose **Archive Report** when nobody needs it any more. Confirm with **Archive Report**. It
   stops running and leaves the list. Its files stay.
