Everything in this chapter is the same for everyone at MyCity. Read it on your first day: it takes
about ten minutes, and the rest of the guide assumes you know it.

## Signing In {#start-sign-in icon=lock}

You sign in with your work email and a password you choose yourself. Nobody else knows your
password, People and the CEO included.

![The sign-in screen](shot:start-sign-in)

### Sign in for the first time {#start-first-time}

People give you a login when you join. You get an email titled *Your MyCity App account is
ready*.

1. Open the email and press **Set Your Password**. The hub opens in your browser.
2. Type a new password in **New Password**. It needs at least 8 characters, with a capital letter,
   a small letter, a number and a symbol.
3. Type it again in **Confirm Password**.
4. Press **Set Password**. The sign-in screen opens with your email already filled in.
5. Type your new password and press **Sign In**.

> **Important:** The link in the email works once and expires after 72 hours. If it says
> **Link Expired**, ask People and Operations to send you a new one.

### Sign in with your password {#start-password}

1. Open the hub. The sign-in screen greets you with **Good morning**, **Good afternoon** or
   **Good evening**.
2. Type your work email in **Email**, for example `ada@mycityapp.io`.
3. Type your password in **Password**.
4. Press **Sign In**. You land on Home, or on the screen you were opening.

If something is wrong, a red bar above the button says what. Read it, correct the field, and try
again.

| The red bar says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **That email and password do not match.** | The email or the password is wrong | Check the email's spelling, then type the password again. Use **Forgot Password** if you are not sure of it |
| **Too many attempts.** | Several wrong passwords in a row | Wait the time it shows, then try again |
| **This account is suspended. Contact People.** | Your login is paused | Ask People and Operations |
| **This account is closed.** | Your login was closed when you left MyCity | Ask People and Operations if this is a mistake |
| **You appear to be offline. Check the connection and try again.** | No internet on this device | Reconnect, then press **Sign In** again |

### Enter a code from your phone {#start-two-step}

Some roles also need a code from an authenticator app on your phone. This is called two-step
sign-in.

1. After your password, the screen asks you to **Enter Code**.
2. Open your authenticator app and find MyCity App.
3. Type the six digits under **Sign-In Code**. The hub opens when you type the last digit.
4. No phone with you? Press **Use Recovery Code** and type one of the ten recovery codes you
   saved when you set it up. Each code works once.

If the screen says **Set Up Two-Step**, your role needs it and you have not set it up yet:

1. Open your authenticator app and scan the square code on the screen.
2. Type the six digits the app shows in **Code From App** and press **Turn On**.
3. The screen shows ten **Recovery Codes**. Press **Copy Codes** and keep them somewhere safe,
   away from your phone.
4. Press **Continue**.

> **Tip:** You choose how often this browser asks for a code on
> [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security): every sign-in, once a day or once a month.

### Sign in with your PIN or a passkey {#start-pin-passkey}

On a phone or computer you have signed in on before, you can sign in faster.

- **Use PIN** appears under the password once you have set a PIN on
  [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security). Type your four digits.
- **Use Passkey** appears once you have added a passkey there. Your phone or computer asks for
  your fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a password.
- **Use Password** takes you back to the password at any time.

After 10 wrong PINs in a row your PIN switches off, and the screen asks for your password.

### Forgot your password {#start-forgot-password}

1. On the sign-in screen, press **Forgot Password**.
2. Type your work email and press **Send Link**. The screen says **Check Your Email**.
3. Open the email titled *Reset your MyCity App password* and press **Choose a New Password**.
4. Type a new password in **New Password** and again in **Confirm Password**.
5. Press **Save Password**. Sign in with the new password.

> **Important:** The reset link works once and expires after 30 minutes. Saving a new password
> signs you out on every device. If the link says **Link Expired**, press **Request New Link**.

## When the Screen Locks {#start-lock icon=lock}

The hub locks itself to keep your work safe. Your work stays underneath, and nothing you were
typing is lost.

- **Session Locked** means you were away for a while (30 minutes, unless MyCity changed it).
- **Session Ended** means your session reached its time limit (2 hours from sign-in, unless MyCity
  changed it).
- **Signed Out** means your session was ended somewhere else, for example by a new password.

![The lock screen](shot:start-lock)

1. Type your PIN, or your password, on the lock screen.
2. Press **Unlock**. The hub says **Welcome back** and you carry on where you were.
3. Press **Sign Out** instead if you are done for the day.

## Finding Your Way {#start-layout icon=grid}

The hub is laid out differently on a computer and on a phone, so each feels natural. Everything
you can do on one you can do on the other.

### On a computer {#start-layout-desktop}

![The hub on a computer](shot:start-desktop)

1. The dark menu on the left lists the modules you can open, grouped under Work, People,
   Business and System. Press one to open it.
2. Each module's screens are tabs along the top of the page. Press a tab to open that screen.
3. **Collapse Menu** at the top left makes the menu narrow, to give the page more room.
   **Expand Menu** widens it again.
4. **Search Anything** at the top finds any screen, action or record (see
   [Search](#start-search)).
5. The bell, **Notifications**, opens your notifications.
6. Your name at the foot of the menu opens your account menu: **My Profile**, **My Security**,
   **User Guide**, the theme and density, **Text Size**, **Install App** and **Sign Out**.

### On a phone {#start-layout-phone}

![The hub on a phone](shot:start-phone)

1. The bar at the bottom opens **Home**, **Workspace**, **Community**, **Self-Service** and
   **More**. More holds every other module.
2. The module's screens are tabs under its name. Swipe them sideways to see them all.
3. The icons at the top right are **Search**, **Notifications** and your account.
4. The round button with a plus at the bottom right holds quick actions: the screen's own action
   (such as *Request Leave*), clocking in, **Praxis AI** ([Ask from any screen on a phone](#praxis-ask-anywhere))
   and **Help**. **Hide Here** tucks it
   away on this page.
5. Your phone's own Back gesture goes back a screen and closes any sheet first.

> **Tip:** Pull the page down from the top to refresh it. Keep pulling past the second notch to
> reload the whole app.

If the internet drops, the page says **No Connection**. Press **Try Again** once you are back
online.

## Search {#start-search icon=srch}

Search finds every screen, action and record you are allowed to open, from anywhere in the hub.

![Search, open](shot:start-search)

1. Press Ctrl and K together (⌘ and K on a Mac), or press **Search Anything** at the top of a
   computer screen, or **Search** on a phone.
2. Type what you are looking for: a person's name, a screen such as `leave`, or something to do
   such as `add employee`.
3. Results come in groups: **Screens**, **Actions**, then records such as **Employees**. Use the
   arrow keys and Enter, or press a result.
4. To look in one kind of record only, start with its word: `staff ada` finds employees called
   Ada, `guide leave` finds parts of this guide about leave.
5. Press Escape to close search.

With nothing typed, search lists what is waiting on you first, then every screen and action you
can open.

## Notifications {#start-notifications icon=bell}

The hub tells you when something needs you: a request to approve, a task, a message.

![The notifications panel](shot:start-notifications)

1. Press the bell, **Notifications**. A dot on the bell means something is unread.
2. Press a notification to open what it is about. It is marked read.
3. Choose **Unread** to see only what you have not read yet.
4. **Mark All Read** marks everything read at once.
5. **Notification Settings** chooses how you are told about each kind of notice: in the hub, by
   email or by push to your phone, and your quiet hours.

An urgent notice stays in a red bar across the top of every screen until you press
**Acknowledge**.

> **Tip:** To get notifications on your phone, press **Turn On** when the hub offers
> *Get notified of new mail and approvals*, and allow it when your phone asks.

## Your Display and the App {#start-display icon=sun}

These choices are yours and are kept on this device only.

- **Dark Theme** or **Light Theme** switches the colours.
- **Compact Density** fits more on the screen; **Comfortable Density** gives everything more room.
- **Text Size** makes all the writing **Small**, **Default**, **Large** or **Largest**.

On a computer they are in your account menu. On a phone they are the icons beside your name in
**More**.

![Theme, density and text size](shot:start-display)

### Install the app {#start-install}

The hub installs like an app, with its own icon, and opens faster.

1. Press **Install App** in your account menu (computer) or in **More** (phone), or on the offer
   at the foot of the screen.
2. Confirm in your browser's own window.
3. On an iPhone or iPad, the hub shows how: tap Share in Safari's toolbar, then choose Add to Home
   Screen.

## Using This Guide {#start-user-guide screen=knowledge/user-guide icon=book}

This guide shows you how to use every screen of the hub. It lives in the Knowledge Hub, on the
[User Guide](/knowledge?tab=user-guide) tab. You only see the parts about what you can open, so two
colleagues may see different chapters.

![The User Guide](shot:start-user-guide)

### Find what you need {#start-user-guide-find}

1. Open [User Guide](/knowledge?tab=user-guide) in the Knowledge Hub.
2. Choose **Quick Start** for short illustrated cards, grouped by role, or **Full Guide** for every
   chapter in full.
3. In the Full Guide, pick a section from the list on the left. On a phone or a narrow window, the
   sections are a row above the text: swipe it sideways. The chapter's name is above the section's
   title.
4. To jump to a topic from anywhere, open search, type `guide` and a few words, such as
   `guide add employee`, and choose the result.

### Do a step and come back {#start-user-guide-return}

1. When a step names a screen as an underlined link, press it. The screen opens.
2. Do what the step says. A small bar at the foot of the screen names the step you came from.
3. Press **Back to Guide** on that bar. The guide opens at the same step, shaded.
4. Press **Close** on the bar if you do not need to go back.

> **Tip:** A link to another part of this guide opens it here, with no bar.

> **Tip:** At the top of a section, **Open Screen** opens the screen the section is about, with the
> same way back.

### Help from any screen {#start-user-guide-help}

- On a phone, press the round button with a plus at the bottom right, then **Help**. The guide opens
  at the section about the screen you were on. A screen with no section yet opens the guide at its
  start.
- On a computer, press your name at the foot of the menu, then **User Guide**. It opens at the same
  place.
- Pictures carry numbered marks. The list under each picture says what each number points at.
  Press a picture to see it larger in **Picture**. On a phone it shows the computer's picture.
