Everything in this chapter is the same for everyone at MyCity. Read it on your first day: it takes
about ten minutes, and the rest of the guide assumes you know it.

## Signing In {#start-sign-in icon=lock}

You sign in with your work email and a password you choose yourself. Nobody else knows your
password, People and the CEO included.

![The sign-in screen](shot:start-sign-in)

### Sign in for the first time {#start-first-time}

People give you a login when you join. You get an email titled *Your MyCity App account is
ready*.

1. Open the email and press **Set Your Password**. The hub opens in your browser.
2. Type a new password in **New Password**. It needs at least 8 characters, with a capital letter,
   a small letter, a number and a symbol. The field says what is missing as you type.
3. Type it again in **Confirm Password**.
4. Press **Set Password**. The sign-in screen opens with your email filled in and a green bar:
   **Password saved. Sign in with it now.**
5. Type your new password and press **Sign In**.

| The field says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **At least 8 characters** | Too short. MyCity may ask for more than 8 | Add characters until the mark goes |
| **At most 200 characters** | Too long | Use a shorter password |
| **Add a lowercase letter** | No small letter yet | Add one, such as `a` |
| **Add an uppercase letter** | No capital letter yet | Add one, such as `A` |
| **Add a number** | No number yet | Add one, such as `7` |
| **Add a symbol** | No symbol yet | Add one, such as `!` or `#` |
| **Contains your email name** | The password holds the first part of your email | Choose one without your name |
| **Found in a breach** | This password was leaked from another website | Choose a password you have never used anywhere |
| **Does not match** | The two passwords differ | Type the same password in both fields |
| **Enter it again** | **Confirm Password** is empty | Type the password a second time |

> **Important:** The link works once and expires after 72 hours. If it says **Link Expired**, press
> **Request New Link**, type your work email and press **Send Link**. An email titled *Reset your
> MyCity App password* arrives: carry on from step 3 of [Forgot your password](#start-forgot-password).
> If no email comes, ask People and Operations to send a new invitation.

If the page says **Link Not Checked**, the hub could not reach the server. Wait a minute and press
**Try Again**. If it keeps saying so, open the link from the email again. A link cut short or
changed on its way, as some mail programs do with long links, reads **Link Expired**.

### Sign in with your password {#start-password}

1. Open the hub. The sign-in screen greets you with **Good morning**, **Good afternoon** or
   **Good evening**.
2. Type your work email in **Email**, for example `ada@mycityapp.io`.
3. Type your password in **Password**.
4. Press **Sign In**. A message says **Signed in as** and your name. You land on Home, or on the
   screen you were opening.

A field that is wrong is marked as you leave it: **Enter your email**, **Needs an @**, **Check the
spelling** or **Enter your password**. Correct it and press **Sign In** again.

If the hub refuses, a red bar under **Sign In** says why. It starts with **Not signed in.**,
**Too many tries.**, **Account suspended.** or **Offline.**

| The red bar says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **That email and password do not match.** | The email or the password is wrong | Check the email's spelling, then type the password again. Use **Forgot Password** if you are not sure of it. If you have never chosen a password, use **Set Your Password** in your invitation email first |
| **Too many attempts.** | Several wrong passwords in a row | Wait the time it shows, then try again |
| **Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again.** | Many failed sign-ins came from this network in the last 15 minutes | Wait 15 minutes, then try again |
| **This account is suspended. Contact People.** | Your login is paused | Ask People and Operations |
| **This account is closed.** | Your login was closed when you left MyCity | Ask People and Operations if this is a mistake |
| **You appear to be offline. Check the connection and try again.** | No internet on this device | Reconnect, then press **Sign In** again |

### Enter a code from your phone {#start-two-step}

Some roles also need a code from an authenticator app on your phone. This is called two-step
sign-in.

1. After your password or your PIN, the screen asks you to **Enter Code**.
2. Open your authenticator app and find MyCity App.
3. Type the six digits under **Sign-In Code**. The hub opens when you type the last digit.
4. No phone with you? Press **Use Recovery Code** and type one of your ten recovery codes in
   **Recovery Code**, then press **Sign In**. Each code works once. **Use App Code** goes back to
   the app's code.
5. **Back** returns to the sign-in screen.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter 6 digits** | The code is not six digits | Type all six digits from the app |
| **Check the code** | A recovery code has ten letters and numbers | Type the whole code. Spaces and the dash do not matter |
| **That code is not right.** | The code is wrong, used already or too old | Wait for the next code in the app and type it |
| **This request has expired. Start again.** | More than 5 minutes passed since your password | Press **Back** and sign in again |
| **This request was already used. Start again.** | This step was already finished, perhaps in another tab | Press **Back** and sign in again |
| **Too many attempts.** | Several wrong codes in a row | Wait the time it shows, then try again |

If the screen says **Set Up Two-Step**, your role needs it and you have not set it up yet:

1. Open your authenticator app and scan the square code on the screen. Or type the key printed
   under it.
2. Type the six digits the app shows in **Code From App**. Two-step turns on when you type the last
   digit, or press **Turn On**.
3. The screen shows ten **Recovery Codes**. Press **Copy Codes**. A message says **Recovery codes
   copied**. If it says **Copy them by hand**, write them down.
4. Keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone, then press **Continue**.

If you take longer than 15 minutes, the sign-in screen says **This request has expired. Start
again.** Sign in again to start afresh. **Back** leaves the set-up without turning anything on. A
wrong code says **That code is not right.** **Two-step sign-in is already on. Sign in again.**
means it was turned on meanwhile: press **Back** and sign in with a code.

> **Tip:** You choose how often this browser asks for a code on
> [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security): every sign-in, once a day or once a month.

### Sign in with your PIN or a passkey {#start-pin-passkey}

On the browser you last signed in on, you can sign in faster.

- **Use PIN** and **Use Passkey** appear under **Sign In** while your email is in **Email**. They
  need a PIN or a passkey set up on [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security).
- **Use PIN** opens **Enter PIN**. Type your four digits in **PIN**; the hub signs in at the fourth.
  Two-step may then ask for a code.
- **Use Passkey** asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a password. Closing that
  window does nothing; press it again to try again.
- **Use Password** takes you back to the password at any time.

After 10 wrong PINs in a row, or 20 in 30 days, your PIN switches off. The screen goes back to your
password.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter 4 digits** | The PIN is not four digits | Type all four |
| **That PIN is not right.** | Wrong PIN. It may say how many tries are left | Type it again, or press **Use Password** |
| **Too many wrong PINs.** | Several wrong PINs in a row | Wait the time it shows, or press **Use Password** |
| **Too many wrong PINs, so your PIN is off. Use your password.** | Your PIN was switched off | Sign in with your password, then set a new PIN on [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security) |
| **Use your password to sign in on this device.** | No PIN works here | Sign in with your password |
| **No PIN is set up. Use your password.** | Your PIN was removed | Sign in with your password |
| **This passkey is not on any account here. Use your password.** | The passkey you chose was removed, or belongs to no login | Sign in with your password |
| **That passkey belongs to someone else.** | You picked another person's passkey on this device | Press **Use Passkey** again and pick your own |
| **Your passkey could not be checked. Try again.** | The check failed | Press **Use Passkey** again, or use your password |
| **Passkeys do not work on this address** | The browser refuses passkeys here | Use your password |

### Forgot your password {#start-forgot-password}

1. On the sign-in screen, press **Forgot Password**. The screen says **Reset Password**.
2. Type your work email in **Email** and press **Send Link**. The screen says **Check Your Email**.
   Press **Sign In** to go back. **Back** returns without sending.
3. Open the email titled *Reset your MyCity App password* and press **Choose a New Password**.
4. Type a new password in **New Password** and again in **Confirm Password**. The field says what is
   missing, as in [Sign in for the first time](#start-first-time).
5. Press **Save Password**. The sign-in screen says **Password saved. Sign in with it now.** Sign in
   with the new password.

> **Important:** The reset link works once and expires after 30 minutes. Saving a new password
> signs you out on every device. If the link says **Link Expired**, press **Request New Link**.
> **Link Not Checked** means the hub could not reach the server: press **Try Again** in a minute.

No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder. A new link goes at most once every 2 minutes,
and never to a suspended or closed login. **Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again.** means
several requests came from this network: wait 15 minutes.

### Confirm it's you {#start-confirm-you}

Changing how you sign in, and some settings, ask you to prove it is you if you have not signed in or
confirmed in the last 15 minutes.

1. The window **Confirm It's You** opens.
2. If you have a PIN or a passkey, choose under **Confirm With**: **Password**, **PIN** or
   **Passkey**.
3. Type your password, or your four digits, and press **Confirm**. With **Passkey**, press **Use
   Passkey**.
4. The change you asked for then goes ahead. **Cancel** stops it.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Enter your password** | Type your password |
| **Not your password** | Type your current password again |
| **Enter 4 digits** | Type all four digits of your PIN |
| **Not the right PIN** | Type your PIN again, or choose **Password** |
| **Too many wrong PINs.** | Wait the time it shows, or choose **Password** |
| **Use one of your own passkeys.** | Pick your own passkey when the device asks |
| **Your passkey could not be checked. Try again.** | Press **Use Passkey** again, or choose **Password** |
| **Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again.** | More than ten tries in 15 minutes. Wait a few minutes |

## When the Screen Locks {#start-lock icon=lock}

The hub locks itself to keep your work safe. Your work stays underneath, and nothing you were
typing is lost.

- **Session Locked**, *Locked while you were away*: no key or tap for a while (30 minutes, unless
  MyCity changed it).
- **Session Ended**, *Your session reached its time limit*: 2 hours from sign-in, unless MyCity
  changed it.
- **Signed Out**, *This session was ended*: ended somewhere else, for example by a new password,
  **Sign Out Others** or People.

![The lock screen](shot:start-lock)

1. If you have a PIN, the lock screen asks for it first. Type your four digits; it unlocks at the
   fourth.
2. Otherwise type your password in **Password** and press **Unlock**. **Use Password** and
   **Use PIN** switch between them.
3. Or press **Use Passkey** and use your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
4. If it asks to **Enter Code**, type the six digits from your authenticator app. **Use Recovery
   Code** takes a recovery code instead. **Back** returns to your PIN or password.
5. A message says **Welcome back**, and you carry on where you were.
6. Press **Sign Out** instead if you are done for the day.

> **Important:** **Sign Out** loses anything you had not saved. Unlock first, save, then sign out.

| The lock screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **That email and password do not match.** | Wrong password. After a new password, the old one stops working | Type your current password |
| **That PIN is not right.** | Wrong PIN | Type it again, or press **Use Password** |
| **Too many wrong PINs, so your PIN is off. Use your password.** | Your PIN was switched off | Type your password. Set a new PIN on My Security later |
| **Your role now needs two-step sign-in. Sign out, then sign in to set it up.** | Your role now requires an authenticator app | Press **Sign Out**, then sign in and set it up as in [Enter a code from your phone](#start-two-step) |
| **This account is suspended. Contact People.** | Your login was paused while you were away | Press **Sign Out** and ask People and Operations |
| **This request has expired. Start again.** | More than 5 minutes passed at **Enter Code**; the lock screen goes back to its first step | Unlock again with your PIN, password or passkey |
| **That passkey belongs to someone else.** | You picked another person's passkey | Press **Use Passkey** again and pick your own |

## Finding Your Way {#start-layout icon=grid}

The hub is laid out differently on a computer and on a phone, so each feels natural. Everything
you can do on one you can do on the other.

### On a computer {#start-layout-desktop}

![The hub on a computer](shot:start-desktop)

1. The dark menu on the left lists the modules you can open: Home, My Workspace and Employee
   Self-Service first, then the rest grouped under Work, People, Business and System. Press one
   to open it.
2. Each module's screens are tabs along the top of the page. Press a tab to open that screen.
3. A number beside a module counts what waits for you there. It turns red when something urgent
   waits.
4. Each tab of the module shows its share of that number, so you see which screen it waits on (see
   [See where something waits](#start-where-it-waits)).
5. The MyCity App logo at the top of the menu goes back to Home.
6. **Collapse Menu** at the top left makes the menu narrow, to give the page more room.
   **Expand Menu** widens it again.
7. **Refresh** at the top left reloads the numbers on the screen, keeping your place. Hold it to
   reload the whole app.
8. In the installed app, **Back** and **Forward** sit beside **Refresh**. Hold either, or the logo,
   to see your **Trail**: the last places you opened.
9. **Search Anything** at the top finds any screen, action or record (see
   [Search](#start-search)).
10. {needs="ess view"} **Clock In** at the top right opens the clock (see
    [Clock in and out](#ess-attendance-clock)).
11. The bell, **Notifications**, opens your notifications.
12. Your name at the foot of the menu opens your account menu: **My Profile**, **My Security**,
    **User Guide**, the theme and density, **Text Size**, **Install App** and **Sign Out**.

### On a phone {#start-layout-phone}

![The hub on a phone](shot:start-phone)

1. The bar at the bottom opens **Home**, **Workspace**, **Community**, **Self-Service** and
   **More**.
2. **More** opens a sheet: your name with **Dark Theme**, **Compact Density**, **Text Size** and
   **Settings** (**My Security** when you do not look after Settings), a **Search** bar, every
   other module as a tile, **Install App** and **Sign Out**. Tiles use short names: **Knowledge**
   is the Knowledge Hub, **Assistant** is Praxis AI.
3. The module's screens are tabs under its name. Swipe them sideways to see them all. A number on
   a tab counts what waits for you on that screen.
4. The icons at the top right are **Search**, **Notifications** and **Your Account**, your picture,
   which opens More too. The MyCity App logo at the top left goes Home.
5. Press **Quick Actions**, the round button with a plus at the bottom right. It holds the screen's
   own action, such as *Request Leave*, the clock, and **Help**, which opens this guide at the
   screen you are on. It slides away while you scroll down; scroll up to bring it back.
6. {needs="praxis_ai view"} **Praxis AI** in Quick Actions asks a question from any screen
   ([Ask from any screen on a phone](#praxis-ask-anywhere)).
7. **Hide Here** tucks the round button away on this page. It comes back when you return to the
   page.
8. Your phone's own Back gesture goes back a screen and closes any sheet first.

> **Tip:** Pull the page down from the top to refresh it. Keep pulling past the second notch to
> reload the whole app. In the installed app, hold the logo to see your **Trail** of recent places.

### Sign out {#start-sign-out}

1. On a computer, press your name at the foot of the menu, then **Sign Out**.
2. On a phone, press **More** or your picture, then **Sign Out** at the bottom.
3. Every tab of the hub on this device signs out with it. Save your work first.

> **Tip:** On a shared device, always sign out. **Sign Out Others** on
> [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security) signs out your other devices.

### Save a file you download {#start-downloads}

Files you download, such as an export or a PDF, are saved on this device. On an iPhone or iPad with
the hub on the Home Screen, the file opens the share sheet: choose where to keep it. If a message
says **Your file is ready**, press **Save File**.

## When a Screen Cannot Open {#start-states icon=alert}

Some screens show a message instead of their content. None of them means your login is broken.

### No connection {#start-offline}

If the internet drops, the page says **No Connection**, *Reconnect to see live data.* The menu still
works. Press **Try Again** once you are back online.

### A new version is ready {#start-new-version}

1. When MyCity updates the hub, a bar at the foot of [Home](/home) says **A new version is ready**.
   It shows on Home only, so open Home to see it.
2. Save what you are typing, then press **Reload**. The hub restarts on the same screen.

### Coming Soon {#start-coming-soon}

Some modules in the menu are not built yet. Their screens show a **Coming Soon** card instead.

- The pill names the milestone and the date it is due, such as *Milestone 3*.
- *Opens with* names the milestone that delivers it.
- The lines under it say what the screen will do.

There is nothing to press. The screen opens by itself once it is delivered. Staging may open a
screen before the live hub does. Until then, [Search](#start-search) does not offer the screen or
its records, and Home shows no card for it.

### A screen that does not load {#start-screen-errors}

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This did not load** | The server did not answer | Press **Try Again**. If it fails again, tell IT the **Reference** shown |
| **This screen hit a problem** | Something broke while drawing this screen | Press **Try Again**. The rest of the hub still works |
| **You are offline** | No internet on this device | Reconnect, then press **Try Again** |
| **You do not have access to this** | Your role cannot open this record | Ask the person who sent it, or your manager |
| **This was not found** | The record was removed, or the link is wrong | Check the link, or search for the record |

When a press does not go through because the hub could not be reached, a message says so and ends
in *Try again*, such as **Not saved. Try again.** Check your connection and press the button again.
What you typed stays.

### A link you cannot open {#start-no-access}

- A link to a module your role does not open takes you to Home instead. A link to a tab you cannot
  open takes you to the module's first tab.
- A record you may not see says **You do not have access to this**.
- A notification about something you may not open is marked read when you press it, and nothing
  opens.
- In this guide, a screen you cannot open is plain text, not a link.

### Unsaved changes {#start-unsaved}

1. If you close a form, open another screen or reload the app with changes not saved, the hub asks
   **Discard Changes**.
2. Press **Keep Editing** to go back and save, or **Discard Changes** to leave without saving.

## Search {#start-search icon=srch}

Search finds every screen, action and record you are allowed to open, from anywhere in the hub.

![Search, open](shot:start-search)

1. Press Ctrl and K together (⌘ and K on a Mac), or press **Search Anything** at the top of a
   computer screen, or **Search** on a phone, or the **Search** bar in **More**.
2. Type what you are looking for: a person's name, a screen such as `leave`, or something to do
   such as `change password`.
3. Results come in groups: **Waiting on You** first when anything waits, **Screens**, **Actions**,
   then records, and **User Guide**. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or press a result.
4. To look in one kind of record only, start with its word: `guide leave` finds parts of this guide
   about leave.
5. {needs="people view"} `staff ada` finds employees called Ada, in the **Employees** group.
6. {needs="people view"} A person's name finds the person first, then at most three of their
   **Attendance Days**, newest first, each with its status and date, such as
   *Present · 26/10/2026*. Press a day to open it on [People · Attendance](/people?tab=attendance).
   The last row, such as *All Days of Ada Obi*, opens all of their days there. Arrow keys move
   through the results and Enter opens the one chosen.
7. Press Escape to close search. On a phone, press **Close Search**, the cross beside the line, or
   your phone's Back.

With nothing typed, search lists what is waiting on you first, then every screen and action you
can open. **Nothing matches** means no result you may open has those words. Try fewer words; one
typing slip is forgiven.

Some actions happen at once, wherever you are: **Mark All Read**, **Notification Settings**,
**Turn On Notifications**, **Send Test Notification**, **Use Dark Theme**, **Use Light Theme**,
**Change Density**, **Change Text Size**, **Install App**, **Refresh Screen**, **Reload App** and
**Sign Out**. A message confirms some of them, such as **All notifications read** or **Test sent**.

| Search says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications are blocked here** | This browser refuses notifications for the hub | Allow them in the browser's site settings, then try again |
| **Install the app first** | An iPhone or iPad only notifies an installed app | [Install the app](#start-install), open it from the Home Screen, then turn notifications on |
| **Not available on this device** | This browser cannot receive notifications | Use another browser or device |
| **Turn on notifications on this device first.** | No device of yours has notifications on | Choose **Turn On Notifications** first |
| **Could not send the test** | The test did not go | Try again in a minute |

## Notifications {#start-notifications icon=bell}

The hub tells you when something needs you: a request to approve, a task, a message.

![The notifications panel](shot:start-notifications)

1. Press the bell, **Notifications**. A dot on the bell means something is unread.
2. Press a notification to open what it is about. It is marked read. If it is about something you
   may not open, it is only marked read, and a message says **You cannot open this record**.
3. Choose **Unread** to see only what you have not read yet, **All** to see everything. They are
   grouped **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Earlier**.
4. To mark one read without opening it, point at it and press **Mark Read** on a computer, or swipe
   it left on a phone.
5. **Mark All Read** marks everything read at once. **Not marked read. Try again.** means it did
   not reach the hub.
6. **Notification Settings**, the cog, chooses how you are told (see
   [Choose how you are told](#start-notification-settings)).

On a phone the notifications fill the screen; press Back to close them, pull down to refresh.
**All Caught Up** means nothing is unread; **Nothing Yet** means you have none. If the pane says
**Notifications are off on this device**, press **Turn On** to get them here.

An urgent notice stays in a red bar under the top bar of every screen. Press **Open** to see it, or
**Acknowledge** on an urgent announcement. The bar goes once you do, on every device; if it cannot
reach the hub, a message says why (**Not acknowledged. Try again.** when offline). Until then it
alerts you again every 30 minutes of the working day. With several, the bar says *1 of 3*: press
**Previous Notice** or **Next Notice**, or swipe the bar sideways on a phone.

While the hub is open, a new notification drops in at the top for a few seconds. Press it to open
it, or **Dismiss** to close it. With notifications turned on, your phone or computer also shows it
like any app's. Press it to open the record; the hub asks you to sign in first if needed. **Mark
Read** on it marks it read without opening the hub.

> **Tip:** To get notifications on this phone or computer, press **Turn On** when the bar at the foot
> of Home says **Get notified of new mail and approvals**, and allow it when the device asks. A message says
> **Notifications are on**. **Not Now** hides the offer for 14 days. On an iPhone or iPad,
> [install the app](#start-install) first. **Notifications are blocked here** means the browser
> refuses: see [Choose how you are told](#start-notification-settings). **Notifications not turned
> on** means you closed the browser's question without allowing it; **Not available on this
> device** means the hub cannot send to this device.

### See where something waits {#start-where-it-waits}

![A tab's count and a request with something new](shot:start-where-it-waits)

1. A number beside a module in the menu counts the notifications waiting for you there.
2. Open the module. Each of its tabs shows how many of them wait on that screen.
3. Open that tab. Its switches, filters and board columns show their share too, and the screen
   opens on the one that holds them.
4. Each record with something new for you has a yellow dot and a soft tint.
5. Open the record. Its dot goes at once, and the numbers on the tab, the menu and the bell drop
   with it, on your other devices too.

> **Tip:** A number turns red while something urgent waits there. A notice about something the list
> does not show, such as a document, still counts on the tab: open it from the bell.

### Choose how you are told {#start-notification-settings}

![Notification Settings](shot:start-notification-settings)

1. Press the bell, then **Notification Settings**, the cog. On a phone it opens as a page.
2. **This Device** says whether this phone or computer gets notifications: **On**, **Off**,
   **Blocked**, **Install First**, **Not Supported** or **Not Available**.
3. Press **Turn On** and allow it when the browser asks. A message says **Notifications are on**.
   **Send Test** sends one to this device (**Test sent**). **Turn Off** stops them here
   (**Notifications are off here**).
4. **Blocked** means the browser refuses: point at **Allow in Browser** for how to allow it.
   **Install First** (iPhone or iPad) means press **Install App** first.
5. **Sound in App** plays a soft chime for new mail, messages and approvals while the hub is open.
   On Android it is off at first, and the phone only vibrates.
6. **Other Devices** lists your other phones and computers with notifications on. **Remove Device**
   stops them there. A message says **Device removed**, or why it was not (a device already
   removed elsewhere leaves the list).
7. Under **Notice Categories**, switch **In App**, **Email** and **Push** for each kind of notice. On
   a phone, tap a category to see its three switches. The Security row, with a padlock, is
   **Always on** and cannot be turned off.
8. Under **Quiet Hours and Digest**, switch on **Quiet Hours** and set **From** and **To**. Pushes
   wait until they end; urgent notices and security alerts still come.
9. **Daily Digest**, where MyCity offers it, emails what you have not read each morning at the time
   it shows. Switch it off if you do not want it.

| The message says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick two different times** | **From** and **To** are the same | Choose a different **To** |
| **Could not save that** | The switch did not save | Check your connection and press it again |
| **Turn on notifications on this device first.** | This device has no notifications on at the hub | Press **Turn Off**, then **Turn On**, then try again |
| **Could not send the test** | The connection failed | Check your connection and press **Send Test** again |
| **Notifications are blocked here** | The browser refuses notifications | Allow them in the browser's site settings |

## Your Display and the App {#start-display icon=sun}

These choices are yours and are kept on this device only.

- **Dark Theme** or **Light Theme** switches the colours.
- **Compact Density** fits more on the screen; **Comfortable Density** gives everything more room.
- **Text Size** opens a window: pick **Small**, **Default**, **Large** or **Largest**. It applies
  at once, and a message says **Text size:** and the size.

On a computer they are in your account menu. On a phone they are the icons beside your name in
**More**. There, **Settings** opens My Security, or Settings · Appearance if you manage Settings.

![Theme, density and text size](shot:start-display)

### Install the app {#start-install}

The hub installs like an app, with its own icon, and opens faster.

1. Press **Install App** in your account menu (computer) or in **More** (phone). From your second
   visit, a bar on [Home](/home) also offers **Install MyCity App on this device**: press
   **Install App** there.
2. Confirm in your browser's own window.
3. On an iPhone or iPad, the window **Install MyCity App** shows how: tap Share in Safari's toolbar,
   choose Add to Home Screen, then press **Done**.

> **Tip:** **Install App** only shows where this browser can install the hub and it is not installed
> yet. **Not Now** hides the offer for 30 days. Once the hub is installed, the bar never shows again.
> The bar shows on Home only, so it never covers another screen.

## Using This Guide {#start-user-guide screen=knowledge/user-guide icon=book}

This guide shows you how to use every screen of the hub. It lives in the Knowledge Hub, on the
[User Guide](/knowledge?tab=user-guide) tab. You only see the parts about what you can open, so two
colleagues may see different chapters.

![The User Guide](shot:start-user-guide)

### Find what you need {#start-user-guide-find}

1. Open [User Guide](/knowledge?tab=user-guide) in the Knowledge Hub.
2. Choose **Quick Start** for short illustrated cards, or **Full Guide** for every chapter in full.
   Quick Start groups its cards by role: **Everyone**, **Line Managers**, **People & HR** and
   **Administrators**. You see the groups for what you can do.
3. In the Full Guide, pick a section from the list on the left. On a phone or a narrow window, the
   sections are a row above the text: swipe it sideways. The chapter's name is above the section's
   title.
4. To find a topic, use the search box at the top of the guide ([Search the guide](#start-user-guide-search)).
   From anywhere else, open search, type `guide` and a few words, such as `guide add employee`, and
   choose the result.

### Search the guide {#start-user-guide-search}

Describe what you need in your own words. The search finds the parts of the guide that mean the
same, even when you use other words or make a typing slip.

![Searching the guide](shot:start-user-guide-search)

1. Open [User Guide](/knowledge?tab=user-guide) in the Knowledge Hub.
2. Type in **Search the Guide** at the top, in your own words, such as *I cannot get into my account*.
3. The results appear as you type, the best first. Each shows its chapter, the words it found marked
   in yellow and, when one step answers it, that step's number.
4. Press a result. The guide opens at it, with the step marked.
5. The words stay in the box. Change them to see the results again.
6. To speak instead, press the microphone beside the box, say what you need, then press
   **Stop Recording**. Your words appear in the box and are searched. The recording is not kept.

> **Tip:** You only find the parts of the guide you can read, so two colleagues may see different
> results for the same words.

### Ask the guide a question {#start-user-guide-answer}

![An answer from the guide](shot:start-user-guide-answer)

1. Type your question in **Search the Guide**, such as *How do I request leave?*
2. Press **Get Answer** above the results, or press Enter in the box.
3. The **Answer** card shows a short answer written from the parts of the guide you can read. The
   small number after each sentence is its source.
4. Press a source under the answer to open the guide at it.

> **Important:** The answer is written by AI from the guide. Check it against the steps it names
> before you act on it.

When the guide has no answer, the card says so, and the results below it may still help. When
**Get Answer** does not show, answers are switched off: the results still work.

### Do a step and come back {#start-user-guide-return}

1. When a step names a screen as an underlined link, press it. The screen opens.
2. Do what the step says. A small bar at the foot of the screen names the step you came from.
3. Press **Back to Guide** on that bar. The guide opens at the same step, marked with a coloured
   band.
4. Press **Close** on the bar if you do not need to go back.

> **Tip:** A link to another part of this guide opens it here, with no bar.

> **Tip:** At the top of a section, **Open Screen** opens the screen the section is about, with the
> same way back.

### Help from any screen {#start-user-guide-help}

- On a phone, press **Quick Actions**, the round button with a plus at the bottom right, then
  **Help**. The guide opens at the section about the screen you were on. A screen with no section
  yet opens the guide at its start.
- On a computer, press your name at the foot of the menu, then **User Guide**. It opens at the same
  place.
- Pictures carry numbered marks. The list under each picture says what each number points at.
  Press a picture to see it larger in the **Picture** window. On a phone it shows the computer's
  picture.
