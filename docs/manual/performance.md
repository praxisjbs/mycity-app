Performance holds MyCity's goals and how each person's work is scored. It follows MyCity's own
system: the goals are planned each quarter, each person has a scorecard, and the work is scored
every month.

- **Goals** holds the organisational goals, and the company's and each function's objectives with
  their key results.
- **Scorecards** holds each person's scorecard for the quarter, and the month's **Commitments**.
- **KPIs** holds each function's operational measures and their monthly results.
- **Reviews** holds each month's review of every scorecard.
- **Analytics** shows the quarter's figures. It is for people who manage others, and for the CEO.

Your own scorecard, check-in and reviews are on
[Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance) (see
[My Performance](#performance-my)).

Scores run from 0 to 1. A key result scoring 0.7 or more is **Good**, 0.4 to 0.7 is **Watch**,
and under 0.4 is **Recovery**, which needs a recovery plan. A scorecard's rating is **Exceeds**
(0.90 or more), **Meets** (0.75 or more), **Partly Meets** (0.50 or more) or **Below**.

## Goals {#performance-goals screen=performance/goals icon=target}

Goals is the quarter's plan, from the year's organisational goals down to each function's key
results. Everyone can read all of it.

![Performance, Goals](shot:performance-goals)

### Read the goals {#performance-goals-read}

1. Open [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals). The quarter and its dates show under the
   title on a computer.
2. Choose the **Quarter**, and the **Function**: **All Functions**, **Company** or one function.
3. The tiles show the **Company Score**, and how many key results are **On Track**, **In Recovery**
   or **Awaiting Report**.
4. **Organisational Goals** lists the year's goals: **Goal ID**, **Strategic Theme**, **Goal** and
   **Success Measure**. Press one to see its targets for each quarter.
5. Under it, each objective is a card with its code, its title and its score. Its table lists
   each key result's **Code**, **Owner**, **Finish**, **Status** and **Score**.
6. Press a key result to open its panel (see [Open a key result](#performance-key-result)).

| A key result's status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **On Track** | Going to plan this month |
| **At Risk** | Behind, but can still be met |
| **Off Track** | Will not be met without a change |
| **Done** | Delivered |
| **Missed** | Not delivered by its date |
| **Not Started** | Work has not begun |
| **Awaiting Report** | The head has not reported this month yet |
| **TBC** | Its target is not confirmed yet; it is set before the quarter's lock date |

### Open a key result {#performance-key-result}

![A key result's panel](shot:performance-key-result)

The panel shows what the key result is and how this month went:

- **Key Result**, **Type**, **Owner**, **Start**, **Finish** and **Baseline**.
- **Target**, with *Was* and the old target after a change. **Change Waiting** shows a new target
  the CEO has not decided yet.
- **Feeds** names the company key results it feeds, **Fed By** the ones that feed it, and **Rolls
  Up To** the objective and goal above it.
- **This Month**, with its pill (**Good**, **Watch**, **Recovery** or **Not Scored**): **Status**,
  **Actual**, **Source**, **Update**, the **Proposed** score and the **Confirmed** score.
- **History** lists the months before, and **Initiatives** the tasks set for its owner.

### Record an organisational goal {#performance-goals-org needs="performance create, cap goals.organisation"}

The CEO records the year's organisational goals.

1. Press **New Goal** at the top right of [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Choose **Organisational Goal** in **What**.
3. Type the **Goal**. Add the **Goal ID**, **Strategic Theme**, **Success Measure** and
   **Baseline** if you have them.
4. Press **Record Goal**. The hub says **Goal recorded**.
5. To change one, press it under **Organisational Goals**, then **Edit Goal**. Change the
   **Goal**, **Description** or a quarter's target, and press **Save Goal**.
6. To remove one, press **Delete Goal** and confirm. A goal a company objective serves cannot be
   deleted: the hub says **A company objective serves this goal**.

### Set objectives and key results {#performance-goals-set needs="performance create, cap goals.function"}

The CEO and Management set the company's objectives; each function's head sets the function's.
They can be added and changed while the plan is a draft, or after it comes back.

1. Press **New Goal** on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals) and choose **Objective** in
   **What**.
2. Pick the **Function**, type the **Objective**, and pick the **Organisational Goal** it serves.
   Press **Set Objective**.
3. For a key result, press **New Goal** again and choose **Key Result**.
4. Pick its **Objective**, and type the **Key Result**, such as `Grow monthly active users to
   64,000`.
5. Choose its **Measure**: **Numeric**, **Dated** or **Yes or No**.
6. Fill in the **Target** as a number, and **Target in Words** as people say it, such as
   `64,000 users`. Tick **Target to be confirmed** if it is not settled yet.
7. Add the **Unit**, the **Finish** date and the **Owner**. For a function's key result, pick the
   company key results it **Feeds**.
8. Press **More** for **Co-Owner**, **Direction** (**Higher Is Better** or **Lower Is Better**),
   **Baseline**, **Start**, **Type** and **Owner Note**. A company key result also has
   **Scored From** here.
9. Press **Set Key Result**. The hub gives it its code, such as BM-KR1.3.

To change an objective, press the three dots on its card and choose **Edit Objective** or
**Delete Objective**. To change a key result, open it and press **Edit Key Result** or **Delete
Key Result**. A delete asks you to confirm, naming what goes.

An objective holds at most four key results. A fifth is refused with **An objective holds at
most four key results**.

### Send the quarter plan {#performance-plan-send needs="performance create, cap goals.function"}

Each function sends one plan a quarter: its objectives, key results, KPIs and every scorecard in
it. It is approved by the head's line manager, then Operations and People, then the CEO. Only the
CEO's approval makes it binding.

1. On [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals), pick your function in **Function**.
2. The **Plan Approval** card shows the plan's status, its **Objectives** and **Scorecards**, and
   anything that stops it in a red bar.
3. Fix each problem the card names, such as **Add an objective first**.
4. Press **Send Plan**. The hub says **Plan sent for approval**.
5. If it comes back, the card reads **Returned** with the reason. Change what was asked and press
   **Send Plan** again.

| The plan reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being prepared |
| **Awaiting Approval** | Sent, with one of its approvers |
| **Returned** | An approver asked for changes |
| **Binding** | Approved by the CEO; the quarter runs on it |

Once a plan is binding, each person and their reviewer is asked to agree the scorecard.

### Decide a plan, a target change or a scorecard {#performance-decide needs="performance approve"}

What waits for you sits in **Waiting On You** near the top of
[Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals). It shows only when something waits.

![A quarter plan waiting for a decision](shot:performance-plan-approval)

1. Read the **Request** (a function's plan, a target change or a scorecard), who it is **From**,
   its **Stage** and how long it has been **Waiting Since**.
2. Press the row. Its sheet opens.
3. A **Quarter Plan** shows its function, quarter, stage, who sent it, its objectives, key results
   and scorecards, and the chain of approvers.
4. Press **Approve Plan**. The hub says it was sent on to the next approver, or **Plan approved**
   at the last stage.
5. Or press **Request Changes**, type the **Reason** and press **Request Changes**. It goes back to
   the head with your reason.
6. {needs="ceo"} A **Target Change** shows the **Old Target**, **New Target**, **From Month** and
   **Reason**. Press **Approve Change**, or **Refuse Change** with a reason.
7. A scorecard opens its own sheet. Press **Approve Scorecard**, or **Request Changes** with a
   reason.

### Report the month {#performance-report needs="performance edit, cap goals.function"}

By the third weekday of each month, each function's head reports how its key results did.

1. Press **Report Month** at the top of [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Pick the **Function** and the **Month**. It starts on the month just ended.
3. For each key result you report, choose its **Status** and fill in the **Actual** and a short
   **Update**.
4. If the score falls under 0.4, or the status is **At Risk**, **Off Track** or **Missed**, name
   the **Recovery Owner** and the **Recovery Date**.
5. Press **Report Month**. The hub says **Month reported**. Only the key results you changed are
   sent.

| The hub says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Change a key result first** | Fill in at least one key result |
| **Nothing To Report** | The function has no binding key results this quarter |
| **A key result in Recovery needs an owner and date** | Name the **Recovery Owner** and **Recovery Date** |
| **Report a month once the plan is binding** | Wait for the CEO to approve the plan |
| **Only the function's head reports its month** | Ask the head to report it |

### Confirm a score {#performance-confirm needs="cap goals.confirm"}

The CEO confirms each month's key result scores at the monthly review.

1. Open the key result on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Check its **Proposed** score and press **Confirm Score**. The hub says **Score confirmed**.

### Change a target {#performance-target needs="performance edit"}

A binding target changes only with the CEO's approval. The old target stays beside the new one,
and scores already given do not change.

1. Open the key result on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals). Its owner and the
   function's head see the buttons.
2. Before the quarter's lock date, a **TBC** target is set in place: press **Set Target**, fill
   in **Target** and **Target in Words**, and press **Set Target**.
3. After it, press **Change Target**. Fill in the **New Target**, the **From Month** it applies
   from, and the **Reason**.
4. Press **Send Change**. The hub says **Sent to the CEO**, and the key result shows **Change
   Waiting** until the CEO decides.

Only one change to a target waits at a time. A second is refused with **A change to this target
is already waiting for a decision**.

### Set up a quarter {#performance-quarter needs="performance create, cap performance.manage"}

Operations and People set up each quarter before it starts, with its scoring rules. A new quarter
starts with a copy of the last quarter's rules.

1. On [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals), press **Manage Quarters**, then **Set Up
   Quarter**.
2. On **Quarter**, check **Starts**; **Ends** follows by itself. Add the **Name** (Q1 2027 unless
   you type another), the **Lock Date** for TBC targets, and the **Status**: **Planning** or
   **Active**. Press **Next**.
3. On **Bands**, check **Good From** and **Watch From** for key results, and **Exceeds From**,
   **Meets From** and **Partly Meets From** for ratings. Press **Next**.
4. On **Key Result Scoring**, check **Done On Time**, **Grace Days**, **Done Late** and **Partly
   Done**. Press **Next**.
5. On **Line Scoring**, check **Milestone On Time**, **Grace Business Days**, **Milestone Late**,
   **Standard Met**, **Standard Near Miss** and **Near Miss Points**. Press **Next**.
6. On **Section Weights**, check **Results**, **KPIs** and **Accountability**.
7. Press **Set Up Quarter**. **Back** returns to a step before.

To change the quarter on screen, press **Manage Quarters**, then **Edit Quarter**, and press **Save
Quarter** at the end. A quarter only moves forward, from **Planning** to **Active** to **Closed**.

## Scorecards {#performance-scorecards screen=performance/targets icon=sheet}

Each person has one scorecard a quarter, in three sections: **Results** (60%), **KPIs** (25%) and
**Accountability** (15%). Each section holds weighted lines. You see your own scorecard, and those
of the people you manage.

![Performance, Scorecards](shot:performance-scorecards)

### Read the scorecards {#performance-scorecards-read}

1. Open [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets).
2. The tiles count the **Scorecards**, how many are **Agreed**, **Awaiting Agreement**, and
   **Below Meets**.
3. Narrow the list with **Quarter**, **Department** and **Status**, or type in **Search People**.
4. Each row shows the **Employee**, **Department**, **Reviewer**, **Status**, **Score to Date**
   and **Rating**.
5. Press a row to open the scorecard.

| A scorecard reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Its lines are being written |
| **Awaiting Agreement** | Binding; the person or the reviewer has not agreed yet |
| **Agreed** | Both have agreed |
| **Closed** | The quarter or the person's time at MyCity has ended |

### Open a scorecard {#performance-scorecard}

![A scorecard](shot:performance-scorecard)

1. The top shows the **Employee**, their **Designation**, **Department**, **Reviewer**, **Dotted
   Line** and **Scope**, then the **Status**, **Score to Date**, **Rating**, **Weights** and who
   has **Agreed**.
2. Each section lists its lines: the **Goal** or **KPI**, its **Target**, what it is **Linked
   To**, when it is **Due**, its **Weight**, each month's score and the **Score**.
3. **Auto** on a line means the hub scores it itself, such as on-time commitments. Your reviewer
   can change that score, with a reason.
4. **Contribution** shows a key result shared between people: their shares against its target,
   **OK** or **Rebalance**.
5. Press a line to open it on its own, with its **Target**, **Linked To**, **Due**, **Weight** and
   **Score**.

If a section's weights do not add up, a bar names it, such as *Results lines total 50, not 60*.

### Draft a scorecard {#performance-scorecard-new needs="performance create, manager"}

Managers draft the scorecards of the people who report to them, before the function's plan is
sent.

1. Press **New Scorecard** at the top right of
   [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets).
2. Pick the **Employee**.
3. Choose **Lines From**: **Last Quarter** to copy their lines, or **Start Blank**.
4. Press **Draft Scorecard**. The hub says **Scorecard drafted** and opens it.
5. Press **Edit Lines**. Each line is a card: pick its **Section**, type the **Goal**, and choose
   its **Measure**.
6. Fill in its **Target** and **Weight**, and link it to its **Key Result**, or its **KPI** for a
   KPI line. A milestone line also needs its **Due Date**.
7. Press **Add Line** for another, or **Remove Line** to take one away.
8. Press **Save Lines**. The hub says **Lines saved**.

Someone who joins after the plan is binding gets a scorecard too. Draft it the same way, then press
**Send Scorecard**. It goes through the plan's approvers on its own.

| The hub says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Your manager drafts your scorecard** | Nobody drafts their own |
| **They already have a scorecard this quarter** | Open the one they have |
| **This function's plan is already sent for the quarter** | Wait until it is binding, then draft and send it on its own |

### Agree a scorecard {#performance-scorecard-agree}

Once the plan is binding, the person and their reviewer each agree the scorecard.

1. Open the scorecard. As the person, you can also do this on
   [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance).
2. Press **Agree Scorecard**. The hub says **Scorecard agreed**.
3. If something is wrong, press **Disagree With Reason**, type the **Reason**, and press **Send
   Reason**. Your reviewer is told.

### Change a line's target {#performance-line-target needs="performance edit"}

The scorecard's reviewer and the function's head may ask to change a line's target. The person
whose scorecard it is cannot.

1. Open the scorecard and press the line.
2. Press **Change Target**. Fill in the **New Target**, the **From Month** and the **Reason**.
3. Press **Send Change**. The hub says **Sent to the CEO**. The line shows **Change Waiting** until
   the CEO decides.

### Change the reviewer {#performance-reviewer needs="performance edit, cap performance.manage"}

1. Open the scorecard and press **Change Reviewer**.
2. Pick the **Reviewer** and type the **Reason**.
3. Press **Change Reviewer**. The hub says **Reviewer changed**.

### The month's commitments {#performance-commitments}

The **Commitments** view lists each person's commitments for the month: the priority tasks they
marked on [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks) (see
[Commitments](#workspace-commitments)).

![The Commitments view](shot:performance-commitments)

1. On [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets), press **Commitments** at the top.
2. Choose the **Month**, **Department** and **State**, or type in **Search People**.
3. The tiles count the **Commitments**, those **On Time**, those **Late or Missed**, and those
   **Awaiting Approval**. On a phone they are one line of figures.
4. Each row shows the **Commitment**, the **Person**, its **Original Date**, the date it is **Due
   Now** and its **State**.
5. Press a row to open its task on My Workspace. Your browser's Back brings you to the same
   list.

### Approve a month of commitments {#performance-commitments-approve needs="performance approve"}

When someone sends you their commitments, their month waits for you in **Waiting On You** on the
Commitments view.

1. Open [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets) and press **Commitments**.
2. Read the **Person**, **Month** and how many **Commitments** were sent.
3. Press **Approve Month**. The hub says **Month approved**, and each commitment's date is fixed.
4. Or press **Request Changes**, type the **Reason** and press **Request Changes**. The month goes
   back to them.

### Export the month-end report {#performance-commitments-report needs="performance export"}

1. On the Commitments view, choose the **Month**, and a **Department** if you want only one.
2. Press **Export Report**. The hub makes the report and saves it to your device: a summary sheet
   and one sheet per function.

## KPIs {#performance-kpis screen=performance/kpis icon=chart}

KPIs are each function's standing operational measures, such as *Brand assets delivered on time*.
Each has a result every month: **Met**, **Not Met** or **N/A**.

![Performance, KPIs](shot:performance-kpis)

### Read the KPIs {#performance-kpis-read}

1. Open [Performance · KPIs](/performance?tab=kpis) and choose the **Function**.
2. The tiles count the **KPIs**, those **Met Last Month**, those **Not Met**, and those
   **Awaiting Actual**.
3. Each row shows the **KPI ID**, the **KPI**, its **Function**, **Target**, **Direction** and its
   **Latest** result with the figure.
4. Press a KPI to see its **Formula**, **Unit**, **Frequency**, **Data Source**, its **Months**
   and the **Lines Using It**.

**N/A** means there were no cases that month. It never counts as met.

### Add a KPI {#performance-kpi-new needs="performance create, cap goals.function"}

1. Press **New KPI** at the top right of [Performance · KPIs](/performance?tab=kpis).
2. Pick the **Function** and type the **KPI**, such as `Social response within 24 hours`.
3. Add the **Formula** and **Unit** if it has them.
4. Choose the **Direction**, fill in the **Target** or **Target Wording**, and choose the
   **Frequency**.
5. Name the **Data Source**, where the figure comes from, and press **Set KPI**.

### Record a month's result {#performance-kpi-actual needs="performance edit, cap goals.function"}

1. Open the KPI on [Performance · KPIs](/performance?tab=kpis) and press **Record Actual**.
2. Pick the **Month**, choose the **Result** (**Met**, **Not Met** or **N/A**), and fill in the
   **Actual** and a **Comment**.
3. Press **Record Actual**. The hub says **Actual recorded**.
4. To change a KPI's words, press **Edit KPI** and then **Save KPI**.

## Reviews {#performance-reviews screen=performance/reviews icon=ticks}

Every month each scorecard is reviewed: the person checks in first, their reviewer scores it, the
reviewer's own manager approves it, and Operations and People close it. You see your own reviews,
those of the people you manage, and any that wait on you.

![Performance, Reviews](shot:performance-reviews)

### Read the month's round {#performance-reviews-read}

1. Open [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews). The round and its closing date show
   under the title on a computer.
2. Choose the **Month**. Press **PIPs** for improvement plans, and **Reviews** to come back.
3. The tiles show **Check-Ins In**, such as 4 of 6, and how many are **With Reviewers**, **With
   HR** and **Closed**. On a phone they are one line.
4. **Waiting On You** lists the reviews that need you, with what to do **Next**: **Score**,
   **Give Input** or **Approve**.
5. Narrow the list with **Department**, **Stage** and **Kind** (**Monthly** or **Probation**), or
   type in **Search People**.
6. Each row shows the **Employee**, **Reviewer**, **Stage**, when it is **Due**, its **Score** and
   **Rating**. Press a row to open the review.

| A review's stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Checking In** | The person is filling in their check-in |
| **With Reviewer** | The reviewer is scoring it |
| **Returned** | Sent back to the reviewer with a reason |
| **Second Level** | With the reviewer's own manager |
| **With HR** | With Operations and People to close |
| **Closed** | Done: the person can read the final scores |
| **Withdrawn** | Ended without a score, with a note saying why |

### Open a review {#performance-review}

![A review](shot:performance-review)

1. The top shows the **Employee**, the **Month**, the **Reviewer**, the **Stage** and when it is
   **Due**, then the chain: **Check-In**, **Reviewer**, **Second Level** and **HR**.
2. **Lines** shows each line's **Target**, **Actual**, **Evidence**, the person's **Action**, and
   the **Proposed**, **Self** and **Final** scores.
3. **Not Assessable** on a line means no evidence was given, so it counts neither for nor against.
4. Under the lines: the month's tasks (**Done This Month**, **Ongoing**, **Next Month**), the
   attendance (**This Month**) and **Recognitions**.
5. Once scored: **Score This Month**, **Score to Date** and **Rating**, and the **Corrective
   Actions**.
6. Once closed, press **Download PDF** for the letterheaded copy.

A reviewer cannot open a review until the person has checked in. It reads **Waiting for the
check-in.** until then.

### Score a review {#performance-review-score needs="manager"}

1. Open [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews) and press the review under **Waiting On
   You**.
2. For each line, check the **Final Score**. It starts as the proposed score.
3. If you change it, type the **Reason**. Add a **Comment** if it helps.
4. For a line scored under 1, add a **Corrective Action**, its **Owner** and its **Due Date**. It
   becomes a task for the owner.
5. Press **Save Scores** as you go. The hub says **Scores saved**.
6. Press **Submit Review**. The hub says **Review submitted**, and it goes to your own manager.

Other buttons at the foot:

- **Ask For Input** asks the person's dotted-line manager for a comment before you score.
- **Return Check-In** sends the check-in back to the person: type the **Reason** and press
  **Return Check-In**.
- Once a check-in is late, **Score Without Check-In** lets you score without it. Type the
  **Reason** and press **Score Review**. The person is told, and their own lines read **Not
  Assessable**.

| The hub says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Score every line first** | Give each line a **Final Score** |
| **Add a corrective action to each missed line** | Fill in the action, owner and date on each line under its target |
| **The check-in is not due yet** | Wait for its due date before scoring without it |

### Give your input {#performance-review-input needs="manager"}

If you are someone's dotted-line manager, their review asks for your comment.

1. Open the review from **Waiting On You**. Its **Next** reads **Give Input**.
2. Type in **Your Input** and press **Give Input**. The hub says **Input given**, and the reviewer
   reads it before scoring.

### Approve or close a review {#performance-review-approve needs="performance approve"}

1. Open the review from **Waiting On You** on [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews).
2. Read the lines and the reviewer's scores.
3. At **Second Level**, press **Approve**. The hub says **Review approved**, and it goes to
   Operations and People.
4. {needs="cap performance.manage"} At **With HR**, press **Close Review**. The hub says **Review
   closed**; the person is told and can read the final scores.
5. To send it back to the reviewer, press **Request Changes**, type the **Reason** and press
   **Request Changes**.

### Conduct, improvement plans and probation {#performance-review-hr needs="performance edit, cap performance.manage"}

Operations and People handle these on an open or closed review.

1. **Mark Conduct** records a confirmed conduct breach. Choose **Breach Confirmed** or **No
   Breach**, type the **Note** and press **Mark Conduct**. A breach caps the rating at **Partly
   Meets**.
2. **Start PIP** appears on a closed review rated **Below**. Check **Starts On** and each line to
   improve, add a **Summary**, and press **Start PIP**.
3. Under **PIPs**, press a plan to read it. Press **Close Plan**, choose the **Outcome**
   (**Passed**, **Extended** or **Not Met**), type the **Note**, and press **Close Plan**.
   **Extended** asks for new **Ends On** and **Review On** dates.
4. A probation review shows **Confirm**, **Extend 3 Months** and **End Employment** at **With
   HR**. Press the outcome you decide.
5. **Withdraw Review** ends an open review without a score. Type the **Reason** and press
   **Withdraw Review**.

### Open or close a round {#performance-round needs="performance create, cap performance.manage"}

Each month's round opens by itself on the first weekday of the next month. You only open one by
hand when it started late.

1. Press **Open Round** at the top of [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews).
2. Pick the **Month** and press **Open Round**.
3. When the round is done, press **Close Round**.

A round already open is refused with **That month's round is already open**, and a month not over
yet with **The month has not ended**.

## Analytics {#performance-analytics screen=performance/analytics icon=chart}

Analytics shows the quarter's figures for the company, or one function. It is open to people who
manage others, and to the CEO.

![Performance, Analytics](shot:performance-analytics)

### Read the figures {#performance-analytics-read}

1. Open [Performance · Analytics](/performance?tab=analytics). Choose the **Quarter** and the
   **Function**.
2. The tiles show the **Company Score**, and the share of **Commitments On Time**, **Check-Ins On
   Time** and **Reviews Closed On Time**, each with what it is out of, such as 10 of 13.
3. **Scores by Month** shows the company's and each function's score for each month.
4. **Key Results by Status** counts the key results by their status, with those **In Recovery**.
5. **Rating Spread** counts the ratings of the latest closed round. Before one closes it reads
   **No Closed Round**.
6. **Commitments On Time** shows each function's commitments on time for each month.
7. **Self Against Final** shows how far people's own scores are above or below the final scores,
   on average. Above zero means people rate themselves higher. It is left off a phone.
8. **People** lists each scorecard's **Employee**, **Department**, **Reviewer**, **Score to Date**
   and **Rating**, highest score first. Type in **Search People** to find someone.
9. {needs="performance export"} Press **Export** to save the people list as a spreadsheet.

On a phone, **People** shows the first five. Press **View All** for everyone.

## My Performance {#performance-my screen=ess/my-performance icon=user}

My Performance is your own page: your scorecard, your monthly check-in, your commitments and your
reviews. It is a tab of Employee Self-Service.

![Employee Self-Service, My Performance](shot:performance-my)

### Read your performance {#performance-my-read}

1. Open [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance). The quarter and your
   reviewer show under the title on a computer.
2. A bar at the top says when something waits for you, such as *Your check-in is due 03/11/2026*,
   with its button.
3. The tiles show your **Score to Date**, your **Rating**, your check-in for **This Month**, your
   **Commitments On Time** and your **Agreement**.
4. **My Scorecard** lists your lines in their three sections, with each month's score.
5. **This Month's Check-In** shows its **Status** and when it is **Due**.
6. **Rolls Up To** shows how each of your lines leads up to the company's goals.
7. **My Commitments** lists this month's commitments with their **Original Date** and **State**.
   Press **Send Commitments** while some are not sent.
8. **My Reviews** lists your reviews by **Month**, with the **Score**, **Rating** and **Status**.

On a phone, each part is a card. Press a card to open it.

**Score to Date** is your scored lines, weighted. December's is your quarter's final score.

### Agree your scorecard {#performance-my-agree}

1. When your scorecard is ready, the bar says **Your scorecard waits for your agreement.** Press
   **Agree Scorecard** on it.
2. Read your lines, then press **Agree Scorecard**. The hub says **Scorecard agreed**.
3. If something is wrong, press **Disagree With Reason**, type the **Reason** and press **Send
   Reason**. Your reviewer is told.

### Do your monthly check-in {#performance-check-in}

Each month you report how your lines went, before your reviewer scores them. Your reviewer sees
nothing of it until you submit it.

![The monthly check-in](shot:performance-check-in)

1. Press **Start Check-In** on [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance).
   It shows the **Month**, your **Reviewer** and when it is **Due**.
2. For each of your lines, fill in the **Actual**. A milestone asks when it was **Delivered On**,
   and a yes or no line whether it was **Met**.
3. Choose your **Evidence**: **Source** to name a report, **Task** to pick one of your tasks,
   **Link** for a web address, or **File** to press **Add Evidence** and attach one.
4. Write what you will do about anything behind in **Action**. To speak it instead, press
   **Dictate**, talk, then press **Stop Recording**. Your words appear in the field.
5. Press **Tidy With AI** to have the wording tidied. Pick **Use Tidied** or **Keep Original**.
   Nothing changes until you pick.
6. The hub proposes each score from your actual. To give another, press **Change Score**, type
   **Your Score** and the **Reason**. Your reviewer sees both.
7. **Automatic Lines** are filled in by the hub, such as on-time commitments. You do not fill them.
8. Press **Save Draft** as you go. The hub says **Draft saved** and works out the proposed scores.
9. Press **Submit Check-In** when you are done. The hub says **Check-in submitted** and tells your
   reviewer.

A line with no evidence reads **Not Assessable**: it counts neither for nor against you.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No check-in is open.** | There is no round for you this month | Wait for the next round |
| **Your check-in is already sent** | You submitted it | Wait for your review |
| **Microphone not allowed** | The browser may not use your microphone | Allow it in the browser, or type instead |
| **Could not transcribe** | The recording could not be written down | Press **Dictate** again, or type |
| **Tidy is off** | The assistant is switched off | Keep your own words |

If your reviewer sent it back, a red bar at the top gives the reason. Change what was asked and
submit again.

### Read a closed review {#performance-my-review}

1. Under **My Reviews**, press a review.
2. Your own check-in shows from the start. The reviewer's scores, comments and corrective actions
   show once it is **Closed**.
3. Press **Download PDF** for the letterheaded copy.
