Performance holds MyCity's goals and how each person's work is scored. It follows MyCity's own
system: the goals are planned each quarter, each person has a scorecard, and the work is scored
every month.

- **Goals** holds the organisational goals, and the company's and each function's objectives with
  their key results.
- **Scorecards** holds each person's scorecard for the quarter, and the month's **Commitments**.
- **KPIs** holds each function's operational measures and their monthly results.
- **Reviews** holds each month's review of every scorecard.
- **Analytics** shows the quarter's figures. It is for people who manage others, Operations and
  People, and the CEO.

Your own scorecard, check-in and reviews are on
[Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance) (see
[My Performance](#performance-my)).

Scores run from 0 to 1. A key result scoring 0.7 or more is **Good**, 0.4 to 0.7 is **Watch**,
and under 0.4 is **Recovery**, which needs a recovery plan. A scorecard's rating is **Exceeds**
(0.90 or more), **Meets** (0.75 or more), **Partly Meets** (0.50 or more) or **Below**.

## Goals {#performance-goals screen=performance/goals icon=target}

Goals is the quarter's plan, from the year's organisational goals down to each function's key
results. Everyone can read all of it.

![Performance, Goals](shot:performance-goals)

### Read the goals {#performance-goals-read}

1. Open [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals). The quarter and its dates show under the
   title on a computer.
2. Choose the **Quarter**, and the **Function**: **All Functions**, **Company** or one function.
3. The tiles show the **Company Score**. **Scoring Good** counts key results scoring **Good** in
   their latest month. **In Recovery** counts those scoring **Recovery**. **Awaiting Report** counts those
   never reported.
4. **Organisational Goals** lists the year's goals: **Goal ID**, **Strategic Theme**, **Goal** and
   **Success Measure**. Press one to open it: its **Strategic Theme**, **Success Measure**,
   **Baseline**, **Description**, a target for each quarter, the **Objectives** serving it and its
   **Progress**.
5. Under it, each objective is a card with its code, its title and its score. Its table lists each
   key result's **Code**, **Key Result**, **Owner**, **Finish**, **Status** and **Score**.
6. Press a key result to open its panel (see [Open a key result](#performance-key-result)).
7. Search finds key results, objectives and organisational goals by code or words. An objective
   found this way opens on its own: **Objective**, **Function**, **Plan**, **Score** and its
   **Key Results**. It reads **No Key Results Yet** before one is set.

Before any objective is set for the quarter, Goals reads **No Objectives Yet**. Before the first
quarter is set up, only **Manage Quarters** is offered.

| A key result's status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **On Track** | Going to plan |
| **At Risk** | Behind, but can still be met |
| **Off Track** | Will not be met without a change |
| **Done** | Delivered |
| **Missed** | Not delivered by its date |
| **Not Started** | Work has not begun |
| **Awaiting Report** | Nothing has been reported for it yet |
| **TBC** | Its target is not confirmed yet; it is set before the quarter's lock date |

A key result shows its latest reported month. It keeps that month's status until the head reports
the next one.

On a phone, the buttons at the top sit under the title. **Quick Actions** offers a function's head
Report Month, and others New Goal. The tiles sit two to a row. **Organisational Goals**
shows the **Goal ID** and **Goal**; press one for the rest. Each key result shows its **Code**,
**Key Result**, **Status** and **Score**; press it for its owner and dates. The three dots open a
list from the bottom, and the larger forms fill the screen.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This was not found** | The goal, objective or key result has been deleted | Close it. Goals shows what remains |
| **That goal does not exist** or **That key result does not exist** | Someone deleted it while you had it open | Close it and open Goals again |
| **This key result is binding: change its target instead** | Its plan was sent, or its lock date came, while it was open | Close it, open it again and use **Change Target** |

### Open a key result {#performance-key-result}

![A key result's panel](shot:performance-key-result)

The panel shows what the key result is and how its latest month went:

- **Key Result**, **Type**, **Owner**, **Start**, **Finish** and **Baseline**.
- **Target**, with *Was* and the old target after a change. **Change Waiting** shows a new target
  the CEO has not decided yet.
- **Feeds** names the company key results it feeds, **Fed By** the ones that feed it, and **Rolls
  Up To** the objective and goal above it.
- The month last reported, named such as *October 2026* (**Not Reported** before any), with its
  pill (**Good**, **Watch**, **Recovery** or **Not Scored**): **Status**, **Actual**, **Source**,
  **Update**, the **Proposed** score and the **Confirmed** score.
- **History** lists the months before, and **Initiatives** the tasks set for its owner.

| Type | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Diagnostic Gate** | A one-off check. It ends once it is signed off |
| **Visibility Gate** | Makes a figure visible. It ends once two monthly reports agree |
| **Enabling** | Builds something other key results need. It ends once it is in place |
| **Compliance, Retiring** | A compliance rate that becomes a KPI next year |
| **Compliance, Floor** | A minimum standard that becomes a KPI next quarter |
| **Outcome** | A result for customers or the business |
| **Output** | Something delivered |

### Record an organisational goal {#performance-goals-org needs="performance create, cap goals.organisation"}

The CEO records the year's organisational goals.

1. Press **New Goal** at the top right of [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Choose **Organisational Goal** in **What**.
3. Type the **Goal**. Add the **Goal ID**, **Strategic Theme**, **Success Measure** and
   **Baseline** if you have them.
4. Press **Record Goal**. The hub says **Goal recorded**. If **Goal ID** reads **Already in use**,
   another goal this year has that ID: type another.
5. {needs="performance edit"} To change one, press it under **Organisational Goals**, then
   **Edit Goal**. Change the **Goal** or **Description**, or a quarter's target, such as
   *Q4 2026 Target*. Each quarter set up in the goal's year has one. Press **Save Goal**. The hub
   says **Goal saved**.
6. {needs="performance delete"} To remove one, press **Delete Goal** and confirm. The hub says
   **Goal deleted**.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **A company objective serves this goal** | A company objective names it | Point that objective at another goal first, then delete |
| **Another record names this goal** | An older scorecard line or goal names it | Keep the goal; ask Operations and People if it must go |

### Set objectives and key results {#performance-goals-set needs="performance create, cap goals.function"}

The CEO and Management set the company's objectives; each function's head sets the function's.
Operations and People and the CEO may set any function's. They can be added and changed while the
plan is a draft, or after it comes back. A new objective goes into the quarter chosen in
**Quarter** on Goals.

![New Goal, an objective](shot:performance-new-goal)

1. Press **New Goal** on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals) and choose **Objective** in
   **What**.
2. Pick the **Function**, type the **Objective**, and pick the **Organisational Goal** it serves.
   Press **Set Objective**. The hub gives it its code, such as *BM-O3 set*.
3. {needs="cap goals.company"} For a company objective, choose **Company** in **Function**.
4. For a key result, press **New Goal** and choose **Key Result**.
5. Pick its **Objective**, and type the **Key Result**, such as `Grow monthly active users to
   64,000`. The **Objective** list holds every objective of the quarter whose plan is a draft or
   returned and that you may add to, whatever **Function** Goals shows. If the form reads
   **No Objectives Yet**, set an objective first, or the plan is already sent.
6. Choose its **Measure**: **Numeric**, **Dated** or **Yes or No**.
7. Fill in the **Target** as a number, and **Target in Words** as people say it, such as
   `64,000 users`. Tick **Target to be confirmed** if it is not settled yet.
8. Add the **Unit**, the **Finish** date and the **Owner**. For a function's key result, pick the
   company key results it **Feeds**.
9. Press **More** for **Co-Owner**, **Direction** (**Higher Is Better** or **Lower Is Better**),
   **Baseline**, **Start**, **Type** and **Owner Note**. A company key result also has
   **Scored From** here. The types are explained under [Open a key result](#performance-key-result).
10. Press **Set Key Result**. The hub gives it its code, such as *BM-KR1.3 set*.

To change an objective, press the three dots on its card and choose **Edit Objective**. Change the
**Objective** or **Organisational Goal** and press **Save Objective**. The hub says, for example,
*BM-O1 saved*.

To change a key result, open it and press **Edit Key Result**. Change any field and press
**Save Key Result**. The hub says **Key result saved**.

**Delete Objective** (on the three dots) and **Delete Key Result** (on the key result) ask you to
confirm, naming what goes. Deleting an objective deletes its key results too. The hub says, for
example, *BM-O1 deleted*.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **An objective holds at most four key results** | The objective has four already | Add it to another objective |
| **Finishes before it starts** under **Finish** | **Finish** is before **Start** | Change one of the two dates |
| **Like 64000 or 0.95** under **Target** | The figure is unfinished or has more than four decimals | Type the figure only, such as `64000`. Put `64,000 users` in **Target in Words** |
| **This plan is sent or binding: change a target instead** | That function's plan for the quarter is sent or approved | Choose a quarter still in **Planning** in **Quarter**, or change a target |
| **That quarter is closed** | The quarter chosen in **Quarter** has ended | Choose the current or the next quarter |
| **Only the function's head sets its goals** | You picked another function | Pick your own function in **Function** |
| **Pick a function** under **Function** | No function picked, and your role does not set the company's | Pick your function |
| **You cannot set a function's goals** | Your role sets only the company's objectives | Choose **Company** in **Function** |

### Send the quarter plan {#performance-plan-send needs="performance create, cap goals.function"}

Each function sends one plan a quarter: its objectives, key results, KPIs and every scorecard in
it. It is approved by the head's line manager, then Operations and People, then the CEO. The first
stage is skipped when the head reports to the CEO. The second is skipped when Operations and People
sent it. The company's plan goes to the CEO alone. Only the CEO's approval makes it binding.

1. On [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals), pick your function in **Function**.
2. {needs="cap goals.company"} For the company's plan, choose **Company** in **Function**.
3. The **Plan Approval** card shows the plan's status, its **Objectives** and **Scorecards**, and
   anything that stops it.
4. Fix each problem in a red bar. An amber bar only warns. **Send Plan** shows once no red bar is
   left.
5. Press **Send Plan**. The hub says **Plan sent for approval**.
6. If it comes back, the card reads **Returned** with the reason. Change what was asked and press
   **Send Plan** again.

| The bar reads | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add an objective first** | Set at least one objective |
| *Ada Obi: Results weights add to 50, not 60* | Open that scorecard and change its line weights |
| *BM-KR1.3: shares are 2 off the target* | Change the shares on the lines that split that key result |
| *Ada Obi: Grow users links another quarter's key result* | Link that line to a key result of this quarter |
| *BM-O2 holds one key result* (amber) | Add a key result, or fold the objective into another |

If the hub says **This plan is already sent**, someone sent it first. Close and reopen Goals: the
card reads **Awaiting Approval**.

| The plan reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being prepared |
| **Awaiting Approval** | Sent, with one of its approvers |
| **Returned** | An approver asked for changes |
| **Binding** | Approved by the CEO; the quarter runs on it |

Once a plan is binding, each person and their reviewer is asked to agree the scorecard.

### Decide a plan, a target change or a scorecard {#performance-decide needs="performance approve"}

What waits for you sits in **Waiting On You** near the top of
[Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals). It shows only when something waits.

![A quarter plan waiting for a decision](shot:performance-plan-approval)

1. Read the **Request** (a function's plan, a target change or a scorecard), who it is **From**,
   its **Stage** and how long it has been **Waiting Since**. A **Stage** in red has waited longer
   than it should.
2. Press the row. Its sheet opens.
3. A **Quarter Plan** shows its function, quarter, stage, who sent it, its objectives, key results
   and scorecards, and the chain of approvers.
4. Press **Approve Plan**. The hub names the next approver, such as
   *Sent on to Operations & People*, or says **Plan approved** at the last stage.
5. Or press **Request Changes**, type the **Reason** and press **Request Changes**. The hub says
   **Plan sent back**, and the head reads your reason.
6. {needs="ceo"} A **Target Change** shows the **Old Target**, **New Target**, **From Month** and
   **Reason**. Press **Approve Change**: the hub says **Change approved**. Or press
   **Refuse Change**, type the **Reason** and press **Refuse Change**: the hub says
   **Change refused**.
7. A scorecard opens its own sheet. Press **Approve Scorecard**: the hub says
   **Scorecard approved**, or names the next approver. Or press **Request Changes** with a reason:
   the hub says **Scorecard sent back**.

**Reason** must be filled in. A request opened from an old notice, already decided, shows its
**Status** and no buttons.

### Report the month {#performance-report needs="performance edit, cap goals.function"}

By the third working day of each month (Monday to Friday, not a public holiday), each function's
head reports how its key results and KPIs did. Management report the company's key results.

1. Press **Report Month** at the top of [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Pick the **Function** (or **Company**, for Management) and the **Month** first. The **Month**
   starts on the month just ended. Changing it once you have typed asks first: **Change Month**
   clears what you typed, **Keep It** stays.
3. For each key result you report, choose its **Status** and fill in the **Actual** and a short
   **Update**. Type the **Actual** as a figure: a comma or a unit stays out, so `64,000` goes in
   as `64000`. A minus is kept, such as `-5`. A figure it cannot take is marked as you leave it.
   ![Report Month](shot:performance-report)
4. If the score falls under 0.4, or the status is **At Risk**, **Off Track** or **Missed**, name
   the **Recovery Owner** and the **Recovery Date**.
5. Under **KPIs**, for each of the function's KPIs you report, choose its **Result** and fill in the
   **Actual** and a **Comment**.
6. Press **Report Month**. The hub says **Month reported**. Only the key results and KPIs you
   changed are sent.

A function with many objectives or KPIs shows them in pages. What you typed is kept as you turn
them. When a row is refused, the message names it, such as *Check BM-KR1.2*, so you can find it on
its page.

| The hub says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Change a key result or KPI first** | Fill in at least one key result or KPI |
| **Nothing To Report** | The function has no binding key results and no KPIs this quarter |
| **Name owner and date** under **Recovery Owner** | Name the **Recovery Owner** and **Recovery Date** on that key result |
| **Pick a status** under **Status** | Choose the key result's status |
| **Like 64000 or 0.95** under **Actual** | Finish the figure, with at most four decimals, such as `0.95`. A key result on another page is named in the message |
| **Pick a date** under **Recovery Date** | Pick the date the key result should recover by |
| **Shorten the update** under **Update** | Keep the update shorter |
| **Only the function's head reports its month** | Ask the head to report it |

### Confirm a score {#performance-confirm needs="performance approve, cap goals.confirm"}

The CEO confirms each month's key result scores at the monthly review.

1. Open the key result on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Check its **Proposed** score and press **Confirm Score**. The hub says **Score confirmed**.

### Change a target {#performance-target needs="performance edit"}

A binding target changes only with the CEO's approval. The old target stays beside the new one,
and scores already given do not change.

1. Open the key result on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals). Its owner and the
   function's head see the buttons.
2. Before the quarter's lock date, a **TBC** target is set in place: press **Set Target**, fill
   in **Target** and **Target in Words**, and press **Set Target**. The hub says **Target set**. It
   still reads **TBC** until the lock date, so you may set it again until then.
3. After it, press **Change Target**. Type the **New Target** as a figure only, such as `70000`,
   and the **Target in Words** if it has them. Choose the **From Month**, a month of its quarter.
   Type the **Reason**.
4. Press **Send Change**. The hub says **Sent to the CEO**, and the key result shows **Change
   Waiting** until the CEO decides.

| The form says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter the new target** | **New Target** is empty | Type the new figure |
| **Like 64000 or 0.95** under **New Target** | It is unfinished or has more than four decimals | Type the figure only |
| **A change to this target is already waiting for a decision** | Only one change waits at a time | Wait for the CEO's decision |

### Set up a quarter {#performance-quarter needs="performance create, cap performance.manage"}

Operations and People set up each quarter before it starts, with its scoring rules. A new quarter
starts with a copy of the last quarter's rules.

1. On [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals), press **Manage Quarters**, then **Set Up
   Quarter**. You can also search for **Set Up Quarter**.
2. On **Quarter**, check **Starts**: 1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 October. **Ends** follows by
   itself. Add the **Name** (Q1 2027 unless you type another), the **Lock Date** for TBC targets,
   and the **Status**: **Planning** or **Active**. Only one quarter is **Active** at a time. Press
   **Next**.
   ![Set Up Quarter, its first step](shot:performance-quarter)
3. On **Bands**, check **Good From** and **Watch From** for key results, and **Exceeds From**,
   **Meets From** and **Partly Meets From** for ratings. Press **Next**.
4. On **Key Result Scoring**, check **Done On Time**, **Grace Days**, **Done Late** and **Partly
   Done**. Press **Next**.
5. On **Line Scoring**, check **Milestone On Time**, **Grace Business Days**, **Milestone Late**,
   **Standard Met**, **Standard Near Miss** and **Near Miss Points**. Press **Next**.
6. On **Section Weights**, check **Results**, **KPIs** and **Accountability**.
7. Press **Set Up Quarter**. The hub says, for example, *Q1 2027 set up*, and Goals shows the new
   quarter. **Back** returns to a step before.

When the hub refuses a field, the wizard goes back to that field's step, marks it, and a message
says why, such as **That quarter is already set up**.

| The form says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick a quarter start** under **Starts** | It is not a quarter's first day | Pick 1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 October |
| **Already set up** under **Starts** | That quarter exists | Pick the next quarter, or change this one |
| **Another is active** under **Status** | Another quarter is **Active** | Choose **Planning**, or close the active quarter first |
| **Outside the quarter** under **Lock Date** | The lock date is not in the quarter | Pick a day between **Starts** and **Ends** |
| **Must exceed Watch** under **Good From** | Good must start above Watch | Raise **Good From**, or lower **Watch From** |
| **Must exceed Meets** under **Exceeds From** | Exceeds must start above Meets | Raise **Exceeds From**, or lower **Meets From** |
| **Must exceed Partly Meets** under **Meets From** | Meets must start above Partly Meets | Raise **Meets From**, or lower **Partly Meets From** |
| **Sections must total 100** under **Results** | **Results**, **KPIs** and **Accountability** do not add to 100 | Change them so they total 100 |
| **From 0 to 1** under a score | A score above 1 | Type a figure from 0 to 1, such as 0.7 |

### Change a quarter {#performance-quarter-edit needs="performance edit, cap performance.manage"}

1. Choose the quarter in **Quarter** on [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals).
2. Press **Manage Quarters**, then **Edit Quarter**. It is not offered for a **Closed** quarter.
3. Change what you need on each step. **Starts** cannot change.
4. Press **Save Quarter**. The hub says **Quarter saved**.

A quarter only moves forward, from **Planning** to **Active** to **Closed**.

## Scorecards {#performance-scorecards screen=performance/targets icon=sheet}

Each person has one scorecard a quarter, in three sections: **Results** (60%), **KPIs** (25%) and
**Accountability** (15%). Each section holds weighted lines. You see your own scorecard, and those
of the people you manage.

![Performance, Scorecards](shot:performance-scorecards)

### Read the scorecards {#performance-scorecards-read}

1. Open [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets).
2. The tiles count the **Scorecards**, how many are **Agreed**, **Awaiting Agreement**, and
   **Below Meets**.
3. Narrow the list with **Quarter**, **Department** and **Status**, or type in **Search People**.
4. Each row shows the **Employee**, **Department**, **Reviewer**, **Status**, **Score to Date**
   and **Rating**. On a phone, each card shows the **Employee**, **Status**, **Score to Date** and
   **Rating**.
5. Press a row to open the scorecard.

| A scorecard reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Its lines are being written |
| **Awaiting Agreement** | Binding; the person or the reviewer has not agreed yet |
| **Agreed** | Both have agreed |
| **Closed** | The quarter or the person's time at MyCity has ended |

### Open a scorecard {#performance-scorecard}

![A scorecard](shot:performance-scorecard)

1. The top shows the **Employee**, their **Designation**, **Department**, **Reviewer**, **Dotted
   Line** and **Scope**, then the **Status**, **Score to Date**, **Rating**, **Weights** and who
   has **Agreed**.
2. Each section lists its lines: the **Goal** or **KPI**, its **Target**, what it is **Linked
   To**, when it is **Due**, its **Weight**, each month's score and the **Score**. On a phone, a
   line shows its **Goal**, **Target**, **Weight** and **Score**; press it for the rest, its month
   scores among them.
3. **Auto** on a line means the hub scores it itself, such as on-time commitments. Your reviewer
   can change that score, with a reason.
4. **Contribution** lists each key result shared between people: its **Key Result**, each person's
   **Shares**, its **Target** and **Status**, **OK** or **Rebalance**.
5. Press a line to open **Scorecard Line**: its **Line**, **Section**, **Target**, **Linked To**,
   **Due**, **Weight**, each month's score (such as *October 2026*) and the **Score**. **Change Waiting** shows a new target the CEO has not
   decided. Press **Close** to go back.

| Row | What it reads |
|---|---|
| **Rating** | **Exceeds**, **Meets**, **Partly Meets**, **Below**, or **Not Rated** before the first review closes |
| **Weights** | **100%** when every section's lines add up; **Check** when one does not |
| **Agreed** | **Both**, **Employee**, **Reviewer** or **Not Yet** |
| **Approval** | Who a late joiner's scorecard waits on |
| **Disagreed** | The reason the person gave, until both agree again |

If a section's weights do not add up, a bar names it, such as *Results lines total 50, not 60*.

### Draft a scorecard {#performance-scorecard-new needs="performance create, manager or cap performance.manage"}

Managers draft the scorecards of the people who report to them, before the function's plan is
sent. Operations and People may draft one for anyone but themselves.

1. Press **New Scorecard** at the top right of
   [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets). On a phone, it is also in
   **Quick Actions**. It drafts into the **Quarter** chosen above the list.
   ![New Scorecard](shot:performance-scorecard-new)
2. Pick the **Employee**.
3. Choose **Lines From**: **Last Quarter** to copy their lines, or **Start Blank**. Either way the
   **Accountability** lines come filled in: *On-time commitment delivery*, *Monthly update on
   time*, and *Team scorecards and reviews* for someone who manages others.
4. Press **Draft Scorecard**. The hub says **Scorecard drafted** and opens it.
5. Press **Edit Lines**. Each line is a card, *Line 1*, *Line 2* and so on.
6. Pick its **Section**: **Results**, **KPIs** or **Accountability**. Type the **Goal**.
7. Choose its **Measure**. A **Results** line is **Numeric** or **Milestone**. A **KPIs** or
   **Accountability** line is **Standard** or **Yes or No**.
8. Fill in the **Target** if it has one, and the **Weight**, such as `15` or `12.5`.
9. Pick its **Key Result**, or its **KPI** on a **KPIs** line, or **None** for a line the person
   reports themselves. A team result line ticks its **Team Key Results** instead. A **Milestone**
   line also needs its **Due Date**.
10. Press **Add Line** for another, or **Remove Line** to take one away.
11. Press **Save Lines**. The hub says **Lines saved** and the scorecard closes. Press its row to
    open it again.

> **Tip:** A line copied from last quarter loses its key results and its milestone date, because
> those belong to their quarter. Pick its **Key Result** (or tick its **Team Key Results**) and its
> **Due Date** again before you save.

**Edit Lines** shows only while the scorecard reads **Draft** and its function's plan is not with
its approvers.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Your manager drafts your scorecard** | Nobody drafts their own | Ask your manager |
| **Already has one** under **Employee** | They already have a scorecard this quarter | Press **Cancel** and open theirs from the list |
| **Not active** under **Employee** | The person is suspended or has left | Pick someone who works here now |
| **That quarter is closed** | The **Quarter** chosen above the list has ended | Choose the current quarter in **Quarter**, then press **New Scorecard** |
| **Not in your team** under **Employee** | The person does not report to you | Pick someone who reports to you, or ask Operations and People |
| **This function's plan is already sent for the quarter** | The plan is with its approvers | Wait until it is binding, then draft and send it on its own |
| **Link a key result** | The line takes its figures from a key result, and none is picked | Pick its **Key Result** |
| **Link a KPI** | The line takes its figures from a KPI, and none is picked | Pick its **KPI** |
| **Pick a date** | A **Milestone** line has no **Due Date** | Pick the **Due Date** |
| **Above zero** | The **Weight** is 0 | Give it a weight above zero |
| **Check the weight** | The **Weight** is not like 15 or 12.5 | Type a number with at most two decimals |
| **Pick the key results** | A team result line has none ticked | Tick its **Team Key Results** |
| **Required** under **Goal** | The **Goal** is empty | Type the goal |
| *At most 300 characters* under **Goal** | The **Goal** is too long | Type the goal in fewer words |
| **This scorecard is binding: change a target instead** | The plan was sent or approved while you edited | Press **Cancel**, then [Change a line's target](#performance-line-target) |
| **A line is not on this scorecard** | Someone else changed the lines while you edited | Press **Cancel** and open **Edit Lines** again |
| *Line 2:* and a message | That line has a problem no field shows | Correct that line and press **Save Lines** again |

## Reviews {#performance-reviews screen=performance/reviews icon=ticks}

Every month each scorecard is reviewed: the person checks in first, their reviewer scores it, the
reviewer's own manager approves it, and Operations and People close it. You see your own reviews,
those of the people you manage, and any that wait on you.

![Performance, Reviews](shot:performance-reviews)

### Read the month's round {#performance-reviews-read}

1. Open [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews). The round and its closing date show
   under the title on a computer.
2. Choose the **Quarter**, then the **Month**: that quarter's rounds, newest first. The list shows
   that month's reviews. Press **PIPs** for improvement plans, and **Reviews** to come back.
3. The tiles show **Check-Ins In**, such as 4 of 6, and how many are **With Reviewers**,
   **Second Level**, **With HR** and **Closed**. On a phone they are one line: how many check-ins
   are in, and how many are closed.
4. **Waiting On You** lists every review that needs you, whatever its month, a probation review
   among them, a page at a time. **Next** says what to do: **Score**, **Give Input**, **Approve**,
   or **Close Review** at **With HR**. On a phone, **Next** is left off: each card shows the
   **Employee**, **Stage** and **Due**.
5. Narrow the list with **Department**, **Stage** and **Kind** (**Monthly** or **Probation**), or
   type in **Search People**.
6. Each row shows the **Employee**, **Reviewer**, **Stage**, when it is **Due**, its **Score** and
   **Rating**. On a phone, **Reviewer**, **Score** and **Rating** are left off. Press a row to open
   the review.

Before this quarter's first round, the list reads **No Round Yet**.

A probation review belongs to no month: choose **Probation** in **Kind** to list them all.

| A review's stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Checking In** | The person is filling in their check-in |
| **With Reviewer** | The reviewer is scoring it |
| **Returned** | Sent back to the reviewer with a reason |
| **Second Level** | With the reviewer's own manager |
| **With HR** | With Operations and People to close |
| **Closed** | Done: the person can read the final scores |
| **Withdrawn** | Ended without a score, with a note saying why |

### Open a review {#performance-review}

![A review](shot:performance-review)

1. The top shows the **Employee**, the **Month**, the **Reviewer**, the **Stage** and when it is
   **Due**, then the chain: **Check-In**, **Reviewer**, **Second Level** and **HR**. A probation
   review shows its **Outcome** once chosen. **No Check-In** gives the reason it was scored without
   the check-in, and **Withdrawn** why it was withdrawn.
2. When the second level or Operations and People sent it back, a red bar gives their reason.
3. **Lines** shows each line's **Section**, **Target**, **Actual**, **Evidence**, the person's
   **Action**, and the **Proposed**, **Self** and **Final** scores. Point at a **Self** score, or
   tap it on a phone, to read the person's reason.
4. **Not Assessable** on a line means no evidence was given, so it counts neither for nor against.
5. **Input** shows the dotted-line manager's comment and their name.
6. Under the lines: the month's tasks (**Done This Month**, **Ongoing**, **Next Month**), and
   **Tasks** listing each with **When** and **Due**; the attendance (**This Month**) and
   **Recognitions**.
7. Once scored: **Score This Month**, **Score to Date** and **Rating**, and **Conduct** when a note
   is marked. **Corrective Actions** lists each **Action**, its **Owner**, **Due** and **Task**.
8. Once closed, press **Download PDF** for the letterheaded copy.

A reviewer cannot open a review until the person has checked in, or its due date has passed. Until
then it reads **Waiting for the check-in.** Once the date has passed it opens, without the person's
drafts (see [Score a review](#performance-review-score)).

On a phone the review fills the screen. **Lines** shows each line's **Target**, **Actual**,
**Proposed**, **Self** and **Final**; **Section**, **Evidence** and **Action** are left off.

### Score a review {#performance-review-score needs="manager"}

1. Open [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews) and press the review under **Waiting On
   You**.
2. Each line is a card, except an automatic line with no cases this month. A line that reads
   **Not Assessable** has no evidence: its **Final Score** is optional, and if you give one, type
   the **Reason**.
3. Check the **Final Score**, from 0 to 1, such as 0.75. It starts as the proposed score.
4. If you change it, type the **Reason**. Add a **Comment** if it helps.
5. Once a line's score is under 1, **Corrective Action**, **Owner** and **Due Date** appear.
   **Owner** starts as the person. The action becomes a task for the owner.
6. Press **Save Scores** as you go. The hub says **Scores saved**.
7. Press **Submit Review**. The hub says **Review submitted**, and it goes to your own manager.

Other buttons at the foot:

- **Ask For Input** shows while the scorecard names a dotted-line manager who has not answered. The
  hub already asked them when the check-in came in; this asks again. The hub says
  **Input asked for**.
- **Return Check-In** sends the check-in back to the person: type the **Reason** and press
  **Return Check-In**. The hub says **Check-in returned**. The review reads **Checking In** again,
  and you cannot open it until they send it back.
- A late check-in is not in **Waiting On You**. Choose **Checking In** in **Stage** and press the
  review; it reads **Waiting for the check-in.** Press **Score Without Check-In**, type the
  **Reason** and press **Score Review**. The hub says **Scoring without the check-in**. Then open
  it from **Waiting On You** to score it. *Monthly update on time* scores 0, and **No Check-In**
  shows your reason. The person is told, and their own lines read **Not Assessable**: score one
  with a reason if you have evidence.

| The hub says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Score every line first** | Give each line a **Final Score** |
| **Add a corrective action to each missed line** | Fill in the action, owner and date on each line under its target |
| **The check-in is not due yet** | Wait for its due date before scoring without it |
| **Check the highlighted lines**, and **Give a reason** under **Reason** | You changed a score from the proposed one without saying why: type the **Reason** |
| **Give a score** under **Final Score** | It is empty, or not from 0 to 1: type a score such as 0.75 |
| **The check-in is already in** | The person sent it while you wrote your reason: press **Cancel** and open the review from **Waiting On You** |
| **This review is not with its reviewer** | It moved on while you had it open: close it and open it again |
| **Input is already given** | The dotted-line manager has just answered: read their **Input** |
| **That review does not exist** | It is no longer yours to open, such as after a change of reviewer: ask Operations and People |

### Give your input {#performance-review-input needs="manager"}

### Conduct, improvement plans and probation {#performance-review-hr needs="performance edit, cap performance.manage"}

Operations and People handle these on an open or closed review.

1. **Mark Conduct** shows once the check-in is in. In **Conduct**, choose **Breach Confirmed** or
   **No Breach**, type the **Note** and press **Mark Conduct**. The hub says **Conduct marked**. A
   breach caps the rating at **Partly Meets**. On a closed review the rating changes at once, and

2. **Start PIP** shows on a closed review rated **Below**, until a plan is started from it. The

6. **Withdraw Review** ends any open review without a score, even one still **Checking In**. Type

*The employee left.*

![The monthly check-in](shot:performance-check-in)

1. Press **Start Check-In** on [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance).
   It shows the **Month**, your **Reviewer**, when it is **Due** and its **Stage**. Each line is a
   card with its **Section**, **Target** and **Proposed** score.
2. For each of your lines, fill in the **Actual**. A milestone asks when it was **Delivered On**,
   and a yes or no line whether it was **Met**: **Yes**, **No** or **Not Yet**.
3. Choose your **Evidence**. It starts on **None Yet**. Choose **Source** to name a report,
   **Task** to pick one of your tasks, **Link** for a web address, or **File** to press
   **Add Evidence** and attach one.
4. Write what you will do about anything behind in **Action**. To speak it instead, press
   **Dictate**, talk, then press **Stop Recording**. It stops by itself after three minutes. It reads
   *Writing it down*, then your words replace what is in **Action**.
5. Press **Tidy With AI** to have the wording tidied. The **As Spoken** and **Tidied** versions show
   together. Pick **Use Tidied** or **Keep Original**. Nothing changes until you pick.
6. The hub proposes each score from your actual. To give another, press **Change Score**, type
   **Your Score**, from 0 to 1, and the **Reason**. Your reviewer sees both.
7. **Automatic Lines** lists each **Line**, its **Figure** and **Proposed** score, filled in by the
   hub, such as on-time commitments. **No Cases** means nothing to count this month, so it is left
   blank.
8. **Done This Month**, **Ongoing** and **Next Month** count your tasks, and **Tasks** lists each
   with **When**.
9. Press **Save Draft** as you go. The hub says **Draft saved** and works out the proposed scores.
10. Press **Submit Check-In** when you are done. It saves first: the hub says **Draft saved**, then
    **Check-in submitted**, and tells your reviewer.
11. {needs="ceo"} Your own check-in closes your review as soon as you submit it, scored on your own
    figures.

Once sent, **View Check-In** under **This Month's Check-In** opens it to read, with *Sent* and the
date at the top. It can no longer be changed.

A line with no evidence reads **Not Assessable**: it counts neither for nor against you.

Near the end of your probation, your check-in may be a probation review. Without a scorecard it has
a card for each of **Objectives**, **Quality**, **Timeliness**, **Attendance** and **Conduct**.
Write how each went in **Action**, and use **Change Score** to give **Your Score** if you wish.

If your check-in was not in by its due date, your reviewer may score without it, and you are told.
Your check-in then closes: **View Check-In** shows it, but you cannot change it. Your own lines
read **Not Assessable**, and *Monthly update on time* scores 0.

| The hub says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No check-in is open.** | There is no round for you this month | Wait for the next round |
| **Your check-in is already sent** | You sent it, or your reviewer is scoring without it | Wait for your review |
| **Check the highlighted lines** | A line needs something | Read the field marked in red on that line |
| **Give a reason** under **Reason** | You changed a score without saying why | Type the **Reason** |
| **Give a score** under **Your Score** | It is not from 0 to 1 | Type a score such as 0.75 |
| **Like 64000 or 0.95** under **Actual** | The figure is unfinished or has more than four decimals | Type the figure only, such as 12 or 0.95 |
| **Name the source** | **Source** is chosen but empty | Name the report it came from |
| **Add the link** | **Link** is chosen but empty | Paste the web address |
| **Pick a task** under **Task** | **Task** is chosen but none is picked | Pick one of your tasks |
| **Add the file** | **File** is chosen but nothing is attached | Press **Add Evidence** |
| **Microphone not allowed** | The browser may not use your microphone | Allow it in the browser, or type instead |
| **This browser cannot record** | This browser cannot use a microphone | Type instead, or use another browser |
| **Could not transcribe** | The recording could not be written down | Press **Dictate** again, or type |
| **Tidy is off** | The assistant is switched off | Keep your own words |
| **The tidy changed a figure. Try again or keep yours.** | The tidied words held a figure yours did not | Press **Tidy With AI** again, or keep your own words |
| **You cannot write that text now** | Your check-in closed while you had it open | Press **Close**, then **View Check-In** |

If your reviewer sent it back, a red bar at the top gives the reason. Change what was asked and
submit again.

### Read a closed review {#performance-my-review}

1. Under **My Reviews**, press a review. It opens with its month as the title, or
   **Probation Review**.
2. Your own check-in shows from the start. The reviewer's scores, comments and corrective actions,
   the dotted-line manager's **Input** and any **Conduct** note show once it is **Closed**.
3. Press **Download PDF** for the letterheaded copy.
