01What it is
The cascade · the rhythm · the words

MyCity already ran a complete performance system in two spreadsheets: company and function OKRs, operational KPIs, a scorecard for every person, fixed scoring rules and a commitment register. The Performance Management System makes that system live, linked and quick without changing how MyCity works. The two Q4 2026 workbooks went in as they are: their words, codes, sections, weights and rules are kept.

04The quarter's plan: Goals
Performance › Goals

Goals is the OKR tree. Everyone sees all of it: the OKRs are the company's shared plan, as the workbook was.

Reading the screen

  • The figures: Company Score (the company's, for the quarter), then On Track, In Recovery and Awaiting Report for the function picked in Function.
  • Organisational Goals: the year's goals, recorded by the CEO with New Goal and changed with Edit Goal.
  • Codes follow the workbook: CO for the company, then TBO, BM, PG and PC for the functions. They freeze when the plan binds.
  • TBC a provisional target. Until the quarter's lock date its owner or the head sets it with Set Target, no approval needed; after that, Change Target.
  • Editing: while the plan is draft or returned, an objective has Edit Objective and Delete Objective, a key result Edit Key Result and Delete Key Result. A key result's Feeds names the company key results it serves.
  • Scored From: a company key result wholly delivered by one function key result takes its figures, so nothing is entered twice.

Scores run from 0 to 1

Good 0.70 and above   Watch 0.40 to 0.70   Recovery below 0.40, which needs a recovery owner and date. A dated key result scores 1 when done by its date.
Try itAnyone, for example the CEO
  1. Open Performance › Goals.
  2. Pick Function › Branding & Marketing.
  3. Press BM-KR1.3.
You should see its panel: owner, target, This Month, Initiatives (tasks for its owner) and Rolls Up To: CO-KR4.3 › CO-O4.

05Reporting the month, confirming scores
Report Month · Confirm Score · Change Target

The head reports, by the 3rd business day

  1. Goals › Report Month. Your own function is picked; choose the Month.
  2. For each key result that moved: Status, Actual, Update. Only the rows you change are sent.
  3. A score under 0.40 asks for Recovery Owner and Recovery Date.
  4. Press Report Month. The score is proposed from actual ÷ target and flows to any company key result Scored From it and to the scorecard lines it feeds.

The CEO confirms

Open a key result › Confirm Score. The confirmed score counts; until then the proposed one shows.

Targets written in words

Most Q4 key results state their target in words ("At least 64,000 new users"). Until a figure is set, Report Month keeps the actual and the update but cannot score them. On a TBC key result its owner or head sets the figure with Set Target until the lock date, no approval needed. Any other figure goes through Change Target, which the CEO approves on Goals › Waiting On You. Dated key results need no figure: Done by the finish date scores 1.
Try itHead of department, then the CEO
  1. Report Month: a dated key result, Status Done, with an update.
  2. As the CEO, open it › Confirm Score.
  3. On a key result with a target in words: Set Target if it is TBC, otherwise Change Target (figure, from month, reason).
  4. As the CEO, Goals › Waiting On You › the change › Approve Change.
You should see the dated key result scored 1.00, Good, then confirmed; the function's score on Analytics; the new target, with Was showing the old one.

06KPIs and scorecards
Performance › KPIs · Performance › Scorecards

KPIs

  • Each KPI keeps the workbook's formula, unit, direction, target, frequency and data source.
  • Each month is Met Not Met or N/A. N/A means no eligible cases and never counts as met.
  • The function's head records a KPI's month with Record Actual on the KPI. Lines Using It lists the people scored on it.

Scorecards

  • One per person per quarter, in three sections: Results 60%, KPIs 25%, Accountability 15%.
  • Each line: goal, target, Linked To (the key result or KPI it feeds), due date, weight, October, November and December.
  • Auto lines are worked out by the system: from a key result or KPI, on-time commitments, the monthly update and team reviews.
  • Contribution: when a key result is shared out among people, the shares must add up: OK or Rebalance.
Try itManager, then the head
  1. Performance › Scorecards; filter by Department or Status. Press a person; read the sections and Auto lines.
  2. As the function's head (or the CEO): KPIs, open a KPI › Record Actual.
You should see the month under Months, and Met Last Month move when it is Met. Lines using the KPI take that month in the month's review.

07Planning a quarter: approval and agreement
Send Plan · approvals · agreement
  1. Operations & People set up the quarter: Goals › Manage Quarters › Set Up Quarter, its dates, lock date and scoring rules, starting from the last quarter's.
  2. The CEO records the year's goals: Goals › New Goal › Organisational Goal.
  3. Management set the company's objectives and key results; each head sets the function's (New Goal › Objective or Key Result; Edit and Delete while the plan is draft or returned). A key result's Feeds names the company key results it serves.
  4. Managers draft scorecards: Scorecards › New Scorecard, pick the Employee and Lines From (Last Quarter or Start Blank), then Edit Lines. Never one's own.
  5. The head sends the plan: the Plan Approval card on Goals › Send Plan. It refuses an empty plan, a section whose line weights miss its share (60, 25, 15), a Rebalance, or a link to another quarter.
  6. Approval: the head's line manager, then Operations & People, then the CEO (7.1 says when a stage is skipped). Each approver is told on the bell and Home › Need You, and decides on Goals › Waiting On You: Approve Plan, or Request Changes with a reason, which returns it to the head.
  7. The CEO's approval makes it binding: codes freeze; each person and reviewer is told the scorecard is ready to agree, and reminded each working day from three business days after it binds.
  8. Agreement: Agree Scorecard, one tap each, or Disagree With Reason, which goes back to the reviewer.
Try itCEO or head
  1. Goals › Function › General Management.
  2. Read the Plan Approval card.
  3. As a manager, New Scorecard for someone new to you.
You should see Draft, with "Add an objective first" until the plan has one. On a binding plan a new scorecard is drafted too; Send Scorecard sends it alone for approval, and its approvers decide it on Goals › Waiting On You (Approve Scorecard).

11The monthly check-in
Start Check-In · Save Draft · Submit Check-In

A month's round opens on the first business day after the month ends (October's on 2 November): one review for each binding scorecard, and each person is told. The check-in replaces the monthly update, the work report and the paper form.

  1. Start Check-In on My Performance: in the banner or under This Month's Check-In.
  2. Automatic lines need nothing: they come from key results, KPIs and commitments.
  3. On your own lines: the actual, its evidence (a task, a link, a file, or the source named) and an action for anything behind.
  4. Type the action, or Record Answer to say it. Tidy With AI tidies the wording only when pressed: Use Tidied or Keep Original.
  5. A score is proposed. Change Score with a reason; your reviewer sees both.
  6. Save Draft any time, then Submit Check-In by the end of the 2nd business day (heads the 3rd).

Two rules people ask about

Self first. The reviewer cannot open a check-in until it is submitted. No evidence, no score. A line without evidence reads Not Assessable: it counts neither for nor against.

Late or returned

A late check-in is still taken until the reviewer presses Score Without Check-In, which they may once its due date has passed, with a reason you are told; either way its Monthly update on time line reads Late, proposed at 0. Once sent it opens as View Check-In. If the reviewer presses Return Check-In, the reason shows at the top: fix it and Submit Check-In again.
Try itEmployee in Checking In
  1. Start Check-In; fill one line; Save Draft.
  2. Reopen it; Submit Check-In.
You should see the stage move to With Reviewer and the reviewer told.

12The review: reviewer, second level, HR
Performance › Reviews
Check-In›Reviewer · 5th›Second Level · 7th›HR · 10th business day›Closed

Reviewer

Sees the check-in beside each line. Sets each final score (a reason where it differs from the proposed), a corrective action with owner and date for each missed line (it becomes a task) and a comment. Save Scores, Return Check-In (back to the employee with a reason) or Submit Review. Ask For Input asks the dotted-line manager first. Once the check-in's due date passes, Score Without Check-In, with a reason.

Second level

The reviewer's own line manager: Approve or Request Changes. Skipped when the reviewer is the CEO.

HR (Operations & People)

Close Review writes the score to date and rating onto the scorecard, files the PDF and tells the employee. Mark Conduct records a confirmed breach, which caps the rating at Partly Meets. Withdraw Review closes a leaver's review; it also closes by itself when People archive them.

Dotted-line input

Asked on the bell when the check-in is submitted. Open the review from the notice, write Your Input and press Give Input while it is with the reviewer. Never needed to submit.

Sent back

Request Changes at second level or HR returns the review to its reviewer with a reason. It reads Returned, the reviewer is told, and after Submit Review it runs the chain again.

Rounds

A round opens by itself on the first business day after a month ends. Open Round is for an ended month with no round yet; Close Round marks it closed, and open reviews carry on.

13Scores, ratings and what follows
The rules MyCity's workbooks set

13.1How a line scores

NumericActual ÷ target, capped at 1. Lower is better: target ÷ actual.
Milestone1.0 accepted on time; 0.5 within 10 business days after; 0 later.
StandardKPI and accountability lines: 1.0 target met; 0.5 missed by up to 5 points; 0 otherwise.
Yes or no1 met, 0 not.
N/ANo eligible cases: blank, never 1.
Over the quarterA Results line takes its latest month; a KPI or Accountability line the mean of its months.
Score to dateEach line's weight × score, over the weight of the lines scored so far.

Example: John Ojelola's Results line 3, weight 15: 4 South West placements targeted, 3 secured by November: 0.75 × 15 = 11.25.

13.2Ratings

RatingScore
Exceeds0.90 and above
Meets0.75 to under 0.90
Partly Meets0.50 to under 0.75
Belowunder 0.50

October and November are progress against the whole quarter; December's is the quarter's final. A confirmed conduct breach caps the rating at Partly Meets.

13.3What follows a review

Corrective actions

A milestone, standard or yes-or-no line scored under 1 needs an action with an owner and a date before Submit Review; a numeric line only in December. Each becomes a task for its owner.

Improvement plan (PIP)

After a Below rating, Operations & People may Start PIP: a start date, the lines to improve and a summary. It runs 30 days and is reviewed on its last day, with a reminder 3 business days before. Close Plan (Reviews › PIPs): Passed, Extended or Not Met.

Probation

A probation review opens 14 days before the probation end date, on the same form, with the line manager as reviewer. HR closes it: Confirm, Extend 3 Months (once) or End Employment.

13.4Questions people ask

"My target is wrong."Its owner or head sends Change Target on the key result (Set Target while it is TBC); a line's reviewer or head sends it on the line. Once approved, Was shows the old figure.
"I do not agree with my scorecard."Press Disagree With Reason on My Performance. It clears both agreements and goes back to your reviewer.
"My commitment's date moved."Rescope Commitment with a reason. The original date stays on record; once approved, it leaves the on-time count.
"Why can my reviewer not see my check-in?"Self first: your reviewer opens it only after you press Submit Check-In.
"Why is one of my lines blank?"It had no eligible cases (N/A) or no evidence (Not Assessable). Neither counts for or against you.
"Who reviews me?"Your line manager, or the person above a vacant seat. Operations & People can change it, with a reason.
"Does it still count if I disagree?"Yes. The scorecard stays in force and each month is still reviewed on its lines as they stand. Your reason goes to your reviewer.
"Can I change my check-in after sending it?"No: it opens as View Check-In. If your reviewer returns it with Return Check-In, fix it and press Submit Check-In again.
