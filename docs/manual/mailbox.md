# Mailbox

Mailbox is your own MyCity email (`name@mycityapp.io`) inside the app. Only you can open your
mailbox. Nobody else can, People and the CEO included.

## Connect your mailbox

The first time you open **Mailbox**, the **Connect Mailbox** sheet opens.

1. Your work email address is already filled in.
2. Type your email password, the same one you use on your phone or in webmail.
3. Press **Connect**. Your inbox starts to fill straight away.

If the password is wrong, the sheet says **Password refused**. Only company addresses can be
connected, not Gmail or Yahoo.

If you change your email password later, Mailbox shows a banner with **Reconnect**. Press it and
type the new password.

You can see your connection under **Settings · Integrations**.

## Read

- The folders are **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Archive**, **Spam** and **Trash**.
- New mail arrives on its own. **Refresh** checks for new mail now.
- Opening a message marks it read, on your phone too.
- Pictures from the internet stay hidden until you press **Load Images**.
- Press an attachment to download it.
- The menu on a message moves it: **Archive**, **Move to Spam**, **Move to Trash** or
  **Move to Inbox**. **Empty Spam** and **Empty Trash** delete those folders for good.

## Write and send

1. **Compose** opens a new message. **Reply**, **Reply All** and **Forward** open it filled in.
2. Add people in **To**. **Cc Bcc** shows the copy rows.
3. Write the message. The bar above it formats text and adds links. **Attach a File** adds files.
4. Your draft saves as you type. You will find it in **Drafts**.
5. Press **Send**.

To send later, press **Schedule**: tomorrow at 08:00, Monday at 08:00, or
**Pick Date and Time** (Lagos time). A scheduled message waits in **Drafts** marked **Scheduled**.
Open it and press **Cancel Schedule** to stop it, or **Continue Editing** to change it.

**Discard** throws a draft away. Press **Discard Draft** to confirm, or **Keep Editing**.

If a message cannot be delivered, for example because an address does not exist, you get a
notification.
