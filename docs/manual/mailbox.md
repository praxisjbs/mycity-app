Mailbox is your own MyCity email, `name@mycityapp.io`, inside the hub. Only you can open your
mailbox. Nobody else can, People and the CEO included.

Open it from **Mailbox** in the menu on a computer. On a phone, press **More** in the bar at the
bottom, then **Mailbox**. Its folders are tabs along the top: **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Drafts**,
**Archive**, **Spam** and **Trash**, then **Signature**. On a phone, slide the tabs sideways to reach
**Trash** and **Signature**.

## Inbox {#mailbox-inbox screen=mailbox/inbox icon=mail}

The mail you receive. New mail arrives by itself, and the bell tells you when it does.

![The Inbox](shot:mailbox-inbox)

### Connect your mailbox {#mailbox-connect needs="mailbox create"}

The first time you open Mailbox, it asks you to connect it.

1. Open [Mailbox · Inbox](/mailbox?tab=inbox). The **Connect Mailbox** sheet opens.
2. Your work address is already in **Email Address**.
3. Type your email password in **Password**: the same one you use for your MyCity mail on your phone
   or in webmail.
4. Press **Connect**. The message **Mailbox connected** confirms it, and your Inbox fills.

If you press **Cancel**, the page shows **Connect Your Mailbox**. Press **Connect Mailbox** to open
the sheet again.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not an email address** | Check the address, with one @ |
| **Use your company address** | Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, Hotmail and iCloud addresses cannot connect. Type your `@mycityapp.io` address |
| **Enter the password** | Type your email password |
| **Password refused** | The mail server did not accept the password. Type it again, or ask People and Operations to reset it |
| **Mail server not found** | No mail server answers for that address. Check the part after the @: it should be mycityapp.io |

If you change your email password later, the bell says **Reconnect your mailbox**, and a bar over
the list says **Mail server refused the password**. No new mail arrives until you reconnect. Press
**Reconnect**, type the new password, then press **Connect**.

### Read your mail {#mailbox-read}

1. Open [Mailbox · Inbox](/mailbox?tab=inbox). The line under the title counts your messages and
   those unread.
2. The figures show the **Folder**, its **Messages** and those **Unread**. **Attachments** counts the
   conversations with files among the 25 on screen. On a computer, **Mailbox** shows the address you
   are reading. On a phone, the line under the title and the **Mailbox** figure are not shown, and
   **Refresh** and **Compose** sit under the title.
3. Press a message in the list. It opens beside the list on a computer, or full screen on a phone.
   A dot beside a message means it is unread. Opening it marks it read, on every device.
   ![A message, open](shot:mailbox-message)
4. Pictures from the internet are held back to keep you safe, and the message says
   **Remote images are hidden.** Press **Load Images** to see them once, or **Always Show Images**
   for every message from that sender. The hub says Images shown from, then the sender's address.
   To stop, open a message from that sender, press the three dots, **Move this message**, then
   **Stop Showing Images**. The hub says Images hidden from, then the address.
5. **Show Quoted Text** opens the earlier mail the message quotes. **Hide Quoted Text** folds it
   away again.
6. Press an email address link inside a message to write to it. A **New Message** opens with the
   address in **To**.
7. Press an attachment to open it. A photo, a video or a PDF opens full screen. **Download** saves
   it, **Close** returns to the message, and a photo has **Zoom In** and **Zoom Out**. Any other file
   downloads. On a phone, the back arrow closes the file, and **Share** sends it to another app.
8. On a phone, press **Back** to return to the list.

The list shows 25 conversations at a time. Press **Older** at its foot for the next 25, and
**Newer** to go back.

Type in the search box above the list to find a conversation in this folder by its subject or an
address. Words inside a message are not searched. **Search Anything** at the top finds your own mail
in every folder, by subject, address or sender's name.

Press **Refresh** to check for new mail now. The message **Mailbox up to date** confirms it.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not refreshed. Try again.** | The hub could not ask for your mail. Check your connection and press **Refresh** again |
| **Not changed. Try again.** | Your choice about images did not save. Press it again |
| **There is no such message** | It was deleted, perhaps on another device. Press **Refresh** and open another |
| **There is no such draft** | It was sent or discarded, perhaps on another device. Press **Refresh** |

### Reply or forward {#mailbox-reply needs="mailbox create"}

1. Open the message.
2. Press **Reply** to answer the sender, **Reply All** to answer everyone on it, or **Forward** to
   send it to someone else with its attachments. **Reply All** shows only when the message went to
   more than one person. The window's title reads **Reply**, **Reply All** or **Forward**.
3. The message opens ready to write, with the earlier mail quoted under it. Write your answer above
   the quotation.
4. Press **Send**.

### Write a new message {#mailbox-compose needs="mailbox create"}

1. Press **Compose** at the top right. A **New Message** opens. **From** shows your own address. On
   a phone, **Compose** is under the title, and the message opens full screen.
   ![A new message](shot:mailbox-compose)
2. In **To**, type an address and press Enter, a comma or a semicolon. Each address becomes a chip;
   press its cross to take it off. Backspace in an empty row takes off the last one. **Cc Bcc** adds
   the copy rows.
3. Type the **Subject**.
4. Write the message. The bar above it has **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**,
   **Heading**, **Bulleted List**, **Numbered List**, **Quote**, **Link** and **Clear Formatting**,
   then **Undo** and **Redo**.
5. For a link, select the words, press **Link**, type the address and press **Add Link**.
6. To add a file, press the paper clip, **Attach File**, and choose it. Each file shows its size and
   a cross to take it off.
7. Press **Show Signature** to see the signature your message will carry. **Hide Signature** closes
   it. A new message or a forward to anyone outside MyCity carries the full signature. A reply, or
   mail only to `@mycityapp.io` addresses, carries the short line.
8. Press **Send**. The message names who it went to.

Your message is saved as you write: the line beside the paper clip says **Saving…**, then
**Draft saved**. If you close it, it waits in [Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts).

A sent message waits a moment in [Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts), marked **Scheduled**, then moves to
[Sent](/mailbox?tab=sent). Each person may send to 50 people an hour, unless IT set another limit.
Past it, the message waits in Drafts and goes by itself a few minutes later.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add a recipient** | Type at least one address in **To** |
| **Not an email address** | Check the address marked in red, with one @. Pointing at it says **Not an Email** |
| **Starts with https:// or mailto:** | Type the whole link address, starting with `https://` |
| Larger than 25 MB | Each file can be at most 25 MB. Send a smaller file |
| A file shows a reason and a round arrow | Press the round arrow to try again, or the cross to take it off |
| **Signature not available** | The preview could not be drawn just now. Your message is not affected |
| **Could not send** | The message did not reach the hub. Check your connection and press **Send** again |
| **Reconnect your mailbox** | The mail server refused your saved password. Press **Reconnect** above the list, then send again |

To throw a message away, press **Discard**, then **Discard Draft**. The message
**Draft discarded** confirms it. **Keep Editing** goes back to it. On a phone, **Discard** is the
bin at the foot.

### Send later {#mailbox-schedule needs="mailbox create"}

1. In the message, press **Schedule** beside **Send**. On a phone it is the clock.
2. Choose tomorrow at 08:00 or Monday at 08:00 (each shows its date), or **Pick Date and Time**.
3. For your own time, choose the **Send Date** and type the **Send Time** in Lagos time, such as
   `17:45`. Press **Schedule Send**. **Cancel** closes the time fields.
   ![Schedule Send](shot:mailbox-schedule)
4. The hub says Scheduled for, with the date and time. The message waits in
   [Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts) until then, and goes by itself.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not a real date** | Pick a date |
| **Not a valid time** | Type the time as two digits, a colon and two digits: `08:30` |
| **Time already passed** | Pick a later time, or a date within 90 days: a message can wait at most 90 days |

### Write with AI {#mailbox-ai needs="mailbox create"}

Write With AI drafts the message for you from the conversation and a line of what to say, or tidies
what you wrote. Nothing is sent until you press **Send** yourself.

1. In the message, press **Write With AI**.
   ![Write With AI](shot:mailbox-ai)
2. If you have already written something, **Write or Tidy** offers **Write** for a new draft, or
   **Tidy Up** to correct your own words. **Tidy Up** is chosen at first.
3. In **What To Say**, type a line about what the mail should say, such as
   `agree to Monday at 11, ask for the venue address`.
4. To speak instead, press **Dictate** and talk. Press **Stop Recording** when you finish; it stops
   by itself after two minutes. Your words then appear in the box. **Note** and each answer have
   their own **Dictate**.
5. Tick **Add a Note** to add anything else it should know, in **Note**.
6. Press **Write Draft**, or **Tidy Email** when you tidy.
7. If a fact only you know is missing, it asks up to three questions. Answer them and press
   **Write Draft**, or press **Write Anyway** to skip them.
8. Read the draft. Press **Try Again** for another, or **Use Draft** to use it. It takes the place
   of what you wrote, above any quoted mail, and fills **Subject** only when that was empty. The
   message **Draft added** confirms it.
9. Check the draft, change anything you like, and press **Send**.

**Back** at the top returns to your message with nothing changed.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Say what to write** | Type a line in **What To Say** |
| **Too long** | **What To Say** takes up to 2,000 characters, **Note** up to 4,000. Shorten it |
| **Microphone not allowed** | Allow the microphone in your browser, or type instead |
| **This browser cannot record** | Type instead, or open the hub in another browser |
| **Could not transcribe** | Press **Dictate** again, or type instead |
| **Write With AI is off** | MyCity has switched it off for now. Write the message yourself |
| **The AI service is not answering. Try again in a few minutes.** | Wait a few minutes and press the button again, or write it yourself |
| **The AI answer could not be used. Try again, or write it yourself.** | Press **Write Draft** again, or write it yourself |
| **The AI service is not set up correctly. An administrator has been told.** | Write the message yourself for now |
| **Spam is not read by AI** | The conversation you are answering is now in Spam. Move it back to the Inbox, or write it yourself |

### Summarise a long conversation {#mailbox-summary}

1. Open the conversation and press **Summarise Thread**. On a phone it is the sparkle beside
   **Reply**.
2. A **Summary** card opens above the messages: the gist, **Key Points**, and what is
   **Asked of You**.
   ![A summary](shot:mailbox-summary)
3. Press the copy icon, **Copy Summary**, to copy it. The message **Summary copied** confirms it.
   Press the cross, **Close Summary**, to close it.

Pressing **Summarise Thread** again shows the same summary at once, unless new mail has arrived.

The summary is written by AI from the conversation. Check dates and amounts against the mail before
you act on them.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **AI summaries are off** | MyCity has switched AI off for now, or its monthly limit is reached. Read the conversation yourself |
| **The AI service is not answering. Try again in a few minutes.** | Wait a few minutes and press **Summarise Thread** again |
| **The AI answer could not be used. Try again, or write it yourself.** | Press **Summarise Thread** again |
| **Not copied. Try again.** | Your browser did not allow copying. Press **Copy Summary** again |
| **Spam is not read by AI** | The conversation is now in Spam. Move it back to the Inbox first |
| **There is no such message** | It was deleted, perhaps on another device. Press **Refresh** |

### File a message away {#mailbox-move}

1. Open the message and press the three dots, **Move this message**.
2. Choose **Archive** to keep it out of your Inbox, **Move to Spam** for unwanted mail, or
   **Move to Trash**. It stays in Trash until you empty it.
3. The menu lists the other folders. **Move to Inbox** shows in Archive, Spam and Trash, not in Sent.
4. The message leaves the list. The hub says Moved to, then the folder, or **Moved back to Inbox**.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your mail server has no folder for that** | Your mailbox on the mail server lacks that folder, often Archive. Ask IT to add it, then press **Refresh** |
| **Not moved. Try again.** | The move did not reach the hub. Check your connection and try again |

### Send an attachment to a chat {#mailbox-to-chat needs="community create"}

1. Open the message and find the attachment.
2. Press the arrow beside it, or open the file and press **Forward** at the top.
3. **Forward To** opens. Type in the search box to find a channel, a conversation or a colleague.
4. Press up to five. Each becomes a chip; its cross takes it off.
5. Press **Forward**. **Cancel** closes it without sending. The message **Forwarded** confirms it,
   or Forwarded to with the number of chats. The file is copied there; the mail stays here.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Choose a chat** | Pick at least one chat, then press **Forward** |
| **At most 5 chats** | Take a chip off before you pick another |
| **This file is still being checked. Try again in a minute.** | Wait a minute and press **Forward** again |
| **This file failed its virus check and cannot be sent.** | Do not open it. Ask the sender for a clean copy |
| You cannot post in, then a channel's name | That channel takes posts from some people only. Pick another chat |

## Sent {#mailbox-sent screen=mailbox/sent icon=send}

Every message you have sent, newest first.

![Sent](shot:mailbox-sent)

1. Open [Mailbox · Sent](/mailbox?tab=sent).
2. Press a message to read it again, or **Forward** it.

If a message cannot be delivered, the bell says Not sent:, then its subject and the reason. The
message waits in [Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts). Open it, press **Continue Editing**, put it right and
press **Send**.

| The bell says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The mail server refused a recipient** | An address does not exist. Correct it and send again |
| **The mail server refused the password** | Press **Reconnect** above the list, then send again |
| **Reconnect your mailbox** | Press **Reconnect** above the list, then send again |
| **Could not reach the mail server** | The hub tried three times. Send it again later |
| **The mail server refused the sender address** | Ask IT to check your mailbox on the mail server |
| **The mail server refused the message** | Send it again; if it is refused again, ask IT |

## Drafts {#mailbox-drafts screen=mailbox/drafts icon=edit}

Messages you started and have not sent, and messages waiting to go later.

![Drafts](shot:mailbox-drafts)

1. Open [Mailbox · Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts).
2. {needs="mailbox create"} Press a draft, then **Continue Editing** to finish it and send it. It
   opens **Continue Draft**.
3. {needs="mailbox create"} A message waiting to go later shows **Scheduled** with its time.
   **Continue Editing** opens **Edit Scheduled Mail**. Change what you need and press **Done**: it
   keeps its time, and the message **Scheduled mail updated** confirms it. The button beside
   **Done** shows when it goes: press it to pick another time, or **Send Now**.
4. {needs="mailbox create"} Press **Cancel Schedule** to stop it going. It stays here as a draft,
   and the message **Schedule cancelled** confirms it.
5. {needs="mailbox create"} To delete a draft, press it, then **Continue Editing**, **Discard** and
   **Discard Draft**. A message waiting to go is stopped too.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **It has already gone** | Its time came before you pressed. Look for it in [Sent](/mailbox?tab=sent) |
| **Not cancelled. Try again.** | The hub could not be reached. Press **Cancel Schedule** again before its time |

## Archive {#mailbox-archive screen=mailbox/archive icon=book}

Messages you filed away from your Inbox with **Archive**. They are kept, and search finds them.

![Archive](shot:mailbox-archive)

1. Open [Mailbox · Archive](/mailbox?tab=archive).
2. Press a message to read it. To bring it back, press the three dots, then **Move to Inbox**.

## Spam {#mailbox-spam screen=mailbox/spam icon=alert}

Unwanted mail. To keep you safe, a message in Spam is not shown: it reads **Spam is not shown.**

![Spam](shot:mailbox-spam)

1. Open [Mailbox · Spam](/mailbox?tab=spam).
2. If a message is not spam, press it, then the three dots, then **Move to Inbox**. It shows as plain
   text, without its layout and pictures.
3. {needs="mailbox delete"} To delete everything here for good, press **Empty Spam** at the top. It
   shows only when Spam holds mail.
4. {needs="mailbox delete"} The question says how many messages go. Press **Empty Spam** again to
   confirm, or **Cancel**. This cannot be undone. The message counts the messages deleted.

If emptying fails, the hub says why, or **Not emptied. Try again.** Press the button again.

**Reply**, **Reply All**, **Forward** and **Summarise Thread** are not offered in Spam, and its files
cannot be sent to a chat. Its attachments still open: open one only if you trust the sender.

## Trash {#mailbox-trash screen=mailbox/trash icon=x}

Messages you deleted. They stay here until you empty the folder.

![Trash](shot:mailbox-trash)

1. Open [Mailbox · Trash](/mailbox?tab=trash).
2. To bring a message back, press it, then the three dots, then **Move to Inbox**.
3. {needs="mailbox delete"} To delete everything here for good, press **Empty Trash** at the top. It
   shows only when Trash holds mail.
4. {needs="mailbox delete"} The question says how many messages go. Press **Empty Trash** again to
   confirm, or **Cancel**. This cannot be undone. The message counts the messages deleted.

If emptying fails, the hub says why, or **Not emptied. Try again.** Press the button again.

## Signature {#mailbox-signature screen=mailbox/signature icon=doc}

Every message you send carries MyCity's signature: your name, job title and department, your
numbers, your address and the company's details. You only set your numbers. The rest comes from your
record.

![Your signature](shot:mailbox-signature)

### See your signature {#mailbox-signature-see}

1. Open [Mailbox · Signature](/mailbox?tab=signature).
2. Under **Your Signature**, choose **New Mail** for the full signature, or **Reply** for the short
   line replies carry.
3. **Copy Signature** copies the full signature, even while **Reply** is chosen. Paste it into your
   phone's mail app, whose mail carries no signature from the hub. The message **Signature copied**
   confirms it. On a phone it is the copy icon under the signature.
4. **Details** shows your **Name**, **Job Title**, **Department** and **Email**. People change the
   first three on your record. On a phone, press the **Details** row at the foot of **Numbers** to
   see them.

If the hub says **Not copied. Try again.**, your browser did not allow copying. Press
**Copy Signature** again.

### Change your numbers {#mailbox-signature-numbers needs="mailbox edit"}

1. Under **Numbers**, press **Edit Numbers**.
2. Type your **Mobile**, and your **Office Line** if you have one.
3. Press **Save Numbers**. The message **Numbers saved** confirms it.
4. Switch **Show Mobile** off to keep your mobile off the signature, and **WhatsApp Link** off to
   leave out the link that opens a WhatsApp chat with you. Each switch saves at once. The hub says
   **Mobile hidden** or **Mobile shown**, **WhatsApp link off** or **WhatsApp link on**.
   **WhatsApp Link** shows only when MyCity allows WhatsApp links.

Your **Mobile** is the one on your employee record, so changing it here changes it there too.
Leaving **Mobile** empty keeps the number you had; switch **Show Mobile** off to hide it.

If the field says **Not a phone number**, type it as `0803 123 4567`, or with + and the country code
for a number abroad. **Not saved. Try again.** means the change did not reach the hub: try again.

### Set the company's links {#mailbox-signature-configure needs="cap mailbox.signature"}

1. Press **Configure** at the top right of [Mailbox · Signature](/mailbox?tab=signature). On a
   phone, press the cog there, **Configure Signature**.
   ![Configure Signature](shot:mailbox-configure)
2. On **App Links**, paste the **App Store** and **Google Play** addresses. Press **Next**.
3. On **Social Links**, paste MyCity's **Instagram**, **TikTok**, **LinkedIn**, **X**, **Facebook**
   and **YouTube** addresses. Leave any you do not use empty. Press **Next**.
   ![Social Links](shot:mailbox-configure-social)
4. On **What Shows**, tick what everyone's signature carries: **WhatsApp Links**, **App Links**,
   **Social Links** and the **Legal Line**. Add a **Confidentiality Note** if you want one.
   ![What Shows](shot:mailbox-configure-shows)
5. Press **Save Signature**. The message **Signature saved** confirms it, and everyone's signature
   changes at once.

**Back** returns to the step before. **Cancel** on the first step closes it without saving.

If a link field says **Not a web address**, paste the whole address, starting with `https://`. If
**Confidentiality Note** says **Too long**, keep it under 300 characters. **Not saved. Try again.**
means the change did not reach the hub: press **Save Signature** again.
