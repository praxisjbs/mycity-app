Mailbox is your own MyCity email, `name@mycityapp.io`, inside the hub. Only you can open your
mailbox. Nobody else can, People and the CEO included.

Open it from **Mailbox** in the menu on a computer. On a phone, press **More** in the bar at the
bottom, then **Mailbox**. Its folders are tabs along the top: **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Drafts**,
**Archive**, **Spam** and **Trash**, then **Signature**.

## Inbox {#mailbox-inbox screen=mailbox/inbox icon=mail}

The mail you receive. New mail arrives by itself, and the bell tells you when it does.

![The Inbox](shot:mailbox-inbox)

### Connect your mailbox {#mailbox-connect}

The first time you open Mailbox, it asks you to connect it.

1. Open [Mailbox · Inbox](/mailbox?tab=inbox). The **Connect Mailbox** sheet opens.
2. Your work address is already in **Email Address**.
3. Type your email password in **Password**: the same one you use for your MyCity mail on your phone
   or in webmail.
4. Press **Connect**. The message **Mailbox connected** confirms it, and your Inbox fills.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not an email address** | Check the address, with one @ |
| **Enter the password** | Type your email password |
| **Password refused** | The mail server did not accept the password. Type it again, or ask People and Operations to reset it |
| **Mail server not found** | Only MyCity addresses connect, not Gmail or Yahoo. Use your `@mycityapp.io` address |

If you change your email password later, a bar says **Mail server refused the password**. Press
**Reconnect** and type the new password.

### Read your mail {#mailbox-read}

1. Open [Mailbox · Inbox](/mailbox?tab=inbox). The line under the title counts your messages and
   those unread.
2. The figures show the **Folder**, its **Messages**, those **Unread**, and those with
   **Attachments**.
3. Press a message in the list. It opens beside the list on a computer, or full screen on a phone.
   A dot beside a message means it is unread. Opening it marks it read, on every device.
   ![A message, open](shot:mailbox-message)
4. Pictures from the internet are held back to keep you safe, and the message says
   **Remote images are hidden.** Press **Load Images** to see them once, or **Always Show Images**
   for every message from that sender.
5. **Show Quoted Text** opens the earlier mail the message quotes.
6. Press an attachment to open it. A photo, a video or a PDF opens full screen, with **Download**.
   Any other file downloads.
7. On a phone, press **Back** to return to the list.

Type in the search box above the list to find a message in this folder. Press **Refresh** to check
for new mail now. The message **Mailbox up to date** confirms it.

### Reply or forward {#mailbox-reply}

1. Open the message.
2. Press **Reply** to answer the sender, **Reply All** to answer everyone on it, or **Forward** to
   send it to someone else with its attachments.
3. The message opens ready to write, with the earlier mail quoted under it. Write your answer above
   the quotation.
4. Press **Send**.

### Write a new message {#mailbox-compose}

1. Press **Compose** at the top right.
   ![A new message](shot:mailbox-compose)
2. In **To**, type an address and press Enter or a comma. Each address becomes a chip; press its
   cross to take it off. **Cc Bcc** adds the copy rows.
3. Type the **Subject**.
4. Write the message. The bar above it makes text bold or italic, adds lists and headings, and adds
   a link.
5. To add a file, press the paper clip, **Attach File**, and choose it. Each file shows its size and
   a cross to take it off.
6. Press **Show Signature** to see the signature your message will carry.
7. Press **Send**. The message names who it went to.

Your message is saved as you write, and the line beside the paper clip says **Draft saved**. If you
close it, it waits in [Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts).

| The To row says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add a recipient** | Type at least one address in **To** |
| **Not an email address** | Check the address marked in red, with one @ |

To throw a message away, press **Discard**, then **Discard Draft**. **Keep Editing** goes back to it.

### Send later {#mailbox-schedule}

1. In the message, press **Schedule** beside **Send**. On a phone it is the clock.
2. Choose tomorrow at 08:00, Monday at 08:00, or **Pick Date and Time**.
3. For your own time, choose the **Send Date** and type the **Send Time** in Lagos time, such as
   `17:45`. Press **Schedule Send**.
4. The message waits in [Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts) until then, and goes by itself.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not a real date** | Pick a date |
| **Not a valid time** | Type the time as two digits, a colon and two digits: `08:30` |
| **Time already passed** | Pick a later time |

### Write with AI {#mailbox-ai}

Write With AI drafts the message for you from the conversation and a line of what to say, or tidies
what you wrote. Nothing is sent until you press **Send** yourself.

1. In the message, press **Write With AI**.
   ![Write With AI](shot:mailbox-ai)
2. If you have already written something, choose **Write** for a new draft, or **Tidy Up** to
   correct and smooth your own words.
3. In **What To Say**, type a line about what the mail should say, such as
   `agree to Monday at 11, ask for the venue address`. Press **Dictate** to say it out loud instead.
4. Tick **Add a Note** to add anything else it should know, in **Note**.
5. Press **Write Draft**, or **Tidy Email** when you tidy.
6. If a fact only you know is missing, it asks up to three questions. Answer them and press
   **Write Draft**, or press **Write Anyway** to skip them.
7. Read the draft. Press **Try Again** for another, or **Use Draft** to put it in your message.
8. Check the draft, change anything you like, and press **Send**.

**Back** at the top returns to your message with nothing changed.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Say what to write** | Type a line in **What To Say** |
| **Microphone not allowed** | Allow the microphone in your browser, or type instead |
| **Could not transcribe** | Press **Dictate** again, or type instead |
| **Write With AI is off** | MyCity has switched it off for now. Write the message yourself |

### Summarise a long conversation {#mailbox-summary}

1. Open the conversation and press **Summarise Thread**. On a phone it is the sparkle beside
   **Reply**.
2. A **Summary** card opens above the messages: the gist, **Key Points**, and what is
   **Asked of You**.
3. **Copy Summary** copies it. The cross closes it.

The summary is written by AI from the conversation. Check dates and amounts against the mail before
you act on them.

### File a message away {#mailbox-move}

1. Open the message and press the three dots, **Move this message**.
2. Choose **Archive** to keep it out of your Inbox, **Move to Spam** for unwanted mail, or
   **Move to Trash** to delete it.
3. A message in another folder offers **Move to Inbox** to bring it back.

### Send an attachment to a chat {#mailbox-to-chat}

1. Open the message and find the attachment.
2. Press the arrow beside it, *Forward to a chat*.
3. Pick up to five chats in Staff Community and press **Forward**. The file is copied there; the mail
   stays here.

## Sent {#mailbox-sent screen=mailbox/sent icon=send}

Every message you have sent, newest first.

![Sent](shot:mailbox-sent)

1. Open [Mailbox · Sent](/mailbox?tab=sent).
2. Press a message to read it again, or **Forward** it.

If a message cannot be delivered, for example because an address does not exist, the bell tells you.

## Drafts {#mailbox-drafts screen=mailbox/drafts icon=edit}

Messages you started and have not sent, and messages waiting to go later.

![Drafts](shot:mailbox-drafts)

1. Open [Mailbox · Drafts](/mailbox?tab=drafts).
2. Press a draft, then **Continue Editing** to finish it and send it.
3. A message waiting to go later is marked with its time. Press **Continue Editing** to change it,
   then **Done**; the menu beside it offers **Send Now**. Press **Cancel Schedule** to stop it
   going: it stays here as a draft.

## Archive {#mailbox-archive screen=mailbox/archive icon=book}

Messages you filed away from your Inbox with **Archive**. They are kept, and search finds them.

![Archive](shot:mailbox-archive)

1. Open [Mailbox · Archive](/mailbox?tab=archive).
2. Press a message to read it. To bring it back, press the three dots, then **Move to Inbox**.

## Spam {#mailbox-spam screen=mailbox/spam icon=alert}

Unwanted mail. To keep you safe, a message in Spam is not shown: it reads **Spam is not shown.**

![Spam](shot:mailbox-spam)

1. Open [Mailbox · Spam](/mailbox?tab=spam).
2. If a message is not spam, press it, then the three dots, then **Move to Inbox**.
3. To delete everything here for good, press Empty Spam at the top, then confirm. This cannot be
   undone.

Write With AI, Summarise Thread and Forward are not offered in Spam.

## Trash {#mailbox-trash screen=mailbox/trash icon=x}

Messages you deleted. They stay here until you empty the folder.

![Trash](shot:mailbox-trash)

1. Open [Mailbox · Trash](/mailbox?tab=trash).
2. To bring a message back, press it, then the three dots, then **Move to Inbox**.
3. To delete everything here for good, press Empty Trash at the top, then confirm. This cannot be
   undone. The message counts the messages deleted.

If emptying fails, the message at the foot of the screen says why. Press the button again.

## Signature {#mailbox-signature screen=mailbox/signature icon=doc}

Every message you send carries MyCity's signature: your name, job title and department, your
numbers, your address and the company's details. You only set your numbers. The rest comes from your
record.

![Your signature](shot:mailbox-signature)

### See your signature {#mailbox-signature-see}

1. Open [Mailbox · Signature](/mailbox?tab=signature).
2. Under **Your Signature**, choose **New Mail** for the full signature, or **Reply** for the short
   line replies carry.
3. **Copy Signature** copies it, to paste somewhere else.
4. **Details** shows your **Name**, **Job Title**, **Department** and **Email**. People change the
   first three on your record.

### Change your numbers {#mailbox-signature-numbers}

1. Under **Numbers**, press **Edit Numbers**.
2. Type your **Mobile**, and your **Office Line** if you have one.
3. Press **Save Numbers**. The message **Numbers saved** confirms it.
4. Switch **Show Mobile** off to keep your mobile off the signature, and **WhatsApp Link** off to
   leave out the link that opens a WhatsApp chat with you.

If the field says **Not a phone number**, type it as `0803 123 4567`.

### Set the company's links {#mailbox-signature-configure needs="cap mailbox.signature"}

1. Press **Configure** at the top right of [Mailbox · Signature](/mailbox?tab=signature).
   ![Configure Signature](shot:mailbox-configure)
2. On **App Links**, paste the **App Store** and **Google Play** addresses. Press **Next**.
3. On **Social Links**, paste MyCity's **Instagram**, **TikTok**, **LinkedIn**, **Facebook** and
   **YouTube** addresses, and X's. Leave any you do not use empty. Press **Next**.
4. On **What Shows**, tick what everyone's signature carries: **WhatsApp Links**, **App Links**,
   **Social Links** and the **Legal Line**. Add a **Confidentiality Note** if you want one.
5. Press **Save Signature**. Everyone's signature changes at once.

If a link field says **Not a web address**, paste the whole address, starting with `https://`.
