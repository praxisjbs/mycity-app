## Sign In {#qs-sign-in role="Everyone" icon=lock}

![The sign-in screen](shot:qs-sign-in)

1. Open the hub and type your work email and password.
2. Press **Sign In**.
3. If it asks to **Enter Code**, type the six digits from your authenticator app.
4. Forgot your password? Press **Forgot Password**. Read
   [Signing In](#start-sign-in) for every case.

## Find Anything {#qs-search role="Everyone" icon=srch}

![Search, open](shot:qs-search)

1. Press Ctrl and K (⌘ and K on a Mac), or **Search** on a phone.
2. Type a name, a screen or something to do.
3. Press the result to open it. Read [Search](#start-search).

## Read Your Notifications {#qs-notifications role="Everyone" icon=bell}

![The notifications panel](shot:qs-notifications)

1. Press the bell, **Notifications**.
2. Press a notification to open what it is about.
3. **Mark All Read** clears them all. Read [Notifications](#start-notifications).

## Get Help Anywhere {#qs-help role="Everyone" icon=help}

![Help, from any screen](shot:qs-help)

1. On a phone, press the round button with a plus at the bottom right, then **Help**.
2. On a computer, press your name at the foot of the menu, then **User Guide**.
3. The guide opens at the screen you were on. Read [Using This Guide](#start-user-guide).

## Ask Praxis AI {#qs-praxis role="Everyone" icon=spark screen=praxis_ai/ask needs="praxis_ai create"}

![Praxis AI, Ask](shot:qs-praxis)

1. Open [Praxis AI · Ask](/praxis-ai?tab=ask), or on a phone press **Quick Actions**, then
   **Praxis AI**.
2. Type a question in **Ask a Question**, such as "How many leave days do I have left?", and press
   **Send**.
3. Press a record under **From** to open it. Read [Ask](#praxis-ask).

## Clock In {#qs-clock-in role="Everyone" icon=clock screen=ess/my-attendance}

![The clock](shot:qs-clock-in)

1. Press **Clock In** at the top right of a computer, or the round button with a plus on a phone,
   then **Clock In**. [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance) has it too.
2. Check your **Clock Location** and **This Device**.
3. Press **Clock In**, and allow your location if the browser asks.
4. At the end of the day, open the clock again and press **Clock Out**. Read
   [Clock in and out](#ess-attendance-clock).

## Request Leave {#qs-request-leave role="Everyone" icon=calendar screen=ess/my-leave needs="ess create"}

![Request Leave](shot:qs-request-leave)

1. Open [My Leave](/ess?tab=my-leave) and press **Request Leave**.
2. Pick the **Leave Type**, then the **First Day** and the **Last Day**.
3. Add a **Note** for your manager if it helps.
4. Press **Send Request**. Your line manager is told. Read [Request leave](#ess-leave-request).

## Read Your Mail {#qs-mail role="Everyone" icon=mail screen=mailbox/inbox}

![The Inbox](shot:qs-mail)

1. Open [Mailbox · Inbox](/mailbox?tab=inbox).
2. {needs="mailbox create"} The first time, type your email **Password** and press **Connect**.
3. Press a message to open it. Read [Read your mail](#mailbox-read).
4. {needs="mailbox create"} Press **Reply** to answer it, or **Compose** to write a new one.

## Post in a Channel {#qs-channel role="Everyone" icon=msg screen=community/channels}

![A channel, open](shot:qs-channel)

1. Open [Staff Community · Channels](/community?tab=channels) and press a channel, such as
   **#general**.
2. Type your message in the box at the bottom.
3. Press **Send**. On a computer, Enter sends too.
4. Press the plus, **Add to message**, to send a file or recognise a colleague. Read
   [Post a message](#community-channels-post).

## Approve Leave {#qs-approve-leave role="Line Managers" icon=check screen=people/leave needs="people approve"}

![A request waiting on you](shot:qs-approve-leave)

1. Open [People · Leave](/people?tab=leave) and press **Pending**. A request in **Need You** on
   [Home · Overview](/home?tab=overview) opens here too.
2. Press the request. Check **Also Away** for who else is off.
3. Press **Approve**, or **Reject** and type the **Reason**. The person is told. Read
   [Approve or reject leave](#people-leave-decide).

## See Your Team {#qs-team role="Line Managers" icon=users screen=home/team needs="manager"}

![The Team tab](shot:qs-team)

1. Open [Home · Team](/home?tab=team).
2. Read **Present Today**, **On Site**, **Remote** and **Flags**.
3. Press a status chip, such as **Late**, to see only those people.
4. Press a person to see their day. Read [See who is in today](#home-team-today).

## Post an Announcement {#qs-announcement role="People & HR" icon=bell screen=community/announcements needs="cap announcements.post"}

![New Announcement](shot:qs-announcement)

1. Open [Staff Community · Announcements](/community?tab=announcements) and press
   **New Announcement**.
2. Type the **Headline** and the **Message**.
3. Choose the **Level** and the **Audience**.
4. Press **Publish Announcement**. Everyone in the audience is told. Read
   [Post an announcement](#community-announcements-new).

## Publish a Policy {#qs-policy role="People & HR" icon=book screen=knowledge/policies needs="knowledge create"}

![New Policy](shot:qs-policy)

1. Open [Knowledge Hub · Policies](/knowledge?tab=policies) and press **New Policy**.
2. Type its **Title** and press **Start Policy**.
3. Choose who it **Applies To**, write the **Policy Text**, and press **Save Draft**.
4. Press **Publish Policy**, choose the **First Day**, sign, and press **Publish Policy**. Read
   [Publish a policy](#knowledge-policies-publish).

## Add an Employee {#qs-add-employee role="People & HR" icon=users needs="people create"}

![Add Employee, first step](shot:qs-add-employee)

1. Open [People · Employees](/people?tab=employees) and press **Add Employee**.
2. Fill in **Person**, **Job**, **Pay and Bank** and **Next of Kin**, pressing **Next** after each.
3. Pick a **Worksite** for anyone On Site or Hybrid.
4. Press **Add Employee**. Read [Add an employee](#people-add-employee).

## Give Someone a Login {#qs-invite role="People & HR" icon=send needs="settings create"}

![Settings, Users](shot:qs-invite)

1. Open [Settings · Users](/settings?tab=users) and press **Invite User**.
2. Pick the **Employee** and their **Roles**.
3. Press **Send Invitation**. They choose their own password from the email. Read
   [Give someone a login](#settings-users-invite).

## Import a Staff List {#qs-import role="People & HR" icon=sheet needs="people create"}

![Import Staff on Employees](shot:qs-import)

1. Open [People · Employees](/people?tab=employees) and press **Import Staff**.
2. Upload the sheet, check the columns, then read the **Preview**.
3. Press **Commit Import**. Read [Import a spreadsheet](#settings-imports-run).

## Change a Role {#qs-roles role="Administrators" icon=shield screen=settings/roles-and-permissions needs="settings edit"}

![Settings, Roles & Permissions](shot:qs-roles)

1. Open [Settings · Roles & Permissions](/settings?tab=roles-and-permissions) and pick a role.
2. Press **Edit Role**, change its access, and press **Save Role**.
3. Check the review and press **Save Role** again. Read
   [Change or delete a role](#settings-roles-edit).

## Help With a Login {#qs-login-help role="Administrators" icon=key screen=settings/users needs="settings edit"}

![A login's actions](shot:qs-login-help)

1. Open [Settings · Users](/settings?tab=users) and press the person.
2. **Send Reset Link** for a forgotten password, **Reset Two-Step** for a lost phone,
   **Sign Out Everywhere** for a lost device.
3. Read [Help someone with their login](#settings-users-help).

## Check the System {#qs-status role="Administrators" icon=chart screen=settings/status}

![Settings, Status](shot:qs-status)

1. Open [Settings · Status](/settings?tab=status). The pill reads **All Up** when all is well.
2. Press **Open** on any check that is not up.
3. Open [Settings · Faults](/settings?tab=faults) for the errors behind it. Read
   [Work through the faults](#settings-faults-work).

## Watch AI Spend {#qs-ai-spend role="Administrators" icon=wallet screen=ai_control/spend}

![AI Control, Spend](shot:qs-ai-spend)

1. Open [AI Control · Spend](/ai-control?tab=spend).
2. **All AI** shows this month's spend against its cap; each provider shows its own.
3. Top up each provider's own account on its website before its balance runs out. Read
   [Spend](#ai-control-spend).
4. {needs="ai_control edit"} **At Cap** means MyCity's own monthly cap is reached. Press
   **Set Cap** to raise it.

## Plan Your Day {#qs-plan-day role="Everyone" icon=check screen=workspace/today}

![My Workspace, Today](shot:qs-plan-day)

1. Open [My Workspace · Today](/workspace?tab=today).
2. **Your Day** lists what is late and due today. Press a task to open it.
3. **From Your Manager** lists the tasks set for you.
4. Under **My Week**, press **Write Support Needed** to say what would help this week. Read
   [Today](#workspace-today).

## Add a Task {#qs-add-task role="Everyone" icon=plus screen=workspace/tasks needs="workspace create"}

![The New Task form](shot:qs-add-task)

1. Open [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks) and press **New Task**.
2. Type the **Task** and choose its **Due** date and **Priority**.
3. Press **Create Task**. It joins your board under **To Do**.
4. Drag it along the board as the work moves. Read [Add a task](#workspace-add-task).

## Send Your Commitments {#qs-commitments role="Everyone" icon=flag screen=workspace/tasks needs="workspace create"}

![Send Commitments](shot:qs-commitments)

1. On each of the month's priority tasks, press **Edit Task** and tick **Mark as Commitment**.
2. Write its **Acceptance Criteria** and press **Save Task**.
3. Press **Send Commitments** on [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks).
4. Check the **Month** and press **Send Commitments**. Read [Commitments](#workspace-commitments).

## Do Your Check-In {#qs-check-in role="Everyone" icon=check screen=ess/my-performance}

![The monthly check-in](shot:qs-check-in)

1. Open [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance) and press **Start
   Check-In**.
2. For each line, fill in the **Actual** and choose its **Evidence**.
3. Write your **Action** for anything behind, or press **Dictate** and say it.
4. Press **Save Draft**, then **Submit Check-In**. Read
   [Do your monthly check-in](#performance-check-in).

## Agree Your Scorecard {#qs-agree role="Everyone" icon=target screen=ess/my-performance}

![My Performance](shot:qs-agree)

1. Open [Employee Self-Service · My Performance](/ess?tab=my-performance).
2. Read your lines under **My Scorecard**.
3. Press **Agree Scorecard**, or **Disagree With Reason** if something is wrong. Read
   [Agree your scorecard](#performance-my-agree).

## Set Your Team's Tasks {#qs-team-tasks role="Line Managers" icon=users screen=workspace/tasks needs="workspace create, manager"}

![My Team on Tasks](shot:qs-team-tasks)

1. Open [My Workspace · Tasks](/workspace?tab=tasks) and press **My Team**.
2. Press **New Task**, then pick the person in **Assign To**.
3. Choose a **Cadence**, **Daily** or **Weekly**, and the **Due** date.
4. Press **Create Task**. They are told. Read [Add a task](#workspace-add-task).

## Approve Commitments {#qs-approve-commitments role="Line Managers" icon=ticks screen=performance/targets needs="performance approve"}

![The Commitments view](shot:qs-approve-commitments)

1. Open [Performance · Scorecards](/performance?tab=targets) and press **Commitments**.
2. Under **Waiting On You**, read each person's month.
3. Press **Approve Month**, or **Request Changes** with a reason. Read
   [Approve a month of commitments](#performance-commitments-approve).

## Score a Review {#qs-score-review role="Line Managers" icon=star screen=performance/reviews needs="manager"}

![Performance, Reviews](shot:qs-score-review)

1. Open [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews).
2. Press the review under **Waiting On You**. Its **Next** reads **Score**.
3. Check each line's **Final Score**, and add a **Corrective Action** to any line under 1.
4. Press **Submit Review**. Read [Score a review](#performance-review-score).

## Report the Month {#qs-report-month role="Line Managers" icon=chart screen=performance/goals needs="performance edit, cap goals.function"}

![Report Month on Goals](shot:qs-report-month)

1. Open [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals) and press **Report Month**.
2. Pick the **Function** and the **Month**.
3. For each key result, choose its **Status**, fill in the **Actual** and an **Update**.
4. Press **Report Month**. Read [Report the month](#performance-report).

## Set Up a Quarter {#qs-quarter role="People & HR" icon=calendar screen=performance/goals needs="performance create, cap performance.manage"}

![Set Up Quarter](shot:qs-quarter)

1. Open [Performance · Goals](/performance?tab=goals) and press **Manage Quarters**.
2. Choose **Set Up Quarter**. Check its **Starts** date and **Lock Date**.
3. Press **Next** through **Bands**, **Key Result Scoring**, **Line Scoring** and **Section
   Weights**.
4. Press **Set Up Quarter**. Read [Set up a quarter](#performance-quarter).

## Close a Review {#qs-close-review role="People & HR" icon=ticks screen=performance/reviews needs="performance approve, cap performance.manage"}

![A review with Operations and People](shot:qs-close-review)

1. Open [Performance · Reviews](/performance?tab=reviews).
2. Press a review under **Waiting On You** whose stage reads **With HR**.
3. Read the lines and the reviewer's scores.
4. Press **Close Review**, or **Request Changes** with a reason. Read
   [Approve or close a review](#performance-review-approve).
