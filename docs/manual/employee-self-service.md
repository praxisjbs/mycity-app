Employee Self-Service is your own corner of the hub. Everything in it is about you: your details,
your attendance, your leave, your documents and how you sign in. Nobody else sees it as you do.

Open it from **Employee Self-Service** in the menu on a computer, or **Self-Service** in the bar at
the bottom of a phone. Its screens are tabs along the top. On a phone, swipe the tabs sideways to
see them all.

## My Profile {#ess-profile screen=ess/my-profile icon=user}

My Profile shows your record as People keep it: your personal details, your job and your work
schedule, your bank details and your next of kin.

![My Profile](shot:ess-profile)

### Read your record {#ess-profile-read}

1. Open [My Profile](/ess?tab=my-profile). Your name, job and department are at the top, with
   **Confirmed** or **On Probation**, how you work (such as **Remote**) and who you report to.
2. **Personal** holds your **Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Phone**, **Work Email** and
   **Address**.
3. **Employment** holds your **Job Title**, **Department**, **Line Manager**, **Start Date**,
   **Employment Type**, **Work Arrangement** and **Status**.
4. Under it, **Work Schedule** lists each day of your week: **Office**, **Remote** or **Off**, and
   its hours. The hub measures lateness against these hours.
5. **Bank Details** shows your bank and the last four digits of your account. Expense refunds are
   paid into it.
6. **Next of Kin** shows who to call in an emergency.
7. **Record History** lists every change to your record, when, and who made it.

On a phone, each part is a card. Tap a card to open it.

Only People can change your job, your manager and your work schedule. If something there is wrong,
ask People and Operations.

### Change your contact, next of kin or bank {#ess-profile-edit}

1. Press **Edit Profile** at the top right.
   ![Edit Profile, the first step](shot:ess-profile-edit)
2. On **Contact**, correct your **Phone**. For your **Home Address**, press **Change**, start
   typing, and pick your address from the list, or pin it on the map. You can add **Details** such
   as a flat number. Press **Next**.
3. On **Next of Kin**, correct their **Full Name**, **Relationship** and **Phone**. Press **Next**.
4. On **Bank**, correct the **Bank**. Type a **New Account Number** of ten digits only if your
   account changed; leave it empty to keep the one on record. Press **Next**.
5. On **Review**, check what will change. Each line shows what it was and what it becomes.
6. Press **Save Changes**. The message **Profile saved** confirms it.

A bank change is logged and People are told. The message then reads
**Saved. People are told of the bank change.**

| The field says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Not a phone number** | Not a Nigerian mobile, or a foreign number without its country code | Type it as `0803 123 4567` |
| **Check the format** | The account number is not ten digits | Type all ten digits, with no spaces |

### Add your photo {#ess-profile-photo}

1. Press the round picture with your initials, at the top left of your profile.
2. Press **Choose a Photo** and pick a clear photo of your face: JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 5 MB.
3. The hub crops it square and shows it at once. The message **Photo updated** confirms it.
4. To take it off, press the picture again, then **Remove Photo**.

Colleagues see your photo wherever your name appears.

### Download a copy of your data {#ess-profile-data}

1. Press **Download My Data**.
2. Wait while the button spins. The file is saved to your device, and the message
   **Your data downloaded** confirms it.

You can make one copy a day. A second try the same day says
**Your copy was already made today. Try tomorrow.**

To change your password, press **Change Password**. It opens [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security).

## My Attendance {#ess-attendance screen=ess/my-attendance icon=clock}

My Attendance is your clock: you clock in when you start work and clock out when you finish. It also
shows every day of the month, how it went, and any deduction or overtime it earned.

![My Attendance](shot:ess-attendance)

### Clock in and out {#ess-attendance-clock}

On your first sign-in of a working day, the hub opens the clock by itself and asks you to
**Start Your Day**. At the end of your shift, it asks you to **Finish Your Day**.

1. Open the clock: press **Clock In** at the top right of a computer screen, or the round button
   with a plus on a phone, then **Clock In**. On [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance), the
   **Today** card has the same button.
   ![The clock](shot:ess-clock)
2. Check what the clock shows: your **Shift**, your **Work Arrangement**, your **Clock Location**
   (the office or site your clock-in is matched to) and **This Device**.
3. Press **Clock In**.
4. If your browser asks to use your location, allow it. The hub reads your location once, at this
   moment. It does not follow you during the day.
5. The clock shows the result: the time you clocked in, and where. Press **Done**.
6. At the end of the day, open the clock again and press **Clock Out**.

At the end of your shift, if you are still working, press **Remind Me** and choose when the clock
should come back: in 15 minutes, in 30 minutes or in an hour.

> **Important:** Clock in from your phone. A computer cannot place you closely enough, so the hub
> may send a clock-in from a computer to People to check.

### When the clock cannot place you {#ess-attendance-location}

| The clock says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Share Your Location** | The hub needs your location to match you to your office | Press **Allow Location**, then allow it when your browser asks |
| **Finding Your Location** | It is reading your location | Wait a few seconds |
| **Weak Location Signal** | Your phone's reading is too rough | Move near a window or outside, then press **Retry Location** |
| **Location Not Found** | No reading came | Move near a window or outside, then press **Retry Location** |
| **Location Blocked** | Your browser is set to refuse location | Follow the steps the clock shows to allow it, then try again |
| **Precise Location Off** | Your phone shares only a rough location | Follow the steps the clock shows to turn on precise location |
| **Use Your Phone** | You are on a computer | Clock in from your phone |
| **No Approved Home** | You work from home today and People have not approved your home address | Press **Set Home** and send your home for approval |
| **No Office Assigned** | People have not given you an office | Ask People to set your worksite |

If the reading still cannot place you, the clock offers to clock you in anyway. Your clock-in then
goes to People to check, with a note you can write.

### What the result means {#ess-attendance-result}

| The clock shows | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Clocked In** or **Clocked Out**, with the time | Recorded and counted | Nothing. Press **Done** |
| **Review Required** | Recorded, and sent to People to check, with the reason | Type what happened in **Note for People** and press **Send Note**, or press **Skip** |
| **Not Counted** | Refused, with the reason, such as a device that is not registered | Press **Register Device** if it says so, then **Try Again** |
| **Not Recorded** | The hub refused the press, such as **You are already clocked in.** | Read the reason. Press **Close** |
| **No Connection** | Your device lost the internet before the hub answered | Reconnect, then press **Try Again**. You will not clock in twice |
| **Day Complete** | You have clocked in and out today | Nothing more to do today |

If you forget to clock out, the hub clocks you out at the end of your shift. That day reads
**System Clock-Out**. Open the day and press **Give Real Time** to tell People when you really left.

### Read your month {#ess-attendance-month}

1. Open [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance).
2. Use the arrows beside the month to see another month, or press its name to pick one.
3. **Today** shows your clock-in and clock-out today and where.
4. The four cards count this month's **Days Present**, **Late Days**, **Absent** days and
   **Hours Worked**. Press one of the first three to see those days.
5. **Deductions** lists the days a late arrival or an early departure cost pay, and **Overtime**
   the days you worked longer. Each shows its minutes, its amount and its status, such as
   **Pending**.
6. **My Days** lists each day: **Clock In**, **Clock Out**, **Hours** and **Status**. Choose
   **Chart** to see your hours as bars, with late days standing out.
7. Press a day to see its punches, where each was made and its **Result**.
8. Press **Export Month** to save the month as a file.

If a punch needs your word, **Open Exceptions** lists it. Press it, write **Your Note** and press
**Send Note**. People read it when they decide.

## Work Location Requests {#ess-work-location screen=ess/work-location-requests icon=pin}

Here you ask to work somewhere other than your usual place for a few days, and follow your requests.
Once People approve one, you clock in there on those days.

![Work Location Requests](shot:ess-work-location)

### Ask to work somewhere else {#ess-work-location-add}

1. Open [Work Location Requests](/ess?tab=work-location-requests).
2. Press **Add Work Site**. The form is the same as on Home: follow
   [Add a work site](#home-overview-work-site).
3. Press **Submit for Approval**. The request shows under **My Temporary Sites** as
   **Awaiting Approval**.

### Follow a request {#ess-work-location-follow}

1. Press a site under **My Temporary Sites**. It opens with its **Purpose**, **Hours**,
   **First Day**, **Last Day**, its addresses on a map, the **People** on it, and who it is
   **Waiting On**.
2. The state reads **Awaiting Approval**, then **Upcoming** once approved, **In Progress** on its
   days, and **Done** after. **Rejected** means People said no, with their reason.
3. To take back a request still waiting, press **Withdraw Submission**, type the **Reason** and
   press **Withdraw Submission** again.

**My Requests** under it lists requests made in the older way, with their status. Open one to
withdraw or cancel it.

## My Devices {#ess-devices screen=ess/my-devices icon=phone}

The devices you clock in from. You can register up to three. A clock-in from a device you have not
registered may go to People to check, or not count at all, depending on MyCity's rules.

![My Devices](shot:ess-devices)

### Register this device {#ess-devices-register}

Register your own phone, on the phone itself.

1. Open [My Devices](/ess?tab=my-devices) on the device you want to register.
2. Press **Register This Device**.
3. Give it a **Device Name**, such as `Work Phone`, if you like.
4. Press **Register Device**. If the hub asks **Confirm It's You**, type your password or PIN.
5. The message **Device registered** confirms it. It now reads **Registered**.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **New** | Seen on a clock-in, not registered |
| **Registered** | One of your three |
| **Removed** | Taken off. It can be registered again |

If you already have three, the screen says
**You have three registered devices. Remove one to add this one.** Press an old device, then
**Remove Device**, and confirm.

## Policy Updates {#ess-policies screen=ess/policy-updates icon=book}

Every MyCity policy that applies to you, and the ones waiting for you to read and acknowledge.

![Policy Updates](shot:ess-policies)

### Acknowledge a policy {#ess-policies-acknowledge}

When a policy is new or changes, the hub shows **Policy Update** once you have clocked in. It names
the policy, its version, when it is due, and what changed.

![A policy to acknowledge](shot:ess-policy-prompt)

1. Press **Read Full Policy** to read it in full. It opens on
   [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates).
2. Read it. The list under its version says what changed since the version you last acknowledged.
3. Press **Acknowledge**. The message names the policy, such as *Data Protection acknowledged*.

**Later** puts it off for now, when the policy allows it. Past its due date it reads **Overdue**,
and the hub asks again at your next sign-in.

### Find a policy {#ess-policies-find}

1. Open [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates).
2. Choose **To Acknowledge** for those waiting on you, or **All Policies** for every policy that
   applies to you. **All Acknowledged** means nothing is waiting.
3. Type in **Search policies** to find one by name.
4. Press a policy to read it. **Download PDF** saves a copy with MyCity's letterhead.

**Waiting On You** lists the policies you are asked to approve before they are published, when a
policy names you as one of its approvers. Open one, read it, and press **Approve Policy** or
**Reject Policy**.

## My Leave {#ess-leave screen=ess/my-leave icon=calendar}

Your leave this year: what you are entitled to, what you have taken and booked, and your requests.

![My Leave](shot:ess-leave)

### Read your leave {#ess-leave-read}

1. Open [My Leave](/ess?tab=my-leave).
2. The four cards show your Annual leave: **Entitlement**, **Taken**, **Approved Upcoming** (with
   its dates) and **Remaining**.
3. **My Requests** lists every request: its **Type**, **From**, **To**, **Days**, **Approver** and
   **Status**.
4. **Entitlement** shows each type of leave with what you have taken, booked and left.
   **No set allowance** means the type has no yearly limit.
5. Press **Leave Calendar** to see a month of leave: yours marked **You**, and your team's. Leave
   still waiting for approval is marked **Waiting**.

On a phone, tap **My Requests** or **Entitlement** to open them.

### Request leave {#ess-leave-request needs="ess create"}

1. Press **Request Leave** at the top right of [My Leave](/ess?tab=my-leave).
   ![Request Leave](shot:ess-request-leave)
2. Pick the **Leave Type**. Each type shows how many days you have left, such as
   *Annual · 12 days left*.
3. Choose the **First Day** and the **Last Day**. Count only the days you will be away; weekends and
   public holidays are not counted.
4. Add a **Note** for your manager if it helps, such as `Family event`.
5. Press **Send Request**. The message **Sent to your line manager** confirms it.

Your request reads **Submitted** until your line manager decides. You are told when they do.

| The field says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | It cannot be left empty | Pick a type or a date |
| **Already past** | The first day is before today | Pick today or a later day |
| **Ends before it starts** | The last day is before the first | Correct the last day |
| **Ends in another year** | The leave runs past 31 December | Make two requests, one for each year |
| **No working days** | Every day picked is a weekend or a public holiday | Pick days you would be working |
| **Overlaps other leave** | You already asked for leave on some of those days | Change the days, or cancel the other request |
| **More than you have left** | You do not have that many days left this year | Ask for fewer days, or another type |

### Cancel a request {#ess-leave-cancel}

You can cancel a request while it waits, or approved leave before it starts.

1. Press the request in **My Requests**.
2. Press **Cancel Request**. The message **Request cancelled** confirms it.

If it says **This leave can no longer be cancelled**, the leave has started. Ask People and
Operations.

## My Documents {#ess-documents screen=ess/my-documents icon=doc}

Your document vault: your contract, certificates, ID and anything People asked you for.

![My Documents](shot:ess-documents)

### Read your documents {#ess-documents-read}

1. Open [My Documents](/ess?tab=my-documents).
2. The figures count your **Documents**, and those **Signed**, **Verified**, **Expiring** and
   **Missing**.
3. **Vault** lists each document: when it was **Uploaded**, its **Status** and when it **Expires**.
4. Press a document to see it. **View File** opens it full screen. **Download** saves a copy.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Signed** | Signed by you and MyCity |
| **Verified** | People checked it |
| **Awaiting Check** | You uploaded it, and People have not checked it yet |
| **Expiring** | It expires soon. Upload a new one before it does |
| **Missing** | People asked for it and it is not uploaded yet |

### Upload a document {#ess-documents-upload}

1. Press **Upload Document**. For a document marked **Missing**, press it and then
   **Upload Document**, so it answers People's request.
2. Say **What Is It**, such as `NDPA certificate`.
3. Drop the file on the box, or press it and choose the file: a PDF or a picture, up to 25 MB.
4. The message **Uploaded. People will check it** confirms it. It reads **Awaiting Check** until
   they do.

## My Security {#ess-security screen=ess/my-security icon=shield}

How you sign in, and where you are signed in: your password, two-step sign-in, passkeys, your PIN,
and your devices.

![My Security](shot:ess-security)

On a phone each part is a card. Tap one to open it.

![My Security on a phone](shot:ess-security-phone)

### Change your password {#ess-security-password}

1. Open [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security) and press **Change Password**.
2. Type your **Current Password**.
3. Type a **New Password**: at least 8 characters, with a capital letter, a small letter, a number
   and a symbol. Type it again in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Press **Change Password**. The message **Password changed** confirms it.

Your other devices are signed out, so sign in there again with the new password.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not your password** | Type your current password again. Use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen if you do not know it |
| **Same as current** | Choose a password you have not used here |
| **Does not match** | Type the new password the same way twice |

### Set up two-step sign-in {#ess-security-two-step}

Two-step sign-in asks for a code from an authenticator app on your phone after your password.

1. Press **Set Up Two-Step**. If the hub asks **Confirm It's You**, type your password or PIN.
2. Open your authenticator app and scan the square code. Or type the key shown under it.
3. Type the six digits the app shows in **Code From App** and press **Turn On**.
4. The hub shows ten recovery codes. Press **Copy Codes** and keep them safe, away from your phone.
   Each one signs you in once if you lose your phone.

Once it is on:

- **Ask for a Code** chooses how often: **Every Sign-In**, **Once a Day** or **Once a Month**.
- **New Codes** makes ten new recovery codes. The old ones stop working.
- **Change App** moves two-step to a new phone.
- **Turn Off** switches it off. It is not offered when your role requires two-step.

### Set a PIN {#ess-security-pin}

A PIN is four digits that unlock the hub, and sign you in on a device you have signed in on before.

1. Press **Set PIN**.
2. Type four digits in **New PIN** and again in **Confirm PIN**.
3. Press **Save PIN**. The message **PIN saved** confirms it.

**Change PIN** sets a new one. **Remove PIN** takes it off.

### Add a passkey {#ess-security-passkey}

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a password.

1. Under **Passkeys**, press **Add Passkey** on the device you want to use.
2. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock when your device asks.
3. The message **Passkey added** confirms it.

Press a passkey to rename it or to **Remove Passkey**.

### Sign out a device {#ess-security-sessions}

1. Under **Signed In**, find the device. **This Device** is the one you are using.
2. Press **Sign Out** beside another device, and confirm. Or press **Sign Out Others** to sign out
   every device but this one.

**Recent Sign-Ins** lists every sign-in: when, on which device, how, and from where. If you see one
that was not you, change your password at once and tell People and Operations.

## Org Chart {#ess-org-chart screen=ess/org-chart icon=users}

Who works at MyCity, and who reports to whom.

![The Org Chart](shot:ess-org-chart)

### Find someone {#ess-org-chart-find}

1. Open [Org Chart](/ess?tab=org-chart). The line under the title counts the seats, those filled and
   those open.
2. Type a name or a job in **Name or role**.
3. On a computer, the chart is a tree from the CEO down. Press **Collapse** or **Expand** on a card to
   fold its team away or open it.
4. On a phone, **Focus** shows one person with their manager, their reports and the people who share
   their manager. Tap a card to move to that person. **Everyone** shows the whole company as a list.
5. Press a person to see their **Department**, who they **Reports To**, and their team. Press
   **Send Message** to write to them in Staff Community.

A solid line is the reporting line. A dashed line is a **Dotted Line**: a second manager for shared
work, who does not approve leave or expenses. A dashed card is an open role nobody holds yet.

Press **Download PDF** to save the chart as a file.
