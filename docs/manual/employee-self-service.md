Employee Self-Service is your own corner of the hub. Everything in it is about you: your details,
your attendance, your leave, your documents and how you sign in. Nobody else sees it as you do.

Open it from **Employee Self-Service** in the menu on a computer, or **Self-Service** in the bar at
the bottom of a phone. Its screens are tabs along the top. On a phone, swipe the tabs sideways to
see them all.

## My Profile {#ess-profile screen=ess/my-profile icon=user}

My Profile shows your record as People keep it: your personal details, your job and your work
schedule, your bank details and your next of kin.

![My Profile](shot:ess-profile)

### Read your record {#ess-profile-read}

1. Open [My Profile](/ess?tab=my-profile). Your name, job and department are at the top, with
   **Confirmed** or **On Probation**, how you work (such as **Remote**) and who you report to.
2. **Personal** holds your **Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Phone**, **Work Email** and
   **Address**.
3. **Employment** holds your **Job Title**, **Department**, **Line Manager**, **Start Date**,
   **Employment Type**, **Work Arrangement** and **Status**.
4. Under it, **Work Schedule** lists each day of your week: **Office**, **Remote** or **Off**, and
   its hours. The hub measures lateness against these hours.
5. **Bank Details** shows your bank and the last four digits of your account. Expense refunds are
   paid into it.
6. **Next of Kin** shows who to call in an emergency.
7. **Record History** lists every change to your record, when, and who made it.

On a phone, each part is a card. Tap a card to open it.

Only People can change your job, your manager and your work schedule. If something there is wrong,
ask People and Operations.

### Change your contact, next of kin or bank {#ess-profile-edit}

1. Press **Edit Profile** at the top right.
   ![Edit Profile, the first step](shot:ess-profile-edit)
2. On **Contact**, correct your **Phone**. For your **Home Address**, press **Change**, start
   typing, and pick your address from the list, or pin it on the map. You can add **Details** such
   as a flat number. Press **Next**.
3. On **Next of Kin**, correct their **Full Name**, **Relationship** and **Phone**. Press **Next**.
4. On **Bank**, the step shows your **Bank on Record** and **Account** first. Correct the **Bank**.
   Type a **New Account Number** of ten digits only if your account changed; leave it empty to keep
   the one on record. Press **Next**.
5. On **Review**, check what will change. Each line shows what it was and what it becomes. If
   nothing changed, it says so and the button reads **Close**.
6. Press **Save Changes**. A message says **Profile saved**.

A bank change is logged and People are told. The message then reads
**Saved. People are told of the bank change.**

| It says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Not a phone number** | Not a Nigerian mobile, or a foreign number without its country code | Type it as `0803 123 4567` |
| **Ten digits** | The account number is not ten digits | Type all ten digits, with no spaces |
| **Not saved. Try again.** | The connection failed | Press **Save Changes** again |

To change your password, press **Change Password**. It opens [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security)
with **Change Password** ready. Follow [Change your password](#ess-security-password).

### Add your photo {#ess-profile-photo}

1. Press the round picture at the top left of your profile: your initials with a plus
   (**Add Photo**), or your photo with a pencil (**Change Photo**). **Profile Photo** opens.
2. Press **Choose a Photo** and pick a clear photo of your face: JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 5 MB.
3. The hub crops it square and shows it at once. A message says **Photo updated**.
4. To take it off, press the picture, then **Remove Photo**, and confirm with **Remove Photo**. A
   message says **Photo removed**, and your initials show again.

Colleagues see your photo wherever your name appears.

| It says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Larger than 5 MB* | The photo is too big | Choose a smaller photo |
| **This file type is not accepted here.** | It is not a JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC picture | Choose a photo in one of those types |
| **Not removed. Try again.** | The connection failed | Press **Remove Photo** again |

### Download a copy of your data {#ess-profile-data}

1. Press **Download My Data**.
2. Wait while the button spins. You can move to another screen meanwhile; keep the hub open. The
   file is saved to your device, and a message says **Your data downloaded**.

One copy is made a day. Pressing again the same day downloads that same copy again. If it says
**A copy of your data was made for People today. Ask again tomorrow.**, someone in People or the CEO already made a copy of your
data today, and that copy is theirs. Try again tomorrow. A copy that failed does not count.

If it says **That file could not be made. Try again.** or **That file is taking too long. Try
again.**, press **Download My Data** again.

## My Attendance {#ess-attendance screen=ess/my-attendance icon=clock}

My Attendance is your clock: you clock in when you start work and clock out when you finish. It also
shows every day of the month, how it went, and any deduction or overtime it earned.

![My Attendance](shot:ess-attendance)

### Clock in and out {#ess-attendance-clock}

On your first sign-in of a working day, the hub opens the clock by itself and asks you to
**Start Your Day**. It also opens it at the start of your shift if you have not clocked in. At the
end of your shift, it asks you to **Finish Your Day**.

1. Open the clock: press **Clock In** at the top right of a computer screen, or **Quick Actions**,
   the round button with a plus on a phone, then **Clock In**. On
   [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance), the **Today** card has the same button.
   ![The clock](shot:ess-clock)
2. Check what the clock shows: your **Shift**, your **Work Arrangement**, your **Clock Location**
   (the office or site your clock-in is matched to) and **This Device**, **Registered** or
   **Not Registered**. The pill under the time says **Not Clocked In**, the time you clocked in, or
   **Review Required**.
3. Press **Clock In**.
4. If your browser asks to use your location, allow it. The hub reads your location once, at this
   moment. It does not follow you during the day. While it reads, the clock says
   **Finding Your Location**, then **Processing**.
5. The clock shows the result: the time you clocked in, and where. Press **Done**.
6. At the end of the day, open the clock again and press **Clock Out**.

Press **Not Now** to close the clock without clocking in. When the hub opened it by itself, tapping
outside does not close it.

At the end of your shift, **Finish Your Day** offers **Clock Out** and **Remind Me**. If you are
still working, press **Remind Me**, then *In 15 Minutes*, *In 30 Minutes* or **In 1 Hour**. A
message names the time, such as *Reminder set for 17:30*. If you close it without choosing, it
comes back in 15 minutes.

Once you have clocked out, the clock shows **Day Complete** with your times, or
**System Clock-Out** if the hub clocked you out. **My Attendance** opens your month. The button at
the top then reads **Done For Today**: press it (or the clock in **Quick Actions** on a phone) to
see the day's times again. It punches nothing. On a leave day it reads **On Leave Today**.

> **Important:** Clock in from your phone. A computer cannot place you closely enough, so the hub
> may send a clock-in from a computer to People to check.

### Check in at each stop {#ess-attendance-check-in}

On a day you work at an approved temporary site, the clock shows **Temporary Site** with its number,
and its **Address** or **Addresses**. Clock in once, at your first stop. Then check in at each stop.

1. Clock in as usual. Follow [Clock in and out](#ess-attendance-clock).
2. At your next stop, open the clock and press **Check In**. Allow your location if asked.
3. Wait while it reads **Finding Your Location**. A line confirms it, such as
   *Checked in at Main Hall.*
4. Each check-in is listed under the button: its time, its address, and **Inside**, **Recorded** or
   **Flagged**.
5. Press **Check In** again at the next stop. Clock out at the end of the day as usual.

**Flagged** means you were outside every address of the site, or your reading could not place you.
People check it, like a clock-in under review. A check-in never changes your clock-in or clock-out
time.

To see the whole day later, open the day on [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance). **Route** lists
your clock-in, each check-in and your clock-out in time order.

| The clock says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Check-in not sent yet.** | Your device lost the internet | Reconnect, then press **Try Again**. It is not recorded twice |
| **Clock in before you check in.** | You are not clocked in now, or the hub clocked you out | Clock in first |
| **Check In is for a temporary site's day.** | You are no longer on an approved site today | Clock in and out as usual. Ask People if this is wrong |
| *At most 20 check-ins a day.* | You reached the day's limit | Nothing more is recorded today |

### When the clock cannot place you {#ess-attendance-location}

| The clock says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Share Your Location** | The hub needs your location to match you to your office | Press **Allow Location**, then allow it when your browser asks |
| **Finding Your Location** | It is reading your location | Wait a few seconds |
| **Weak Location Signal** | Your phone's reading is too rough | Move near a window or outside, then press **Retry Location** |
| **Location Not Found** | No reading came | Move near a window or outside, then press **Retry Location** |
| **Location Blocked** | Your browser is set to refuse location | Follow the steps the clock shows to allow it, then try again |
| **Precise Location Off** | Your phone shares only a rough location | Follow the steps the clock shows to turn on precise location |
| **Use Your Phone** | You are on a computer | Clock in from your phone |
| **Secure Address Needed** | The hub was opened from an address that is not secure | Open the address the clock shows, then clock in |
| **No Location Support** | This browser cannot share a location | Clock in from your phone, or press **Clock In Anyway** |
| **No Approved Home**, and **No approved home location yet.** | You work from home today and have no approved home | Press **Set Home** and follow [Set your home location](#ess-attendance-home). You can still clock in; People check it |
| **Home Awaiting Approval**, and **Your home location is waiting for approval.** | People have not decided your home yet | Clock in. People check it until they approve your home |
| **Not Checked** beside **Clock Location** | MyCity does not check where you work from home | Press **Clock In**. No location is asked |
| **No Office Assigned**, and **No office assigned yet.** | People have not given you an office | Clock in; People check it. Ask People to set your worksite |
| **This device is not registered.** | Your clock-in from this device goes to People to check | Press **Register Device** and follow [Register this device](#ess-devices-register) |
| **Register this device to clock in.** | Clock-ins from devices you have not registered do not count | Press **Register Device**, register it, then clock in |

If the reading still cannot place you, press **Clock In Anyway** (or *Clock Out Anyway*). Your
punch then goes to People to check, with a note you can write.

### What the result means {#ess-attendance-result}

| The clock shows | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Clocked In** or **Clocked Out**, with the time | Recorded and counted | Nothing. Press **Done** |
| **Review Required** | Recorded, and sent to People to check, with the reason | Type what happened in **Note for People** and press **Send Note**. A message says **Note sent to People**. Or press **Skip** and explain later from **Open Exceptions** |
| **Not Counted** | Refused, with the reason, such as a device that is not registered | If it names the device, press **Register Device**, register it on My Devices, then open the clock and press **Clock In** again. Otherwise fix what it says and press **Try Again** |
| **Not Recorded** | The hub refused the press, such as **You are already clocked in.** | Read the reason. Press **Close** |
| **Not Recorded**: **You have clocked out for today.** | Your day already has a clock-in and a clock-out | Nothing more today |
| **Not Recorded**: **You have already clocked out.** or **You have not clocked in yet.** | Another device, or the hub, punched first | Press **Close** and open the clock again |
| **No Connection** | Your device lost the internet before the hub answered | Reconnect, then press **Try Again**. You will not clock in twice |
| **On Leave**, with your leave's dates | You are on approved leave today | Nothing. Press **Close** |
| **Not Available** | Only people who work at MyCity now can clock in | Ask People if this is wrong |
| **Not Loaded** | The clock could not read today | Press **Try Again**, or **Not Now** and open it later |
| **Note not sent. Try again.** | Your note did not reach People | Check your connection and press **Send Note** again |

If you are still clocked in after your shift ends, the hub reminds you with **Time to clock out**,
every 30 minutes. About 90 minutes after the end, it clocks you out for you. That day reads
**System Clock-Out**, and only the hours to your shift's end count. MyCity's Attendance policy sets
both times.

### Give the time you really left {#ess-attendance-real-time}

1. Open [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance) and press the day that reads
   **System Clock-Out**.
2. Press **Give Real Time** at the foot of the day.
3. Choose the **Real Time** you left.
4. Say what happened in **Your Note**, such as `Left at 16:00, forgot to clock out`.
5. Press **Send Time**. A message says **Time sent to People**.

People keep the system's time or correct it to yours. You are told: **Attendance reviewed**. Once
sent, the day shows **Real Time Given** with your time in place of the button: it is given once.

| The form says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | A field is empty | Choose the time and write a note |
| **After your clock-in** | The time is before you clocked in, or still to come | Give a time between your clock-in and now |
| **You have already given the real time** | You sent it before | Nothing more. People decide from your first one |
| **Time not sent** | The connection failed | Press **Send Time** again |

### Set your home location {#ess-attendance-home}

The **Home Location** card shows on My Attendance only when MyCity checks homes and your week has
days you work from home. Your clock-ins on those days are matched to the home People approve.

1. Go to where you work from home, and open [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance). Or press
   **Set Home** on the clock.
2. Press **Set Home Location** on the **Home Location** card. If a home is approved or waiting, it
   reads **Update Home**.
3. In **Set Home Location**, the step reads **At Home Now**. Press **Use This Location**. Allow your
   location if the browser asks.
4. Wait for **Location Found**. It says how accurate the reading is.
5. In **Address**, start typing your home address and pick it from the list.
6. Press **Send for Approval**. A message says **Home location sent for approval**.

The card now reads **Waiting**, with your address under **Sent for Approval**. People approve or
reject it, and you are told: **Home location approved** or **Home location rejected**. Once
approved, it shows under **Approved Home** and the card reads **Approved**.

If you move, send the new home the same way. Your approved home stays in use until People approve
the new one. A new home sent while one waits replaces the waiting one.

If the card reads **Rejected**, People's reason is beside it. Put right what they say, then press
**Set Home Location** again. The notice **Set your home location** opens this sheet for you.

| The sheet says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Weak Location Signal** | The reading is too rough to set a home | Move near a window, then press **Retry Location** |
| **Location Not Found** | No reading came | Move near a window or outside, then press **Retry Location** |
| **Location Blocked** or **Precise Location Off** | Your browser or phone is not sharing an exact location | Follow the steps shown, then press **Retry Location** |
| **No Location Support** | This browser cannot share a location | Open the hub on your phone and try there |
| **Pick an address** | No address was picked | Start typing and pick your address from the list |
| **The location reading was not accurate enough.** | The reading was too rough when sent | Press **Retry Location**, then send again |
| **Home locations are off in the Attendance policy.** | MyCity stopped checking homes | Nothing to do. Press **Not Now** |
| **Home location not sent** | The connection failed | Check your connection and press **Send for Approval** again |

### Read your month {#ess-attendance-month}

1. Open [My Attendance](/ess?tab=my-attendance).
2. Use the arrows beside the month to see another month, or press its name to pick one. You can go
   back two years.
3. **Today** shows your clock-in and clock-out today and where: your office, **Approved Home**,
   **Temporary Home**, **Remote** or **No Worksite**.
4. The four figures count the month. **Days Present** includes late days. **Late Days** says what
   share of your days present were late. **Absent** counts working days with no clock-in.
   **Hours Worked** runs from each first clock-in to the last clock-out; a System Clock-Out counts
   only to your shift's end.
5. Press **Days Present**, **Late Days** or **Absent** to list those days: **Date**, **Clock In**,
   **Clock Out**, **Hours** and **Status**. **No Days** means there were none that month.
   **Hours Worked** does not open.
6. **Temporary Sites** lists your work sites that wait, are in progress, or are still to come:
   **Site**, **Dates** and **State**. Press one to open it on
   [Work Location Requests](/ess?tab=work-location-requests).
7. **Field Assignments** shows only while People have you on one made before temporary sites
   existed: the **Assignment**, its **Dates** and its **Site**. Press one to see its **Client**,
   each site's **Address** and **Radius**, and the **People** on it, **On It** or **Taken Off**.
   You cannot change it. Ask People and Operations.
8. **Deductions** lists the days a late arrival, an early departure or an absence cost pay.
   **Overtime** lists the days you worked past your shift. Each card shows only when the month has
   one. Each row shows its **Minutes**, its **Amount** and its **Status**. Press a row to see how it
   was worked out (**Method**), and any **Reason** and who decided it.
9. **My Days** lists each day: **Clock In**, **Clock Out**, **Hours** and **Status**. On a computer,
   **Review** counts a day's open exceptions. Choose **Chart** to see your hours as bars, with late
   days standing out.
10. Press a day to see its **Status**, **Hours**, and **Late By**, **Left Early By** or
    **Overtime** when they apply. **Punches** lists each **Punch**, its **Time**, **Location**,
    **Device** and **Result**.
11. Press **Export Month** to save the month as a spreadsheet. The button spins while the file is
    made, then a message says **Export downloaded**.

| A day's Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Present** | Clocked in on time |
| *Late 12m* | Clocked in after your start time and its grace minutes, by that much |
| **Absent** | A working day closed with no clock-in |
| **On Leave** | Approved leave |
| **Not In** | A working day, no clock-in yet |
| **Day Off** | Not a working day for you |
| **Not Counted** | The day has no count yet |

A punch's **Location** reads **Inside** or **Outside** with the distance, **Unclear** with why (a
rough reading, at the edge, no home, no office or no reading), or **Not Checked**. Its **Result**
is **Accepted**, **Review** (People will check it), **Not Counted** (refused at the clock),
**Rejected** (People rejected it) or **System Clock-Out**.

| A deduction or overtime Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting. A deduction may still be waived; overtime may still be confirmed |
| **Waived** | People cancelled the deduction. Your pay is not cut |
| **Confirmed** | The overtime is agreed and will be paid |
| **Cut** | The overtime will not be paid. The reason is in the row |
| **In Payroll** | Taken into a payroll run. It no longer changes |

**No Salary Set** in **Amount** means your salary is not on your record yet.

If **Export Month** says **That file could not be made. Try again.** or **That file is taking too
long. Try again.**, press it again.

On a phone, the figures are two to a row and each day is a card. Tap a card to open the day as a
sheet; its **Device**, and a deduction's **Method**, show there.

### Explain an exception {#ess-attendance-explain}

**Open Exceptions** lists the punches waiting for People's decision. **Explain** means People have
no note from you yet; **Sent** means they have.

1. Press the exception in **Open Exceptions**.
2. Write what happened in **Your Note**, such as `Client visit in Ikeja`.
3. Press **Send Note**. A message says **Note sent to People**.

| Exception | What it means | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| **Outside Worksite** or **Outside Home** | You were outside the place's circle | Where you were, and why |
| **Location Unclear** | The reading could not prove where you were | Where you were |
| **Unknown Device** | The device is not registered | Which device, then register it on [My Devices](/ess?tab=my-devices) |
| **Device Time Ahead** | Your device's clock was ahead of the hub's | Set your device's time to automatic |
| **Missing Clock-Out** | No clock-out was made that day | The time you left |
| **System Clock-Out** | The hub clocked you out | The time you left, or use **Give Real Time** |

If it says **This exception was already decided**, People decided before your note arrived. You are
told the outcome. **Note not sent. Try again.** means the connection failed: press **Send Note** again.

### Notices about your attendance {#ess-attendance-notices}

| Notice | What it means | Pressing it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Time to clock in** | Your shift has started and you have not clocked in | The clock |
| **Time to clock out** | Your shift has ended and you are still clocked in | The clock |
| **System Clock-Out** | The hub clocked you out | The day, with **Give Real Time** |
| **Attendance needs review** | A punch went to People to check | The day |
| **Attendance reviewed** | People decided an exception | The day |
| **Late deduction**, **Early departure deduction**, **Absence deduction** | A deduction was added | The day |
| **Deduction waived** | People waived it, with their reason | The day |
| **Overtime recorded**, **Overtime confirmed**, **Overtime cut**, **Overtime approved** | Your overtime changed | The day |
| **Set your home location**, **Home location approved**, **Home location rejected** | Your home location | My Attendance, with **Set Home Location** when needed |
| **Put on a Temporary Site**, **Temporary Site Approved**, **Temporary Site Rejected**, **Temporary Site Cancelled**, **Taken off a Temporary Site** | A site you are on changed | The site on Work Location Requests |
| **Work schedule set** | People set your week | [My Profile](/ess?tab=my-profile) |

## Work Location Requests {#ess-work-location screen=ess/work-location-requests icon=pin}

Here you ask to work somewhere other than your usual place for a few days, and follow your requests.
Once People approve one, you clock in there on those days.

![Work Location Requests](shot:ess-work-location)

### Ask to work somewhere else {#ess-work-location-add}

1. Open [Work Location Requests](/ess?tab=work-location-requests).
2. Press **Add Work Site** at the top right. On a phone, it is under your sites, and in
   **Quick Actions**. The form is the same as on Home: follow
   [Add a work site](#home-overview-work-site).
3. Press **Submit for Approval**. The request shows under **My Temporary Sites** as
   **Awaiting Approval**.

### Follow a request {#ess-work-location-follow}

![A work site opened](shot:ess-site)

1. Press a site under **My Temporary Sites**. Sites a colleague put you on are listed too. It shows
   its **Purpose**, **Hours**, **First Day**, **Last Day** and **Days**, who it was **Added By**, and
   who it is **Waiting On**. Each address shows its **Label**, **Address** and **Radius** on a map.
   The **People** on it read **On It**, or **Taken Off** with People's reason.
2. **Approval** shows each step and each decision, with any reason.
3. The state reads **Awaiting Approval**, then **Upcoming** once approved, **In Progress** on its
   days, and **Done** after. **Rejected** means People said no. **Withdrawn** means it was taken
   back. **Cancelled** means People ended it early; **Closed** and **Reason** say when and why.
4. To take back a request still waiting, press **Withdraw Submission**, type the **Reason** and
   press **Withdraw Submission** again. The message names the site, such as *TS-0012 withdrawn*.
   **Keep It** closes without withdrawing. Only the person who added a site can withdraw it.

| It says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **At least 10 characters** | The reason is too short | Say a little more, such as `Client moved the visit` |
| **This site no longer waits on approval** | People decided it first | Read its state. Ask People to cancel an approved one |
| **Not changed. Try again.** | The connection failed | Press **Withdraw Submission** again |

### Withdraw or cancel an older request {#ess-work-location-older}

**My Requests** shows only while you have requests made before work sites existed: a
**Temporary Home**, a **Remote Day** or a **Client Visit**. Each shows its **Dates**, its **Status**
(**Waiting**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Cancelled**) and, on a computer, its **Place** and who
it is **Waiting On**.

1. Press the request in **My Requests**.
2. Press **Withdraw Request** while it waits, or **Cancel Request** if approved and not yet started.
3. Confirm with the same button. **Keep It** leaves it as it is.
4. A message says **Request withdrawn** or **Request cancelled**.

If it says **This request can no longer be cancelled**, it was decided or has started. Ask People.

## My Devices {#ess-devices screen=ess/my-devices icon=phone}

The devices you clock in from. You can register up to three. A clock-in from a device you have not
registered may go to People to check, or not count at all, depending on MyCity's rules.

![My Devices](shot:ess-devices)

### Register this device {#ess-devices-register}

Register your own phone, on the phone itself.

1. Open [My Devices](/ess?tab=my-devices) on the device you want to register.
2. Press **Register This Device** at the top right. On a phone it is under the title, and in
   **Quick Actions**. It shows only on a device not yet registered.
3. Give it a **Device Name**, such as `Work Phone`, if you like.
4. Press **Register Device**. If the hub asks **Confirm It's You**, follow
   [Confirm it's you](#start-confirm-you).
5. A message says **Device registered**. It now reads **Registered**.

**Devices** lists each **Device**, with **This Device** on the one you are using, its **Status**,
when it was **First Seen** and, on a computer, its **Last Punch**.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **New** | Seen on a clock-in, not registered |
| **Registered** | One of your three |
| **Removed** | Taken off. It can be registered again |

If you already have three, the screen says
**You have three registered devices. Remove one to add this one.** Remove an old one first, as
below. If it says **Sign in again on this device to register it.**, the hub does not know this
device yet: sign out, sign in again on it, then register.

| It says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign in again on this device to register it.** | The hub does not know this device yet | Sign out, sign in again on it, then register |
| **Device not registered** | The connection failed | Check your connection and press **Register Device** again |

### Remove a device {#ess-devices-remove}

Remove a phone you no longer use, or one you lost, so it stops counting as yours.

1. Open [My Devices](/ess?tab=my-devices) and press the device.
2. Check its **Last Punch** and whether it is **This Device**.
3. Press **Remove Device**, then **Remove Device** again to confirm.
4. A message says **Device removed**. It now reads **Removed**.

A removed device can be registered again from the device itself. If it says
**Device not removed**, check your connection and try again.

## Policy Updates {#ess-policies screen=ess/policy-updates icon=book}

Every MyCity policy that applies to you, and the ones waiting for you to read and acknowledge.

![Policy Updates](shot:ess-policies)

### Acknowledge a policy {#ess-policies-acknowledge}

When a policy is new or changes, the hub shows **Policy Update** after you clock in. On a day you
do not clock in, it shows once the clock's question is closed. It names the policy,
says whether it is new or gives its version, when it is **Due**, and what changed. It stays until you press one
of its buttons.

![A policy to acknowledge](shot:ess-policy-prompt)

1. Press **Read Full Policy** to read it in full. It opens on
   [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates).
2. Read it. The list under its version says what changed since the version you last acknowledged.
3. Press **Acknowledge**. The message names the policy, such as *Data Protection acknowledged*.

**Later** puts it off until tomorrow. It is offered only until the due date. After that the
**Due** line reads *Overdue since 20/10/2026*, and the sheet returns at every sign-in until you
acknowledge.

On a phone the **Policy Update** sheet rises from the bottom and cannot be swiped away: press
**Acknowledge**, **Read Full Policy** or **Later**.

### Find a policy {#ess-policies-find}

![A policy opened](shot:ess-policy-read)

1. Open [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates).
2. Choose **To Acknowledge** for those waiting on you, or **All Policies** for every policy that
   applies to you. **All Acknowledged** means nothing is waiting; **No Policies Yet** means none
   applies to you yet.
3. Each row shows the **Policy**, its **Type** on a computer, its **Version** and its **State**.
   **State** reads the due date while it waits for you, **Overdue** after it, **Acknowledged** once
   you have, or the version's state: **In Force**, a start date, **Replaced** or **Withdrawn**.
4. Under **To Acknowledge**, press **Acknowledge** on a row to acknowledge it without opening it.
   The message names the policy.
5. Type in **Search policies** to find one by name.
6. Press a policy to read it. The top card shows its **Number**, **First Day**, **Due** date and
   when you **Acknowledged** it, then what changed. Below are the text, the **Signatures** and its
   **Earlier Versions**. In a rules policy, a section that changed is marked **Changed**.
7. Press **Download PDF** to save a copy with MyCity's letterhead. A message says
   **Policy downloaded**. **Close** closes it.

A notice about a policy opens it here. On a phone, each policy is a card, with **Acknowledge** full
width on those waiting for you, and a policy opens full screen.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Acknowledge the newest version of the policy** | A newer version came out while you were reading | Close it, open the policy again and acknowledge the new version |
| **That policy version does not exist** | The policy was withdrawn or archived | Nothing. It has left your list |
| **This policy is past its due date: acknowledge it now** | **Later** is no longer allowed | Press **Acknowledge** |
| **That file could not be made. Try again.** | The PDF failed | Press **Download PDF** again |
| **That file is taking too long. Try again.** | The PDF was not ready in time | Press **Download PDF** again |

### Approve or reject a policy {#ess-policies-approve}

**Waiting On You** lists the policies waiting for your approval before they are published, and
**Nothing Waiting** when there are none. MyCity's approval route names who approves; by default it
is the CEO.

1. Choose **Waiting On You** on [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates), and press the policy.
2. Read it, with its note and the publisher's signature.
3. To approve, press **Approve Policy**. Sign with your finger or mouse in **Your Signature**,
   then press **Approve Policy**. A message says **Policy approved**.
4. To reject, press **Reject Policy**. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, then press
   **Reject Policy**. A message says **Policy rejected**, and the writer reads your reason.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign in the box** | **Your Signature** is empty | Sign in the box, then press **Approve Policy** |
| **At least 10 characters** | The reason is too short | Say why in a full sentence |
| **This version is not waiting on you** | Another approver decided first, or it was taken back | Nothing to do. Close it |

## My Leave {#ess-leave screen=ess/my-leave icon=calendar}

Your leave this year: what you are entitled to, what you have taken and booked, and your requests.

![My Leave](shot:ess-leave)

### Read your leave {#ess-leave-read}

1. Open [My Leave](/ess?tab=my-leave).
2. The four cards show your Annual leave: **Entitlement**, **Taken**, **Approved Upcoming** (with
   its dates) and **Remaining**.
3. **My Requests** lists every request: its **Type**, **From**, **To**, **Days**, **Approver** and
   **Status**. With none yet, it reads **No Leave Requested Yet**.
4. **Entitlement** shows each type of leave with a bar and a line such as
   *3 taken, 2 booked, 1 pending, 14 left*. Pending days are still waiting for a decision.
   **No set allowance** means the type has no yearly limit.
5. Press a request to open it. It shows its **Type**, **From**, **To**, **Days**, **Approver**,
   **Status** and your **Note**.
6. Press **Leave Calendar** to see a month of approved and waiting leave. Yours is marked **You**.
   Leave still waiting for approval is marked **Waiting**.
7. {needs="manager"} The leave of the people who report to you shows there too, by first name.
8. Use the arrows beside the month to see another month, or press its name to pick one. The
   calendar covers this year and next.

Operations and People and the CEO see everyone's leave in the calendar.

On a phone, the four cards open what is behind them. **Entitlement** and **Remaining** open
**Entitlement**. **Taken** opens **Leave Taken**, the list of your requests. **Approved Upcoming**
opens your next booked leave. Below them, tap **My Requests** or **Entitlement** to open them.

### Request leave {#ess-leave-request needs="ess create"}

1. Press **Request Leave** at the top right of [My Leave](/ess?tab=my-leave). On a phone it is under
   the title, and in **Quick Actions**.
   ![Request Leave](shot:ess-request-leave)
2. Pick the **Leave Type**. Each type shows how many days you have left, such as
   *Annual · 12 days left*.
3. Choose the **First Day** and the **Last Day**. The hub counts MyCity's working days between
   them, both included. Days off in the working week, such as Sunday, and public holidays are left
   out. Saturday counts when it is a working day.
4. Add a **Note** for your manager if it helps, such as `Family event`.
5. Press **Send Request**. A message names who it went to, such as *Sent to Tolu Adeyemi*.

Your request reads **Pending** until it is decided. **Approver** names who it waits on: your line
manager by name, then **Operations & People** for long leave. You are told when they decide.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for a decision. **Approver** names who it waits on |
| **Approved** | Granted. It counts against your allowance |
| **Rejected** | Refused by the approver. **Approver** then reads **Decided** |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it, or People cancelled it. **Approver** reads **Withdrawn** |

Leave People recorded for you reads **People** under **Approver**.

| The field says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | It cannot be left empty | Pick a type or a date |
| **Already past** | The first day is before today | Pick today or a later day |
| *Pick 25/10/2026 or later* | The day typed is before the earliest day allowed: today for the First Day, the First Day for the Last Day | Pick a day on or after the date it shows |
| **Not a real date** | The date typed does not exist, such as 31/02/2026 | Type it as DD/MM/YYYY, or pick it from the calendar |
| **Ends before it starts** | The last day is before the first | Correct the last day |
| **Ends in another year** | The leave runs past 31 December | Make two requests, one for each year |
| **No working days** | Every day picked is a day off or a public holiday | Pick days you would be working |
| **Overlaps other leave** | You already asked for leave on some of those days | Change the days, or cancel the other request |
| *32 working days, 19 left* | The working days you asked for, then the days you have left this year | Ask for fewer days, or another type |
| **Not offered** | People stopped offering that type while you filled the form | Pick another **Leave Type** |
| *At most 500 characters* | The note is over 500 characters | Shorten the **Note** |

### Cancel a request {#ess-leave-cancel}

You can cancel a request while it waits, or approved leave before it starts.

1. Press the request in **My Requests**.
2. Press **Cancel Request**. A message says **Request cancelled**.

If it says **This leave can no longer be cancelled**, the leave has started. Ask People and
Operations.

## My Documents {#ess-documents screen=ess/my-documents icon=doc}

Your document vault: your contract, certificates, ID and anything People asked you for.

![My Documents](shot:ess-documents)

### Read your documents {#ess-documents-read}

1. Open [My Documents](/ess?tab=my-documents).
2. The figures count your **Documents**, and those **Signed**, **Verified**, **Expiring** and
   **Missing**.
3. **Vault** lists each **Document**, when it was **Uploaded**, its **Status** and when it
   **Expires**. Press **Document** or **Uploaded** at the top of the list to sort by it. With
   nothing in it yet, it reads **Nothing Here Yet**.
4. Press a document. It shows **Uploaded**, **Status**, **Expires** and any **Reference**. On a
   computer its pages also show in **Preview**, beside the list.
5. **View File** opens a PDF or a photo full screen. Other files have **Download** only. If
   **Download** says **That file could not be opened. Try again.**, check your connection and press
   it again.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Signed** | Signed, sent or filed by MyCity. It is kept as it is and never replaced |
| **Verified** | People checked it |
| **Awaiting Check** | You uploaded it, and People have not checked it yet |
| **Expiring** | It expires within 60 days, or has expired. Upload a new one with **Upload Document** |
| **Missing** | People asked for it and it is not uploaded yet |

On a phone, **Upload Document** is under the title, and your documents are a list with their upload
date and status. Tap one to see its details, **Expires** among them. In **View File**, **Share**
sends the file to another app; swipe down to close it.

### Upload a document {#ess-documents-upload}

![Upload Document](shot:ess-documents-upload)

1. Press **Upload Document**. For a document marked **Missing**, press it and then
   **Upload Document**, so it answers People's request.
2. Type **What Is It**, such as `NDPA certificate`. For a **Missing** document it is filled in
   already. Fill it before you choose the file.
3. Drop the file on the box, or press **Drop files here, or browse** and choose it: a PDF, a photo
   (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC), or a Word or Excel file, up to 15 MB, as the box says. On a phone you can pick a file or take a photo. The upload
   starts at once.
4. The file shows its progress, then **Done**. The form closes and a message says
   **Uploaded. People will check it**. It reads **Awaiting Check** until they do.

If a file is refused, the reason shows in red under its name. Press the round arrow to send it
again, or the cross to take it off.

| The file says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Larger than 15 MB* | The file is over the limit, so it is not sent | Scan it at a lower quality, or split it into parts |
| **This file type is not accepted here.** | The vault does not keep that kind of file | Save it as a PDF, or take a photo of it |
| **This is an old Office file. Save it as .docx or .xlsx and upload it again.** | An old .doc or .xls file | Save it in the newer format, then upload it |
| **That document has already been given** | The document People asked for was given already, or People withdrew the request | Close the form. The list no longer shows it as **Missing** |
| **Upload failed. Try again.** | The upload stopped part way | Press the round arrow |
| **You appear to be offline. Try again.** | Your device lost the internet | Reconnect, then press the round arrow |

## My Security {#ess-security screen=ess/my-security icon=shield}

How you sign in, and where you are signed in: your password, two-step sign-in, passkeys, your PIN,
and your devices.

![My Security](shot:ess-security)

On a phone each part is a card, with its state under its name, such as **On** or the day your
password changed. Tap a card to open it, then follow the steps below. **Recent Sign-Ins** on a
phone leaves out the **Address**.

![My Security on a phone](shot:ess-security-phone)

Changes to two-step sign-in, a passkey or your PIN may first ask **Confirm It's You**: follow
[Confirm it's you](#start-confirm-you).

### Change your password {#ess-security-password}

1. Open [My Security](/ess?tab=my-security) and press **Change Password**. On a phone, tap
   **Password** first.
2. Type your **Current Password**.
3. Type a **New Password**: at least 8 characters, with a capital letter, a small letter, a number
   and a symbol. Type it again in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Press **Change Password**. A message says **Password changed**. When you were signed in
   elsewhere, it reads **Password changed, other devices signed out**.

Your other devices are signed out, so sign in there again with the new password.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Enter your password** | Type your current password |
| **Not your password** | Type your current password again. Use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen if you do not know it |
| **At least 8 characters** | Make it longer. MyCity may ask for more, and the field says how many |
| **Add a lowercase letter** | Add a small letter |
| **Add an uppercase letter** | Add a capital letter |
| **Add a number** | Add a digit |
| **Add a symbol** | Add a sign such as `!` or `#` |
| **Contains your email name** | Leave out the part of your email before the @ |
| **Found in a breach** | This password is known to attackers. Choose another one |
| **Same as current** | Choose a password you have not used here |
| **Does not match** | Type the new password the same way twice |

### Set up two-step sign-in {#ess-security-two-step}

Two-step sign-in asks for a code from an authenticator app on your phone after your password or
PIN. A passkey needs no code.

1. Press **Set Up Two-Step**. On a phone, tap **Two-Step Sign-In** first.
2. Open your authenticator app and scan the square code. Or type the key shown under it.
   ![Setting up two-step sign-in](shot:ess-security-two-step)
3. Type the six digits the app shows in **Code From App**. It turns on as you type the last digit,
   or press **Turn On**. **Cancel** stops without turning it on.
4. **Recovery Codes** shows ten codes, this once. Press **Copy Codes**. A message says
   **Recovery codes copied**. If it says **Copy them by hand**, write them down.
5. Keep them safe, away from your phone, then press **Close**. Each one signs you in once if you
   lose your phone.

The card then reads **On**, with how many recovery codes are left.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter 6 digits** | The code is not six digits | Type all six from the app |
| **Not the right code** | The code is wrong or has changed | Type the code the app shows now |
| **Set-up expired** | More than 15 minutes passed since you began | Press **Cancel**, then **Set Up Two-Step** again, and scan the new code |
| **Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again.** | Too many codes tried | Wait a few minutes |
| **Your role needs two-step sign-in, so it stays on.** | Your role now requires two-step | Keep it on |
| **Two-step sign-in is not on.** | It was turned off elsewhere, or reset by an administrator, while the page was open | Reopen My Security and set it up again |

Once it is on:

- **Ask for a Code** chooses how often this device asks: **Every Sign-In**, **Once a Day** or
  **Once a Month**. The message confirms it, such as *Code asked once a day*. Every device asks
  once more at its next sign-in.
- **New Codes** asks **New Recovery Codes**. Press **Make New Codes**. Copy the ten new codes,
  then press **Close**. The old ones stop working.
- **Change App** moves two-step to a new phone. Follow the set-up steps on the new phone. The old
  app works until you type the new code. You then get ten new recovery codes; the old ones stop.
- **Turn Off** asks **Turn Off Two-Step**. Press **Turn Off**. A message says
  **Two-step sign-in is off**, and your recovery codes stop working. It is not offered when your
  role requires two-step.

### Set a PIN {#ess-security-pin}

A PIN is four digits that unlock the hub, and sign you in on a device you have signed in on before.

1. Press **Set PIN**. On a phone, tap **PIN** first.
2. Type four digits in **New PIN** and again in **Confirm PIN**.
3. Press **Save PIN**. A message says **PIN saved**.

The card then reads **On**, with the day you changed it. **Change PIN** sets a new one the same
way. **Remove PIN** asks first: press **Remove PIN** again. A message says **PIN removed**.

| The field says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Enter 4 digits** | Type all four digits |
| **Not one digit repeated** | Do not use one digit four times, such as 1111 |
| **Not a run like 1234** | Do not count up or down, such as 1234 or 9876 |
| **Not a repeated pair** | Do not repeat a pair, such as 1212 |
| **Not two doubles** | Do not use two doubles, such as 1122 |
| **Too common** | Pick digits people do not often choose |
| **Not your birthday** | Do not use your birthday or birth year |
| **Does not match** | Type the same four digits in **Confirm PIN** |

### Add a passkey {#ess-security-passkey}

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a password.

1. On the device you want to use, press **Add Passkey** under **Passkeys**. On a phone, tap
   **Passkeys** first.
2. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock when your device asks.
3. A message says **Passkey added**. It is named after this browser and marked **This Device**.

If **Add Passkey** is not there, this browser cannot make passkeys. The list shows each passkey's
**Name**, when it was **Added** and **Last Used**. With none, it reads **No Passkeys Yet**.

To rename one, press it, change its **Name** and press **Save Name**. A message says
**Passkey renamed**. To remove it, press **Remove Passkey**, then **Remove Passkey** again. A
message says **Passkey removed**.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| *You have 10 passkeys. Remove one first.* | Press an old passkey and remove it, then add the new one |
| **This device already has your passkey** | Nothing. This device already signs you in |
| **This passkey is already added.** | Nothing. It is already on your list |
| **Your passkey could not be checked. Try again.** | Press **Add Passkey** again |
| **This request has expired. Start again.** | Press **Add Passkey** again, and answer your device sooner |
| **Passkeys do not work on this address** | Open the hub at its usual address, then try again |
| **The passkey did not work** | Try again, or use your password |
| **Required** or *At most 60 characters* under **Name** | Type a name of up to 60 characters |

If you close your device's prompt, nothing changes.

### Sign out a device {#ess-security-sessions}

1. Under **Signed In**, find the device by its name and address. **This Device** is the one you
   are using. On a phone, tap **Signed In** first.
2. Press **Sign Out** beside another device. **Sign Out Device** names it: press **Sign Out**. A
   message says **Device signed out**.
3. Or press **Sign Out Others**, then **Sign Out**, to sign out every device but this one. The
   message counts them, such as *2 devices signed out*. It shows only when another device is signed
   in.

If it says **That session has already ended.**, that device is signed out already.

**Recent Sign-Ins** shows **When**, the **Device**, the **Method**, the **Address** and the
**Status** of every sign-in. If you see one that was not you, change your password at once and tell
People and Operations.

| Method | How you signed in |
|---|---|
| **Password** | Your password |
| **Password and Code** | Your password, then a code from your app |
| **PIN** | Your PIN |
| **PIN and Code** | Your PIN, then a code from your app |
| **Passkey** | Your fingerprint, face or screen lock |

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Signed In** | Still signed in |
| **Signed out** | Signed out, by you or by an administrator |
| **Timed out** | It reached its time limit |
| **Idle** | It locked after a while unused |
| **Password changed** or **Password reset** | Ended by a new password |
| **Stopped** | Ended because its sign-in was copied. Change your password |
| **Suspended** | Ended when your login was suspended |

### See your clock-in devices {#ess-security-devices}

**Clock-In Devices** lists the devices you clock in from, each **Registered** or **New**, with its
last clock-in. Press **Manage My Devices** to open [My Devices](/ess?tab=my-devices) and register or
remove one. See [Register this device](#ess-devices-register).

## Org Chart {#ess-org-chart screen=ess/org-chart icon=users}

Who works at MyCity, and who reports to whom.

![The Org Chart](shot:ess-org-chart)

### Find someone {#ess-org-chart-find}

1. Open [Org Chart](/ess?tab=org-chart). On a computer, the line under the title counts the seats,

2. Type a name, a job or a department in **Name or role**. On a computer, matching cards are
   outlined; open a folded team to see matches inside it.
3. On a computer, the chart is a tree from the CEO down, open three levels deep and down to you.
   The small round button under a card folds its team away, or opens it again, showing how many
   report to it.
4. On a phone, the chart opens on **Focus** at you. The line from the top shows as names to tap.
   Under **Reports To** is your manager; then you; then **Direct Reports**, **Dotted Line** and
   **Same Manager**. **Also At The Top** lists anyone else without a manager. Tap a card to move
   there. Tap the large card to open that person. **Show Me** brings you back.
5. On a phone, **Everyone** shows the whole company as a list, with **You** on your row. Tap the
   arrow beside a row to open or fold its team. Typing in **Name or role** lists the matching people
   as cards; **No Match** means nobody matches.
6. Press a person's card. **Profile** shows their **Department**, who they **Reports To**, any
   **Dotted Line**, and their **Team**, such as `3 direct · 13 in all`.
7. Press **Send Message** to write to them in Staff Community. On your own card, **My Profile**
   opens [My Profile](/ess?tab=my-profile) instead.
8. {needs="people view"} **Open Record** opens their record on People.
9. {needs="people edit"} **Change Manager** opens **Change Reporting Line**. Follow
   [Change who someone reports to](#people-reporting).

A solid line is the reporting line. A dashed line is a **Dotted Line**: a second manager for shared
work, who does not approve leave or expenses. A dashed card reading **Open role** is a post nobody
holds yet. On a computer the legend names **Reporting Line**, **Dotted Line**, **CEO** and
**Vacant Seat**. If a banner says people and roles sit at the top, People have not given them a
manager yet; there is nothing for you to do.

Press **Download PDF** to save the whole chart as a file. The button spins while it is drawn, then
a message says **PDF downloaded**. If it says **That file could not be made. Try again.**, press
**Download PDF** again.
