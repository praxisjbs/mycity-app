The Knowledge Hub holds what MyCity knows: its policies, each department's SOPs, its training
courses, and this User Guide. Everyone reads the policies that apply to them on
[Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates) in Employee Self-Service, every published SOP on
[Knowledge Hub · SOPs](/knowledge?tab=sops), and takes their courses on
[My Training](/ess?tab=my-training). This chapter is for the people who read SOPs and the people who
write policies, SOPs and courses.

## Policies {#knowledge-policies screen=knowledge/policies icon=book}

Every company policy in one place, each with its versions: what is in force today, what starts
later, and who has acknowledged it. People who hold **Manage Policies** write and publish them:
Operations and People, Implementation and the CEO, unless your roles were changed. Anyone else does
not see this tab.

![Policies](shot:knowledge-policies)

### Find a policy {#knowledge-policies-find}

1. Open [Knowledge Hub · Policies](/knowledge?tab=policies).
2. Press a chip to narrow the list: **In Force**, **Awaiting Approval**, **Starts Soon**, **Draft**
   or **Archived**.
   **All** shows every policy.
3. Type in **Search policies** to find one by its name or number, such as `POL-0002`.
4. Each row shows the **Policy**, its **Type**, who it **Applies To**, the version **In Force**, its
   **State**, and how many have **Acknowledged** it, such as *22 of 24*. On a phone each policy is a
   card: its name, **Applies To**, **State** and **Acknowledged**. **Type** and **In Force** show on
   a computer only.
5. Press a policy to open it. **All Policies** at the top goes back to the list.

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Nothing published yet |
| **Starts Soon** | Its first version is published and starts on a later day |
| **In Force** | A version applies today. It may also have a draft being written |
| **Awaiting Approval** | A version waits for its approvers |
| **Archived** | It no longer applies |

> **Tip:** Search finds a policy from anywhere: type its name or number.

### Write a new policy {#knowledge-policies-new needs="knowledge create, knowledge edit"}

1. Press **New Policy** at the top right. On a phone it is under the title, or press
   **Quick Actions**, then **New Policy**. From anywhere, open search and type `new policy`.
   ![New Policy](shot:knowledge-new-policy)
2. Type its **Title**, such as `Dress Code`, and press **Start Policy**. The message
   **Policy started** confirms it, and its draft opens.
3. Under **Policy Settings**, choose who it **Applies To**: **Everyone**, or **Some People** and
   then the **Departments**, **Work Patterns** or **People** it covers.
4. Keep **Needs Acknowledgement** ticked if each person must confirm they have read it. Set
   **Acknowledge Within**, the days they have after it is published.
5. Tick **Needs Approval** if it must be approved before it is published. The CEO approves it,
   unless People changed the Policy Approval route.
6. {needs="settings edit"} To change who approves, open
   [Settings · Approvals](/settings?tab=approvals) and edit **Policy Approval**.
7. Under **Policy Text**, type the policy, or paste it from a document.
8. Press **Tidy With AI** if you want headings and lists suggested. It shows **Yours** beside
   **Tidied**, with the same words. Press **Use Tidied Text**, or **Keep Mine**.
9. Press **Save Draft**. The message **Draft saved** confirms it. Nobody is told yet.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** under **Title** | The title is empty | Type a title |
| **At least 3 characters** | The title is too short | Type a longer title |
| **Pick the people** under **Applies To** | You chose **Some People** but picked nobody | Pick a department, a work pattern or a person, or choose **Everyone** |
| **At least 1** or **At most 30** | **Acknowledge Within** is out of range | Type a number of days from 1 to 30 |
| **Required** under **Acknowledge Within** | The days are empty | Type the days, such as `7` |
| **Write or paste the policy first** | **Policy Text** is empty when you press **Tidy With AI** | Type or paste the policy, then press **Tidy With AI** |
| **The tidy changed some words. Try again or tidy it by hand.** | The suggestion changed your wording, so it is not shown | Press **Tidy With AI** again, or add the headings yourself |
| **The AI service is not answering. Try again in a few minutes.** | The assistant did not answer | Wait a few minutes and press again |
| **AI features are switched off.** or **AI features are switched off for this area.** | An administrator turned the assistant off | Tidy the text yourself |
| **This month's AI spending limit has been reached.** | MyCity's AI budget for the month is spent | Tidy the text yourself, or ask an administrator |
| **The AI service is not set up correctly. An administrator has been told.** | The assistant is not set up | Tidy the text yourself |

### Change a policy in force {#knowledge-policies-change needs="knowledge edit"}

1. Open the policy. Its card shows its **Number**, **Type**, who it **Applies To**, and the version
   **In Force** with its first day.
   ![A policy, open](shot:knowledge-policy)
2. Change the settings or the text, then press **Save Draft**. On a phone, **Save Draft** is at the
   foot of the page, full width.
3. A bar above **Policy Settings** reads *Draft saved, not published.*, or names how many changes the
   draft makes.

> **Important:** For a written policy, the **Policy Text** box starts empty, even when a version is
> in force. Paste the whole text of the new version, not only the part that changed. What you
> publish replaces the version in force.

**Needs Approval** starts as the version in force had it. Untick it only if this version may be
published without approval.

To throw the draft away, press **Discard Changes** at the foot of the page. The hub asks
**Discard Changes**; press **Discard Changes** again. The message **Draft discarded** confirms it.
The version in force is untouched.

### Publish a policy {#knowledge-policies-publish needs="knowledge edit"}

> **Important:** Only what you saved is published. With changes not saved, **Publish Policy** says
> **Save the draft first**: press **Save Draft**, then publish.

1. Open the policy with its draft saved.
2. Press **Publish Policy** in that bar. For a policy that **Needs Approval**, it reads
   **Submit for Approval**. On a phone the button sits under the bar's words. A written policy
   offers it once its **Policy Text** is saved.
   ![Publish Policy](shot:knowledge-publish)
3. Check the changes listed at the top.
4. Choose the **First Day** the new version applies from.
5. Add a **Note** saying what changed, such as `Grace now 10`.
6. Sign with your finger or mouse in **Your Signature**. **Clear Signature** starts again. On a
   phone the sheet fills the screen and the pad is full width.
7. Press **Publish Policy**, or **Submit for Approval**. The message *Version 2 published* confirms
   it, naming the new version. For approval it reads **Sent for approval**.

Once published, everyone it applies to is told: **New policy**, or **Policy updated** for a later
version. If it **Needs Acknowledgement**, each person is asked to acknowledge it after they clock
in, or after the sign-in prompt on a day they do not. Anyone who has not acknowledged it after 3
days gets one reminder.

A version sent for approval shows **Awaiting approval** with who it waits on and who sent it. The
approvers find it under **Waiting On You** on [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates). While it
waits, the page shows what was sent, and it cannot be changed: its fields are greyed and
**Save Draft** is gone.

When the last approver approves, the version is published by itself. If its first day has passed,
it starts on the day it is approved. If an approver rejects it, you get the notice
**Policy not approved** with their reason. The version is back in the draft. Press the notice to
open the policy, change it, and send it again.

Only the person who sent it, or the CEO, can take it back; **Recall Submission** shows only to them:

1. Press **Recall Submission** in the blue bar.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Changing the text`.
3. Press **Recall Submission**. The message **Back to the draft** confirms it.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Write the policy first** | The saved draft has no text | Press **Cancel**, write the **Policy Text**, press **Save Draft**, then publish |
| **Required** under **Note** | No note | Say what changed, such as `Grace now 10 minutes` |
| **At least 10 characters** | The note or reason is too short | Write a little more |
| **Required** under **First Day** | No first day | Pick a day |
| Pick a later date | The day you typed is in the past | Pick today or later |
| **Within 90 days** | The first day is more than 90 days away | Pick a day within 90 days |
| **After the waiting version** | Another version starts on or after that day | Pick a day after it starts, or withdraw that version |
| **Sign in the box** | The signature is empty | Sign in **Your Signature** |
| **Signature too large** | The drawing is too big to keep | Press **Clear Signature** and sign again |
| **The draft changes nothing** | The draft says what the version in force says | Change the text, a rule, the title or **Applies To**, and save |
| **Save the changes as a draft first** | Someone discarded or published the draft first | Close the sheet, make your changes, press **Save Draft** |
| **A version is already waiting for approval** | Someone sent a version first | Wait for its approval, or recall it |
| **This policy needs approval: submit it** or **This policy needs no approval: publish it** | Someone changed **Needs Approval** while your sheet was open | Close the sheet and press the bar's button again |
| **This policy is archived** | Someone archived it first | Nothing more can be published |
| **Only the person who sent it, or the CEO, can recall it** | Someone else sent it | Ask them or the CEO to recall it |
| **Nothing is waiting for approval** | It was approved, rejected or recalled first | Close the sheet and look at the policy again |

### Withdraw a version before it starts {#knowledge-policies-withdraw needs="knowledge edit"}

A published version that starts on a later day shows a blue bar: *Version 3 starts 01/11/2026.*

1. Press **Withdraw Version** in that bar.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Not ready yet`.
3. Press **Withdraw Version**. The message *Version 3 withdrawn* confirms it.

Everyone it applies to is told it will not apply. If there was no draft, its text becomes the
draft, ready to change.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Only a version waiting to start can be withdrawn** | Its first day has come, or someone withdrew it first | Publish a new version instead |

### See who has acknowledged {#knowledge-policies-acknowledged}

1. Open the policy. **Versions** lists each version, newest first: its **Version**, **First Day**,
   **State**, **Published By** and how many have **Acknowledged** it.
2. **State** reads **In Force**, *Starts 01/11/2026* for one still to start, **Replaced** for an
   older one, or **Withdrawn**.
3. Press a version. Its sheet shows its **State**, **First Day**, **Applies To**, who **Published**
   it, the note, what changed, its text and its **Signatures**.
   ![A version, with who has acknowledged it](shot:knowledge-version)
4. **Download PDF** saves it with MyCity's letterhead. The message **Policy downloaded** confirms it.
   If it says **That file could not be made. Try again.** or **That file is taking too long. Try
   again.**, press **Download PDF** again.
5. Under **Acknowledgements**, choose **Acknowledged**, **Pending** or **Overdue** to see each
   group, or **Everyone**. Type in **Name** to find a person.
6. Each person shows their **Status**, the day it is **Due** and the day they **Acknowledged** it.

A withdrawn version, or one that needs no acknowledgement, has no Acknowledgements list. The list
holds only the people your role covers. On a phone, **Published By** and **Due** are not shown.

### Set the Attendance policy {#knowledge-policies-attendance needs="knowledge edit"}

The Attendance policy is MyCity's rules for the clock: working hours, lateness, deductions, overtime
and location checks. It applies to everyone, and it cannot be archived. It has no **Title** or
**Applies To** to set.

1. Open **Attendance Policy** in the list. Before any version is published, **In Force** reads
   **Default Rules**.
   ![The Attendance policy](shot:knowledge-attendance)
2. Each section is a card: **Working Hours**, **Lateness**, **Late Arrivals**, **Early Departure**,
   **Absence**, **Forgotten Clock-Outs**, **Location Flags**, **Overtime**, **Location Checks**,
   **Devices** and **Clock Prompts**.
3. Change a rule. Tick **On** under a rule to use it: its figures appear beneath. A rule you changed
   is marked **Changed**. Press a rule's name to read what it decides.
4. Press **Save Draft**, then publish it as any policy. The figures are checked when you save.

A new version applies from its first day. Days before it keep the rules they were judged by.

| Card | Rule | What to set |
|---|---|---|
| **Working Hours** | **Company Hours** | Tick **Set Company Hours**, then each day's **Working**, **Starts** and **Ends**. Unticked, the working week on Settings applies |
| **Lateness** | **Grace Minutes** | Minutes after the start before a clock-in is late, 0 to 60 |
| **Late Arrivals** | **Late Arrivals** | **On**, then **Count** (1 to 31), **Or Hours** (optional, 0.5 to 40), **Period** (**Week**, **Month** or **30 Days**), **Alert** and **Raise Query** |
| **Late Arrivals** | **Late Deduction** | **On**, then **Method**: **Pro-Rata by Minute**, **Fixed per Hour** or **Fixed per Day**, and the **Amount** (₦1 to ₦1,000,000) for a fixed one |
| **Early Departure** | **Early Grace Minutes**, **Early Departures**, **Early Deduction** | As for lateness, for leaving before the end |
| **Absence** | **Absences**, **Absence Deduction** | **Absences** counts as above. **Absence Deduction** **On** takes a day's pay for each absent day |
| **Forgotten Clock-Outs** | **Forgotten Clock-Outs** | Counts System Clock-Outs and days with no clock-out |
| **Location Flags** | **Location Flags** | Counts clock-ins outside a place of work that still stand |
| **Overtime** | **Overtime** | **On**, then **After Minutes** (0 to 120), **Approval** (**Line Manager After**, **Pre-Approved** or **Automatic**), **Paid As** (**Multiple of Hourly Rate** or **Fixed per Hour**), **Multiple** or **Amount an Hour**, **Rest Day Multiple** (1 to 3) and **Monthly Cap Hours** (1 to 100) |
| **Location Checks** | **Office Check** | **Off**, **Flag** (accepted and sent to People) or **Refuse** (not accepted) |
| **Location Checks** | **Home Location** | **On** asks home workers to set a home. Then **Home Check** (**Flag** or **Refuse**) and **Home Radius** (150 to 500 metres) |
| **Location Checks** | **Temporary Site Check** | **Flag** or **Refuse** |
| **Location Checks** | **GPS Accuracy** | Metres, 50 to 250. A less accurate reading cannot confirm where someone is |
| **Devices** | **Device Check** | **Off**, **Flag** or **Refuse** a clock-in from an unregistered device |
| **Clock Prompts** | **Clock-Out Reminder** | How often, in minutes (15 to 60), someone still clocked in is reminded |
| **Clock Prompts** | **System Clock-Out** | Minutes after the end (30 to 240) before the system clocks someone out |

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| At least, or At most, then a figure | The figure is out of range | Type a figure in the range the table gives |
| **Required** | A figure is empty | Type it |
| **Enter the amount** | A fixed deduction or **Fixed per Hour** overtime has no amount | Type the amount, or choose another method |
| **₦1 to ₦1,000,000** | The amount is out of range | Type an amount in that range |
| **At least 150**, or *At least 50* | **Home Radius** is under 150 metres while Home Location is on, or under 50 | Type 150 or more |
| **Flag or Refuse** | A check must flag or refuse | Choose **Flag** or **Refuse** |
| **Tick a working day** | No day is ticked **Working** | Tick at least one day |
| **Ends after it starts** under a day's **Ends** | That working day ends before it starts | Correct its **Starts** or **Ends** |
| **Required** under a day's **Starts** or **Ends** | That working day has no time there | Type the time |

### Archive a policy {#knowledge-policies-archive needs="knowledge delete"}

1. Open the policy and press **Archive Policy** at the foot of its card.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Replaced by the handbook`.
3. Press **Archive Policy**. The message **Policy archived** confirms it. It leaves Policy Updates,
   and its versions are kept.

An archived policy cannot be changed or brought back. Its versions and PDFs can still be read.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This policy is already archived** | Someone archived it first | Nothing more to do |
| **A version waits for approval. Recall it first.** | A version is with its approvers | Press **Recall Submission** (or ask whoever sent it), then archive |

### When a change is refused {#knowledge-policies-problems}

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Recall the version waiting for approval first** | Someone sent a version for approval while you edited | Recall it, then save |
| **This policy is archived** | Someone archived it while you edited | Nothing more can be saved |
| **There is no draft** | Someone discarded or published the draft first | Look at the policy again |
| A button you expected is not there | Your role may read policies but not do this: starting a policy needs create, saving and sending need edit, archiving needs delete | Ask People to change your role on Settings · Roles & Permissions |

## SOPs {#knowledge-sops screen=knowledge/sops icon=list}

Each department's own standard operating procedures: how Marketing launches a campaign, how
Operations onboards a partner. Everyone reads every published SOP. A department's head writes and
publishes that department's SOPs. People who hold **Manage SOPs** may write any department's:
Operations and People, Implementation and the CEO, unless your roles were changed. Anyone else reads
them and does not see **New SOP** or **Edit SOP**.

![SOPs](shot:knowledge-sops)

### Find and read an SOP {#knowledge-sops-read}

1. Open [Knowledge Hub · SOPs](/knowledge?tab=sops).
2. Press a department's chip to see only its SOPs. **All** shows every department.
3. Type in **Search SOPs** to find one by its title or number, such as `SOP-0001`.
4. Each row shows the **SOP**, its **Department**, its **Version** and when it was **Updated**. On a
   phone each SOP is a card. **Updated** shows on a computer only.
5. Press an SOP to read it. On a computer it opens beside the list. On a phone it opens in a sheet:
   drag it down to close it.
   ![An SOP, open](shot:knowledge-sop)
6. Read its **Purpose**, **Scope**, **Roles Involved** and numbered **Steps**. A step shows who does
   it. **Open Screen** opens the screen the step happens on. A file's name opens its file.
7. **Versions** lists every version, newest first, with what changed. On a phone, press **Versions**
   under the SOP. Press a version to read it as it was published.

> **Tip:** Search finds an SOP from anywhere: type its title or number.

### Find the procedures for a screen {#knowledge-sops-procedures}

1. Open the help for the screen you are on: on a phone press **Quick Actions**, then **Help**. On a
   computer, press your name at the foot of the menu, then **User Guide**.
2. Under the screen's section, **Procedures** lists the SOPs whose steps happen on that screen, each
   with its department.
3. Press one to read it on [Knowledge Hub · SOPs](/knowledge?tab=sops).

A screen whose steps no SOP names shows no **Procedures**.

### Start a new SOP {#knowledge-sops-new}

1. Press **New SOP** at the top right. On a phone it is under the title, or press
   **Quick Actions**, then **New SOP**. From anywhere, open search and type `new sop`.
   ![New SOP](shot:knowledge-new-sop)
2. Choose the **Department**. A head is offered their own department only.
3. Type its **Title**, such as `Campaign Launch`, and press **Start SOP**. The message **SOP started**
   confirms it. It opens under the **Draft** chip, ready to write.

An SOP stays with its department for good. To move one, start it again in the other department.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** under **Department** or **Title** | It is empty | Choose the department, or type a title |
| **At least 4 characters** | The title is too short | Type a longer title |
| **You may not write this department's SOPs** | You are not its head and do not hold **Manage SOPs** | Ask the department's head |

### Write the draft {#knowledge-sops-edit}

1. Open the SOP and press **Edit SOP**. On a computer it opens at the side. On a phone it fills the
   screen.
   ![Edit SOP](shot:knowledge-sop-edit)
2. Type the **Title** and the **Purpose**: what the procedure is for, at least 10 characters.
3. Type the **Scope** if it covers only some work. Under **Roles Involved**, type each role on a line
   of its own, up to 10.
4. Under **Steps**, write each step in **Step**, in your department's own words. Wrap a word in two
   stars for bold, such as `**brief**`.
5. Type **Who** does it, such as `Campaign Lead`.
6. Choose the **Screen** the step happens on, if it happens in the hub. The SOP then shows under
   **Procedures** in that screen's help.
7. Add a **File** the step needs, such as a template. **Remove File** takes it off.
8. Press **Add Step** for the next step. A step's menu holds **Move Up**, **Move Down** and
   **Remove Step**.
9. Press **Save Draft**. The message **Draft saved** confirms it. Nobody is told yet.

The SOP shows a bar: *Draft saved, not published.* Everyone else still reads the version in force.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **At least 10 characters** under **Purpose** | The purpose is too short | Say what the procedure is for |
| **Write the step** | A step is empty or too short | Write the step, or remove it |
| **Pick a screen** | That screen is no longer in the hub | Choose another screen, or **None** |
| **Upload it again** | The file was not one you added | Press **Remove File**, then add the file again |
| **This SOP is archived** | Someone archived it while you wrote | Nothing more can be saved |

### Draft With AI {#knowledge-sops-ai}

When your department already has the procedure in a document, the assistant can lay it out as
steps for you. It keeps your words and never adds a step.

1. In **Edit SOP**, press **Draft With AI**.
2. Under **From**, choose **Pasted Text** and paste the procedure into **Procedure**. Or choose
   **PDF File** and add the PDF.
3. Press **Draft With AI**. **Drafted** shows beside **Yours**.
   ![Draft With AI](shot:knowledge-sop-ai)
4. Press **Use This Draft** to put it in the form, or **Keep Mine** to leave yours.
5. Choose each step's **Screen** and **File** yourself: the draft never suggests them.
6. Press **Save Draft**. Nothing is kept until you do.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Paste the procedure** | Too little text was pasted | Paste the whole procedure |
| **Pick a PDF** | No file was added, or it is not a PDF | Add a PDF, or paste the text |
| **No text in this file** | The PDF is a scan, a picture of the pages | Paste the text instead |
| **Draft With AI is unavailable right now.** | The assistant is off, or not set up | Write the steps yourself |
| **This month's AI spending limit has been reached.** | MyCity's AI budget for the month is spent | Write the steps yourself, or ask an administrator |
| **The AI service is not answering. Try again in a few minutes.** | The assistant did not answer | Wait a few minutes and press again |
| **No steps found. Try again or write it by hand.** | The answer held no steps | Press **Draft With AI** again, or write it |

### Publish an SOP {#knowledge-sops-publish}

1. Open the SOP with its draft saved.
2. Press **Publish SOP** in the bar.
   ![Publish SOP](shot:knowledge-sop-publish)
3. Type a **Note** saying what changed, such as `Added the budget step`. The first version needs
   none.
4. Press **Publish SOP**. The message *Version 2 published* confirms it, naming the new version.

Everyone in the department is told: **New SOP**, or **SOP updated** for a later version. Everyone
can read it from then on.

To throw a draft away, press **Discard Changes** in the bar. The message **Draft discarded** confirms
it. The version in force is untouched.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** under **Note** | Version 2 and later need a note | Say what changed |
| **At least 10 characters** | The note is too short | Write a little more |
| **Write the purpose first** | The draft has no purpose | Press **Edit SOP**, write the **Purpose**, save |
| **Add a step first** | The draft has no steps | Press **Edit SOP**, add a step, save |
| **The draft changes nothing** | The draft says what the version in force says | Change it and save, or discard it |
| **Save the changes as a draft first** | Someone published or discarded the draft first | Look at the SOP again |
| **Pick a screen the hub has** | A step's screen is no longer in the hub | Press **Edit SOP** and choose another screen |

### Archive an SOP {#knowledge-sops-archive}

The department's head may archive its SOPs, and so may someone with **Manage SOPs** whose role may
delete in the Knowledge Hub.

1. Open the SOP and press **Archive SOP**.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Process replaced`.
3. Press **Archive SOP**. The message **SOP archived** confirms it.

It leaves the list, search and **Procedures**. Its writers still find it under the **Archived** chip,
with its versions. An archived SOP cannot be changed or brought back.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This SOP is already archived** | Someone archived it first | Nothing more to do |

## Training {#knowledge-training screen=knowledge/training icon=book needs="cap training.manage"}

Courses for MyCity's staff, built from files, videos, links to outside courses and lessons, in the
order you set. Assign a course to everyone, to departments, roles or people with a due date, or put
it in the Library for anyone to take. Whoever finishes gets a MyCity certificate carrying your
signature. People who hold **Manage Training** see this tab: Operations and People, Implementation
and the CEO, unless your roles were changed. Everyone takes their courses on
[My Training](/ess?tab=my-training).

![Training](shot:knowledge-training)

### Find a course {#knowledge-training-find}

1. Open [Knowledge Hub · Training](/knowledge?tab=training).
2. Press **Draft**, **Published** or **Archived** to see courses in that state. **All** shows every
   course.
3. Read each row: the **Course**, its **State**, how many **Items** it holds, whether it is in the
   **Library**, how many are **Assigned**, how many **Completed** it, and how many are **Overdue**.
   On a phone each course is a card showing completed of assigned.
4. Press a course to open it. On a computer it opens beside the list. On a phone it opens in a
   sheet: drag it down to close it.
   ![A course, open](shot:knowledge-course)
5. Switch between **Items**, **People**, **Assignments** and **Settings** above the course.

> **Tip:** Search finds a course from anywhere: type its title or number, such as `TRN-0001`.

### Build a course {#knowledge-training-new needs="knowledge create"}

1. Press **New Course** at the top right. On a phone, press **Quick Actions**, then **New Course**.
   From anywhere, open search and type `new course`.
2. Type its **Title**, such as `Fire Safety`, and a **Summary** if it helps.
3. Press **Create Course**. The message **Course created** confirms it. It opens as a draft.
4. Under **Items**, press **Add Item** and choose what to add:
   - **Upload File**: a PDF, a picture, a video file or any other file people read or download.
   - **Video Link**: a YouTube link plays inside the course. Any other video link opens in a new
     tab.
   - **Course Link**: a course on another website, such as a free certification. It opens in a new
     tab.
   - **Lesson**: text you write in the course itself.
   ![Add Item](shot:knowledge-add-item)
5. Type the item's **Title** and, if you know it, how many **Minutes** it takes.
6. Press **Add Item**. Items show in the order people take them.
7. To change the order on a computer, drag an item by its handle. On a phone, or from the keyboard,
   open the item's menu and press **Move Up** or **Move Down**. The menu also holds **Edit Item**
   and **Remove Item**.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Name the item** | The title is empty or too short | Type a title |
| **Use an https link** | The link does not start with `https://` | Copy the full link from the address bar |
| **At least 20 characters** | The lesson is too short | Write a little more |
| *A course holds at most 40 items* | It is full | Split it into two courses |

### Draft a lesson with AI {#knowledge-training-lesson needs="knowledge create"}

When the lesson already exists as text or a PDF, the assistant can lay it out for you.

1. In **Add Item**, choose **Lesson** and type its **Title**.
2. Press **Draft Lesson**.
3. Paste the text into **Source Text**, or add a PDF under **Or a PDF**.
4. Press **Draft Lesson**. **Drafted** shows beside **Yours**.
5. Press **Use Lesson** to put it in the form, or **Keep Mine** to leave yours.
6. Read it through, change what you need, then press **Add Item**. Nothing is kept until you do.

### Choose the course's settings {#knowledge-training-settings needs="knowledge edit"}

1. Open the course and press **Settings**.
2. Switch on **Library** to let anyone take it, assigned or not.
3. Switch on **Proof Required** to ask for proof at the end, such as an outside course's certificate.
   Type what to send under **Proof Prompt**, such as `Link to your certificate`.
4. **Issue Certificate** is on unless you switch it off.
5. Change the **Title** or **Summary** under **Course Details** and press **Save Course**.

The settings are fixed once the course is archived.

### Publish a course {#knowledge-training-publish needs="knowledge edit"}

1. Open the draft course with its items in place.
2. Press **Publish Course**.
3. Sign in **Your Signature** with your finger or the mouse. It is printed on every certificate of
   this course.
4. Press **Publish Course**. The message **Course published** confirms it.

A Library course now shows in everyone's Library. Assign it to make it someone's task.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Add an item first** | The course is empty | Add at least one item |
| **This course is already published** | Someone published it first | Look at the course again |

### Assign training {#knowledge-training-assign needs="knowledge edit"}

1. Open the published course and press **Assign Training**.
   ![Assign Training](shot:knowledge-assign)
2. Under **Who Takes It**, choose **Everyone**, or **Pick Who** and choose any **Departments**,
   **Roles** and **People**.
3. Type **Due Within (Days)** for a due date, such as `30`. Leave it empty for none.
4. Leave **Keep Assigning New Joiners** ticked to give the course to everyone who later joins those
   departments or roles.
5. Press **Assign Training**. Each person gets a task to complete the course, due on the due date,
   and a notice.

**Assignments** lists who each assignment reaches. Press **Withdraw** to take one back: people who
have not started leave the course.

### Follow who has finished {#knowledge-training-people}

1. Open the course and press **People**.
   ![People on a course](shot:knowledge-course-people)
2. Press **Not Started**, **In Progress**, **Completed** or **Overdue** to narrow the list.
   **Everyone** shows them all.
3. Read each person's **Status**, **Due** date, **Progress**, **Completed** date and **Certificate**.
4. When someone is overdue, press **Remind Overdue** to remind everyone past their due date. Each
   is reminded once a day at most.
5. Press **Download Certificate** beside someone who has finished to save their certificate.

### Archive a course {#knowledge-training-archive needs="knowledge delete"}

1. Open the course and press **Archive Course**.
2. Type the **Reason**, such as `Replaced by 2027`.
3. Press **Archive Course**. The message **Course archived** confirms it.

Its assignments are withdrawn and it leaves the Library. People who finished keep their certificates.
