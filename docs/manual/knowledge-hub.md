The Knowledge Hub holds what MyCity knows: its policies, and this User Guide. Everyone reads the
policies that apply to them on [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates) in Employee
Self-Service. This chapter is for the people who write them.

## Policies {#knowledge-policies screen=knowledge/policies icon=book}

Every company policy in one place, each with its versions: what is in force today, what starts
later, and who has acknowledged it. People who hold **Manage Policies** write and publish them:
Operations and People, Implementation and the CEO, unless your roles were changed. Anyone else does
not see this tab.

![Policies](shot:knowledge-policies)

### Find a policy {#knowledge-policies-find}

1. Open [Knowledge Hub · Policies](/knowledge?tab=policies).
2. Press a chip to narrow the list: **In Force**, **Awaiting Approval**, **Draft** or **Archived**.
   **All** shows every policy.
3. Type in **Search policies** to find one by its name or number, such as `POL-0002`.
4. Each row shows the **Policy**, its **Type**, who it **Applies To**, the version **In Force**, its
   **State**, and how many have **Acknowledged** it, such as *22 of 24*. On a phone each policy is a
   card: its name, **Applies To**, **State** and **Acknowledged**. **Type** and **In Force** show on
   a computer only.
5. Press a policy to open it. **All Policies** at the top goes back to the list.

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Nothing published yet |
| **Starts Soon** | Its first version is published and starts on a later day. Only **All** lists it |
| **In Force** | A version applies today. It may also have a draft being written |
| **Awaiting Approval** | A version waits for its approvers |
| **Archived** | It no longer applies |

> **Tip:** Search finds a policy from anywhere: type its name or number.

### Write a new policy {#knowledge-policies-new needs="knowledge create, knowledge edit"}

1. Press **New Policy** at the top right. On a phone it is under the title, or press
   **Quick Actions**, then **New Policy**. From anywhere, open search and type `new policy`.
   ![New Policy](shot:knowledge-new-policy)
2. Type its **Title**, such as `Dress Code`, and press **Start Policy**. The message
   **Policy started** confirms it, and its draft opens.
3. Under **Policy Settings**, choose who it **Applies To**: **Everyone**, or **Some People** and
   then the **Departments**, **Work Patterns** or **People** it covers.
4. Keep **Needs Acknowledgement** ticked if each person must confirm they have read it. Set
   **Acknowledge Within**, the days they have after it is published.
5. Tick **Needs Approval** if it must be approved before it is published. The CEO approves it,
   unless People changed the Policy Approval route.
6. {needs="settings edit"} To change who approves, open
   [Settings · Approvals](/settings?tab=approvals) and edit **Policy Approval**.
7. Under **Policy Text**, type the policy, or paste it from a document.
8. Press **Tidy With AI** if you want headings and lists suggested. It shows **Yours** beside
   **Tidied**, with the same words. Press **Use Tidied Text**, or **Keep Mine**.
9. Press **Save Draft**. The message **Draft saved** confirms it. Nobody is told yet.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** under **Title** | The title is empty | Type a title |
| **At least 3 characters** | The title is too short | Type a longer title |
| **Check this value** under **Applies To** | You chose **Some People** but picked nobody | Pick a department, a work pattern or a person, or choose **Everyone** |
| **At least 1** or **At most 30** | **Acknowledge Within** is out of range | Type a number of days from 1 to 30 |
| **Required** under **Acknowledge Within** | The days are empty | Type the days, such as `7` |
| **Check the highlighted fields** or **Write or paste the policy first** | **Policy Text** is empty when you press **Tidy With AI** | Type or paste the policy, then press **Tidy With AI** |
| **The tidy changed some words. Try again or tidy it by hand.** | The suggestion changed your wording, so it is not shown | Press **Tidy With AI** again, or add the headings yourself |
| **The AI service is not answering. Try again in a few minutes.** | The assistant did not answer | Wait a few minutes and press again |
| **AI features are switched off.** or **AI features are switched off for this area.** | An administrator turned the assistant off | Tidy the text yourself |
| **This month's AI spending limit has been reached.** | MyCity's AI budget for the month is spent | Tidy the text yourself, or ask an administrator |
| **The AI service is not set up correctly. An administrator has been told.** | The assistant is not set up | Tidy the text yourself |

### Change a policy in force {#knowledge-policies-change needs="knowledge edit"}

1. Open the policy. Its card shows its **Number**, **Type**, who it **Applies To**, and the version
   **In Force** with its first day.
   ![A policy, open](shot:knowledge-policy)
2. Change the settings or the text, then press **Save Draft**. On a phone, **Save Draft** is at the
   foot of the page, full width.
3. A bar above **Policy Settings** reads *Draft saved, not published.*, or names how many changes the
   draft makes.

> **Important:** For a written policy, the **Policy Text** box starts empty, even when a version is
> in force. Paste the whole text of the new version, not only the part that changed. What you
> publish replaces the version in force.

> **Important:** **Needs Approval** starts unticked in every new draft. Tick it again if this version
> must be approved.

To throw the draft away, press **Discard Changes** at the foot of the page. The hub asks
**Discard Changes**; press **Discard Changes** again. The message **Draft discarded** confirms it.
The version in force is untouched.

### Publish a policy {#knowledge-policies-publish needs="knowledge edit"}

> **Important:** Only what you saved is published. Press **Save Draft** first, after every change.

1. Open the policy with its draft saved.
2. Press **Publish Policy** in that bar. For a policy that **Needs Approval**, it reads
   **Submit for Approval**. On a phone the button sits under the bar's words.
   ![Publish Policy](shot:knowledge-publish)
3. Check the changes listed at the top.
4. Choose the **First Day** the new version applies from.
5. Add a **Note** saying what changed, such as `Grace now 10`.
6. Sign with your finger or mouse in **Your Signature**. **Clear Signature** starts again. On a
   phone the sheet fills the screen and the pad is full width.
7. Press **Publish Policy**, or **Submit for Approval**. The message *Version 2 published* confirms
   it, naming the new version. For approval it reads **Sent for approval**.

Once published, everyone it applies to is told: **New policy**, or **Policy updated** for a later
version. If it **Needs Acknowledgement**, each person is asked to acknowledge it after they clock
in, or after the sign-in prompt on a day they do not. Anyone who has not acknowledged it after 3
days gets one reminder.

A version sent for approval shows **Awaiting approval** with who it waits on. The approvers find it
under **Waiting On You** on [Policy Updates](/ess?tab=policy-updates). While it waits, the draft
cannot be changed: its fields are greyed and **Save Draft** is gone.

When the last approver approves, the version is published by itself. If its first day has passed,
it starts on the day it is approved. If an approver rejects it, you get the notice
**Policy not approved** with their reason. The version is back in the draft. Open the policy on
[Knowledge Hub · Policies](/knowledge?tab=policies), change it, and send it again.

> **Tip:** The notice may open Policy Updates and say **That policy version does not exist**. Open
> the policy from Knowledge Hub · Policies instead.

Only the person who sent it, or the CEO, can take it back:

1. Press **Recall Submission** in the blue bar.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Changing the text`.
3. Press **Recall Submission**. The message **Back to the draft** confirms it.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing happens when you press **Publish Policy** | The policy has no text yet | Press **Cancel**, write the **Policy Text**, press **Save Draft**, then publish |
| **Required** under **Note** | No note | Say what changed, such as `Grace now 10 minutes` |
| **At least 10 characters** | The note or reason is too short | Write a little more |
| **Required** under **First Day** | No first day | Pick a day |
| Pick a later date | The day you typed is in the past | Pick today or later |
| **Within 90 days** | The first day is more than 90 days away | Pick a day within 90 days |
| **After the waiting version** | Another version starts on or after that day | Pick a day after it starts, or withdraw that version |
| **Sign in the box** | The signature is empty | Sign in **Your Signature** |
| **Signature too large** | The drawing is too big to keep | Press **Clear Signature** and sign again |
| **The draft changes nothing** | The draft says what the version in force says | Change the text, a rule, the title or **Applies To**, and save |
| **Save the changes as a draft first** | Someone discarded or published the draft first | Close the sheet, make your changes, press **Save Draft** |
| **A version is already waiting for approval** | Someone sent a version first | Wait for its approval, or recall it |
| **This policy needs approval: submit it** or **This policy needs no approval: publish it** | Someone changed **Needs Approval** while your sheet was open | Close the sheet and press the bar's button again |
| **This policy is archived** | Someone archived it first | Nothing more can be published |
| **Only the person who made the request can withdraw it** | Someone else sent it | Ask them or the CEO to recall it |
| **Nothing is waiting for approval** | It was approved, rejected or recalled first | Close the sheet and look at the policy again |

### Withdraw a version before it starts {#knowledge-policies-withdraw needs="knowledge edit"}

A published version that starts on a later day shows a blue bar: *Version 3 starts 01/11/2026.*

1. Press **Withdraw Version** in that bar.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Not ready yet`.
3. Press **Withdraw Version**. The message *Version 3 withdrawn* confirms it.

Everyone it applies to is told it will not apply. If there was no draft, its text becomes the
draft, ready to change.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Only a version waiting to start can be withdrawn** | Its first day has come, or someone withdrew it first | Publish a new version instead |

### See who has acknowledged {#knowledge-policies-acknowledged}

1. Open the policy. **Versions** lists each version, newest first: its **Version**, **First Day**,
   **State**, **Published By** and how many have **Acknowledged** it.
2. **State** reads **In Force**, *Starts 01/11/2026* for one still to start, **Replaced** for an
   older one, or **Withdrawn**.
3. Press a version. Its sheet shows its **State**, **First Day**, **Applies To**, who **Published**
   it, the note, what changed, its text and its **Signatures**.
   ![A version, with who has acknowledged it](shot:knowledge-version)
4. **Download PDF** saves it with MyCity's letterhead. The message **Policy downloaded** confirms it.
   If it says **That file could not be made. Try again.** or **That file is taking too long. Try
   again.**, press **Download PDF** again.
5. Under **Acknowledgements**, choose **Acknowledged**, **Pending** or **Overdue** to see each
   group, or **Everyone**. Type in **Name** to find a person.
6. Each person shows their **Status**, the day it is **Due** and the day they **Acknowledged** it.

A withdrawn version, or one that needs no acknowledgement, has no Acknowledgements list. The list
holds only the people your role covers. On a phone, **Published By** and **Due** are not shown.

### Set the Attendance policy {#knowledge-policies-attendance needs="knowledge edit"}

The Attendance policy is MyCity's rules for the clock: working hours, lateness, deductions, overtime
and location checks. It applies to everyone, and it cannot be archived. It has no **Title** or
**Applies To** to set.

1. Open **Attendance Policy** in the list. Before any version is published, **In Force** reads
   **Default Rules**.
   ![The Attendance policy](shot:knowledge-attendance)
2. Each section is a card: **Working Hours**, **Lateness**, **Late Arrivals**, **Early Departure**,
   **Absence**, **Forgotten Clock-Outs**, **Location Flags**, **Overtime**, **Location Checks**,
   **Devices** and **Clock Prompts**.
3. Change a rule. Tick **On** under a rule to use it: its figures appear beneath. A rule you changed
   is marked **Changed**. Press a rule's name to read what it decides.
4. Press **Save Draft**, then publish it as any policy. The figures are checked when you save.

A new version applies from its first day. Days before it keep the rules they were judged by.

| Card | Rule | What to set |
|---|---|---|
| **Working Hours** | **Company Hours** | Tick **Set Company Hours**, then each day's **Working**, **Starts** and **Ends**. Unticked, the working week on Settings applies |
| **Lateness** | **Grace Minutes** | Minutes after the start before a clock-in is late, 0 to 60 |
| **Late Arrivals** | **Late Arrivals** | **On**, then **Count** (1 to 31), **Or Hours** (optional, 0.5 to 40), **Period** (**Week**, **Month** or **30 Days**), **Alert** and **Raise Query** |
| **Late Arrivals** | **Late Deduction** | **On**, then **Method**: **Pro-Rata by Minute**, **Fixed per Hour** or **Fixed per Day**, and the **Amount** (₦1 to ₦1,000,000) for a fixed one |
| **Early Departure** | **Early Grace Minutes**, **Early Departures**, **Early Deduction** | As for lateness, for leaving before the end |
| **Absence** | **Absences**, **Absence Deduction** | **Absences** counts as above. **Absence Deduction** **On** takes a day's pay for each absent day |
| **Forgotten Clock-Outs** | **Forgotten Clock-Outs** | Counts System Clock-Outs and days with no clock-out |
| **Location Flags** | **Location Flags** | Counts clock-ins outside a place of work that still stand |
| **Overtime** | **Overtime** | **On**, then **After Minutes** (0 to 120), **Approval** (**Line Manager After**, **Pre-Approved** or **Automatic**), **Paid As** (**Multiple of Hourly Rate** or **Fixed per Hour**), **Multiple** or **Amount an Hour**, **Rest Day Multiple** (1 to 3) and **Monthly Cap Hours** (1 to 100) |
| **Location Checks** | **Office Check** | **Off**, **Flag** (accepted and sent to People) or **Refuse** (not accepted) |
| **Location Checks** | **Home Location** | **On** asks home workers to set a home. Then **Home Check** (**Flag** or **Refuse**) and **Home Radius** (150 to 500 metres) |
| **Location Checks** | **Temporary Site Check** | **Flag** or **Refuse** |
| **Location Checks** | **GPS Accuracy** | Metres, 50 to 250. A less accurate reading cannot confirm where someone is |
| **Devices** | **Device Check** | **Off**, **Flag** or **Refuse** a clock-in from an unregistered device |
| **Clock Prompts** | **Clock-Out Reminder** | How often, in minutes (15 to 60), someone still clocked in is reminded |
| **Clock Prompts** | **System Clock-Out** | Minutes after the end (30 to 240) before the system clocks someone out |

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| At least, or At most, then a figure | The figure is out of range | Type a figure in the range the table gives |
| **Required** | A figure is empty | Type it |
| **Enter the amount** | A fixed deduction or **Fixed per Hour** overtime has no amount | Type the amount, or choose another method |
| **₦1 to ₦1,000,000** | The amount is out of range | Type an amount in that range |
| **At least 150** | **Home Radius** is under 150 metres | Type 150 or more |
| **Flag or Refuse** | A check must flag or refuse | Choose **Flag** or **Refuse** |
| **At least one working day** | No day is ticked **Working** | Tick at least one day |
| **Check this value** under Monday's **Working** | A working day ends before it starts, or has no times | Check each day's **Starts** and **Ends** |

### Archive a policy {#knowledge-policies-archive needs="knowledge delete"}

1. Open the policy and press **Archive Policy** at the foot of its card.
2. Type the **Reason**, at least 10 characters, such as `Replaced by the handbook`.
3. Press **Archive Policy**. The message **Policy archived** confirms it. It leaves Policy Updates,
   and its versions are kept.

An archived policy cannot be changed or brought back. Its versions and PDFs can still be read.

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This policy is already archived** | Someone archived it first | Nothing more to do |

### When a change is refused {#knowledge-policies-problems}

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Recall the version waiting for approval first** | Someone sent a version for approval while you edited | Recall it, then save |
| **This policy is archived** | Someone archived it while you edited | Nothing more can be saved |
| **There is no draft** | Someone discarded or published the draft first | Look at the policy again |
| **You do not have access to this** | Your role may read policies but not do this | Ask People to change your role on Settings · Roles & Permissions |
